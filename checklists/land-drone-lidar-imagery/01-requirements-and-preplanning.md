## 1. Feasibility, Modality Selection & Ontology Gates

Before committing airframes or field crews, verify that UAS LiDAR and/or RTK/PPK photogrammetry solves the terrain question and beats crewed airborne mapping on cost, resolution, and canopy penetration ([`fable` Ch. 16](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md), [Ch. 26](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md), [Ch. 28](../../fable/chapters/ch28-reducing-cost.md); [`gemini4` §7.3](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-03.md)).

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Platform Break-Even Audit vs. Crewed Airborne ALS:** Confirm project AOI, corridor length, and repeat cadence fall within typical UAS planning estimates (`< 1–2.5 km²/day` multirotor; `< 1–20 km²/day` fixed-wing/VTOL) versus crewed aircraft (`> 20–100 km²` planning estimate; `fable §28.2.4` notes UAVs win below a few `km²` while crewed aircraft amortize mobilization above a few tens of `km²`, rising steeply below `~100 km²`). — *Evidence:* Signed break-even worksheet in [the cost ledger](../_shared/template-cost-ledger.md) (`crew-days + control hours`). | *Ref:* [Ch. 28 §28.2.4](../../fable/chapters/ch28-reducing-cost.md), [`gemini4` §7.3](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-03.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **DSM vs. Bare-Earth DTM Ontology Under Canopy:** Select sensor modality strictly from vegetation cover: multi-return UAS LiDAR (`>= 3–7` returns) is **mandatory** whenever bare-earth DTM is required under forest or dense scrub, because optical SfM requires two unobstructed views per ground point and produces only a DSM under closed canopy (and `+0.10–0.50 m` grass/marsh bias). Budget `30–60 %` ground returns in leaf-off deciduous woods, `5–20 %` leaf-on, and `1–5 %` in dense conifer/tropical canopy, specifying survey-grade scanners (`miniVUX` / `CL-360`, `2–4 cm` `RMSE_V`) over mid-grade solid-state units (`5–10 cm` warm, `10–30 cm` cold/fast turns) where dense canopy continuity is contractual. — *Evidence:* Sensor selection memo matched to canopy cover strata. | *Ref:* [Ch. 16](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md), [Ch. 18](../../fable/chapters/ch18-topographic-lidar.md), [Ch. 22](../../fable/chapters/ch22-photogrammetry-sfm.md), [Ch. 32](../../fable/chapters/ch32-dsm-to-dtm.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **ASPRS Ed. 2 + Addendum V Accuracy Class & CRS Lock:** Freeze target `RMSE_V`, `RMSE_H`, `RMSE_3D`, optical `GSD` (`0.5–3.0 cm`) or LiDAR `ANPD` (`50–400 pts/m²`), and grid spacing (`0.05–0.50 m`) under ASPRS PAS Edition 2 (v2, 2024) + Addendum V (`RMSE_rep = sqrt(RMSE_test^2 + sigma_cp^2)` with no Ed. 1 `1.96×` multiplier or VVA pass/fail gate) and USGS LBS 2025 rev. A. Lock horizontal CRS + epoch (e.g., `NAD83(2011) / UTM`), vertical datum (`NAVD88` via `GEOID18`), and SI meters (banning deprecated US Survey Foot, retired `2022-12-31`). — *Evidence:* Initialed [Project Header](../_shared/template-project-header.md) with explicit EPSG/WKT2 and accuracy targets. | *Ref:* [Appendix D](../../fable/appendices/appendix-d-standards-index.md), [Ch. 53](../../fable/chapters/ch53-accuracy-assessment.md), [Ch. 56 Case 56.6](../../fable/chapters/ch56-case-files.md), [Ch. 70](../../fable/chapters/ch70-specifications-guided-tour.md).

## 2. Prior Data, Phenology & Site Reconnaissance

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Prior DEM, Airspace & Obstacle Desktop Recon:** Screen existing public elevation datasets (`USGS 3DEP`, `OpenTopography`, `NOAA Digital Coast`) for coarse terrain relief (`\Delta Z`), tower/guy-wire/powerline obstacles, canopy top vs. bare-ground height differences, airports/heliports, special-use airspace, and clear LZs (`>= 30°` unobstructed sky view). — *Evidence:* Annotated recon KML/GIS layer showing obstacles, LZs, and airspace classes. | *Ref:* [Ch. 26](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md), [Appendix G §G.1](../../fable/appendices/appendix-g-checklists.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Phenology, Snowpack & Soil-Moisture Verification:** Verify deciduous leaf-off window (`Sentinel-2`/`Landsat` NDVI check), `0 cm` snow cover for bare-earth DTMs (or explicitly scope snow-depth mapping vs. LAS Class 21 perennial snowpack), and absence of surface ponding (`< 100 %` soil saturation) that causes specular matching voids. — *Evidence:* Timestamped satellite NDVI / webcam snow check archived in project folder. | *Ref:* [Ch. 30](../../fable/chapters/ch30-point-cloud-classification.md), [Ch. 56 Case 56.17](../../fable/chapters/ch56-case-files.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** **Intertidal / Riparian White-Ribbon Window Coordination:** *Trigger:* Site borders coastal beaches, tidal marshes, or navigable rivers mapped concurrently by ASV, bathylidar, or crewed vessels. Lock one unified vertical datum/SEP and schedule low-water UAS sorties (`within 1.5 hr` of predicted spring low water) to overlap high-water marine lines by `100–500 m`. — *Evidence:* Synchronized tide-window schedule signed by marine and UAS leads. | *Ref:* [White-Ribbon Sync](../_shared/multimodal-white-ribbon.md), [Ch. 34](../../fable/chapters/ch34-water-in-dems.md).

## 3. Logistics, Environmental Envelope & Governance Gates

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Battery Logistics & Field Charging Architecture:** Size flight-pack count and charging throughput via:
  $$N_{\text{sorties}} = \left\lceil \frac{T_{\text{mission,total}}}{T_{\text{eff}}} \right\rceil, \qquad T_{\text{eff}} = T_{\text{rated}} \times (1 - r_{\text{reserve}}) \times f_{\text{cold}}$$
  with landing reserve $r_{\text{reserve}} = 0.25\text{–}0.30$ and cold-weather derating $f_{\text{cold}} = 0.75\text{–}0.85$ below `5 °C`. Size portable DC/AC stations or inverter generators to match solar windows (`> 30°`) and schedule recharge lead time if packs air-ship at `<= 30 %` SoC under `UN 3480/3481`. — *Evidence:* Sortie-battery matrix & charger load sheet attached to flight plan. | *Ref:* [Ch. 16](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md), [02-permits-safety-insurance-cost.md](02-permits-safety-insurance-cost.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Meteorological Go/No-Go, Statutory Visibility & Abandonment Thresholds:** Enforce quantitative field go/no-go thresholds in Project Instructions: sustained wind `< 8–10 m/s` (`gusts < 12 m/s`, since `10 m/s` gusts force `15–25°` tilt and motion smear), sun elevation `> 30°` (`30–45°` near water/glint), `0 mm/hr` precipitation/fog, cloud clearance `>= 152 m (500 ft)` below and `>= 610 m (2,000 ft)` horizontal (`14 CFR § 107.51(d)`), flight visibility `>= 4.8 km (3 SM)` (`§ 107.51(c)`), `Kp < 5`, and ceiling `<= 120 m` (satisfying both `122 m` / `400 ft AGL` FAA `§ 107.51(b)` and `120 m` EASA Open). Embed `10–50 %` site-month weather standby days and a hard financial abort trigger (`>= 3` consecutive blown days with `< 20 %` window left). — *Evidence:* Signed Go/No-Go & Abandonment clause in Project Instructions. | *Ref:* [Ch. 12](../../fable/chapters/ch12-gnss.md), [Ch. 16](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md), [Ch. 26](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md), [Ch. 28](../../fable/chapters/ch28-reducing-cost.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** **Checkpoint Strategy & Canopy Total-Station Feasibility:** Confirm access and safety permit `>= 30` independent withheld checkpoints per stratum (`>= 30` bare NVA + `>= 30` vegetated VVA where DTM is contracted), budgeting GNSS-RTN in open sky plus closed-loop total-station traverses under closed canopy where GNSS fails (`>= 10–30×` accuracy ratio). — *Evidence:* Preliminary CP siting map + total-station labor estimate. | *Ref:* [Ch. 52](../../fable/chapters/ch52-ground-truth.md), [`gemini4` §12.3](../../gemini4/book/ch-12/sec-12-03.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** **Transient Object & Site Activity Coordination:** Identify dynamic interference (haul trucks, cranes, livestock, watercraft, highway traffic) and coordinate work pauses or multi-view dynamic masking (`withheld` bit / LAS Class 7/18). — *Evidence:* Site hazard/activity coordination notes with facility manager. | *Ref:* [Ch. 27](../../fable/chapters/ch27-moving-and-transient-objects.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** **Data Naming, Formats & 3-2-1 Backup Architecture:** Pre-define sortie naming, LAS 1.4 PDRF `6–10` (`adjusted GPS time`), `COG` (`ZSTD`/`PREDICTOR=3`), `COPC`, and `STAC 1.1.0` schemas plus dual field SSDs + offsite cloud archival (`>= 2×` raw volume margin, retaining raw sensor files, trajectories, and pinned software containers). — *Evidence:* Data Management Plan (DMP) directory & naming convention spec. | *Ref:* [Ch. 47](../../fable/chapters/ch47-file-formats.md), [Ch. 50](../../fable/chapters/ch50-archiving-and-provenance.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** **Field Spares & Redundancy Kit:** Pack matched spare prop sets (`>= 2`), skids, `>= 4` V30/V90 SD cards, hot-shoe trigger cables, tripod/tribrach/batteries, optical targets, anemometer, and backup airframe/payload when remote remob cost exceeds `3×` hardware value. — *Evidence:* Initialed pre-deployment packing manifest. | *Ref:* [Appendix G §G.1](../../fable/appendices/appendix-g-checklists.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** **Project Initiation Header Sign-Off:** Complete [the shared project header](../_shared/template-project-header.md) and record dual sign-off for Gate **G-01** in [00-README-and-signoff.md](00-README-and-signoff.md). — *Evidence:* Completed G-01 row in `00-README-and-signoff.md`. | *Ref:* [Project Header](../_shared/template-project-header.md).
