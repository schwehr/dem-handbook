## 1. Mechanical Isolation, Shutter Physics & Time Synchronization

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Vibration Isolator Resonance & Rigid Antenna Stiffness Check:** Inspect all elastomeric damper balls, Sorbothane bushings, or wire-rope isolators mounting the LiDAR/camera gimbal for cracks, compression set, or loose standoffs. Verify mount natural frequency stays well below rotor blade-passage frequency:
  $$f_n = \frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac{k_{\text{mount}}}{m_{\text{pay}}}} \ll \frac{f_{\text{blade}}}{\sqrt{2}}, \qquad f_{\text{blade}} = N_b \cdot \frac{\mathrm{RPM}}{60}$$
  (`typical f_n = 25–40 Hz vs. f_blade = 100–375 Hz`). Confirm the **primary GNSS/PPK rover antenna is rigidly tied to the IMU/LiDAR structural frame** — never mounted across soft dampers unless dynamically modeled, as isolator sag modulates lever arms under climbs/turns. — *Evidence:* Pre-flight isolator photo + mount stiffness checkoff signed by Flight Engineer & Verifier. | *Ref:* [`gemini4` §7.3](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-03.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Global vs. Rolling Shutter & Motion-Smear Budget:** Record camera shutter architecture and readout duration $t_{\text{readout}}$ (**simultaneous global/mechanical shutter** `<= 1–3 ms` exposure vs. `15–35 ms` row-sequential rolling readout). Quantify rolling-shutter cross-frame displacement:
  $$\Delta x_{\text{ground}}(r) = \left(v_g + H_{\text{AGL}}\,\omega_p\right)\,t_{\text{readout}}\,\frac{r - R_{\text{rows}}/2}{R_{\text{rows}}}$$
  (`30 ms` at `v_g = 12 m/s`, `H_AGL = 80 m`, `ω_p = 5 °/s` shifts rows **`56.9 cm` top-to-bottom**). Mandate global/mechanical shutter for `<= 3 cm` `RMSE_V` engineering surveys; if rolling shutter is unavoidable (`[COND]`, *Trigger:* non-metric RGB payload), cap cruise speed `<= 6–8 m/s` and enable linear rolling-shutter optimization during bundle adjustment. — *Evidence:* Camera shutter datasheet check & mission speed cap entry. | *Ref:* [Ch. 22](../../fable/chapters/ch22-photogrammetry-sfm.md), [`gemini4` §7.3](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-03.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Hot-Shoe Mid-Exposure $\Delta t_{\text{sync}}$ & PPS/ZDA Verification:** Bench-test hardware flash hot-shoe event pulses (`EventIn` to GNSS/INS logger) aligned to **mid-exposure** (`<= 0.2–0.5 ms` sync jitter). Quantify uncalibrated antiparallel-strip vertical stereo warp from trigger delay $\Delta t_{\text{sync}}$:
  $$\delta z \approx \frac{H_{\text{AGL}}}{B}\left(2\,v_g\,\Delta t_{\text{sync}}\right)$$
  (e.g., `5 ms` USB shutter lag at `v_g = 20 m/s` and `B/H = 0.5` warps elevation by **`40 cm`**, while `1 ms` latency at `15 m/s` shifts along-track positions by `1.5 cm` plus $R\dot{\theta}\delta t$). For LiDAR payloads receiving NMEA `$GPZDA` + `1PPS`, verify `>= 115200 bps` and matching `1PPS` edge polarity to prevent off-by-1-second timing jumps. — *Evidence:* Bench event-log parity test (`1:1` shutter count vs `EventIn`) + latency residual check. | *Ref:* [Ch. 13](../../fable/chapters/ch13-imu-ins.md), [`gemini4` §7.3](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-03.md), [`gemini4` §32.2](../../gemini4/book/ch-32/sec-32-02.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Rigid Lever-Arm Survey & Frame Axis Audit (`FRD`/`RFU` vs. `NED`/`ENU`):** Measure physical 3D offsets ($\boldsymbol{\ell}_{\text{IMU}\to\text{ant}}$, $\boldsymbol{\ell}_{\text{IMU}\to\text{LiDAR}}$, $\boldsymbol{\ell}_{\text{IMU}\to\text{cam}}$) to **`<= 2–5 mm` (`1σ`)** via CAD/total-station/calipers. Independently verify vector **origin-to-target direction** ($\boldsymbol{\ell}_{\text{IMU}\to\text{GNSS}}$ vs. $\boldsymbol{\ell}_{\text{GNSS}\to\text{IMU}}$) and distinguish aircraft **body axes** (**`FRD`**: `+x` nose, `+y` starboard, `+z` down; vs. **`RFU`**: `+x` starboard, `+y` nose, `+z` up) from **local-level mapping frames** (`NED` vs. `ENU`). — *Evidence:* Dual-signed lever-arm & sign-convention diagram archived in [Project Header](../_shared/template-project-header.md). | *Ref:* [`gemini4` Notation & Symbols §1](../../gemini4/book/00-front-matter/notation-and-symbols.md), [Ch. 25](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** **Optics Lock, Offline NFZ Unlock, Remote ID & Airframe Pre-Flight:** Clean LiDAR window and lens, tape manual focus/aperture at infinity (`OIS/IBIS off` so decentering/focal length stay fixed), balance props, confirm **offline OEM geofence/NFZ unlock tokens** before leaving cell coverage, and verify active **`14 CFR Part 89` Remote ID** broadcast via RF scanner (`10–15 min` cold-morning IMU warmup where required). — *Evidence:* Bench pre-flight & Remote ID broadcast checkoff signed on `09` Template B. | *Ref:* [Ch. 18](../../fable/chapters/ch18-topographic-lidar.md), [Ch. 22](../../fable/chapters/ch22-photogrammetry-sfm.md), [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md).

## 2. Local RTK/PPK Base Station & Geodetic Control

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Local RTK/PPK Base Station Baseline & OPUS Static Tie:** Deploy a dedicated multi-frequency GNSS base receiver within **`<= 10–15 km`** of every AOI corner (`<= 20 km` ceiling, since baseline errors grow `~1 ppm` plus $2\text{–}3\,\delta_{\text{ZTD}}$ tropospheric vertical amplification over relief). Log raw observables (`RINEX 3.x / UBX / SBF` at `>= 1 Hz`, `>= 5 Hz` preferred for high-dynamic UAS) for **`>= 2–4 hr`** (`>= 4 hr` strongly recommended for vertical ZTD decorrelation) over a verified Class A/B/C mark or submit to **NGS OPUS Static / CSRS-PPP**, logging datum details in [Project Header](../_shared/template-project-header.md). — *Evidence:* Continuous `>= 4 hr` base RINEX file + OPUS/CSRS-PPP solution PDF. | *Ref:* [Ch. 12](../../fable/chapters/ch12-gnss.md), [`gemini4` §5.2](../../gemini4/book/ch-05/sec-05-02.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **ANTEX Calibration, Dual Antenna-Height Check & 2-Mark Tie:** Lock exact antenna model + radome code (`igs20.atx` ANTEX PCO/PCV), have two observers measure **both vertical and slant antenna heights** in meters (`mm` precision) before AND after occupation, capture a timestamped close-up tape photograph against ARP/BHI, and tie the base daily to **at least one independent second benchmark** to catch `0.10–0.20 m` single-base datum blunders. — *Evidence:* Timestamped ARP tape photos (`start/end`) + 2-mark closure check (`<= 2 cm`). | *Ref:* [Ch. 12](../../fable/chapters/ch12-gnss.md), [Ch. 25](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** **VRS / NRTK Cellular Fallback Verification:** *Trigger:* Site topography or corridor length uses cellular NRTK/VRS instead of radio RTK. Require simultaneous `1–5 Hz` onboard raw GNSS logging for PPK fallback and shoot a local published NGS mark before/after flights (`<= 2 cm` H, `<= 3 cm` V `95 %` check). — *Evidence:* Independent NRTK monument check shot + continuous rover RINEX log. | *Ref:* [Ch. 12](../../fable/chapters/ch12-gnss.md).

## 3. Strict GCP vs. Independent Withheld Checkpoint (CP) Wall

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Strict File & Workflow Separation (`gcps.csv` vs. `cps_withheld.csv`):** Maintain two physically separate CSV files enforcing the [Ch. 25 four-question checkpoint independence test](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md): *(1)* zero shared coordinates enter SfM bundle adjustment, LiDAR strip adjustment, or vertical datum removal; *(2)* checkpoints come from an independent observation session (separate base, redundant `180 s` RTN bipod NGS-58/59 occupations at `~2.1 cm` `1σ` vertical, or closed-loop total station); *(3)* checkpoints span interior, perimeter edges (`5–15 %`), slopes, and canopy strata — never clustered near the LZ. — *Evidence:* Separate SHA-256 hashed `gcps.csv` & `cps_withheld.csv` matching [09 templates](09-resources-and-templates.md). | *Ref:* [Ch. 25](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md), [Ch. 52](../../fable/chapters/ch52-ground-truth.md), [Ch. 56 Case 56.23](../../fable/chapters/ch56-case-files.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Convex-Hull + Interior + Full Z-Span GCP Geometry:** Place `>= 5–8` distributed GCPs bracketing the **convex hull perimeter, block interior, and minimum-to-maximum site elevations (`\Delta Z`)**. Never place GCPs solely along valley roads, which permits uncompensated focal-length correlation ($\delta f \leftrightarrow \delta H$) to hide `3 cm` valley fit while inducing `-46.3 cm` ridge scaling error. — *Evidence:* GCP elevation histogram & spatial convex-hull map overlay. | *Ref:* [`gemini4` §12.3](../../gemini4/book/ch-12/sec-12-03.md), [Ch. 22](../../fable/chapters/ch22-photogrammetry-sfm.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Minimum Checkpoint Sample Size (`>= 30` per Stratum):** Survey **`n >= 30` independent bare/hard NVA checkpoints** plus **`n >= 30` vegetated VVA checkpoints** where DTM bare-earth accuracy is contracted (`60–120` on major blocks). Report `95 %` $\chi^2$ RMSE uncertainty bounds explicitly:
  $$\left[\mathrm{RMSE}\sqrt{\frac{\nu}{\chi^2_{0.975,\nu}}},\;\mathrm{RMSE}\sqrt{\frac{\nu}{\chi^2_{0.025,\nu}}}\right], \qquad \nu = n$$
  (`0.80–1.34×` at $n = 30$, narrowing to `0.88–1.16×` at $n = 100$). — *Evidence:* Stratified CP summary table (`N_NVA >= 30`, `N_VVA >= 30`) + expected $\sigma_{\text{cp}}$ entered in [Error Budget](../_shared/template-error-budget.md). | *Ref:* [Ch. 52](../../fable/chapters/ch52-ground-truth.md), [Ch. 53](../../fable/chapters/ch53-accuracy-assessment.md), [Ch. 70](../../fable/chapters/ch70-specifications-guided-tour.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Target Material, Size (`5–10 GSD`) & Retroreflective Ban:** Deploy **matte Lambertian checkerboards, crosses, Siemens stars, or AprilTags sized `5–10 GSD`** (`8–12 GSD`, e.g., `15–30 cm` arms @ `2 cm` GSD) staked flush (`<= 2 mm` sag). For **LiDAR intensity targets**, deploy **`1.0–2.0 m` diffuse `~50 %`–reflectance pads/chevrons** and **strictly ban retroreflective glass-bead tape/signs**, which saturate APD/SiPM receivers and create **`3–8 cm` leading-edge range walk**. — *Evidence:* Target specification sheet + deployed pad photos (`09` Template C1/C2). | *Ref:* [Ch. 25](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md), [`gemini4` §9.1](../../gemini4/book/ch-09/sec-09-01.md), [`gemini4` §12.3](../../gemini4/book/ch-12/sec-12-03.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** **Under-Canopy VVA Total-Station Traverse & Marsh Settlement Plate:** *Trigger:* Site requires bare-earth VVA validation under closed forest canopy or across unconsolidated marsh peat. Survey `>= 30` closed-loop total-station checkpoints tied between open-sky GNSS control pairs (`<= 5–10 mm` `1σ`, never trusting under-canopy GNSS `fix`), and rest a `>= 15–20 cm` rigid perforated marsh settlement plate flush on peat before taking elevation shots. — *Evidence:* Adjusted total-station traverse closure report (`<= 1:20,000`) & marsh-plate field photos. | *Ref:* [Ch. 52](../../fable/chapters/ch52-ground-truth.md), [`gemini4` §9.7](../../gemini4/book/ch-09/sec-09-07.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** **Target Geo-Photo Log & Boresight Gable-Roof Test Setup:** Photograph every GCP/CP with point ID, horizon context, and plumb rod bubble level ([09-resources-and-templates.md](09-resources-and-templates.md)), log $\sigma_{\text{cp}}$ into [the error budget template](../_shared/template-error-budget.md), establish an orthogonal opposite-heading gable-roof/asphalt boresight patch if unverified, and record Gate **G-03** in [00-README-and-signoff.md](00-README-and-signoff.md). — *Evidence:* Target photo archive (`IMG_*`) + signed G-03 row. | *Ref:* [Ch. 25](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md), [09-resources-and-templates.md](09-resources-and-templates.md).
