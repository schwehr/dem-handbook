## 2. Immediate Field Triage Gates (Preserve Life First, Evidence Second)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Flyaway, Lost-Link or Controlled Airspace Incursion Response:** Immediately log GCS telemetry (`heading`, `groundspeed`, `altitude`, `battery %`, last lat/lon, UTC time). Execute RTH/flight termination only clear of bystanders/structures; if entering `Class B/C/D`, heliport routes, or crewed traffic corridors, **call ATC Tower / FSS / CTAF immediately** with last vector/altitude and log `C/N0` + AGC drops for GNSS spoofing/jamming forensics. — *Evidence:* Timestamped GCS telemetry log + ATC call log on [Accident Card](../_shared/template-accident-card.md). | *Ref:* [Ch. 56 Case 56.19](../../fable/chapters/ch56-case-files.md), [Ch. 69](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Tree, Powerline or Structure Strike:** **Prohibit climbing trees or approaching downed powerlines!** Maintain **`>= 10 m`** standoff from energized lines/poles until the electric utility verifies de-energization and grounding. Secure ground exclusion (`>= 15–30 m`), coordinate emergency services/arborist rigging if overhead hang-up threatens roads/pedestrians, and capture pre-removal photos. — *Evidence:* Utility clearance ticket + *in situ* pre-removal photos. | *Ref:* [Accident Card](../_shared/template-accident-card.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Bystander / Property Strike & Medical Response:** Disarm all motors immediately, administer first aid, and dial `911` for any head trauma, laceration, eye injury, or loss of consciousness. De-escalate bystanders, gather witness/owner contacts and scene photos without admitting legal fault, and log initial entries on [the accident card](../_shared/template-accident-card.md). — *Evidence:* Completed medical triage & witness block on Accident Card. | *Ref:* [Accident Card](../_shared/template-accident-card.md), [Ch. 68](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Airframe Safe-State & Crash-Site Containment:** Before handling downed wreckage, keep clear of jammed propellers, check LiPo temperature (`< 55 °C`, zero hissing/swelling/smoke), disconnect main flight power wearing insulated gloves and eye protection, and keep non-crew `>= 15 m` away. — *Evidence:* Disarm/power-isolation verification logged before transport. | *Ref:* [Ch. 16](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** **Public Roadway / Traffic Stoppage & Law Enforcement Liaison:** *Trigger:* Forced landing or debris lands on a roadway, shoulder, or public right-of-way. Post a spotter in ANSI/ISEA 107 Class 2/3 vest upstream of traffic, call local police/DOT immediately for traffic control, and clear lanes only after *in situ* reference photos unless immediate life-safety requires removal. — *Evidence:* Police CAD/incident number + lane-clearance photo. | *Ref:* [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **LiPo Battery Thermal Runaway / Fire Suppression (`SDS` Rule):** Standard dry-chemical `ABC` and `CO2` extinguishers **fail** to halt internal lithium-polymer cell thermal runaway. Evacuate upwind from toxic HF/CO smoke and deploy **copious water deluge, AVD (`Lith-EX`), or `F-500` encapsulator** per the battery **SDS** (**note:** `Class D` dry powder applies **strictly** to primary lithium-metal `UN 3090` cells, NOT rechargeable LiPo `UN 3480/3481` packs). Submerge/bury cooling cells in dry sand or a fire-rated steel drum **outside vehicles and buildings**, quarantining **`>= 24–48 hr`** before transport. — *Evidence:* SDS-compliant suppression log + 24–48 hr quarantine tag. | *Ref:* [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** **Water Ditching / Immersion Payload Salvage:** *Trigger:* Aircraft ditches into river, marsh, pond, or coastal surf. Recover only with **`>= 2` personnel**, PFD, and safe non-swiftwater footing. Disconnect LiPos underwater/immediately upon retrieval (quarantining wet cells outdoors), extract SD cards for drying, rinse saltwater off sealed optics/sensors with clean freshwater/deionized rinse *after* battery removal, and seal desiccant-packed sensors for factory service. — *Evidence:* Water-immersion salvage log & battery quarantine entry. | *Ref:* [Accident Card](../_shared/template-accident-card.md).

## 3. Statutory Reporting, Evidence Freeze & Post-Impact Recalibration

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Flight Telemetry, SD Media & Airspace Record Freeze (`49 CFR § 830.10` / `FRE 901–902`):** Immediately write-protect and copy flight controller logs (`.DAT`/`.ULG`/`.BIN`), GNSS/IMU raw files, base RINEX, GCS screen recordings, METAR/wind logs, LAANC/NOTAM/COA screenshots, and `sha256sum` manifests into an immutable incident vault supporting **`FRE 901 / 902(13)–(14)`** authentication. Protect injured-person medical/PII records (e.g., EMS/laser OCT files) in restricted access; never format SDs or disturb wreckage until FAA/NTSB release. — *Evidence:* Immutable SHA-256 incident manifest & restricted PII log. | *Ref:* [Accident Card](../_shared/template-accident-card.md), [Ch. 47](../../fable/chapters/ch47-file-formats.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Independent Repair-Cost vs. Fair Market Value Appraisal:** Capture multi-angle time-stamped photos of third-party property damage and obtain two written repair vs. FMV quotes within `<= 48 hr` to document objectively whether FAA `> $500` (`§ 107.9`) or NTSB `> $25,000` (`§ 830.5(a)(6)`) triggers apply (lesser of repair vs. FMV). — *Evidence:* Appraisal quotes & photo packet attached to incident file. | *Ref:* [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Mandatory Statutory Notification Execution (Explicit Separate Clocks):** Execute exact statutory clocks without delay:
  - *Immediate (`0 min–1 hr`):* Call **NTSB ROC (`+1-844-373-9922`)** if `49 CFR § 830.2` (death, serious injury, or substantial damage to an airworthiness-certificated UAS) or `§ 830.5(a)(6)` (`> $25,000` third-party property, midair, or control malfunction) triggers.
  - *`<= 8 hr` / `<= 24 hr`:* Notify **OSHA (`29 CFR § 1904.39`)** within `8 hr` for fatality or `24 hr` for hospitalization, amputation, or eye loss.
  - *`<= 24–72 hr` / `<= 72 hr`:* Notify **Aviation Insurer** per policy clock (`24–72 hr` typical) and **EASA NAA** (`(EU) 376/2014`, `<= 72 hr`) if non-U.S.
  - *`<= 10 calendar days`:* File **FAA DroneZone `14 CFR § 107.9`** report AND **NTSB Form `6120.1/6120.2` (`49 CFR § 830.15`)** if triggered.
  — *Evidence:* Confirmation numbers & UTC timestamps for each triggered filing. | *Ref:* [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md), [Accident Card](../_shared/template-accident-card.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** **Cyber, RF Spoofing or Sensitive Imagery/PII Breach:** *Trigger:* Theft/loss of unencrypted SSD, tablet, or airframe carrying PII (`GDPR Art. 4(1)`), protected cultural sites (`ARPA`/`CARE`), or `EAR/ITAR` hardware (`7A003`/`6A008`). Notify Legal/Security immediately, file **`GDPR Art. 33` supervisory notice within `<= 72 hr`** where applicable, revoke RF keys, and remote-wipe tablets. — *Evidence:* Security/GDPR Art. 33 filing & key-revocation log. | *Ref:* [Ch. 69](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md), [`gemini4` §27.2](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-02.md), [`gemini4` §27.3](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-03.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** **Lost-Aircraft / Downed Beacon Search Protocol:** Plot last GCS coordinate + wind-drift cone within `<= 30 min`, deploy two-person search teams with radios/flags along grid transects, and ping LoRa/Bluetooth/Cellular Remote ID or onboard acoustic buzzer trackers before batteries deplete. — *Evidence:* Search track GPX & recovery timestamp. | *Ref:* [Accident Card](../_shared/template-accident-card.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Post-Impact Isolator, Boresight & Camera Interior-Orientation (`IO`) Recalibration Gate:** Any hard landing (`> 3–4 g` peak), tip-over, prop strike, transport drop, or water immersion invalidates prior IMU–LiDAR–camera lever arms, boresight angles, **AND camera lens interior orientation (`f, c_x, c_y, k_1..k_3, p_1, p_2`)** (crash shock shifts lens decentering even with taped focus rings). Ground the payload until replacing damaged isolators ($f_n \ll f_{\text{blade}}/\sqrt{2}$), inspecting structural mounts, and passing fresh gable-roof boresight + oblique camera calibration (`<= 1–2 cm` opposite-heading closure). — *Evidence:* Post-incident boresight & camera-IO re-cal report signed by Verifier. | *Ref:* [Ch. 13](../../fable/chapters/ch13-imu-ins.md), [Ch. 22](../../fable/chapters/ch22-photogrammetry-sfm.md), [Ch. 25](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md), [`gemini4` §7.3](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-03.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** **Crew Stand-Down & Return-to-Flight Briefing:** Conduct a non-punitive safety stand-down reviewing root cause before resuming sorties; record clearance or Gate **G-06** sign-off in [00-README-and-signoff.md](00-README-and-signoff.md). — *Evidence:* Signed safety stand-down memo & G-06 row. | *Ref:* [Accident Card](../_shared/template-accident-card.md).
