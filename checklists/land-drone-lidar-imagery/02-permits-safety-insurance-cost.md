## Part A: Pre-Mobilization Administration (Permits, Legal, Insurance, Cost)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Airspace, Weight-Class & Operational Authorization (`14 CFR Part 107` / `49 U.S.C. § 44807` / EASA SORA):**
  - *US `< 25 kg` (`< 55 lb`):* Verify Remote Pilot Certificate (`14 CFR Part 107`), FAADroneZone registration, active broadcast **Remote ID (`14 CFR Part 89`)**, and **LAANC / DroneZone** clearance (`Class B/C/D/E`). Confirm standard compliance or approved waivers for: **`§ 107.29`** (civil twilight/night requires flashing anti-collision light visible `>= 4.8 km` / `3 SM` + recurrent training; waiver needed only to fly unlit), **`§ 107.31`** (VLOS; waiver for BVLOS), **`§ 107.35`** (multi-UAS ops waiver), **`§ 107.39`** (ops over people Categories 1–4 or waiver), and **`§ 107.51(b)`** (`122 m` / `400 ft AGL` or `122 m` structure radius; waiver/COA to exceed).
  - *US Heavy-Lift `>= 25 kg` (`>= 55 lb`):* Require active **`49 U.S.C. § 44807` exemption + Part 47 registration + COA** (ineligible for Part 107).
  - *Europe / International:* Secure **EASA `(EU) 2019/947`** Open (`A1–A3`, `120 m` AGL cap) or Specific **SORA** operational authorization (`SAIL I–VI`), GEO-zone unlocks, and host-nation permits.
  — *Evidence:* Approved LAANC/DroneZone PDF, COA/§ 44807 exemption, Remote ID serial & PIC certificate scan in flight binder. | *Ref:* [Ch. 16](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md), [Ch. 69](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md), [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Landowner Right-of-Entry (ROE) & Restricted Public-Land Permits:** Secure signed ROE agreements for LZs, staging areas, and ground-control traverses. Confirm zero unauthorized entry onto **National Park Service** (`36 CFR § 1.5`), **USFS Wilderness** (`36 CFR § 261.18`), **USFWS Refuges**, **Bureau of Reclamation**, or DoD lands without written Special Use Permits. — *Evidence:* Executed ROE PDFs and federal/park permit numbers logged in binder. | *Ref:* [Ch. 68](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md), [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **PLS Surveying Licensure (*Ritter*), CARE/OCAP® & Privacy Compliance:**
  - *PLS Licensure (`*360 Virtual Drone Services LLC v. Ritter*, 102 F.4th 263, 4th Cir. 2024; SCOTUS cert denied Apr 2026`):* Verify state/national surveying-board rules before selling measurable topographic DEMs, contours, volumes, or boundary orthomosaics; operate under active **PLS/PE** seal where required, noting that an informal "not-for-survey" disclaimer alone may not suffice if selling measurable mapping products.
  - *Tribal Sovereignty & Cultural Protection:* Complete Tribal consultation enforcing **CARE** & **OCAP®** governance on sovereign lands, and redact archaeological feature coordinates (`NHPA § 106` / `ARPA`, `16 U.S.C. § 470hh`).
  - *Privacy & Mosaic Risk:* Enforce **GDPR `(EU) 2016/679`**, **Texas Gov. Code Ch. 423**, and **Florida § 934.50** by keeping sensors off outside target polygons, blurring faces/license plates (`GSD <= 8 cm`), and completing release screening.
  — *Evidence:* PLS sign-off/scope letter, Tribal/CARE consultation record, and privacy-masking SOP. | *Ref:* [Ch. 68](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md), [Ch. 69](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md), [`gemini4` §27.1](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-01.md), [`gemini4` §27.2](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-02.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Dedicated Aviation Insurance — Rejecting CGL Aviation Exclusion:** Audit Certificates of Insurance (COIs) in [the shared insurance template](../_shared/template-insurance-coi.md) to eliminate **ISO `CG 00 01` Exclusion (g)** (standard CGL excludes aircraft/UAS BI & PD, and narrow `CG 24 50` endorsements omit hull, mounted sensors, and survey E&O). Verify active policies meeting contract limits (`$________`, quote-dependent): (1) **Third-Party Aviation Liability**, (2) **UAS Hull (agreed value)**, (3) **Scheduled Payload Inland Marine Floater** with **Condition & Value (`C&V`) appraisal**, (4) **Professional / Tech E&O**, (5) **Cyber Liability / Data Breach** (raw imagery, PII, GCS/cloud telemetry), (6) **Commercial Auto** (trucks hauling LiPos/trailers), (7) **Workers' Compensation (+ `FVWC / Medevac` if OCONUS)**, (8) **Waiver of Subrogation & Primary/Non-Contributory** endorsements, and (9) **COI expiry `>= 30 days` past weather-extended completion**. — *Evidence:* Endorsed COI PDFs + policy exclusion audit table signed in binder. | *Ref:* [Insurance COI](../_shared/template-insurance-coi.md), [Ch. 68](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md), [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md).
  - [ ] **[COND]** *Overwater / Class 3B–4 Laser Endorsements:* *Trigger:* Flight polygon crosses navigable water, marsh, or coastal shoreline OR payload emits Class 3B/4 laser radiation. Add explicit **Overwater Operations Endorsement** and/or **Class 3B/4 Laser/Ocular Endorsement** on Aviation Liability. — *Evidence:* Policy endorsement schedule uploaded to binder. | *Ref:* [Insurance COI](../_shared/template-insurance-coi.md), [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Lithium Battery Hazmat Transport (`UN 3480` / `UN 3481` / `UN 3090` / `UN 3091`):** Enforce **IATA DGR 67th Edition (eff. Jan 1, 2026)**, FAA PackSafe, and **`49 CFR Parts 171–180`** compliance:
  - **`UN 3480` (PI 965 — standalone spare Li-ion/LiPo packs):** Forbidden on passenger aircraft; Cargo Aircraft Only (`CAO`) mandatory **`<= 30 %` SoC**, terminal protection, and DG declaration. *Passenger carry-on (`FAA PackSafe`):* `<= 100 Wh` unrestricted in carry-on; `100–160 Wh` max **`2` spare packs** in carry-on only with airline approval (**never checked baggage**).
  - **`UN 3481` (PI 966 — packed *with* equipment, Section I & Section II `> 2.7 Wh`):** Effective **Jan 1, 2026**, mandatory **`<= 30 %` SoC** (`<= 25 %` indicated fuel gauge). **`PI 967`** (installed *in* equipment): `<= 30 %` SoC strongly enforced operationally.
  - **`49 CFR 171–180` & `UN 3090/3091`:** Follow U.S. ground transport Class 9 placarding/packaging for heavy-lift packs `> 300 Wh` and declare primary lithium-metal cells (`UN 3090/3091`) in recovery beacons/trackers. Carry printed **UN 38.3 Test Summaries** and **SDS**; reject swollen or `> 0.05 V/cell` imbalanced packs from air transport.
  — *Evidence:* Pre-ship battery SoC log (`09` Template A) + printed UN 38.3 & SDS folder. | *Ref:* [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md), [Ch. 16](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** **Export Control (EAR/ITAR), Foreign Geofencing & RF Security:** *Trigger:* Cross-border mobilization, foreign nationals on crew, or dual-use tactical IMU/anti-jam GNSS/high-power LiDAR hardware. Verify **EAR ECCNs** (`7A003` inertial, `7A005` GNSS, `6A008` LiDAR; `ITAR USML` where applicable) + ATA Carnet prior to shipment, obey national mapping bans (e.g., China GCJ-02 / India 2021 guidelines), lock `AES-128/256` telemetry (`FCC Part 90` if licensed), and disable cloud auto-sync on field tablets. — *Evidence:* Export control / Carnet clearance & encrypted link checkoff. | *Ref:* [Ch. 68](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md), [`gemini4` §27.3](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-03.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** **Lifecycle Cost Ledger & Standby Trigger Lock:** Populate [the cost ledger](../_shared/template-cost-ledger.md) separating combined field-crew + control-survey labor (planning share `~40–60 %` of small-AOI UAS budget, quote-dependent estimate), sensor amortization, permits/COIs/hazmat freight, software seats, cloud storage (**hot/standard tier `~$0.02/GB-month`** vs. **cold archive `~$0.001–$0.004/GB-month`**) & egress (`~$0.05–$0.09/GB`), `5–10 %` QA budget, and standby abort cap. — *Evidence:* Approved project cost baseline sheet. | *Ref:* [Cost Ledger](../_shared/template-cost-ledger.md), [Ch. 28](../../fable/chapters/ch28-reducing-cost.md), [`gemini4` §27.4](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-04.md).

## Part B: Daily Pre-Sortie Safety & Burn-Rate Gates (Offline Field Ready)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Live Airspace, TFR & Multi-Asset Deconfliction Check:** Before first daily launch, check live **TFRs / NOTAMs / B4UFLY / LAANC**, verify zero active wildfire/VIP TFRs, and complete radio check (`CTAF` monitor near non-towered fields; VHF Ch 16 if synchronizing coastal white-ribbon vessel/ALB ops). — *Evidence:* Dated offline screenshot / voice-log entry of TFR/NOTAM check. | *Ref:* [White-Ribbon Sync](../_shared/multimodal-white-ribbon.md), [Ch. 69](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Density Altitude Hover-Power Derating:** Compute local density altitude ($\rho_{\text{air}}$) from site pressure and temperature before takeoff. Hover power scales as:
  $$P_{\text{hover}} \propto m_{\text{tot}}^{3/2}\,\rho_{\text{air}}^{-1/2}$$
  producing **`+10.3–13.2 %` ISA hover power at `2,000–2,500 m` MSL** (`+12–15 %+` on warm summer afternoons) and **`+25.6 %` (`~+26 %`) at `4,500 m` MSL**, cutting climb margin and endurance by `20–35 %`. Hold or shed payload if takeoff weight falls below `>= 30 %` excess hover-thrust margin. — *Evidence:* Calculated density-altitude & thrust margin entry on `09` Template B card. | *Ref:* [`gemini4` §7.3](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-03.md), [Ch. 16](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **RTH Altitude, Lost-Link Failsafe & Perimeter Exclusion:** Program Return-to-Home (`RTH`) **`>= 15–30 m` above tallest tree, tower, or ridge** along any return azimuth while remaining strictly **`<= 120 m`** (clearing both `122 m` / `400 ft AGL` FAA `§ 107.51(b)` and `120 m` EASA Open). Verify lost-link failsafe, enforce `>= 15–30 m` public exclusion (`>= 100 m` heavy-lift/fixed-wing), and brief Visual Observer (`VO`) sectors. — *Evidence:* GCS RTH parameter check & crew tailgate safety briefing signed on Template B. | *Ref:* [Ch. 16](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md), [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** **Wildlife Buffers, Class 3B/4 Laser NOHD & Roadside Crew Safety:** *Trigger:* Site contains sensitive wildlife, Class 3B/4 UAS LiDAR, or roadside GCP/CP traverses. Enforce `>= 100–300 m` wildlife buffers (`abort immediately` on flush/disturbance); for Class 3B/4 lasers compute Nominal Ocular Hazard Distance:
  $$\mathrm{NOHD} = \frac{1}{\beta_t}\left(\sqrt{\frac{4\Phi_{\text{eff}}}{\pi\,\mathrm{MPE}}} - D_0\right)$$
  (watching `ENOHD` for `Class 1M` binocular hazard), file **`FAA AC 70-1B / Form 7140-1`** if applicable, and lock laser interlock below `NOHD` AGL; equip road control crews with **ANSI/ISEA 107 Class 2/3 vests**, MUTCD cones/signs, and lone-worker check-in timers. — *Evidence:* Wildlife standoff log, NOHD calculation / Form 7140-1, or MUTCD tailgate checkoff. | *Ref:* [Ch. 69](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md), [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Crew Fatigue & Daily Weather Burn-Rate Check:** Enforce max `10–12 hr` crew duty day with mandatory VO rotation every `45–60 min`. Compare cumulative weather standby burn against [the cost ledger](../_shared/template-cost-ledger.md) abort cap prior to calling hold days; record Gate **G-02** in [00-README-and-signoff.md](00-README-and-signoff.md). — *Evidence:* Daily crew duty/burn-rate ledger entry & G-02 sign-off. | *Ref:* [Cost Ledger](../_shared/template-cost-ledger.md).
