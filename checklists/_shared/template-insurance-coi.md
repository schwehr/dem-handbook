# Shared Template 0.5 — Pre-Mobilization Insurance & Certificate of Insurance (COI) Matrix

> **Purpose:** Verify active Certificates of Insurance (COIs), statutory maritime/aviation/professional lines, policy exclusions, and institutional self-insurance boundaries **before** any vessel sails, aircraft lifts, drone launches, or field crew deploys. Private survey firms do **not** share government sovereign immunity or FTCA discretionary-function protection ([Chapter 68](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md) §68.4, [Gemini4 Sec. 27.1.1](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-01.md)).

## 2. UNOLS / NSF / Federal & Institutional Self-Insurance Protocol

When operating aboard UNOLS, NOAA, or other public/academic vessels or aircraft, verify statutory risk allocation in writing rather than assuming "the ship covers our gear":

- [ ] **Institutional / Federal Property-at-Sea Verification:** Confirm whether PI-owned or NSF/NDSF pooled sensors (`Sentry`, `Jason`, shipboard vans, user-supplied sonars/AUVs) are **self-insured by the home institution/agency** or require an explicit commercial **Marine Equipment Floater** prior to crane lift.
- [ ] **UNOLS Charter Party & Hold-Harmless Review:** Verify University/Operator P&I and Jones Act status for embarked science personnel vs. crew, and document any federal agency self-insurance letters in the Marine Facilities Planning (MFP) package.
- [ ] **Subcontractor / Vendor Flow-Down:** Verify that commercial vessels of opportunity (VOOs), chase boats, helicopter contractors, or Part 107 subcontractors supply independent COIs naming the PI institution/client as Additional Insured.
