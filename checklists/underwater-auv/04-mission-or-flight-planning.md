## 1. Flight-Mode Envelope, Swath Overlap & SLAM Crossline Design

- [ ] **[GATE]** Select and document **altitude control mode** per sub-polygon based on terrain slope and obstacle risk:
  - **Terrain-following mode (`h_alt = 5–70 m` above bottom):** Default over benign-to-moderate seabed (`slopes <= 20–25°`), holding constant acoustic footprint (`e.g., 0.44 m along-track x 0.87 m across-track at 50 m altitude for 0.5° x 1.0° MBES`) and uniform swath width (`W = 2 h_alt tan(theta/2)`).
  - **Constant-depth isobaric mode (layered depth steps):** Mandatory over vertical walls, volcanic scarps (`> 35°`), hydrothermal chimneys, or dense rig/wreck superstructure where steep forward terrain can stall altitude-hold loops or saturate pitch/OAS thrusters; enforce forward-looking Obstacle Avoidance Sonar (`OAS`) range threshold `>= 10–15 m` (or `>= 5 s` stopping distance) and hard minimum depth ceiling.
  - *Evidence:* Mission Line File (`.pln` / `.xml` / `GeoJSON`) tagged by mode + max terrain slope check against prior DEM. *Ref:* [ch16-platforms.md](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md), [sec-07-04.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-04.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Design **limiting-case line spacing** for **`100–200 %+` swath coverage** (`>= 20–50 %` sidelap for single-pass holiday prevention; `100 %` overlap = `200 %` double coverage for `CUBE` noise suppression, outer-beam grazing trimming to usable `110–130°`, and **SAS/SSS nadir-gap fill** where the `15–20°` directly under-keel nadir wedge has poor cross-track resolution), checking **minimum vehicle turning radius (`R_min ~ 10–30 m`)** so lines narrower than `2 R_min` fly teardrop or racetrack turn interleaving without missing turn-entry lock. *Threshold:* Compute swath width using the **shallowest / minimum altitude (`h_min`)** in each block (`e.g., 50 m` line spacing for `100–120 m` usable MBES swath gives `>= 100 %` overlap margin; for SAS with a `20 m` nadir blind zone at `200 m` swath, offset parallel lines so opposite swath sweet spots cover every nadir track). *Evidence:* Swath Coverage & Turn-Geometry Simulation Plot confirming `0 %` planned holidays. *Ref:* [ch26-survey-planning.md](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md), [ch20-sonar.md](../../fable/chapters/ch20-sonar.md), [sec-13-01.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-13/sec-13-01.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Incorporate **orthogonal crosslines and bathymetric SLAM (`mbnavadjust`) loop-closure tie-lines** explicitly into every mission plan (never treat an AUV block as unconstrained parallel strips). *Threshold:* Include `>= 5–10 %` total line-km in orthogonal crosslines PLUS perimeter tie-lines spaced so dead-reckoned DVL-INS loop durations remain `<= 30–60 min` (limiting raw INS drift between tie crossings to `<= 2–8 m` for `0.1 % DT` at `2.0 m/s`), ensuring distinct seafloor morphology at crossings to avoid false loop closures over flat abyssal mud. *Evidence:* Line Plan showing tie-line spacing, loop durations, and crossing morphology verification. *Ref:* [ch15-slam.md](../../fable/chapters/ch15-slam.md), [ch26-survey-planning.md](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md), [sec-07-04.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-04.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** Eliminate **SAS/SSS nadir-gap blind zones** and lock acoustic backscatter comparability across adjacent lines by offsetting parallel lines for `200 %` swath interleave (`>= 20–30 m` inner-beam overlap across the `15–20°` nadir wedge) and fixing sonar pulse length, transmit power, and receiver gain schedules per polygon. *Evidence:* SAS/MBES Nadir-Overlap & Gain-Lock Table. *Ref:* [ch20-sonar.md](../../fable/chapters/ch20-sonar.md), [sec-10-02.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-10/sec-10-02.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** Plan coordinated **multi-modal white-ribbon tie-lines** (`>= 100 %` swath overlap with hull-mounted MBES, USV, or topo-bathy LiDAR polygons within the same `2–72 h` tidal/sediment window) (*Trigger:* Survey abuts `< 50 m` shelf edges or combines ship `12/30 kHz` bathymetry with AUV `200–400 kHz` patches). *Evidence:* White-Ribbon Overlap Polygon & Timing Schedule per [multimodal-white-ribbon.md](../_shared/multimodal-white-ribbon.md). *Ref:* [ch48-compositing.md](../../fable/chapters/ch48-compositing.md), [sec-13-01.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-13/sec-13-01.md).

## 2. Patch Test, Descent Drift & Battery Abort Reserves

- [ ] **[GATE]** Plan a dedicated **4-step AUV MBES/SAS Patch Test** at the start of survey operations (and after any payload re-seat or hard knock) following strict dependency order so parameters remain numerically orthogonal:
  1. **Latency (`Delta t`):** **Collinear same-direction (identical track & azimuth) passes at `>= 2x` speed ratio** (`1.0 m/s` vs `2.3 m/s`) over a steep ridge/feature (`slope 15–30°`) — never opposite headings, which confound pitch with latency.
  2. **Pitch (`Delta theta`):** Exact opposite reciprocal (`180°`) passes at matching speed over the same perpendicular feature (`15–30°` slope).
  3. **Roll (`Delta phi`):** Reciprocal opposite (`180°`) passes over a smooth, flat seabed (`slope <= 2°`).
  4. **Yaw (`Delta psi`):** **Adjacent parallel same-direction passes** offset so opposite outer swaths straddle a distinct proud 3D feature/slope (keeping roll/pitch signs unchanged).
  - *Threshold:* Include `>= 30–50 m` (`15–25 s`) straight settle lead-in before each line so INS roll/yaw transients settle after turns; verify patch-test Jacobian observability matrix has full rank (`4`). *Evidence:* Patch-Test Waypoint Plan & Turn-Settle Verification. *Ref:* [ch20-sonar.md](../../fable/chapters/ch20-sonar.md), [sec-10-06.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-10/sec-10-06.md), [sec-13-05.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-13/sec-13-05.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Calculate **water-column descent drift & spiral-descent offset** for the period between surface GNSS fix release and seabed DVL bottom-lock acquisition (`~30–200 m` above bottom). *Threshold:* Model integrated horizontal drift over full sink time (`e.g., 3,000 m` depth at `0.8–1.2 m/s` sink rate `= 42–63 min` un-aided descent; a `0.4 m/s` mean current drifts the AUV `1.0–1.5 km` laterally); place the descent waypoint up-current and outside hazard corridors, schedule shipboard USBL aiding pings every `30–60 s` during descent, and program a post-lock transit/align leg (`>= 200 m`) before starting Line 1. *Evidence:* Descent Drift Vector & Bottom-Lock Acquisition Waypoint Plan. *Ref:* [sec-07-04.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-04.md), [ch14-positioning-beyond-gnss.md](../../fable/chapters/ch14-positioning-beyond-gnss.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Compute the complete **energy budget & hard `20–30 %` battery SoC abort/ascend reserve** before approving mission waypoints. *Threshold:* Sum descent hotel+propulsion energy + line-survey energy (using `P_prop ~ v^3` corrected for worst-case head current, plus MBES/SAS/SBP/DVL/INS continuous draw) + turn overhead + ascent reserve; set automated mission-abort threshold strictly at **`20–30 %` remaining State of Charge (`SoC`)** (never `< 20 %`) so the vehicle retains propulsion and telemetry power for safe surface recovery. *Evidence:* Power & Endurance Budget Spreadsheet showing planned end-of-mission `SoC >= 25 %`. *Ref:* [ch16-platforms.md](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md), [sec-07-04.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-04.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** Program hard **3D geofence corridors, minimum seabed safety altitude (`>= 3–5 m` hardware/software floor), emergency ascent corridors, and a pre-designated safe surfacing/abort rally waypoint** cleared of surface vessel traffic lanes, overhead ice keels, or suspended cables. *Evidence:* Geofence & Rally-Point Check Plot exported from mission planner. *Ref:* [ch26-survey-planning.md](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** Pre-plan the support vessel's **underway acoustic-tracking station/racetrack** inside the `<= 30–45°` optimal USBL acoustic cone above the AUV (`avoiding propeller cavitation wake` and thermocline acoustic shadow rays) to guarantee continuous two-way telemetry and aiding updates. *Evidence:* Support-Vessel Tracking Corridor Overlay. *Ref:* [ch14-positioning-beyond-gnss.md](../../fable/chapters/ch14-positioning-beyond-gnss.md), [ch20-sonar.md](../../fable/chapters/ch20-sonar.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** Coordinate **acoustic frequency guard-bands or TDMA trigger synchronization** (*Trigger:* Multi-AUV swarming or simultaneous shipboard `12/30 kHz` MBES, `ADCP`, and `USBL` operations). *Threshold:* Maintain `>= 10–20 kHz` spectral separation or hardware trigger gating so ship emitters do not inject cross-talk noise stripes into AUV swaths. *Evidence:* Acoustic Interference Compatibility Matrix. *Ref:* [sec-10-06.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-10/sec-10-06.md), [ch20-sonar.md](../../fable/chapters/ch20-sonar.md).

## 3. Worked *A Priori* TPU (`THU_95%` / `TVU_95%`) & Observability Check

- [ ] **[GATE]** Compute and verify the quantitative ***a priori* Error Budget** in [template-error-budget.md](../_shared/template-error-budget.md) before launch, combining every underwater-specific source in quadrature (`RSS`) and checking against `IHO S-44 Ed. 6.2.0 (Oct 2024; core Table 1 TVU/THU a/b values unchanged from Ed. 6.1.0, adding §3.8 seabed characteristics)` limits (note `fable/` cites Ed. 6.1.0 baseline). Below is the benchmark `d = 3,050 m` seabed baseline (`3,000 m` AUV operating depth, `p = 3,046.26 dbar`, `h_alt = 50 m`, `theta = 60°` outer beam, `10 min` USBL/sparse-LBL smoothing window per `gemini4 §5.4`):

| Error Component (`d = 3,050 m`, `h_alt = 50 m`) | Vertical `1 sigma` (`m`) | Horizontal Per-Axis `1 sigma` (`m`) | Physical Mechanism & Mitigation |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| Paroscientific Digiquartz (`4,000 dbar` FS, `0.01 %` FS) | `0.40` | `—` | Raw pressure offset (`-45.4 m` at `3,000 dbar`) removed via TEOS-10 + deck tare |
| Atmospheric Barometric Tare (`P_atm(t)`) | `0.05` | `—` | Continuous ship barometer subtracted in post-processing |
| TEOS-10 / Saunders–Fofonoff Steric Profile (`Delta D`) | `0.12` | `—` | Integrated from full-depth calibrated CTD profile + `g(lat)` |
| Surface GNSS / Tide / `SEP` Datum Transfer | `0.10` | `0.05` | Vessel PPP-AR/RTK GNSS + hydrodynamic tide / `SEP` model |
| Raw Single-Ping USBL (`R = 3,100 m`, `sigma_alpha = 0.15°`) | `—` | `8.12` (unfiltered) | Per-axis `1 sigma` (`THU_95%,raw = 2.4477 * 8.12 = 19.87 m`; MRU roll/pitch `5.24 m` at `0.1°`) |
| INS/DVL + USBL + Sparse-LBL (RTS Smoothed) | `—` | `1.15` (filtered) | DVL-INS `0.1 % DT` drift subsumed into filtered `1.15 m` per-axis `1 sigma` (`10 min` ties) |
| AUV MBES (`h_alt = 50 m`, `60°` beam, `sigma_phi = 0.02°`) | `0.05` | `0.08` | Short `100 m` slant range suppresses deep-water roll amplification |
| **Combined `d = 3,050 m` Total Uncertainty** | **`0.435` (`TVU_95% = 0.85 m`)** | **`1.154` per-axis (`THU_95% = 2.82 m`)** | **`TVU_95% = 1.96 * sigma_z`; 2D Rayleigh `THU_95% = 2.4477 * sigma_1D = 1.7308 * DRMS`** |

  - *Threshold:* Modeled `TVU_95% = 0.85 m` and `THU_95% = 2.82 m` pass **S-44 Order 2** (which governs `> 100 m` depth: `TVU_max = 70.16 m`, `THU_max = 325.0 m` at `d = 3,050 m`) AND **Order 1a** reference (`TVU_max = 39.65 m`, `THU_max = 157.5 m`) across the outermost retained beam. On steep seabed slopes (`alpha`), compute slope-coupled vertical uncertainty at consistent 1D/2D scaling: `sigma_z,eff = sqrt(sigma_z,0^2 + tan^2(alpha) * sigma_1D,H^2)` so **`TVU_95%,eff = 1.9600 * sigma_z,eff = sqrt(TVU_95%,0^2 + (1.9600 / 2.4477)^2 * tan^2(alpha) * THU_95%^2)`**. *Evidence:* Signed *A Priori* TPU Spreadsheet. *Ref:* [sec-05-04.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-05/sec-05-04.md), [ch14-positioning-beyond-gnss.md](../../fable/chapters/ch14-positioning-beyond-gnss.md), [sec-14-02.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-14/sec-14-02.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** Audit **state observability** for DVL scale (`s_DVL`), DVL yaw (`delta_psi_DVL`), USBL lever arms, and ocean current: ensure the mission plan contains non-collinear reciprocal/orthogonal headings and speed variations where required rather than single-heading lawnmowers that leave horizontal scale/current and yaw/roll biases confounded. *Evidence:* Observability / Turn-Geometry Review Note. *Ref:* [ch13-imu-ins.md](../../fable/chapters/ch13-imu-ins.md), [sec-13-05.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-13/sec-13-05.md).
