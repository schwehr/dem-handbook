## 1. Preserve Life & Immediate Stand-Off Priorities

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Human life and vessel safety override vehicle salvage in every emergency (`100 %` non-negotiable priority):** sound shipboard General Alarm/Emergency Muster if any fire, gas leak, crane/LARS rigging failure, or man-overboard occurs; clear all non-essential personnel from aft working deck and crane swing zones immediately. *Evidence:* Bridge Emergency Log & Muster Count. *Ref:* [template-accident-card.md](../_shared/template-accident-card.md); LARS deck operations per `UNOLS RVSS`.
- [ ] **[GATE]** Establish **Li-Ion Battery Thermal Runaway & Over-Pressurized Housing Exclusion Zone (`>= 15–30 m` clearance)** immediately upon detecting smoke, acrid electrolyte odor, housing temperatures `> 60–70 °C`, hissing relief valves, or suspected internal hull flooding after ascent:
  - **NEVER loosen end-cap bolts on an unvented suspect housing:** seawater reacting with compromised Li-ion cells produces pressurized hydrogen gas (`H_2`), turning sealed titanium/aluminum end-caps into lethal projectiles.
  - Deploy thermal imaging camera from cover, verify automated pressure-relief bleed from safe stand-off, apply **continuous copious seawater boundary cooling (`>= 60 min` past ambient cooldown) or Aqueous Vermiculite Dispersion (`AVD` / `Lith-EX`) per Li-ion `SDS`** (reserve **`Class D` dry powder strictly for primary lithium-metal `UN 3090` cells** — Class D does not arrest Li-ion thermal runaway), and execute pre-briefed overboard containment/jettison SOP only if uncontained fire threatens ship integrity.
  - *Evidence:* Deck Safety Officer Hazard Clearance & Thermal Monitoring Log. *Ref:* Li-ion `SDS` & OEM pressure-hull emergency SOP via [External Regulatory Basis Register](./09-resources-and-templates.md#external-regulatory--standards-basis-register-non-handbook-authorities).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Stage **marine medical & hazmat first-aid response** alongside active emergency communications (**`DSC VHF Ch. 70` / `Voice Ch. 16`**, satellite phone, Coast Guard `SAR` / **Telemedical Assistance Service (`TMAS`)**) during any on-deck casualty: verify eyewash/shower flush readiness for electrolyte/chemical splash, trauma kit accessibility, and unimpeded helideck/small-boat `MEDEVAC` clearing. *Evidence:* Deck Casualty Response & Comms Readiness Entry. *Ref:* [template-accident-card.md](../_shared/template-accident-card.md); flag-state SOLAS / medical watchbill.

## 2. Non-Surfacing, Entanglement & Under-Ice Search Protocol

- [ ] **[GATE]** Execute the structured **Overdue / Non-Surfacing AUV Search Sequence** within `15 min` of a missed scheduled ascent (`ETA + 15 min` acoustic confirmation window):
  1. **Verify acoustic burn-wire / drop-weight command:** transmit emergency abort + ascent ballast drop continuously on primary and backup acoustic frequencies (`USBL / acoustic modem`) across expanding concentric vessel circles.
  2. **Do NOT leave the dive box early:** hold station above last USBL/LBL fix and project a **drift-box corridor** incorporating surface windage + integrated water-column current profile over elapsed ascent time.
  3. **Respect the galvanic corrodible-link release timer (`~1.5x–2.0x` planned dive duration):** track countdown carefully on bridge clock — premature departure before galvanic link dissolution risks abandoning a positively buoyant vehicle surfacing hours later.
  4. **Listen on all recovery frequencies:** activate directional acoustic emergency pinger hydrophone (`8–16 kHz` transponder interrogation, `12 kHz`, and `27–37.5 kHz` dedicated pinger bands), scan **Iridium SBD (`1616–1626.5 MHz`)** and **Argos (`401.65 MHz`)** satellite telemetry portals for surfacing bursts, sweep **VHF RF direction-finding beacon channels (`156–162 MHz` marine / assigned DF frequency)**, and post elevated bridge visual + X-band radar lookouts for **Xenon strobe** flashes (**`5.6–9.3 km (3–5 nmi)`** night visibility).
  - *Evidence:* Time-Stamped Search Grid Plot, Drift Vector Table & Beacon Reception Log. *Ref:* [ch14-positioning-beyond-gnss.md](../../fable/chapters/ch14-positioning-beyond-gnss.md), [sec-05-04.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-05/sec-05-04.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** Escalate **surface drift tracking & maritime recovery advisory** (*Trigger:* Iridium, Argos, or VHF beacon confirms AUV on the surface beyond immediate visual/radar horizon for `> 30 min`). *Threshold:* Plot every satellite/DF hit inside a hydrodynamic wind-leeway + surface-current forecast cone, confirm safe sea state (`Hs <= 2.5 m`) before dispatching small-boat RHIB recovery, and broadcast `PAN-PAN` / `NAVWARN` if drifting near vessel lanes. *Evidence:* Live Surface Drift Forecast Track & Bridge Relay Log. *Ref:* [ch16-platforms.md](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md), [ch68-legal-issues.md](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** Execute **Seabed Entanglement (Fishing Gear / Kelp / Cable) Recovery Protocol** (*Trigger:* USBL/LBL confirms vehicle stationary near bottom despite full-up thruster/ballast drop commands). *Threshold:* **Halt blind high-RPM reverse commands** (reversing tangles monofilament/lines tighter around prop hubs); record locked 3D coordinates (`THU_95%` pinned via multi-azimuth USBL passes), evaluate acoustic release status, and mobilize work-class ROV or diver intervention (within safe `< 40 m` air/nitrox depth) with sharp line-cutting tooling. *Evidence:* Stationary Beacon Triangulation Box & Salvage Mobilization Request. *Ref:* [ch16-platforms.md](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md), [sec-07-04.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-04.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** Execute **Under-Ice Shelf / Sea-Ice Cavity Trapping Contingency** (*Trigger:* AUV loses upward open-water acoustic homing corridor or collides with keel draft under pack/shelf ice). *Threshold:* Switch immediately to pre-programmed autonomous **ice-edge homing / reciprocal exit track** toward the launch polynya/icebreaker transponder; if pinned under mobile floes, deploy shipboard through-ice acoustic relays (`10–15 km` range tracking) and clear a surface extraction lead with icebreaker support before battery `SoC` drops below beacon-survival reserve. *Evidence:* Ice-Keel Acoustic Homing Log & Beacon Coordinates. *Ref:* [ch66-coastal-marine-polar-lakes-rivers.md](../../fable/chapters/ch66-coastal-marine-polar-lakes-rivers.md), [ch14-positioning-beyond-gnss.md](../../fable/chapters/ch14-positioning-beyond-gnss.md).

## 3. LARS Failure, Data Loss & Chain-of-Custody Evidence Freeze

- [ ] **[COND]** Respond to **LARS Crane / A-Frame Wire Snap, Hook Release Failure, or Deck Crush Incident** (*Trigger:* Mechanical rigging failure or personnel contact during launch/recovery). *Threshold:* Freeze failed wire rope, shackles, snatch blocks, and crane load-cell logs untouched (tag `"DO NOT ALTER — EVIDENCE"`); inspect AUV titanium pressure hulls, syntactic foam, and sonar transducers via NDT/vacuum check before any subsequent dive (`0` uninspected re-launches after deck impact). *Evidence:* Rigging Failure Tag & NDT/Vacuum Re-Inspection Record. *Ref:* [ch16-platforms.md](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md) (LARS); rigging evidence handling per `UNOLS RVSS`.
- [ ] **[GATE]** Immediately freeze **Black-Box Telemetry & Raw Evidence Chain-of-Custody (with Privacy/PII Protection)** after any vehicle loss, grounding, flood, or hardware casualty: isolate raw topside `USBL/LBL` ping files, acoustic modem transcripts, ship bridge ECDIS/GNSS tracks, ADCP current profiles, CTD/SVP casts, pre-dive ballasting/O-ring/vacuum logs, and BMS telemetry with cryptographic **SHA-256 digests (`FRE 901 / 902(13)–(14)`)** onto write-protected offline media; segregate witness statements, crew medical/SMI logs, and personal identifiers (`PII`) under restricted HR/legal-counsel access. *Threshold:* `100 %` logs archived untouched within `4 h` of incident before topside software restarts or circular ring-buffers overwrite. *Evidence:* Signed Incident Evidence & PII Segregation Manifest (`sha256sum`). *Ref:* [template-accident-card.md](../_shared/template-accident-card.md), [ch50-archiving-and-provenance.md](../../fable/chapters/ch50-archiving-and-provenance.md), [ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md), [sec-19-01.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-19/sec-19-01.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** Execute **Personal / Medical PII & Data-Breach Notification (`GDPR Art. 33`)** (*Trigger:* Unencrypted witness/casualty medical PII, crew SMI records, or bystander/vessel-crew identifiers are exposed, lost, exfiltrated, or breached during incident response or storage failure). *Threshold:* Isolate affected storage/cloud buckets (preserve volatile logs, revoke credentials), notify Institutional Data Protection Officer (`DPO`) / Legal Counsel immediately, and file **GDPR Art. 33 supervisory-authority breach notification within `<= 72 h`** where GDPR jurisdiction applies (or equivalent regional breach statute). *Evidence:* Breach Containment Log, DPO Notification Timestamp & Regulatory Filing Receipt (`<= 72 h`). *Ref:* [template-accident-card.md](../_shared/template-accident-card.md), [ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md), [sec-27-02.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-02.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** Execute **Raw Survey Data Corruption / Ransomware / Shipboard Storage Failure Recovery** (*Trigger:* SSD read errors, NAS RAID degradation, or cyber compromise). *Threshold:* Disconnect affected workstation from vessel/satellite networks immediately, protect the untouched physical AUV NVMe cartridges (halt wipe/format across all fleet vehicles), verify clean `3-2-1` offline air-gapped copy via `SHA-256`, and re-ingest cleanly onto quarantined hardware. *Evidence:* Air-Gapped Checksum Recovery Log. *Ref:* [ch50-archiving-and-provenance.md](../../fable/chapters/ch50-archiving-and-provenance.md), [ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** Document **Unrecoverable Seabed Loss Environmental Closure** (*Trigger:* Active search & salvage deferred or suspended inside protected/coastal-state waters). *Threshold:* File exact last-known acoustic coordinates (`THU_95%`), battery cell chemistry/mass, sacrificial anodes, and biodegradable compensator oil volume (`< 5 L`) with environmental regulators so permit conditions are cleanly closed. *Evidence:* Seabed Abandonment Coordinate & Environmental Dossier. *Ref:* [ch68-legal-issues.md](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md), [ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md).
