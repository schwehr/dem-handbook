## 1. Decision Loss Function & Specification Lock

- [ ] **[GATE]** Define the downstream engineering or scientific decision loss function and map it to a formal hydrographic standard (`IHO S-44 Ed. 6.2.0 (Oct 2024; core Table 1 TVU/THU a/b values unchanged from Ed. 6.1.0, adding §3.8 seabed characteristics)` Order or Custom Survey Matrix criteria, `NOAA HSSD 2024`, or site-specific engineering spec; note `fable/` cites the Ed. 6.1.0 baseline). *Threshold:* Explicitly state maximum allowable Total Vertical Uncertainty (`TVU_95% <= sqrt(a^2 + (b * d)^2)`), Total Horizontal Uncertainty (`THU_95%`), target grid cell size (`m`), and minimum feature-detection cubic size (`m`) before selecting altitude or sonar frequency. *Evidence:* Signed Survey Specification Sheet citing [template-project-header.md](../_shared/template-project-header.md). *Ref:* [ch70-specifications-guided-tour.md](../../fable/chapters/ch70-specifications-guided-tour.md), [sec-24-01.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-24/sec-24-01.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Lock the physical surface ontology in writing: true bare seabed versus kelp canopy, dense pelagic deep-scattering layers (DSL), hydrothermal/cold-seep gas plumes, fluid mud (`1.15–1.25 t/m^3` nautical bottom vs hard consolidated sub-bottom), or wreck/infrastructure least depth. *Threshold:* Zero ambiguity on whether 200–400 kHz MBES, dual-frequency backscatter, or SBP horizons define the primary DEM surface; wreck/hazard surveys mandate shoal-biased least-depth retention. *Evidence:* Ontology annex inside Project Instructions. *Ref:* [ch03-fitness-for-use.md](../../fable/chapters/ch03-fitness-for-use.md), [ch66-coastal-marine-polar-lakes-rivers.md](../../fable/chapters/ch66-coastal-marine-polar-lakes-rivers.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** Align grid resolution targets to depth-dependent footprint physics and `NOAA HSSD` brackets (**Object Detection:** `0–20 m` depth `<= 0.5 m` cell, `18–40 m` `<= 1.0 m`; **Complete/General:** `0–40 m` `<= 1.0 m`, `36–80 m` `<= 2.0 m`, `72–160 m` `<= 4.0 m`, `144–320 m` `<= 8.0 m`), or explicitly define AUV-altitude cell ratios (`0.25–1.0 m` at `10–60 m` altitude; `0.02–0.05 m` for SAS imagery/bathymetry). *Evidence:* Spatial Resolution Rationale table. *Ref:* [sec-24-01.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-24/sec-24-01.md), [ch44-resolution-and-sampling.md](../../fable/chapters/ch44-resolution-and-sampling.md).

## 2. Geodetic CRS, Epoch, Vertical Datum & Sign Lock

- [ ] **[GATE]** Lock the horizontal CRS (with explicit EPSG code and realization/epoch, e.g., `ITRF2020 @ survey_epoch` or `NAD83(2011) epoch 2010.0` + projected UTM zone) and verify antimeridian (`180°` longitude) or polar latitude (`> 70°`) projection behavior if operating near zone boundaries. *Threshold:* Zero unversioned `"WGS84"` strings; grid convergence `γ` and scale factor documented for converting INS true heading `ψ` to projected grid azimuth (`α_grid = ψ - γ`). *Evidence:* Geodetic Parameters Block verified via PROJ `projinfo`. *Ref:* [ch08-horizontal-datums.md](../../fable/chapters/ch08-horizontal-datums.md), [sec-02-02.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-02/sec-02-02.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Lock the vertical datum, separation model (geoid `N` or Chart Datum `SEP = N + (MSL - geoid) + (CD - MSL)` with **`h = z_CD + SEP = SEP - d_CD`**), tidal epoch (e.g., `NTDE 1983–2001` or `2002–2020`), and **explicit vertical sign convention** (`+z ↑` for `BAG`, `+d ↓` for `IHO S-102` and `S-44` depth equations) across navigation, sound-speed ray tracing, and gridding tools. *Threshold:* `100 %` sign-convention consistency audited between pressure-depth conversion, SVP cast table, and target output grid; separation uncertainty `sigma_SEP` budgeted explicitly (`0.05–0.10 m` open coast, `0.10–0.20 m` estuary). *Evidence:* Signed Geoid/SEP Test Record showing one worked sample point transformation. *Ref:* [ch09-vertical-datums.md](../../fable/chapters/ch09-vertical-datums.md), [notation-and-symbols.md](../../gemini4/book/00-front-matter/notation-and-symbols.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** Verify time-coordinate discipline (`UTC` vs `TAI`/`GPST` time offset, currently `18 s` GPST–UTC leap offset) across support-ship GNSS receivers, USBL topside, AUV INS, and sonar loggers so acoustic slant-range timestamps cannot suffer integer-second or leap-second drift. *Evidence:* Timing Architecture Note. *Ref:* [ch06-time-as-coordinate.md](../../fable/chapters/ch06-time-as-coordinate.md), [sec-32-01.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-32/sec-32-01.md).

## 3. Prior Data, Hazard & Platform Break-Even Audit

- [ ] **[GATE]** Audit all existing bathymetry, environmental priors, and data-licence terms covering the operational box (`GEBCO_2026` with Type Identifier `TID` codes, `GMRT`, `NOAA NCEI/DCDB` BAGs, `EMODnet`, local ENC `CATZOC`, `WOA` climatology, tide/current atlases). *Threshold:* Every prior grid annotated with its `TID` provenance (distinguishing direct `TID 10–17` soundings from satellite-gravity `TID 40` / `<= 250 m` chart voids) AND source licence; document worst-case bottom current (`m/s`), thermocline/halocline stratification, bottom slope (`deg`), terrain roughness, plus deliverable ownership/licence/embargo terms (`CC0`/`CC BY`/restricted). *Evidence:* Prior Bathymetry, Environmental Hazard & Licence Dossier. *Ref:* [ch49-metadata.md](../../fable/chapters/ch49-metadata.md), [ch55-public-products.md](../../fable/chapters/ch55-public-products.md), [ch68-legal-issues.md](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md), [sec-24-02.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-24/sec-24-02.md), [ch56-case-files.md](../../fable/chapters/ch56-case-files.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Map every seabed obstruction, anthropogenic hazard, and white-ribbon seam within `5 km` of the polygon: submarine telecom/power cables (`ICPC` / chart corridors), active pipelines, unexploded ordnance (`UXO`), fishing trap/longline zones, steep escarpments (`> 35°`), hydrothermal chimneys, and adjacent shallow/coastal data boundaries (`< 20 m` depth) covered by ship, USV, or topo-bathymetric LiDAR. *Threshold:* Hard lateral horizontal exclusion (`>= 250 m` or site permit minimum) and minimum terrain clearance (`>= 5 m` MBES or safety-envelope floor) encoded into mission-planning constraints, plus white-ribbon overlap zone defined per [multimodal-white-ribbon.md](../_shared/multimodal-white-ribbon.md). *Evidence:* Hazard & Multi-Modal Seam Map (`GeoPackage` / `GeoJSON`). *Ref:* [ch26-survey-planning.md](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md), [ch66-coastal-marine-polar-lakes-rivers.md](../../fable/chapters/ch66-coastal-marine-polar-lakes-rivers.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** Execute a **vessel-interface engineering & deck-integration pre-check** with the ship operator before mobilization: verify crane/A-frame outreach and Safe Working Load (`SWL`), deck tie-down bolt pattern for workshop/charging van, 3-phase clean ship power for Li-ion chargers, hull USBL pole/gate-valve mechanical flange fit, and bridge NMEA/GNSS/MRU Ethernet feeds. *Evidence:* Signed Shipboard Interface & Deck Plan. *Ref:* [ch16-platforms.md](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md), [sec-07-04.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-04.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** Execute a modality break-even comparison (AUV vs hull-mounted deepwater MBES vs deep-tow sled vs ROV vs USV) comparing vessel + AUV mobilization, transit, dive turn-around (`2–6 h` launch/deck-charge/download cycle), daily production rate (`10–50 km^2/day` for `200–400 kHz` MBES at `50 m` altitude vs `2–5 cm` SAS corridors), and validation effort (`5–10 %` of budget). *Evidence:* Modality Selection Memo. *Ref:* [ch16-platforms.md](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md), [ch28-reducing-cost.md](../../fable/chapters/ch28-reducing-cost.md), [sec-07-04.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-04.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** Plan multi-modal white-ribbon overlap strips (`>= 100 %` swath width overlap between AUV lines and ship/USV/LiDAR data within the same `2–72 h` tidal/sedimentation window) (*Trigger:* Any survey abutting coastline, shelf edge `< 50 m`, or combining shipboard `12/30 kHz` MBES with AUV `200–400 kHz` patches). *Evidence:* Co-timed Multi-Modal Tie Strip Plan referencing [multimodal-white-ribbon.md](../_shared/multimodal-white-ribbon.md). *Ref:* [sec-13-01.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-13/sec-13-01.md), [ch48-compositing.md](../../fable/chapters/ch48-compositing.md).

- [ ] **[GATE]** Draft and freeze the three pre-deployment control documents before mobilization: (1) **Project Instructions** (polygon bounds, S-44 targets, abort criteria, DTON/hazard escalation), (2) **Metadata & Provenance Skeleton** (`ISO 19115-1/-2/-3`, `STAC 1.1.0`, `PREMIS v3` / `BagIt` shell), and (3) **File Naming & SHA-256 Checksum Rules** (immutable raw datagrams, zero overwrites, float64 coordinates — never float32 UTM truncation). *Threshold:* All three files version-controlled in the campaign repository (`git` tag or immutable folder) prior to sailing. *Evidence:* Links/hashes to Project Instructions, Metadata JSON/XML, and Naming Spec. *Ref:* [ch26-survey-planning.md](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md), [ch47-file-formats.md](../../fable/chapters/ch47-file-formats.md), [ch49-metadata.md](../../fable/chapters/ch49-metadata.md), [ch50-archiving-and-provenance.md](../../fable/chapters/ch50-archiving-and-provenance.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** Audit single-point-of-failure field spares kit before crating: spare pressure-housing O-ring sets (main hull, vacuum port, battery hatches), `O-ring` silicone grease & durometer gauge, spare `wet-mateable` SubConn/Impulse whips & dummy plugs, spare acoustic transponder/modem boot, calibrated torque wrench, spare sacrificial zinc anodes, spare emergency burn-wire assemblies & calibrated corrodible magnesium/steel links, spare `UN 3090` beacon/strobe primary cells, spare ballast weights (`kg` increments), at least one backup CTD/SVS probe, spare removable data cartridges/NVMe SSDs (`>= 3x` expected dive volume), and deck vacuum-pump gauge. *Evidence:* Signed Spares & Tools Packing Manifest. *Ref:* [ch26-survey-planning.md](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md) (§26.9 spares lead times); vehicle-specific BOM per OEM manual.
