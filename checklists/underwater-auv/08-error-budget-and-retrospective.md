## 2. Stratified Empirical Statistics & Anomaly Root-Cause Diagnostics

- [ ] **[GATE]** Compute and report **both Gaussian and robust stratified accuracy statistics** across independent crosslines, ship-MBES overlap, and `mbnavadjust` tie-points — explicitly distinguishing **modeled TPU (`CUBE` / S-44)** from **empirically tested crossline/overlap accuracy** — broken out by **terrain slope (`< 5°`, `5–20°`, `> 20°`)**, **beam angle (`0–25°` nadir vs `45–65°` outer swath)**, and **dive ID / operating altitude (`m`)**. *Threshold:* Report per stratum: sample count (`n`), mean bias (`m`), sample standard deviation (`sigma`, `m`), `RMSE` (`m`) with exact **chi-squared 95 % confidence interval `[RMSE * sqrt(nu / chi2_{nu, 0.975}), RMSE * sqrt(nu / chi2_{nu, 0.025})]` (`nu = n - 1`)**, **`NMAD` (`1.4826 * median(|r - median(r)|)`)**, **`P95` (`95th percentile |Delta z|`)**, and empirical **variogram decorrelation length (`L_corr`)** yielding effective independent sample count **`N_eff approx A / (pi * L_corr^2)`** (preventing inflated significance from spatially correlated sonar footprints). *Evidence:* Stratified Statistical Report CSV & Summary Table. *Ref:* [ch53-accuracy-assessment.md](../../fable/chapters/ch53-accuracy-assessment.md), [sec-14-02.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-14/sec-14-02.md), [sec-33-01.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-33/sec-33-01.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Execute **systematic anomaly root-cause attribution** for every localized error exceeding `2 sigma`:
  - **Outer-beam parabolic curvature (`smile/frown`):** Attributable to stale SVP cast, surface `SVS` offset (`> 1 m/s`), or missing TEOS-10 sound-speed gradient -> apply nearest-time CTD cast & test `SVS` bias.
  - **Asymmetric across-track swath tilt (`roll` bias):** Attributable to uncorrected patch-test roll offset (`delta_phi`) or unapplied UTM grid convergence (`gamma = psi - alpha_grid`).
  - **Along-track ridge offset on reciprocal lines:** Attributable to pitch boresight (`delta_theta`), `1PPS` latency (`Delta t`), or `18 s` GPST–UTC leap-second mismatch.
  - **Bulk decimeter-to-meter `Z` discrepancy:** Distinguish omitting the **full atmospheric tare (`~1,013 hPa ≈ 10.1 m` vertical offset)** from **synoptic weather-front drift (`~0.10 m per 10 hPa`)**, uncorrected TEOS-10 pressure-to-depth conversion (`-45.4 m` at `3,000 dbar`), or flipped `+z ↑ / +d ↓` SEP sign.
  - *Evidence:* Diagnostic Root-Cause Annex mapping each anomaly ID to verified software/sensor fix. *Ref:* [sec-10-06.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-10/sec-10-06.md), [sec-14-03.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-14/sec-14-03.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** Archive a benchmark **cross-modal comparison summary** if ship/USV/LiDAR data overlapped the AUV box, documenting resolution-ratio artifacts (e.g., `0.5 m` AUV resolving boulder fields smoothed into flat `50 m` cells by ship `12 kHz` MBES) so downstream users do not mistake footprint sharpening for true seafloor erosion/deposition. *Evidence:* Footprint-Matched Cross-Modal Residual Plot. *Ref:* [ch41-change-detection.md](../../fable/chapters/ch41-change-detection.md), [ch54-evaluating-others-data.md](../../fable/chapters/ch54-evaluating-others-data.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** Quantify **realized spatial sampling & swath sidelap sufficiency** (`soundings per cell`, % valid grid nodes without interpolation fill, and retained outer-beam angle after refraction trimming) to verify whether future line spacing can widen slightly or must tighten. *Evidence:* Sounding Density Histogram & Usable Beam-Angle Summary. *Ref:* [ch44-resolution-and-sampling.md](../../fable/chapters/ch44-resolution-and-sampling.md), [ch53-accuracy-assessment.md](../../fable/chapters/ch53-accuracy-assessment.md).
