- [ ] **[GATE]** Inspect, clean, measure, lubricate, and seal every **pressure-housing O-ring and mateable subsea bulkhead connector**, test **overpressure/vent relief valves**, and pass a **pressure-housing vacuum leak test** before every deployment cycle that opens a main hull or battery section. *Threshold:* Zero O-ring nicks, flat spots, or hair/lint contamination; overpressure relief valve seats cleanly within cracking spec; pass manufacturer vacuum pull per OEM manual: `______` (typical: `-50 to -85 kPa` gauge, drift `<= 0.2 kPa` over `30–60 min` hold, `0` audible hiss); bleed/top off pressure-compensated oil-filled junction boxes cleanly (`0 %` trapped air bubbles). *Evidence:* Signed [O-Ring & Vacuum Leak-Test Log](./09-resources-and-templates.md#c-pressure-housing-o-ring--vacuum-leak-test-log). *Ref:* OEM hull manual & [External Regulatory Basis Register](./09-resources-and-templates.md#external-regulatory--standards-basis-register-non-handbook-authorities).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Inspect, arm, and wet-test the **emergency drop-weight / ascent-ballast release system** incorporating **dual redundant electro-burn wires** AND an independent **calibrated corrodible galvanic link** (magnesium/mild steel sized to `~1.5x–2.0x` planned dive duration). *Threshold:* Release drop-weight mass exceeds vehicle maximum negative buoyancy margin (including flooded syntactic foam / tail cone scenario); continuity resistance on burn-wire circuit nominal per OEM manual, mechanical pin corrosion-free, and release verified under load on deck. *Evidence:* Drop-Weight Inspection & Continuity Tag signed by Lead Engineer & Independent Verifier. *Ref:* Vehicle OEM emergency-release manual.
- [ ] **[GATE]** Verify **LARS crane/A-frame lifting bale, certified shackles, swivel, and quick-release hook load-test certification** (`UNOLS RVSS Appendix A & B` / marine rigging standard) prior to deck lift. *Threshold:* Rigging Safe Working Load `SWL >= 2.0x` wet AUV weight plus dynamic sea-state shock factor; zero frayed wires, bent safety latches, or expired annual load-test tags. *Evidence:* LARS Pre-Dive Rigging Sign-Off & Load-Test Tag ID. *Ref:* [ch16-platforms.md](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md) (LARS operations); `UNOLS RVSS App. A & B` via [External Regulatory Basis Register](./09-resources-and-templates.md#external-regulatory--standards-basis-register-non-handbook-authorities).

## 2. Pressure, Sound Velocity, INS/DVL & Synchronization Calibration

- [ ] **[GATE]** Verify current **ISO/IEC 17025** laboratory calibration certificates (`<= 12 months` old or post-shock-event) for the AUV **Paroscientific Digiquartz pressure sensor** (`0.01 %` full scale, e.g., `0.40 m` on a `4,000 dbar` sensor), AUV payload **CTD / Sound Velocity Sensor (`SVS`)**, and support-vessel **CTD / Moving Vessel Profiler (`MVP`)**; physically inspect the **Digiquartz Bernoulli pressure-port orifice** (zero tape, paint, or biofouling obstructions that cause speed-dependent dynamic pressure errors). *Threshold:* Field pressure-tare comparison against an external calibrated barometer `<= 2 hPa` (`~0.02 m` equivalent); deck/bucket `SVS` vs `CTD` sound-speed intercomparison `<= 0.5 m/s`. *Evidence:* Calibration Certificate Bundle + Field SVS/Pressure Tare & Orifice Check Sheet. *Ref:* [ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md), [ch20-sonar.md](../../fable/chapters/ch20-sonar.md), [sec-05-04.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-05/sec-05-04.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Audit the **3D dimensional lever-arm and boresight table** in the AUV right-handed `NED`-aligned body frame (`+x_b` nose forward, `+y_b` starboard, `+z_b` keel down) against [template-project-header.md](../_shared/template-project-header.md): IMU center of navigation, 4-beam phased-array DVL, MBES/SAS transmit/receive arrays, USBL acoustic transponder/responder, Paroscientific pressure port, and surface GNSS mast antenna. *Threshold:* Physical CAD/total-station survey reconciliation accurate to `<= 3–5 mm` on linear lever arms (`<= 0.1°` mechanical angular install); verify sign conventions match sonar processor and INS export axes (`+z_b` down vs `ENU` up). *Evidence:* Signed Lever-Arm Sign-Convention Diagram & Calibration Parameter Table. *Ref:* [ch13-imu-ins.md](../../fable/chapters/ch13-imu-ins.md), [sec-10-06.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-10/sec-10-06.md), [notation-and-symbols.md](../../gemini4/book/00-front-matter/notation-and-symbols.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Verify **1PPS / IEEE 1588 PTP sub-millisecond timing synchronization** across AUV INS, MBES/SAS, DVL, SBP, and support-ship USBL topside (`UTC` vs `GPST` `18 s` leap-second offset strictly locked). *Threshold:* Measured inter-sensor clock skew and pulse jitter `<= 1.0 ms` (`<= 0.5 ms` preferred; at `2.0 m/s` forward speed, `10 ms` uncompensated latency offsets features by `0.02 m`, while `1 s` integer-second error creates `2.0 m` longitudinal step). *Evidence:* Timing Diagnostic Capture & Ping-to-INS Time-Tag Audit. *Ref:* [ch13-imu-ins.md](../../fable/chapters/ch13-imu-ins.md), [ch06-time-as-coordinate.md](../../fable/chapters/ch06-time-as-coordinate.md), [sec-32-01.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-32/sec-32-01.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** Validate **DVL-to-INS yaw boresight (`delta_psi_DVL`) and velocity scale factor (`s_DVL`)** alignment prior to production lines (phased-array DVLs cancel first-order local sound-speed errors via Snell's law, leaving `delta_psi_DVL` and `s_DVL` as dominant dead-reckoning drift sources: `0.1°` heading error = `1.7 m/km` cross-track drift). *Threshold:* Calibration loop / reciprocal-run residuals yield `delta_psi_DVL` uncertainty `<= 0.03–0.05° (1 sigma)` and `s_DVL` within `[0.9995, 1.0005]` scale-factor tolerance (`1 sigma`); nav-grade INS+DVL drift verified at `0.05–0.2 %` of distance traveled (`DT`). *Evidence:* DVL/INS Alignment Convergence Plot entered into [template-error-budget.md](../_shared/template-error-budget.md). *Ref:* [ch13-imu-ins.md](../../fable/chapters/ch13-imu-ins.md), [sec-05-04.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-05/sec-05-04.md).

## 3. Support Vessel USBL/LBL Acoustic Box-In, CTD & Tide Gauge

- [ ] **[GATE]** Execute full **USBL / iUSBL acoustic head box-in calibration** (`IMCA S 017` / manufacturer procedure) over an anchored seabed transponder at representative survey depth before launching AUV survey lines (critical because `0.1°` ship MRU **roll/pitch** misalignment creates `5.24 m` lateral error at `3,000 m` depth directly below the vessel, while `0.1°` heading error creates `1.7–3.0 m` lateral error at `30°–45°` horizontal cone offset). *Threshold:* Steam orthogonal cloverleaf passes (`>= 4` cardinal lines at offset radii `~0.5x–1.0x` water depth) plus `360°` CW/CCW spin circles; solve acoustic head roll, pitch, heading, latency, and transceiver lever arms until post-calibration scatter achieves `<= 0.1–0.2 %` of slant range (`1 sigma` per-axis horizontal). *Evidence:* USBL Calibration Solution Report (`residuals < 0.05°` angular, zero residual radial bias). *Ref:* [ch14-positioning-beyond-gnss.md](../../fable/chapters/ch14-positioning-beyond-gnss.md), [sec-05-04.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-05/sec-05-04.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** Deploy and **box-in Long Baseline (`LBL`) or Sparse-LBL seabed transponders** (*Trigger:* High-precision engineering/archaeology site, deep ocean `> 2,500 m` requiring `< 3 m` `THU_95%`, or rugged canyon/steep-slope survey where USBL precision or INS+DVL lock degrades). *Threshold:* Bench-test every transponder's **deck burn-wire/motor release latch & primary `UN 3090` battery voltage** prior to deployment; anchor `>= 3–4` acoustic beacons (or `1–2` sparse beacons for OWTT/CSAC-aided INS) with good GDOP (`>= 30°` intersection angles), box-in each beacon via vessel GNSS + sound-velocity cast until baseline closure error `<= 0.2–0.5 m` (`1 sigma`), and record release codes. *Evidence:* LBL Bench-Test Log & Inter-Beacon Baseline Closure Table. *Ref:* [ch14-positioning-beyond-gnss.md](../../fable/chapters/ch14-positioning-beyond-gnss.md), [sec-05-04.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-05/sec-05-04.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Collect a full-water-column **shipboard CTD / Sound Velocity Profile (`SVP`) cast** at the dive site **within `2–4 h` prior to AUV launch**, and verify support-vessel continuous **hull surface sound-speed sensor** + calibrated **barometric pressure (`P_atm`) logger** (`<= 0.5 hPa` drift) for TEOS-10 / Saunders–Fofonoff depth reduction. *Threshold:* Cast extends from surface to `>= 90–100 %` of maximum seabed depth; compare downcast vs upcast (`<= 0.5 m/s` hysteresis) and load clipped monotonic SVP into both USBL topside and AUV mission computer. *Evidence:* Timestamped SVP `.asvp`/`.svp` File + Down/Up Hysteresis Plot. *Ref:* [ch20-sonar.md](../../fable/chapters/ch20-sonar.md), [sec-05-04.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-05/sec-05-04.md), [sec-13-02.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-13/sec-13-02.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** Install, benchmark-level, and validate the **project tide gauge / seabed bottom pressure recorder** (or verify regional **VDatum / VORF / OTPS2** hydrodynamic separation accuracy) (*Trigger:* Coastal, shelf, estuary, or shallow `< 200 m` AUV survey requiring Chart Datum / MLLW / LAT tidal accuracy `<= 0.10–0.20 m`). *Threshold:* Reference station leveled via GNSS@TG / optical leveling to **`>= 3` minimum tidal benchmarks (`fable §26.3`; `5+` recommended for coastal redundancy)** with `<= 1–2 cm` leveling closure (`IOC Manuals & Guides No. 14` reference in `ch25`/`ch26`); in deeper open ocean (`> 500 m`), verify global tidal model (`FES2022`/`TPXO9` + inverse barometer `~1 cm/hPa`) residuals (`<= 0.05–0.10 m`). *Evidence:* Tide Benchmark Tie Certificate or Model Validation Note. *Ref:* [ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md), [ch26-survey-planning.md](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md), [ch09-vertical-datums.md](../../fable/chapters/ch09-vertical-datums.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** Survey or verify a local **MBES Reference Check Surface** (flat hard-bottom seabed patch at survey depth or ship/AUV overlap zone) using calibrated surface/AUV passes so repeatability across multi-day deployments can be tracked objectively. *Evidence:* Reference Surface GeoTIFF/BAG & Baseline Stats. *Ref:* [ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md).
