## Part A — Pre-Mobilization Administration, Permits, COIs & Cost Ledger

### 1. Maritime Jurisdiction, MSR Permits & Environmental Compliance

- [ ] **[GATE]** Secure coastal-state **Marine Scientific Research (MSR)** consent under **UNCLOS Part XIII (Articles 245–249)** or commercial hydrographic survey authorization for every polygon touching territorial seas (`<= 22.2 km / 12 nmi`), Exclusive Economic Zone (`EEZ <= 370.4 km / 200 nmi`), or Extended Continental Shelf (`ECS` / Art. 76). *Threshold:* Submit diplomatic MSR dossier at least `6–7 months` (`>= 180 days`, per UNCLOS Art. 248 + routing lead) before sailing, attaching AUV specs, exact acoustic frequencies/source levels, port-call logistics, host-nation observer berths, and data-sharing timetable (`Art. 249`). *Evidence:* Written Coastal-State Consent / Diplomatic Note Number on ship's bridge and in shore ops folder. *Ref:* [ch68-legal-issues.md](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md), [ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md), [sec-27-01.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-01.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Obtain **NOAA NMFS Marine Mammal Protection Act (MMPA) / Endangered Species Act (ESA)** Incidental Harassment Authorization (`IHA`) or Letter of Authorization (`LOA`), or equivalent **JNCC (2017)** / host-nation protected-species clearance covering all acoustic emitters (MBES, SAS, SBP chirp/parametric, DVL, USBL/LBL pingers, and acoustic emergency beacons). *Threshold:* Verify source-level (`dB re 1 uPa @ 1 m`) and frequency thresholds (`< 100–200 kHz` — especially `<= 12 kHz` pingers and `2–24 kHz` SBPs); enforce **`30 min` pre-watch (`60 min` in water depths `> 200 m`)**, `500 m` exclusion zone, and **`>= 20 min` soft-start ramp** where governed by JNCC/permit. *Evidence:* Active Permit / Mitigation Protocol card signed by Protected Species Observer (`PSO` / `MMO`). *Ref:* [ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md), [sec-27-01.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-01.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** Issue **Local Notice to Mariners (`LNM` / `NAVWARN`)**, secure **FCC / national RF telemetry & beacon spectrum licenses** (`47 CFR Part 80/90` `VHF` DF beacons, surface telemetry modems), and coordinate with **Harbor Master, `VTS`, submarine cable owners (`ICPC`), and local fishery/trap cooperatives** (`48–72 h` minimum pre-launch to deconflict fixed gillnets/crab pots from LBL arrays and surfaced AUVs). *Evidence:* Broadcast `LNM` Serial Number, RF License ID & Fishery Liaison Log. *Ref:* External authorities via [External Regulatory Basis Register](./09-resources-and-templates.md#external-regulatory--standards-basis-register-non-handbook-authorities).
- [ ] **[COND]** Enforce **UNESCO 2001 Underwater Cultural Heritage**, Indigenous **CARE / OCAP®**, and **hull/gear biofouling quarantine** compliance (*Trigger:* Survey enters Indigenous/First Nations waters, Marine Protected Areas, historic wreck zones, or transfers an AUV across biogeographic basins such as Hawaii, New Zealand, or Antarctica). *Threshold:* Document non-invasive acoustic-only standoff, Indigenous data-sovereignty governance (`OCAP®` ownership/control/access/possession), and pre-shipping freshwater biofouling inspection/certificate. *Evidence:* Cultural/CARE Agreement & Biosecurity Inspection Certificate. *Ref:* [ch68-legal-issues.md](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md); UNESCO 2001 & biosecurity rules via [External Regulatory Basis Register](./09-resources-and-templates.md#external-regulatory--standards-basis-register-non-handbook-authorities).

### 2. Export Control (ITAR / EAR), National Data Localization & Classified Bathymetry

- [ ] **[GATE]** Screen AUV vehicle, Navigation-Grade INS, SAS/InSAS, Terrain-Relative Navigation (`TRN`), high-frequency DVL, and acoustic arrays against **US ITAR (22 CFR 120–130, USML Category XII / XX)** and **EAR (15 CFR 730–774)** classifications: specifically **ECCN `8A001`** (submersible vehicles), **`7A003`** (inertial navigation/gyro/accelerometer accuracy thresholds), **`7A005`** (GNSS receivers), and **`6A001`** (marine acoustic/bathymetric systems). *Threshold:* Obtain required BIS export licenses, **`DSP-5` permanent export or `DSP-73` temporary export (`ITAR`), or `EAR` License Exception `TMP` (`15 CFR § 740.9`)**, plus a Technology Control Plan (`TCP`) **including deemed-export checks** for foreign-national crew/scientists handling hardware or unencrypted calibration/INS logs; zero unapproved dual-use hardware leaves port. *Evidence:* Signed Export Control Officer Classification & Customs Manifest. *Ref:* [ch68-legal-issues.md](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md), [sec-27-03.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-03.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Verify **host-nation bathymetric restriction & data-localization laws** prior to collecting or exporting raw soundings (e.g., India 2021 Geospatial Guidelines restricting high-resolution underwater bathymetry **finer than `< 10 m` accuracy/resolution inside `500 m` shoreline or `< 100 m` beyond**; China Surveying and Mapping Law / `GCJ-02` datum restrictions; national-defense harbor exclusions). *Threshold:* Establish whether raw sub-meter grids must undergo authorized **tier-down sanitization** (e.g., `100 m` or `400 m` resolution grids for public release vs restricted full-resolution custody) before crossing borders or cloud ingestion. *Evidence:* Legal Compliance & Data-Release Tier Matrix. *Ref:* [ch68-legal-issues.md](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md), [ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md).

## Part B — Daily Deck Safety, Hazmat Batteries & Operational Integrity

### 3. Lithium-Ion Battery Hazmat, Pressure-Housing, LARS & Cyber/RF Safety

- [ ] **[GATE]** Enforce **UN 3480 Packing Instruction 965 (`PI965`)** (standalone Li-ion cells/modules, **`Cargo Aircraft Only (CAO)`** for Section IA/IB), **UN 3481 (`PI966/967`)** (cells packed with or installed inside AUV; **IATA DGR 67th Ed., effective 1 Jan 2026, extends mandatory `<= 30 %` `SoC` limit to `PI966`**), and **UN 3090 (`CAO`) / UN 3091** (primary lithium-metal cells in LBL transponders, acoustic releases, and recovery beacons/PLBs) across air (`IATA DGR`), sea (`IMO IMDG`), and US ground (`49 CFR 171–180`, `> 300 Wh` packs) transport. *Threshold:* Discharge air-freight modules to **`<= 30 %` `SoC`** per `PI965`/`PI966` prior to tender, apply UN-certified Class 9 packaging and terminal short-circuit protection, attach current **`SDS` (Safety Data Sheet) + `UN 38.3` test summaries**, and file certified Dangerous Goods Shipper Declarations (`DGD`). Never ship damaged or swollen cells (`0 %` tolerance — isolate in vermiculite/fireproof drum). *Evidence:* Verified `UN 38.3` Test Summary, `SDS`, SoC Log (`<= 30 %`), and signed `IATA/IMDG` DGD. *Ref:* IATA DGR 67th Ed. (2026) / IMDG / 49 CFR via [External Regulatory Basis Register](./09-resources-and-templates.md#external-regulatory--standards-basis-register-non-handbook-authorities).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Establish written **Pressure-Housing, Hydrogen-Purge, Li-Ion Thermal-Runaway, and Launch/Recovery (`LARS`) Safety SOPs** briefed to every watchstander:
  - **Pressure-housing explosion hazard:** Never open or seal a suspect flooded/overheated pressure hull without wearing full-face shield and blast apron and verifying internal pressure gauge (`<= 0 kPa` / safe atmospheric bleed via relief valve first — seawater reacting with flooded Li-ion cells generates explosive `H_2` gas inside titanium/aluminum tubes).
  - **Charging bay & fire suppression (`per SDS`):** Dedicated forced ventilation, active `H_2` / CO / smoke detectors, **continuous copious seawater deluge or Aqueous Vermiculite Dispersion (`AVD` / `Lith-EX`) per Li-ion `SDS`** + overboard-jettison tongs adjacent to charging bay (reserve **`Class D` dry powder strictly for primary lithium-metal `UN 3090` cells** — standard Class D fails on Li-ion thermal runaway).
  - **LARS rigging (`UNOLS RVSS App. A & B`):** Certified crane/A-frame `SWL >= 2.0x` wet AUV weight + dynamic shock factor, hard-hat/PFD/harness at rail, taglines on all suspended lifts, and mandatory thruster physical/software lockout before diver or RHIB hook-up.
  - *Evidence:* Signed Deck & Battery Safety Briefing Roster + Pressure/LARS Inspection Log. *Ref:* [ch16-platforms.md](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md) (LARS ops); rigging & SDS rules per `UNOLS RVSS App. A & B` via [External Regulatory Basis Register](./09-resources-and-templates.md#external-regulatory--standards-basis-register-non-handbook-authorities).
- [ ] **[REC]** Enforce **STCW watchkeeping & human-fatigue limits** (`>= 10 h` rest in any `24 h` period per `STCW 2010 Manila Amendments`; no sole-operator crane/LARS recovery after `> 12 h` continuous deck shift) and **RF / GNSS spoofing / cyber hygiene** (encrypted deck umbilical, signed firmware hashes, shipboard GNSS dual-constellation spoofing/jamming monitor for `USBL` surface positioning integrity). *Evidence:* Watch Bill & Cyber/GNSS Integrity Log. *Ref:* [ch56-case-files.md](../../fable/chapters/ch56-case-files.md), [ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md) (GNSS spoofing/cyber); STCW limits via [External Regulatory Basis Register](./09-resources-and-templates.md#external-regulatory--standards-basis-register-non-handbook-authorities).

## Part A (Continued) — Marine Insurance & Cost Governance

### 4. Maritime Insurance Certificate of Insurance (COI) Audit

- [ ] **[GATE]** Audit and verify active Certificates of Insurance (`COI`) across all required marine & subsea policies **at least `14 days` prior to mobilization**, referencing [template-insurance-coi.md](../_shared/template-insurance-coi.md):
  1. **Protection & Indemnity (`P&I`)** covering crew, vessel liability, pollution, and **compulsory wreck removal** (`>= USD 5M–10M` or vessel P&I club limit).
  2. **Hull & Machinery (`H&M`)** on support vessel including AUV LARS/davit operations.
  3. **Scheduled Marine / Subsea Equipment Floater (`All-Risk`)** explicitly listing **AUV hull serial number, DVL, INS, MBES, SAS, and LBL transponders** at full replacement value (`USD 1M–8M+`) **while submerged untethered, during crane lift, and in transit** (confirm no "on-deck only" or "tethered-only" exclusion), backed by a current **underwriter Condition & Value (`C&V`) survey / valuation appraisal**.
  4. **Maritime Employer's Liability / Jones Act & USL&H** covering shipboard and dockside engineers/divers.
  5. **Charterer's Legal Liability**, **Foreign Voluntary Workers' Compensation (`FVWC` / Medevac)**, and **Professional Errors & Omissions (`E&O`)** covering charting/engineering negligence.
  - *Threshold:* Zero coverage gaps across transit, harbor trials, submerged autonomous dives, or territorial waters; verify **policy expiry extends `>= 30 days` past weather-standby end**, **Waiver of Subrogation & Primary/Non-Contributory** clauses, **`Institute Warranty Limits (IWL)`** clearances for polar/high-risk waters, and specific **under-ice, `> 3,000 m` depth, or prototype payload endorsements** if applicable. *Evidence:* Signed COI Matrix + `C&V` Survey verified by Risk/Legal Counsel. *Ref:* [sec-27-01.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-01.md), [ch68-legal-issues.md](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md).
