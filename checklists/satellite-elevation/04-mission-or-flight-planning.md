In satellite elevation surveys, acquisition geometry is locked the moment a tasking order enters the constellation scheduler. Poorly bounded off-nadir angles, excessive base-to-height ratios over urban canyons, uncompensated repeat-pass `InSAR` baselines exceeding $B_{\perp,\mathrm{crit}}$, or midday solar sunglint over shallow lagoons cannot be fixed later by clever photogrammetry or unwrapping software. Complete all tasking-geometry gates and sign the *a priori* analytical error budget in [Error Budget](../_shared/template-error-budget.md) before authorizing tasking release.

## 1. Orbital Crossing Windows, Sun Geometry & SDB Conditions

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Lock sun-synchronous orbital crossing time (`LTAN`/`LTDN`), solar elevation, and azimuth constraints.** Confirm exact crossing timing **per satellite** (`10:00–10:30` local solar time typical for `Pléiades`/`Neo` and `Sentinel-2`; `WorldView-3` flies early afternoon `~13:30`, and `WorldView Legion` blends sun-sync and mid-inclination variable-LTAN orbits) to balance convective cumulus risk against illumination, enforce solar elevation **`>= 30°`** over open terrain (`>= 40°–45°` over steep alpine valleys or dense urban corridors), and verify Dawn–Dusk polar orbits (`06:00/18:00` local time) for `NISAR` / `Sentinel-1` / `TanDEM-X` against wet-dew/freeze–thaw diurnal cycles. *Evidence:* Orbital visibility & solar-ephemeris plot archived per [ch21](../../fable/chapters/ch21-radar-sar-insar.md), [ch26](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md), [ch36](../../fable/chapters/ch36-seasonal-variability.md), and [sec-07-05](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-05.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Enforce optical SDB low-glint, wind, turbidity, and tidal-stage windows (*Trigger:* coastal SDB track active).** Specify `sun-to-sensor glint angle >= 20°` (or cross-verify `Hedley` SWIR-band deglinting coverage), surface wind speed `< 5–7 m/s` (suppressing whitecap foam and wave breaking), `>= 72 h` post-storm river-plume clearing, and optimal astronomical tide stage—either **high tide** to maximize overlap with shallow hydrographic vessels across the white ribbon, or **low tide** to expose intertidal flats for optical stereo—synchronized via [White-Ribbon Sync](../_shared/multimodal-white-ribbon.md). *Evidence:* Co-timed coastal window specification citing [ch23](../../fable/chapters/ch23-satellite-derived-bathymetry.md) and [sec-11-03](../../gemini4/book/ch-11/sec-11-03.md).

## 2. Optical Stereo & Tri-Stereo Acquisition Geometry

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Match stereo convergence angle ($\alpha$) and base-to-height ratio (`B/H`) to terrain roughness and urban density.** Constrain vertical matching sensitivity $\sigma_z = \frac{g\,\sigma_{p,\mathrm{px}}}{2\tan(\alpha/2)}$ (where $\sigma_{p,\mathrm{px}}$ is disparity matching noise in pixels and $g$ is ground sample distance; [ch22](../../fable/chapters/ch22-photogrammetry-sfm.md), [sec-07-05](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-05.md)) against perspective occlusion ($B/H = 2\tan(\alpha/2)$ for symmetric stereo pairs):
  - **Dense Urban / Deep Street Canyons:** Contract narrow convergence **`15°–25°`** (**`B/H ~ 0.26–0.44`**), checking street visibility width $w_{\mathrm{stereo}} = \max(0, w_s - h_b \cdot B/H)$ against target street width $w_s$ and building height $h_b$.
  - **Open / Moderate Terrain:** Contract **`30°–45°`** (**`B/H ~ 0.54–0.83`**) for strong vertical geometric strength without perspective distortion.
  - **Tri-Stereo Mandate:** Require **same-pass tri-stereo** (`forward / near-nadir / backward`) wherever steep slopes (`> 30°`) or tall urban clusters exceed `15 %` of the AOI. *Evidence:* Stereo geometry calculation table inside tasking order referencing [ch22](../../fable/chapters/ch22-photogrammetry-sfm.md) and [sec-07-05](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-05.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Cap off-nadir viewing angle (`ONA`), enforce same-pass simultaneity, and validate AOI polygon specs.** Bound individual stereo-frame off-nadir angles (`<= 20°–25°` urban, `<= 30°` open; bisector elevation `60°–90°`), mandate **single-pass along-track** collection (`< 90 s` intra-triplet interval) to freeze shadows, vehicles, snow, and water levels, verify `>= 95 %` AOI stereo overlap inside each strip, and verify distributor AOI geometry rules (`>= 25 km^2` min AOI and narrow-corridor width floor such as `>= 5 km` on narrow-corridor rules for `Pléiades Neo`/`SkySat`; indicative `>= 50 km^2` min / `<= 1,000 km^2` standard request for `WorldView Legion`). *Evidence:* Pre-submission KML/GeoJSON validation log from vendor feasibility API referencing [ch26](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** **Model effective off-nadir cross-track GSD growth before accepting high-oblique angles.** Check cross-track pixel elongation $\mathrm{GSD}_{\mathrm{eff}} = \mathrm{GSD}_0 / \cos^2(\theta_{\mathrm{off}})$ (`~1.33 x` nadir GSD at `30°` off-nadir) and verify degraded edge pixels still meet spatial-detail requirements ([ch22](../../fable/chapters/ch22-photogrammetry-sfm.md), [ch44](../../fable/chapters/ch44-resolution-and-sampling.md)). *Evidence:* Edge-GSD feasibility calculation.
- [ ] **[REC]** **Prohibit uncompensated cross-track multi-date stereo over vegetated, agricultural, snow, or tidal AOIs.** Reject multi-day cross-track pairs whenever solar azimuth shifts `> 10°`, crop canopy/tillage alters surface texture, or intertidal heights shift between acquisitions. *Evidence:* Multi-date exclusion note in tasking brief referencing [ch22](../../fable/chapters/ch22-photogrammetry-sfm.md) and [ch36](../../fable/chapters/ch36-seasonal-variability.md).

## 3. SAR / InSAR / KaRIn Geometry & Pair Selection Planning

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Bound InSAR perpendicular baseline ($B_\perp < B_{\perp,\mathrm{crit}}$) and height of ambiguity ($h_{\mathrm{amb}}$).** Using $h_{\mathrm{amb}} = \frac{\lambda R \sin\theta_i}{p B_\perp}$ and $B_{\perp,\mathrm{crit}} = \frac{\lambda R \tan\theta_i}{p \rho_r}$ ([ch21](../../fable/chapters/ch21-radar-sar-insar.md), [sec-11-06](../../gemini4/book/ch-11/sec-11-06.md))—where $\theta_i$ is local incidence angle, $R$ is slant range, $\rho_r$ is slant-range resolution, and $p \in \{1, 2\}$ ($p=1$ bistatic `TanDEM-X`, $p=2$ repeat-pass `Sentinel-1`/`NISAR`)—design target baseline intervals so $B_\perp <= 0.35\,B_{\perp,\mathrm{crit}}$ (preserving spatial coherence; e.g., Sentinel-1 IW `B_perp,crit ~ 5 km`) while selecting $h_{\mathrm{amb}}$ (`~30–80 m` for topographic DEMs) small enough to keep phase-noise height scatter $\sigma_h = \frac{h_{\mathrm{amb}}}{2\pi}\sigma_\phi$ within budget yet large enough to prevent `2*pi` unwrapping jumps across steep topography. *Evidence:* Orbital baseline & `h_amb` spreadsheet citing [ch21](../../fable/chapters/ch21-radar-sar-insar.md) and [sec-11-06](../../gemini4/book/ch-11/sec-11-06.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Specify dual Ascending + Descending tracks wherever foreshortening, layover, or shadow degrades single-look coverage.** Run forward geometric visibility modeling over `Copernicus GLO-30` to map **layover ($\alpha_{\mathrm{slope}} >= \theta_i$)** and **radar shadow ($-\alpha_{\mathrm{slope}} >= 90^\circ - \theta_i$)**; wherever combined layover + shadow voids exceed `5 %` of the AOI, mandate **both ascending and descending** acquisitions (or multi-baseline `TanDEM-X` combinations). *Evidence:* Layover/shadow simulation GeoTIFF & dual-track tasking schedule referencing [ch21](../../fable/chapters/ch21-radar-sar-insar.md) and [ch35](../../fable/chapters/ch35-voids-and-overhangs.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** **Constrain temporal baseline and ionospheric Kp thresholds (*Trigger:* repeat-pass `Sentinel-1` / `NISAR` InSAR DEM generation).** When building topography from repeat-pass `Sentinel-1` (`6/12-day`) or `NISAR` (`12-day`), require the shortest orbital repeat, dry/frozen stable surface conditions (`no precipitation or snowmelt within 24 h`), multi-pair stacking (`>= 5` interferograms) to suppress atmospheric wet-delay phase screens (`APS`), and geomagnetic **`Kp < 5`** (monitoring `L`/`P`-band equatorial/auroral scintillation). *Evidence:* Weather/ionospheric screening rules documented in [ch21](../../fable/chapters/ch21-radar-sar-insar.md) and [ch26](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** **Configure dual-polarization & multi-baseline `InSAR` modes (*Trigger:* dense forest canopy or dry cold snowpack inside AOI).** Request dual/quad-pol (`HH/HV` or `VV/VH`) and multi-baseline `TanDEM-X`, `NISAR L-band`, or `ESA Biomass P-band` modes when mapping canopy-covered terrain or cold dry snow, using `RVoG` polarimetric-interferometric canopy/ground separation rather than treating raw `VV` single-pol phase centers as bare earth ([ch21](../../fable/chapters/ch21-radar-sar-insar.md), [sec-11-01](../../gemini4/book/ch-11/sec-11-01.md), [sec-12-06](../../gemini4/book/ch-12/sec-12-06.md)). *Evidence:* Polarization & multi-baseline tasking request.
