Satellite sensing rarely requires airspace permits, yet legal, regulatory, licensing, security, and ground-crew risks derail more spaceborne DEM deliveries than orbital hardware failures. A commercial optical stereo EULA that blocks public derivative DEM distribution, a cross-border ground-truth RTK deployment missing `ATA Carnet` / `UN 3480` lithium declarations, or a missed national mapping threshold (`India`, `China`, `GDPR`) can halt publication or create severe statutory liability. This sheet separates **Part A: Pre-Mob Regulatory, EULA, Insurance & Cost Gates** (completed before vendor POs or field deployment) from **Part B: Field-Mob & Daily Ground-Sortie Safety, Access & Burn-Rate Gates** (carried offline in every field binder).

## Part A — Pre-Mob Regulatory, EULA, Insurance & Budget Gates

### 1. Spaceborne Licensing, Shutter Control & National Mapping Laws

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Verify NOAA CRSRA (`15 CFR Part 960`) tier status and shutter-control exposure.** For U.S.-licensed commercial constellations (e.g., `WorldView Legion`, `SkySat`/`Pelican`, commercial SAR), confirm the vendor's current **Tier 1 / Tier 2 / Tier 3** license classification and whether requested modes (e.g., non-Earth imaging, sub-0.4 m agile SAR/EO, high-latitude passes) face **Tier 2/3 limited-operations directives ("shutter control")** or 3-year temporary national security conditions (`Tier 1` systems with foreign equivalents carry no Part 960 shutter control). Check the `Kyl–Bingaman` U.S. commercial resolution cap (`0.40 m` over Israel since July 2020) or conflict-zone rules before quoting high-resolution tasking. *Evidence:* Vendor license/tier confirmation memo citing [ch68](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md), [ch69](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md), [sec-27-03](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-03.md), and the [09 External Register](./09-resources-and-templates.md#5-external-regulatory--technical-standards-register-prdmd-74).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Screen AOI borders against national geospatial localization, export-control (`EAR`/`ITAR`) & sensitivity laws (`§4.20.1`).** Confirm local legal clearance when mapping restricted jurisdictions:
  - **India (DST Geospatial Guidelines 2021):** Unapproved mapping by foreign entities is restricted **finer than `1 m` horizontal, `3 m` vertical, or `10 m` bathymetric inside `500 m` of shore / sensitive buffers, and `100 m` beyond** ([ch68](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md) §68.8.2).
  - **China (Surveying and Mapping Law):** Unlicensed ground surveying is prohibited; screen archive products for **GCJ-02 / BD-09** non-linear coordinate obfuscation before ingest.
  - **Export Control (`EAR ECCN` / `ITAR USML`):** Screen `EAR` dual-use classifications (e.g., `15 CFR 730–774` high-grade inertial/sensor systems and tactical GNSS receivers) and `ITAR USML` (`22 CFR 120–130`) applicability before transferring Precise Orbit Determination (`POD`) star-tracker attitude, rover receivers, or restricted-country DEMs across international partners. *Evidence:* Legal country-clearance & export-screening matrix archived per [ch68](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md), [ch69](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md), and [sec-27-03](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-03.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Audit the commercial imagery EULA for Derivative-DEM vs. Raw-Imagery redistribution rights.** Before purchasing scenes, obtain legal sign-off on the exact vendor End-User License Agreement ([sec-27-01](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-01.md)): confirm **non-reversible derivative elevation models (`COG` DSM/DTM)** may be delivered to the client, shared with subcontractors/validators, or published open-access (e.g., `CC BY 4.0`), while **raw Level-1 panchromatic/multispectral imagery and `RPC00B` files** remain segregated in access-controlled storage without prohibited redistribution. Confirm seat/tier limits (`Single User`, `Group`, `Enterprise`, `Public Good`, or `EOCL`/`NextView`/`CSDAP` federal licenses). *Evidence:* Signed EULA Derivative-Rights Compliance Summary linking [sec-27-01](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-01.md) and [ch68](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Complete Privacy (`GDPR`), Indigenous Sovereignty (`CARE`/`OCAP®`), Cultural & Mosaic Security Reviews (`§4.20.1`).** For `<= 0.5 m` optical stereo or SDB over populated/coastal areas, review **GDPR** personal-data/property privacy rules; over Indigenous lands, obtain formal free, prior, and informed consent (**CARE** / **OCAP®** governance) and redact **NHPA §106 / ARPA** archaeological locations if publicly released; screen critical infrastructure (dams, bases, ports, substations) for **mosaic-effect / Strava-pattern** operational exposure before posting public DEMs or STAC footprints. *Evidence:* Privacy, Tribal Consultation & Security Redaction Clearance logged per [ch69](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md) and [sec-27-02](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-02.md).

### 2. Insurance, Statutory Licensure & Lifecycle Cost Ledger

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Verify insurance certificates (`COI`), endorsements, weather-extension expiry & statutory licensure (`§4.20.6`).** Collect active Certificates of Insurance in [Insurance COI](../_shared/template-insurance-coi.md) covering: (a) **Professional Errors & Omissions (`E&O`)** aligned to engineered flood, volume, or charting liability; (b) **Commercial General Liability (`CGL`)** & **Commercial Auto** for ground-control crews; (c) **Inland Marine / Equipment Floater** backed by a current **Condition & Value (`C&V`) equipment appraisal** for high-value GNSS/total-station/corner-reflector kits; and (d) **domestic Workers' Compensation** plus **Foreign Voluntary Workers' Compensation (`FVWC`) & Emergency Medical Evacuation** for international deployments. Verify **Waiver of Subrogation** and **Primary/Non-Contributory** endorsements where mandated by landowners/DOTs, and confirm policy expiration provides a **`>= 30-day` buffer beyond the seasonal tasking closeout date**. Assign statutory sign-off (`PLS / PE / ASPRS Certified Photogrammetrist / IHO Cat A-B`) where products support legal flood, boundary, or navigation charts. *Evidence:* Verified COIs, endorsements & active license numbers recorded in [Insurance COI](../_shared/template-insurance-coi.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Build a full lifecycle Cost Ledger reconciling commercial tasking, ground control & cloud/HPC (`§4.20.6`).** Populate [Cost Ledger](../_shared/template-cost-ledger.md) before PO release:
  - *Commercial optical tasking benchmarks (public reseller/provider list pricing as of 2025–2026; exact cost quote-dependent on distributor, volume, and priority):* **Pléiades Neo 30 cm** new tasking `~$63.75–$65.00/km^2` stereo, `~$82.50–$85.00/km^2` tri-stereo (vs. `~$42.50` / `~$55.00/km^2` archive; `>= 25 km^2` min AOI, check narrow-corridor width floor e.g., `>= 5 km`), **WorldView Legion** (indicative `>= 50 km^2` min order, up to `1,000 km^2` standard request), **SkySat/Pelican** (`>= 25 km^2` min AOI or `5 km` dia point circle), plus **indicative priority / rush (`+25–100 %`)** and **strict cloud ceiling (`<= 5–10 %`)** quote uplifts.
  - *Ground & calibration costs (project estimates):* GNSS survey crew mobilization, Siemens-star targets, or `1.0–2.5 m` aluminum trihedral SAR corner reflectors (indicative `~$500–$3,000` each fabricated + concrete/anchor pad) plus customs brokerage / `ATA Carnet`.
  - *Compute, storage & egress:* Multi-view stereo / InSAR cluster hours, long-term Level-1 + COG **standard/hot object storage (`~$0.02/GB-month`; cold/archive tier `~$0.001–$0.004/GB-month`)**, and **cross-region / internet egress fees (`~$0.05–$0.09/GB`)** ([ch28](../../fable/chapters/ch28-reducing-cost.md)), keeping a `15–20 %` re-tasking/weather-standby contingency. *Evidence:* Program-Manager-approved [Cost Ledger](../_shared/template-cost-ledger.md) citing [ch28](../../fable/chapters/ch28-reducing-cost.md) and [sec-27-04](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-04.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** **Evaluate agency scientific data programs (*Trigger:* academic, federal, or non-commercial research eligibility).** If eligible for **NASA CSDAP**, **ESA Third Party Missions (`TPM`)**, **NSIDC/PGC EarthDEM/ArcticDEM**, or **Copernicus Contributing Missions**, submit quota requests `>= 60 days` prior to seasonal window closure to save `60–100 %` of commercial scene acquisition fees ([ch51](../../fable/chapters/ch51-finding-data.md)). *Evidence:* Approved CSDAP/TPM allocation letter referencing [ch51](../../fable/chapters/ch51-finding-data.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** **Specify LERC / ZSTD COG compression targets up front to bound storage and egress spend.** Pre-agree floating-point COG delivery with `ZSTD` + `PREDICTOR=3` (`float32`) or `LERC` (`MAX_Z_ERROR` set to `0.01–0.05 m`, `<= 0.1 x sigma_V`) rather than uncompressed TIFFs or integer-meter `int16` (`0.29 m` uniform quantization floor). *Evidence:* Compression specification line in [Cost Ledger](../_shared/template-cost-ledger.md) and [ch28](../../fable/chapters/ch28-reducing-cost.md).

## Part B — Field-Mob & Daily Ground-Sortie Safety, Access & Burn-Rate Gates

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Secure written Landowner Right-of-Entry (`ROE`) & DOT Roadside Encroachment Permits prior to mobilization.** Obtain signed landowner access authorizations (`corner-reflector piers`, `Siemens stars`, `pressure-logger stakes`) and state/municipal **DOT roadside encroachment permits** before painting crosswalk/shoulder targets or occupying road-shoulder checkpoints. Confirm buried utility locates (`811`) before driving any reflector ground anchors or rebar monuments ([ch25](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md), [ch26](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md)). *Evidence:* Carried paper/PDF ROE & DOT permit folder inside every crew vehicle.
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Enforce the Ground-Control Field Safety Plan, PPE, Lone-Worker Check-Ins & Burn-Rate Gate (`§4.20.4`).** Prior to each daily field sortie, the Ground-Control Lead must verify: (a) **ANSI/ISEA 107 Class 2/3** high-visibility vests, hard hats, and advance `MUTCD` cones/warning signs for all roadside work; (b) cliff/steep-slope fall restraint (`>= 2 m` edge setback or harness); (c) **WBGT heat / wind-chill cold limits** and hydration packs; (d) **`<= 10 hours` driving/shift** fatigue cap and two-person verification of antenna `HI`; (e) mandatory **lone-worker check-in cadence (`<= 2–4 hours`)** via **`Garmin inReach` / Iridium SOS** in cellular dead zones; and (f) daily standby vs. tasking burn-rate confirmation ([Cost Ledger](../_shared/template-cost-ledger.md)). *Evidence:* Daily crew safety sign-in sheet cross-linked to [Accident Card](../_shared/template-accident-card.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** **Implement wildlife disturbance avoidance, seasonal nesting clearances & biosecurity (`§4.20.2`).** Confirm corner-reflector anchoring, stake hammering, and foot/ATV access routes avoid protected avian/mammalian breeding buffers and endangered habitats, and apply invasive-species boot/tripod washdown protocols between watersheds. *Evidence:* Biologist ROE clearance + washdown log.
- [ ] **[COND]** **Complete international ATA Carnet, SDS, IATA DGR 67th Ed. (2026) & lithium fire-safety compliance (*Trigger:* cross-border air freight, U.S. ground transport `> 300 Wh`, or crew passenger flights).** When shipping GNSS base/rovers, total stations, radios, or collapsible aluminum SAR corner reflectors internationally, prepare a serialized **ATA Carnet**, FCC/host-nation UHF radio permits, and **Safety Data Sheets (`SDS`) + `UN 38.3` test summary** complying with: (a) **IATA DGR 67th Ed. (eff. Jan 1, 2026)** enforcing **`<= 30 %` state-of-charge** for both **`UN 3480 PI 965 (Cargo Aircraft Only)`** AND **`UN 3481 PI 966` (packed *with* equipment, `> 2.7 Wh`)** plus `UN 3090`/`UN 3091` for primary lithium-metal cells; (b) **FAA PackSafe** passenger rules (`100–160 Wh` max 2 spare batteries carry-on only, never checked); (c) **49 CFR 171–180** for U.S. ground packs `> 300 Wh`; and (d) chemistry-specific **fire suppression per `SDS`** (copious water deluge / AVD for `UN 3480/3481` Li-ion thermal runaway; reserve **`Class D`** strictly for `UN 3090` Li-metal), carrying `>= 1` spare battery/antenna/cable per rover ([ch26](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md), [09 External Register](./09-resources-and-templates.md#5-external-regulatory--technical-standards-register-prdmd-74)). *Evidence:* Customs Carnet manifest and hazmat shipping receipts logged per [ch26](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Enforce cybersecurity, API credential vaulting & daily GNSS spoofing vigilance (`§4.20.5`).** Store commercial tasking portal tokens, `Earthdata` `.netrc`, and Copernicus credentials inside vault secret managers (never in plaintext scripts); isolate commercial EULA-restricted Level-1 imagery on encrypted access-logged buckets; and monitor daily ground-survey GNSS receivers for **`C/N0` collapse, `AGC` anomalies, or spoofing** near ports, borders, and airports. *Evidence:* Daily receiver interference check + credential audit referencing [ch12](../../fable/chapters/ch12-gnss.md) and [ch69](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md).
