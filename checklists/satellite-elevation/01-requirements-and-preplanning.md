Every satellite elevation order fails most expensively before a single scene is tasked—either by buying new imagery where open global/regional DEMs already meet the specification, confusing a radar or optical Digital Surface Model (DSM) with bare-earth terrain, or signing a vendor SLA whose weather/geometry defaults guarantee unusable DEM coverage. Complete this checklist using [Project Header](../_shared/template-project-header.md) and [Chapter 3 (Fitness for Use)](../../fable/chapters/ch03-fitness-for-use.md) before vendor RFPs or ground mobilization.

## 1. Surface Ontology & Vertical Datum Specification

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Lock the physical surface ontology in the project specification.** Explicitly state whether the deliverable is (a) an optical first-reflective-surface **DSM** (`+z ↑`), (b) a filtered bare-earth **DTM** derived via ground-class filtering plus ICESat-2/lidar bias control (`+z ↑`), (c) a **SAR/InSAR scattering phase-center surface** weighted by wavelength (`X`-band `3.1 cm` top-canopy or `3–10 m` dry-snow bias; `C`-band `5.6 cm` mid-canopy or `10–20 m` dry-firn bias; `L`-band `24 cm` NISAR trunk/ground; `P`-band `69 cm` ESA Biomass ground/trunk), or (d) an **SDB seafloor surface** reduced from instantaneous water surface via tide $\eta(t)$ to chart datum (`+d ↓` under `IHO B-13` / `S-44`). *Evidence:* Signed surface-definition addendum in [Project Header](../_shared/template-project-header.md) referencing [ch04](../../fable/chapters/ch04-names-and-definitions.md), [ch21](../../fable/chapters/ch21-radar-sar-insar.md), and [sec-11-01](../../gemini4/book/ch-11/sec-11-01.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Pin both horizontal CRS/epoch and vertical datum/geoid/SEP mathematically.** Specify the exact EPSG horizontal CRS and dynamic epoch (e.g., `ITRF2020@2026.5` vs `WGS 84 (G2296)` vs regional plate-fixed `NAD 83(2011)`) alongside the exact vertical reference (ellipsoidal height $h$ vs orthometric height $H = h - N$ using pinned `EGM2008`, `GEOID18`, or `VDatum` separation model, noting that `TanDEM-X` and `NISAR DEM V1.2` use `WGS 84` ellipsoid heights whereas `Copernicus GLO-30` and `NASADEM` use `EGM2008` / `EGM96`, and that `NATRF2022`/`NAPGD2022`/`GEOID2022` final public beta released Sept 2026 with official adoption targeted 2027 while `NAD 83`/`NAVD 88` remain official). *Evidence:* Explicit `PROJ`/`EPSG` compound transformation string verified per [ch08](../../fable/chapters/ch08-horizontal-datums.md) and [ch09](../../fable/chapters/ch09-vertical-datums.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Express every accuracy target using explicit statistical definitions and conversions.** Ban unqualified `±` tolerances. Specify exact target metrics matching your standard: **ASPRS Ed. 2 (2024)** reports `1σ` `RMSE_V`, `RMSE_H`, and `RMSE_3D` directly (`NVA`/`VVA`), whereas legacy NSSDA / NGA / Copernicus SLAs use `LE90 = 1.6449 × sigma_V`, `LE95 = 1.9600 × sigma_V`, and `CE90 = 1.5175 × RMSE_r` (`= 2.1460 × RMSE_x` circular isotropic)—explicitly caveat that Gaussian percentile multipliers assume zero mean bias ($\mu = 0$), so always report signed mean bias, non-parametric `NMAD = 1.4826 × median(|r_i - median(r)|)`, and empirical `P90`/`P95` whenever $\mu \neq 0$, plus `2 m` depth-binned `TVU_95%` for SDB. *Evidence:* Accuracy specification table logged in [Error Budget](../_shared/template-error-budget.md) per [ch05](../../fable/chapters/ch05-error-and-uncertainty.md), [ch23](../../fable/chapters/ch23-satellite-derived-bathymetry.md), and [ch53](../../fable/chapters/ch53-accuracy-assessment.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** **Align coastal and shallow-marine projects across the land–sea white ribbon.** When the AOI spans intertidal, riverine, or nearshore depths (`0–30 m`), define early whether SDB / ICESat-2 `ATL24` serves as screening control for higher-accuracy airborne bathylidar or ASV/MBES fills rather than claiming `IHO S-44 Special/Order 1a` compliance from optical SDB (`CATZOC C/D` typical). *Evidence:* Completed zone-partition plan in [Multimodal White-Ribbon Sync](../_shared/multimodal-white-ribbon.md) and [ch23](../../fable/chapters/ch23-satellite-derived-bathymetry.md).

## 2. Archive-First Audit vs. New Tasking Justification

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Execute and document a formal Archive-First DEM & Raw-Scene Audit before new tasking.** Search public and institutional archives first against the required posting, epoch, and `LE90`/`RMSE_V` budget: **Copernicus DEM** (`EEA-10` European `10 m`, `GLO-30` `30 m` `<4 m LE90` abs / `<6 m CE90`, `GLO-90`), **Modified Copernicus (`NISAR DEM V1.2`)**, **TanDEM-X / WorldDEM / EDEM**, **ArcticDEM v4.1 / REMA v2 / EarthDEM** (`2 m` `SETSM` strips/mosaics), **NASADEM / SRTM v3**, **AW3D30 v4.1**, **FABDEM v1-2** (forest/built bias-removed Copernicus), **DeltaDTM / CoastalDEM / DiluviumDEM** (coastal floodplains), **GEBCO** (latest public grid release, `GEBCO_2024`/`2025` / handbook `GEBCO_2026` baseline), **ICESat-2** (`ATL06`/`ATL08`/`ATL24`), **GEDI L2A**, **SWOT KaRIn** water elevations, **NISAR L-band** (`L0–L2`), **ESA Biomass P-band**, and historical **CORONA KH-4B / HEXAGON KH-9** film scans for multi-decadal change baselines. *Evidence:* Timestamped catalog search export + rejection rationale table referencing [Appendix E](../../fable/appendices/appendix-e-public-products-tables.md) and [ch51](../../fable/chapters/ch51-finding-data.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Verify archive commercial stereo availability before paying new-tasking multipliers.** Commercial archive stereo/tri-stereo (`Pléiades Neo`, `WorldView-2/3`, `SkySat`) typically costs ~30–35 % less than new collection and eliminates weather-window schedule risk, provided scene age satisfies change-currency thresholds. *Evidence:* Archive footprint overlay (with cloud masks, convergence angles, and acquisition dates) archived in [ch28](../../fable/chapters/ch28-reducing-cost.md) screening memo.
- [ ] **[COND]** **Apply multi-epoch deformation and change-currency screens (*Trigger:* AOI sits in active seismic, volcanic, landslide, permafrost, glacial, coastal-barrier, or urban-construction zones).** Reject archive DEMs older than the last significant surface event (`>1 year` construction/coastal or post-event date) and mandate synchronized new collection ([ch36](../../fable/chapters/ch36-seasonal-variability.md), [ch37](../../fable/chapters/ch37-time-scales-of-change.md), [ch39](../../fable/chapters/ch39-earthquakes-volcanoes-landslides.md)). *Evidence:* Hazard/change currency assessment citing [ch37](../../fable/chapters/ch37-time-scales-of-change.md) and [ch39](../../fable/chapters/ch39-earthquakes-volcanoes-landslides.md).

## 3. Environmental Climatology, Vendor SLA Rejection & De-Scope Governance

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Run a 10-year monthly site climatology and feasibility analysis prior to setting tasking windows.** Quantify historical monthly cloud fraction, fog/cirrus frequency, seasonal snowpack (`NDSI`), leaf-on vs. leaf-off canopy dates, and solar elevation angle (`> 30°` preferred to prevent deep canyon/urban shadows). For SDB, extract multi-year satellite turbidity (`K_d(490)` or Sentinel-2/VIIRS SPM) and astronomical tidal phase to confirm `2–3 / K_d` exceeds target charting depth ([ch23](../../fable/chapters/ch23-satellite-derived-bathymetry.md), [sec-11-03](../../gemini4/book/ch-11/sec-11-03.md)). For InSAR/NISAR/Sentinel-1, check dry/wet seasonal freeze–thaw transitions and ionospheric scintillation forecasts (`Kp >= 5` geomagnetic storms degrade L/P-band phase). *Evidence:* Month-by-month green/amber/red collection matrix referencing [ch26](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md) and [sec-07-05](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-05.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Write binding quantitative SLA Acceptance & Rejection criteria into the vendor tasking order.** Never accept default commercial "flex" weather/geometry clauses without hard rejection thresholds: maximum cloud + cloud-shadow percentage inside the AOI polygon (e.g., `<= 5 %` or `<= 10 %` depending on tier, with `0 %` over primary GCPs/tie zones), maximum off-nadir angle (e.g., `<= 25°` urban / `<= 30°` general), convergence angle bounds (`15°–25°` dense urban, `30°–45°` open terrain; `B/H` within contracted bounds), no panchromatic saturation (`> 0.5 %` pixels clipped over snow/sand), and an explicit **scene-inspection clock** (e.g., `5–10 business days` from delivery) within which rejected scenes trigger free re-tasking or refund. *Evidence:* Redlined vendor Order Form & Statement of Work (SOW) signed per [ch68](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Define quantitative cloud-out, contingency pivot, and de-scope/abandonment rules (`§4.20.9`).** Establish exact decision gates before tasking starts: (a) if cumulative acceptable stereo coverage is `< 50 %` at `T - 14 days` before seasonal snow-in/leaf-on closure, invoke multi-constellation cross-tasking (`WorldView` + `Pléiades Neo` + `Pelican`); (b) if optical stereo cannot clear clouds past the pass deadline, trigger contractual de-scope/cancellation (`0 %` delivery penalty or credit transfer) and pivot to bistatic/repeat-pass **SAR InSAR** (`TanDEM-X` / `NISAR` / `Sentinel-1`) or archive-plus-ICESat-2 fusion; (c) if coastal `K_d` exceeds `0.5 m^-1` for 3 consecutive cloud-free overpasses, abort optical SDB and escalate to airborne bathylidar or USV sonar. *Evidence:* Escalation decision tree signed in [Project Header](../_shared/template-project-header.md) and [ch26](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** **Budget an independent 5–10 % validation and ground-control reserve.** Allocate `5–10 %` of total project spend explicitly to withheld check points (`>= 30` per land-cover stratum where field access permits), ICESat-2/GEDI independent downloads, and independent QA review rather than grading accuracy using vendor self-reported metadata ([ch26](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md), [ch52](../../fable/chapters/ch52-ground-truth.md)). *Evidence:* Line-item validation allocation in [Cost Ledger](../_shared/template-cost-ledger.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** **Document multi-sensor archive fusion tolerances before ordering fill imagery.** When blending commercial optical stereo with `Copernicus GLO-30` or `ArcticDEM v4.1` void fills, lock vertical co-registration tolerances (`<= 0.5 m` mean step bias) and require a per-pixel provenance flag distinguishing fill cells from new tasking ([ch48](../../fable/chapters/ch48-compositing.md), [ch54](../../fable/chapters/ch54-evaluating-others-data.md)). *Evidence:* Blending rule table archived in [Project Header](../_shared/template-project-header.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** **Inspect historical CORONA / KH-9 archive geometry for multi-decadal elevation baselines (*Trigger:* pre-1990 historical elevation baseline requested).** Before committing to KH-4B or KH-9 stereo extraction, verify raw USGS EarthExplorer / NARA film scan resolution (`7 µm` preferred), intact border fiducials/1,081 reseau crosses on KH-9 frames, `< 15 %` cloud over glacier/landform targets, and presence of stable bedrock control surrounding the changing zone. *Evidence:* Film-frame quick-look screening log referencing [ch22](../../fable/chapters/ch22-photogrammetry-sfm.md) and [sec-07-05](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-05.md).
