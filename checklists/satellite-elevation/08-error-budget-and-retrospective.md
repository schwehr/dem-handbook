## 2. ML Bias-Removal, Super-Resolution & Provenance Disclosure ([ch43](../../fable/chapters/ch43-traditional-vs-ml.md), [ch45](../../fable/chapters/ch45-super-resolution.md))

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Execute mandatory ML / Super-Resolution / Void-Fill Honest Disclosure.** Clearly certify in product metadata, `STAC` descriptions, and client reports whether any pixel in the deliverable underwent: (a) ML canopy/building bias correction (`FABDEM`-style random forest/GBM); (b) Single-Image Super-Resolution (`SISR`), diffusion, or neural downscaling; or (c) spline/Laplacian/GAN cloud-void inpainting.
