## 1. Orbital Collection Failures, Cloud-Outs & Corrupted Telemetry (`§4.20.9`)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Execute the Cloud-Out & Window-Slip Escalation Ladder before seasonal closure.** Trigger immediately if cumulative cloud-free stereo/SDB coverage sits `< 50 %` at `T - 14 days` before snow-in, leaf-on, or monsoon closure, OR after `3` consecutive failed orbital passes: (1) freeze discretionary expansion spend; (2) activate pre-approved **multi-constellation cross-tasking** (`Pléiades Neo` + `WorldView` + `SkySat/Pelican`); and (3) if clouds fail to clear past the contractual pass deadline, formally invoke the **de-scope / optical-to-InSAR pivot** (`TanDEM-X` / `NISAR` / `Sentinel-1` + `ICESat-2` control) or terminate remaining optical tasking with `0 %` delivery penalty per [ch26](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md). *Evidence:* Signed escalation memo + vendor SLA modification notice.
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Trigger Vendor Rejection, Free Re-Tasking, or Refund strictly within the SLA clock.** If delivered scenes breach contracted cloud %, off-nadir angle, `B/H` / convergence bounds, overlap (`< 95 %`), pan saturation, `RFI`, or InSAR coherence (`< 0.45`): (1) halt invoice milestone approval immediately; (2) deliver an evidence pack (`AOI KML`, cloud/saturation mask, realized geometry printout) **before the contracted SLA acceptance clock expires (`verify PO; typically 5–10 business days`)**; (3) demand no-cost priority re-tasking or contractual refund; and (4) segregate rejected raw files from downstream DEM processing ([ch68](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md)). *Evidence:* Timestamped rejection ticket + carrier acknowledgment email.
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Quarantine corrupted orbital telemetry and run fallback calibration (`RPC` / `POD` / `KH-9` defects).** When imagery arrives with truncated `RPB` blocks, attitude dropouts (`jitter > 2 px`), `RESORB`-only SAR ephemeris (`POEORB` delayed), or damaged `KH-9` film borders: (1) quarantine affected strips; (2) test rigorous CSM/linescan jitter unmixing (`ASP jitter_solve` where `.xml`/`.json` models exist) and multi-GCP / `ICESat-2` / `Copernicus` bedrock alignment; and (3) downgrade or reject the strip if post-adjustment withheld checkpoint `RMSE_V` breaches spec ([ch22](../../fable/chapters/ch22-photogrammetry-sfm.md), [sec-07-05](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-05.md)). *Evidence:* Quarantine ledger + post-adjustment checkpoint diagnostic plot.
- [ ] **[COND]** **Suspend coastal SDB during storm turbidity or harmful algal blooms (*Trigger:* coastal SDB storm plume, dredging, or red tide drives `K_d > 0.5 m^-1`).** Issue an immediate SDB pause whenever optical extinction collapses below target depth and re-route shallow coverage to airborne bathylidar or USV multibeam per [White-Ribbon Sync](../_shared/multimodal-white-ribbon.md) and [ch23](../../fable/chapters/ch23-satellite-derived-bathymetry.md). *Evidence:* Suspended SDB notice + handoff work order.

## 2. Ground-Truth Crew Roadside, Terrain, Marine & Detention Emergencies (`§4.20.4`, `§4.20.1`)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Execute immediate Roadside, Cliff-Fall, Heatstroke, or Hypothermia Life-Safety Response (`T + 0–15 min`).** Upon any field ground-crew accident during GCP painting, checkpoint occupation, corner-reflector anchoring, or tide-gauge work: (1) halt all field operations immediately; (2) secure traffic/slope hazards (hazard flashers, high-vis cones, remove crew from active lanes if safe; never move suspected spinal trauma unless fire/traffic threat is imminent); (3) initiate first aid/cooling/warming and summon EMS via `911` or **`Garmin inReach` / Iridium SOS**; and (4) log exact UTC, GNSS coordinates, witnesses, and PPE status onto [Accident Card](../_shared/template-accident-card.md). *Evidence:* Completed [Accident Card](../_shared/template-accident-card.md) + EMS/SOS dispatch log.
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Meet statutory OSHA, Commercial Auto, Workers' Comp & FVWC reporting deadlines.** Preserve undisturbed equipment, dashcam/GNSS interference logs, and site photographs ([ch12](../../fable/chapters/ch12-gnss.md)), then notify: (1) **OSHA (`29 CFR § 1904.39`) within `8 hours` for any fatality, or within `24 hours` for any inpatient hospitalization, amputation, or loss of an eye** (`+1-800-321-6742`; or host-nation OSH authority outside U.S. jurisdiction); (2) local police immediately for motor-vehicle collisions; and (3) domestic Workers' Compensation, Commercial Auto/CGL, and **Foreign Voluntary Workers' Comp (`FVWC`) / Medevac** carrier within **`<= 24 hours`**. *Evidence:* OSHA confirmation number + insurer claim numbers logged in [Accident Card](../_shared/template-accident-card.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** **Execute Marine / Small-Boat Casualty & Spill Clocks (*Trigger:* coastal SDB calibration vessel, Secchi team, or tide-gauge small craft operating on navigable waters).** Monitor `VHF Ch 16 (voice) / Ch 70 (DSC)`, activate PLB/EPIRB upon capsizing/MOB, notify USCG Sector/COTP immediately under **`46 CFR § 4.05-1`** + file **`CG-2692` within `5 days`** (death, injury beyond first aid, grounding, or `> $75,000` property damage) with **`46 CFR § 4.06`** Serious Marine Incident (`2 h` alcohol / `32 h` drug) testing, and notify **NRC (`+1-800-424-8802`) immediately** upon any fuel/oil discharge (`33 CFR § 153.203`; report to coastal-state MRCC/flag authority outside U.S. waters per [09 External Register](./09-resources-and-templates.md#5-external-regulatory--technical-standards-register-prdmd-74)). *Evidence:* USCG/MRCC log + `CG-2692` filing confirmation.
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Execute Consular & Legal Escalation upon field-crew detention or access dispute (*Trigger:* cross-border or sensitive field deployment challenged/detained).** If military, police, border guards, or landowners challenge or detain field staff operating survey GNSS, UHF radios, or aluminum corner reflectors: (1) immediately power down active equipment and make no unapproved imagery/recordings; (2) present carried Right-of-Entry letters, ATA Carnet manifests, and radio/survey permits calmly; (3) contact Program Legal Counsel and the nearest **Embassy/Consulate Citizen Services Desk within `<= 2 hours`** (project SOP target); and (4) suspend all AOI ground work until written legal clearance issues ([ch68](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md), [ch69](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md)). *Evidence:* Detention incident log + counsel/consular case ID.
- [ ] **[COND]** **Respond to GNSS jamming, spoofing, or UHF RF interference during ground-control surveys (*Trigger:* field rovers show abrupt `C/N0` drop, `AGC` anomaly, or `> 0.5 m` solution jump).** Disable RTK broadcast immediately, switch to long-baseline **static `PPK` (`>= 45 min`) + optical total-station traverses** anchored outside the RF zone, and archive receiver status logs ([ch12](../../fable/chapters/ch12-gnss.md), [ch14](../../fable/chapters/ch14-positioning-beyond-gnss.md)). *Evidence:* RF anomaly timestamp log + total-station traverse check.
- [ ] **[COND]** **Execute Wildlife, Wildfire Smoke, Heat Illness, or Flash-Flood Evacuation (*Trigger:* environmental or wildlife hazard threatens field crew).** If wildfire smoke, flash flood, extreme `WBGT` heat exceedance, or aggressive wildlife threatens field personnel, abort surveying immediately along pre-briefed egress routes, confirm crew headcount via `inReach`/radio check-in, and hold re-entry until Safety/Biologist clearance ([ch26](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md)). *Evidence:* Evacuation & headcount confirmation log.

## 3. EULA / GDPR / ITAR Data Spills & Cyber / HPC Compromise (`§4.20.1`, `§4.20.5`, `§4.20.7`)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Contain EULA, ITAR/EAR, GDPR (`<= 72 h` Art. 33), or Sovereignty Data Spills within `<= 1 hour` SOP.** If raw commercial imagery (`Pan/MS`, `.RPB`), `ITAR`/`EAR`-restricted POD/attitude files, unmasked `<= 0.5 m` urban orthos (`GDPR`), `India`/`China` restricted-buffer grids, or unredacted `NHPA §106` / `CARE` tribal locations are exposed via misconfigured cloud buckets, Git pushes, or unauthorized email: (1) revoke public ACLs, block bucket access, and purge repository history/CDN caches within **`<= 1 hour`** (project SOP); (2) capture S3/HTTP audit logs to determine exact external download scope; (3) notify Legal Counsel and the commercial vendor's compliance officer within **`<= 24 hours`**; and (4) file statutory supervisory notice within **`<= 72 hours` under `GDPR Art. 33`** whenever personal data/imagery PII is breached ([ch68](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md), [ch69](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md), [sec-27-01](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-01.md), [sec-27-02](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-02.md), [sec-27-03](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-03.md)). *Evidence:* Containment timestamp log + access-log audit + Art. 33 / vendor disclosure receipt.
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Isolate cyber/ransomware incidents and restore solely from immutable `SHA-256` BagIt archives.** Upon suspected ransomware, API-key leakage (`Earthdata`, Copernicus, commercial portals), or HPC scratch corruption: (1) sever affected compute nodes and rotate all API tokens, SSH keys, and cloud IAM credentials **`<= 2 hours`**; (2) verify the read-only off-site **`BagIt SHA-256` Level-1 archive** (`sha256sum -c manifest-sha256.txt`) for zero tampering; and (3) re-provision ephemeral clean containers rather than trusting unverified scratch outputs ([ch50](../../fable/chapters/ch50-archiving-and-provenance.md)). *Evidence:* Key-rotation ticket + `sha256sum -c` pass verification.
- [ ] **[REC]** **Freeze forensic incident logs under `FRE 901/902(13)–(14)` chain-of-custody with medical PII redaction (`<= 24 h`).** Immediately preserve unmodified GNSS RINEX `.obs`, dashcam, safety sign-offs, S3 logs, and vendor rejection correspondence in a read-only `SHA-256` hold bundle authenticated under **`FRE 901 / 902(13)–(14)`**, segregating and redacting injured-person medical records and witness PII before insurer/client sharing ([ch50](../../fable/chapters/ch50-archiving-and-provenance.md), [sec-27-02](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-02.md)). *Evidence:* `SHA-256` litigation-hold manifest ID + redacted PII index.
- [ ] **[REC]** **Enforce a `24-hour` operational stand-down & post-incident root-cause hold.** Suspend field deployments and new scene acceptances after any serious crew injury, detention, or Level-1 spill until interim safeguards are approved for formal capture in Phase `08` ([AAR Retrospective](../_shared/template-aar-retrospective.md)). *Evidence:* Signed Stand-Down Release Memo.
