Commercial and space-agency deliveries invert traditional survey QC: once a satellite scene is accepted past its contractual inspection deadline, re-tasking rights expire and the project absorbs full collection cost. Every quick-look image, SAR burst, and ground-control tie must be screened immediately against quantitative geometry, radiometry, coherence, and cloud thresholds—while unmodified Level-1 raw products and sidecar metadata are locked into an immutable `SHA-256` archive upon receipt ([Chapter 29](../../fable/chapters/ch29-processing-pipelines.md), [Chapter 50](../../fable/chapters/ch50-archiving-and-provenance.md)).

## 1. Active Orbital Window Monitoring & Field Control Synchronization

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Maintain daily tasking-attempt tracking and pre-overpass ground-target checks (`T - 24 h`).** For each scheduled orbital pass (`WorldView`, `Pléiades`/`Neo`, `SkySat`/`Pelican`, `TanDEM-X`, `NISAR`), log predicted weather/cloud cover, orbital azimuth, and off-nadir angle, and verify with the ground team `<= 24 h` prior that painted checkerboards/Siemens stars remain unobstructed (`no parked vehicles, mud, or snow cover`), SAR corner-reflector drainage holes and interior panels are dry and clear, and tide gauges are logging continuously ([ch25](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md), [ch26](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md)). *Evidence:* Daily pass-tracking sheet + `T - 24 h` target-check log referencing [Project Header](../_shared/template-project-header.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Harvest synchronous environmental, GNSS, tidal, and space-weather records for every overpass UTC timestamp.** Record and archive concurrently: (a) `1 Hz` / `30 s` `IGS`/`NOAA CORS` RINEX files bracketing ground GCP/checkpoint/reflector ties; (b) `<= 6 min` interval water-level telemetry $\eta(t)$ (`CO-OPS` / `GLOSS` / local gauge) bracketing every SDB scene UTC to `<= 1 min`; (c) NOAA `Kp` geomagnetic index (`flag Kp >= 5` for L/P-band InSAR); and (d) surface wind speed (`< 5–7 m/s` project target for low-whitecap SDB). *Evidence:* Time-indexed auxiliary data manifest citing [ch12](../../fable/chapters/ch12-gnss.md) and [ch23](../../fable/chapters/ch23-satellite-derived-bathymetry.md).

## 2. Vendor Delivery Quick-Look Acceptance / Rejection Gate (`SLA Clock`)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Execute full scene QC strictly within the contracted vendor inspection window (contract threshold: verify PO, typically `48 h` to `5–10 business days`).** Start an explicit countdown timer upon vendor delivery notification; never sign milestone acceptance or ingest scenes into production until the QA Reviewer confirms every geometry and radiometry gate passes—or issues a formal written rejection before the SLA window lapses ([ch68](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md)). *Evidence:* Timestamped acceptance/rejection disposition log with countdown audit trail.
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Enforce quantitative Optical Stereo / Tri-Stereo Quick-Look Acceptance thresholds.** Reject the delivery and trigger contractual re-tasking/credit if **any** of the following checks fails on Level-1/quick-look inspection:
  - *Cloud & Shadow Coverage:* Cloud + cast cloud shadow inside the AOI exceeds contracted SLA ceiling (e.g., `> 5 %` or `> 10 %`), OR **any** cloud/cirrus haze occludes deployed GCPs, withheld checkpoint zones, or mission-critical infrastructure;
  - *Realized Acquisition Geometry:* Off-nadir angle exceeds cap (`> 25°` dense urban / `> 30°` open), convergence angle falls outside contracted range (`15°–25°` urban, `30°–45°` open), or realized `B/H` drops below `0.25` (causing excessive $\sigma_z = \frac{g\,\sigma_{p,\mathrm{px}}}{2\tan(\alpha/2)}$ inflation; [sec-07-05](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-05.md));
  - *Continuity & Overlap:* Effective stereo/tri-stereo overlap covers `< 95 %` of the AOI, or single-pass interval exceeds `120 s` (introducing vehicle/cloud/shadow mismatch);
  - *Radiometry & Sidecars:* Panchromatic saturation (`> 0.5 %` pixels clipped over bright sand, roofs, or snow), severe haze flattening local contrast, dropped scan lines/banding, or missing `.RPB` (`RPC00B`), `.IMD`, `.ATT`, `.EPH`, or `CSM` `.xml`/`.json` telemetry ([ch22](../../fable/chapters/ch22-photogrammetry-sfm.md), [sec-08-01](../../gemini4/book/ch-08/sec-08-01.md)).
  *Evidence:* Per-strip Quick-Look Pass/Fail Checklist (`q1_disposition.json` / card in [09-resources-and-templates.md](./09-resources-and-templates.md#3-copy-ready-template-b--quick-look-scene-acceptance--rejection-gate-card-phase-05)).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Enforce quantitative SAR / InSAR Quick-Look & Coherence thresholds (*Trigger:* radar / InSAR collection track).** Before accepting `TanDEM-X CoSSC`, `Sentinel-1 SLC`, `NISAR L/S-band`, or `SWOT` scenes, inspect intensity and quick-look wrapped phase to reject acquisitions showing: (a) missing swaths/bursts or burst timing mismatch exceeding project threshold (`> 15 ms` operational gate); (b) broadband Radio-Frequency Interference (`RFI` bright linear stripes corrupting range compression); (c) realized $B_\perp$ exceeding planned tolerance (`> 0.35 B_{\perp,\mathrm{crit}}`); or (d) wrapped coherence **`gamma < 0.45–0.60`** across non-water bare terrain ([ch21](../../fable/chapters/ch21-radar-sar-insar.md), [sec-11-01](../../gemini4/book/ch-11/sec-11-01.md)). *Evidence:* Quick-look amplitude, coherence histogram, and baseline log.
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Enforce quantitative SDB Optical Water-Surface QC (*Trigger:* coastal SDB collection track).** Inspect blue/green/NIR/SWIR bands before accepting commercial coastal scenes for bathymetry: reject collections exhibiting un-correctable specular **sunglint (`> 10 %` of shallow zone)**, breaking surf/whitecap foam across target bars, or river flood sediment plumes hiding bottom reflectance across known calibration transects ([ch23](../../fable/chapters/ch23-satellite-derived-bathymetry.md), [sec-11-03](../../gemini4/book/ch-11/sec-11-03.md)). *Evidence:* NIR/SWIR glint mask and RGB water-clarity preview.

## 3. Target Visibility Check, Immutable Ingest & Field Triage

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Verify physical GCPs and SAR corner reflectors in delivered Level-1 imagery before ground demobilization.** Open full-resolution panchromatic (`~0.3–0.5 m`) or `SLC` amplitude chips around every field target: confirm painted/staked Siemens stars or checkerboards resolve cleanly (`>= 5 x GSD` diameter, sub-pixel centroid pickable within `<= 0.25–0.5 px`), and confirm each SAR corner reflector produces a crisp point impulse response achieving **`SCR >= 20 dB`** above surrounding ground clutter without receiver saturation ([ch25](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md), [sec-12-06](../../gemini4/book/ch-12/sec-12-06.md)). Check GNSS tie closure (`<= 0.03 m` vertical repeatability). *Evidence:* Annotated `128 x 128 px` target chip gallery + SCR measurements.
- [ ] **[COND]** **Survey emergency substitute natural GCPs (*Trigger:* local cloud shadow, dust, or paving occludes `>= 1` painted GCP in an otherwise-accepted strip).** While the field crew remains nearby, dispatch them within **`<= 48 h`** to survey crisp co-visible hard features (`paint corners, culvert headwalls, manhole rims`) visible inside cloud-free patches before closing field support ([ch26](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md), [ch52](../../fable/chapters/ch52-ground-truth.md)). *Evidence:* Supplemental GCP tie sheets + GNSS occupations.
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Lock unmodified raw Level-1 deliverables into an immutable `3-2-1` `SHA-256` BagIt archive (`§4.20.7`).** Immediately upon scene acceptance, compute `sha256sum` across all unmodified vendor tarballs, pan/MS GeoTIFF/NITF files, `.RPB`/`.ATT`/`.EPH`, SAR `SLC` + `POEORB`, tide logs, and RINEX occupations inside a `BagIt` structure (`manifest-sha256.txt`), replicate across two storage media plus one off-site geo-redundant region, verify EULA access controls (`RESTRICTED-RAW-IMAGERY` vs public deliverables), and **prohibit in-place edits inside raw folders** ([ch50](../../fable/chapters/ch50-archiving-and-provenance.md), [sec-27-01](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-01.md)). *Evidence:* Passing `bagit.py --validate` / `sha256sum -c` log.
- [ ] **[REC]** **Run a rapid `24–48 h` coarse triage DEM / interferogram / Stumpf preview.** Execute a fast coarse-grid (`4–8 x GSD` stereo, multi-looked `InSAR`, or un-tuned `Stumpf`) pipeline strictly for operational triage—surfacing gross disparity voids, unwrapping `2*pi` island tears, or water-mask gaps early enough to trigger supplemental tasking while the satellite orbital campaign remains open. *Evidence:* Coarse quick-look hillshade + percentage-void report referencing [ch29](../../fable/chapters/ch29-processing-pipelines.md) and [ch54](../../fable/chapters/ch54-evaluating-others-data.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** **Verify quick-look geolocation offset against `Copernicus GLO-30` / coastline baselines (`<= 24 h`).** Run a fast automated tie comparison between un-adjusted strip footprints and `GLO-30` or `ITRF` reference roads/coastlines so gross `RPC` frame shifts (`> 10–20 m`) or half-pixel header flips trigger vendor inspection before SLA expiry ([ch41](../../fable/chapters/ch41-change-detection.md), [ch54](../../fable/chapters/ch54-evaluating-others-data.md)). *Evidence:* Strip geolocation check log (`median delta E/N`).
- [ ] **[COND]** **Verify multi-strip overlap and tie continuity across rolling AOI deliveries (*Trigger:* AOI requires multi-pass or multi-date strip mosaics).** When large AOIs assemble across multiple dates/passes, inspect `>= 10–15 %` adjacent strip overlap over cloud-free stable terrain to spot inter-strip elevation jumps (`> 2 m` pre-adjustment) while re-tasking remains active ([ch48](../../fable/chapters/ch48-compositing.md)). *Evidence:* Multi-strip seamline offset check referencing [ch48](../../fable/chapters/ch48-compositing.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** **Capture synchronous field photography, snow/ice state & turbidity/Secchi verification logs during overpasses.** Time-stamp ground shoreline photos, foliage/snow notes, or vessel `Secchi` readings within `<= 2 hours` of clear-sky overpasses to constrain Phase `07` empirical models and penetration bias ([ch23](../../fable/chapters/ch23-satellite-derived-bathymetry.md), [ch36](../../fable/chapters/ch36-seasonal-variability.md)). *Evidence:* Geo-tagged overpass field log.
- [ ] **[REC]** **Track daily burn-rate, cloud-rejection ratio, and remaining weather budget.** Update [Cost Ledger](../_shared/template-cost-ledger.md) after every vendor delivery/rejection cycle so re-tasking credits and standby field-crew extensions remain inside the `15–20 %` contingency reserve ([ch26](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md), [ch28](../../fable/chapters/ch28-reducing-cost.md)). *Evidence:* Updated cost-to-complete tracker.
