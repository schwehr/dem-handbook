Spaceborne orbital sensors are calibrated by space agencies and commercial operators, not field technicians—so project preparation concentrates on **ephemeris and sensor-model fidelity**, **traceable GNSS ground control and independent withheld check points**, **SAR corner-reflector engineering**, and **water-level/depth calibration priors for SDB**. Under [JCGM 200](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md), never conflate sensor calibration verification (conducted by the provider) with project geometric calibration (RPC bias removal, corner-reflector phase-screen anchoring, or SDB empirical regression) or independent validation.

## 1. Ephemeris, Sensor-Model Tier & Archive Film Prep

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Verify and lock the orbital ephemeris / Rational Polynomial Coefficient (`RPC`) tier prior to order release.** Confirm which geometric model accompanies Level-1 imagery: (a) standard **80-coefficient `RPC00B`** normalized rational polynomials (`WorldView`, `Pléiades`, `SkySat`, `Pelican`) plus vendor bias-corrected `RPB` metadata (`CE90` nominal un-grounded `~2–5 m`); (b) **rigorous physical pushbroom ephemeris & attitude** (`SPICE`/vendor `.ATT` + `.EPH` at `50–100 Hz` or `CSM` `.xml`/`.json`) required when sub-pixel pointing jitter (`jitter_solve`) must be unmixed; or (c) **InSAR Precise Orbit Ephemeris (`POEORB`)** (`Sentinel-1`/`NISAR`, `<= 5 cm` 3D accuracy vs `~10 cm–1 m` restituted `RESORB`; `TanDEM-X` `< 1 mm` inter-satellite baseline calibration). *Evidence:* Sensor-model delivery checklist verified against [ch21](../../fable/chapters/ch21-radar-sar-insar.md), [ch22](../../fable/chapters/ch22-photogrammetry-sfm.md), and [sec-08-01](../../gemini4/book/ch-08/sec-08-01.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Verify physical film-scan calibration and reseau integrity for historical CORONA / KH-9 tasks.** Before ingesting `KH-1`–`KH-4B` panoramic or `KH-9` mapping frames, confirm `7 µm` calibrated photogrammetric scanning (`<= 1 µm` stage repeatability), full visibility of border fiducials or all `1,081` calibrated reseau crosses (`23 x 47` grid on `KH-9` frame cameras), and documented nominal focal length (`0.305 m` / `0.61 m`) for pre-bundle film distortion modeling. *Evidence:* Pre-ingest scan-QC report citing [ch22](../../fable/chapters/ch22-photogrammetry-sfm.md) and [sec-07-05](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-05.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** **Tie ground networks to global `IGS` / `CORS` / `SONEL` stations and check CEOS Cal/Val telemetry.** Anchor static/PPK ground-control campaigns to active `IGS20` / `NOAA CORS` / `SONEL` benchmarks, and consult CEOS Cal/Val PICS/corner-reflector logs (`Libya-4`, `Rosamond`, `Surat Basin`) when ordering absolute radiometric or SAR sigma-nought calibration products. *Evidence:* Station metadata table archived per [ch25](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md).

## 2. Optical Stereo Ground Control (`GCPs`) & Independent Check Points (`CPs`)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Design and deploy photo-identifiable GCP targets sized to 5–10 x GSD.** For commercial `0.30–0.50 m` panchromatic imagery, paint or stake high-contrast **checkerboards or 4-to-8-arm Siemens stars** with outer target diameter `>= 5–10 x GSD` (e.g., `1.8–3.0 m` matte-black/white painted vinyl or road paint for `0.30 m` imagery so centroid picking reaches `<= 0.25 px`), or select crisp flat ground corners (crosswalk stripes, concrete slab corners, runway markings away from tall structures or shadow traps). Plan `>= 5` well-distributed GCPs per stereo pair (and `10–15` across complex relief, spanning perimeter corners, center, and min/max elevations). *Evidence:* GCP layout map + target dimension spec per [ch25](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md) and [ch52](../../fable/chapters/ch52-ground-truth.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Survey, isolate, and enforce strict independence between GCPs and withheld Check Points (`CPs`).** Survey **`>= 30` independent check points per terrain/land-cover stratum** (satisfying ASPRS Ed. 2 (2024) statistical sampling across bare/open `NVA` and vegetated `VVA` strata) using static/PPK/RTN GNSS (`RMSE_V <= 0.03–0.05 m`, `RMSE_H <= 0.03 m`, verified against published benchmarks). Store CP coordinates in a read-only validation vault **physically and permission-separated from the photogrammetric bundle-adjustment team**—never reuse an adjusted GCP or un-separated GNSS shot as a validation checkpoint. *Evidence:* Separate `gcps_control.csv` and SHA-256-sealed `checkpoints_withheld.csv` files logged per [ch52](../../fable/chapters/ch52-ground-truth.md) and [ch53](../../fable/chapters/ch53-accuracy-assessment.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Enforce GNSS survey QA, antenna-height double-checks, and RF/spoofing hygiene in the field (`§4.20.4`, `§4.20.5`).** Load exact `IGS` absolute `ANTEX` phase-center offsets (`igs20.atx`), enforce `PDOP <= 3.0`, take redundant independent check occupations separated by `>= 2 hours` (or re-initialize RTK twice on different satellite constellations), independently measure and photograph tripod antenna height (`HI`) before and after occupation, and inspect receiver `C/N0` / `AGC` logs for GNSS jamming or spoofing near ports, borders, and airports. *Evidence:* Field GNSS check-shot delta table (`|delta_H| <= 0.03 m` repeatability) and receiver status logs referencing [ch12](../../fable/chapters/ch12-gnss.md).

## 3. SAR Corner Reflectors & Radiometric Calibration Targets

- [ ] **[COND]** **Size triangular trihedral SAR corner reflectors (`CRs`) for target frequency and background clutter (*Trigger:* InSAR/SAR radiometric or geodetic corner-reflector control deployed).** Using the triangular trihedral peak radar cross-section formula $\sigma_{\max} = \frac{4\pi a^4}{3\lambda^2}$ ([sec-12-06](../../gemini4/book/ch-12/sec-12-06.md), [ch25](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md)), size inner leg length $a$ so the reflector achieves a Signal-to-Clutter Ratio **`SCR >= 20–30 dB`** above surrounding bare ground (`sigma_0 ~ -15 to -10 dB`):
  - **X-band (`3.10 cm`, e.g., TanDEM-X):** $a = 0.7\text{–}1.0\text{ m}$ (`+36.39 dBsm` at $1.0\text{ m}$).
  - **C-band (`5.60 cm`, e.g., Sentinel-1):** $a = 1.0\text{–}1.5\text{ m}$ (`+31.26 dBsm` at $1.0\text{ m}$, `+38.30 dBsm` at $1.5\text{ m}$).
  - **L-band (`23.84 cm` NISAR `1.2575 GHz` / `24.0 cm` nominal):** $a = 2.0\text{–}2.5\text{ m}$ (`+34.59 dBsm` at $2.5\text{ m}$ for `23.84 cm` [`+34.53 dBsm` at `24.0 cm`]; $1.0\text{ m}$ yields only `+18.67 dBsm` [`+18.62 dBsm` at `24.0 cm`], insufficient over vegetated clutter).
  Machine drainage holes (`<= lambda / 10` diameter) at the inner vertex so rain, snowmelt, or debris cannot pool inside the reflector. *Evidence:* Reflector fabrication & SCR link-budget sheet referencing [ch25](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md) and [sec-12-06](../../gemini4/book/ch-12/sec-12-06.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Verify CR boresight alignment, frost-free concrete pier mounting & inner-apex GNSS tie.** Align the reflector's symmetric boresight axis (`35.26°` elevation above its base triangle) toward the exact satellite ascending or descending track azimuth and local incidence angle ($\theta_{\mathrm{inc}}$), holding alignment error well within the `-3 dB` trihedral beamwidth (`-18° to +18°` azimuth, `-20° to +20°` elevation). Anchor to bedrock or deep frost-resistant concrete piers in flat, low-backscatter open ground (`>= 50 m` from buildings/trees/fences), and survey the **true inner geometric apex** via total station prism offset from two nearby GNSS monuments (`sigma <= 0.01 m` `1σ`). *Evidence:* Azimuth/elevation inclinometer photos + total-station apex coordinate certificate per [ch25](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md).

## 4. Satellite-Derived Bathymetry (`SDB`) Calibration & Water-Level Infrastructure

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Install or link calibrated water-level gauges (`eta(t)`) synchronized to Chart Datum / Ellipsoid (`SEP`).** Every SDB scene timestamp (UTC accurate to `<= 1 min`) must resolve instantaneous water surface height $\eta(t)$ above Chart Datum using nearby `NOAA CO-OPS` / `GLOSS` gauges or project-deployed vented/barometric pressure transducers surveyed to orthometric/chart datum via static GNSS (`sigma_eta <= 0.10–0.20 m`), applying modeled `VDatum` or harmonic tide zoning if separated by `> 10 km` or tidal constriction. Maintain sign clarity across `+d ↓` bathymetry (`d_image(t) = d_CD + eta(t)` so `d_CD = d_image(t) - eta(t)`) versus `+z ↑` bed elevation (`z_CD = eta(t) - d_image(t)`). *Evidence:* Gauge calibration sheet & signed datum-reduction equation in [Project Header](../_shared/template-project-header.md) referencing [ch09](../../fable/chapters/ch09-vertical-datums.md) and [ch23](../../fable/chapters/ch23-satellite-derived-bathymetry.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Assemble, clean, and partition independent shallow-water calibration vs. validation soundings (`sonar` / `bathylidar` / `ATL24`).** Gather independent non-navigational or hydrographic reference depths (e.g., `MBES`, `ALB`, or `ICESat-2 ATL03/ATL24`) collected within an erosion-stable time window, convert all soundings to the same positive-down chart datum (`+d ↓`), reserve **`>= 30 %` spatially stratified holdout points** across `2 m` depth bins (`0–2 m`, `2–4 m`, ..., extinction depth) strictly off-limits to empirical SDB regression training, and screen out kelp/whitecap/boat-wake artifacts. *Evidence:* Stratified calibration/holdout partition GeoPackage logged per [ch23](../../fable/chapters/ch23-satellite-derived-bathymetry.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** **Apply exact Snell-law refraction correction when calibrating SDB from ICESat-2 photons (*Trigger:* `ATL03` or `ATL24` spaceborne lidar seed depths used in SDB calibration).** Whenever `ATL03` raw green photons (`532 nm`) are used directly instead of pre-corrected `ATL24` products, enforce full ray-refraction depth reduction ($n_a \approx 1.0003$, phase index $n_p \approx 1.341$, group index $n_g \approx 1.364$, $D \approx 0.733\,D_a$ angle-dependent vs bulk $0.746\,D_a$ depending on off-nadir beam geometry and water surface wave slope), and reject segments contaminated by surface foam or suspended sediment plumes. *Evidence:* Refraction-parameter script & photon-filter QC plots referencing [ch23](../../fable/chapters/ch23-satellite-derived-bathymetry.md), [sec-07-05](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-05.md), and [sec-09-05](../../gemini4/book/ch-09/sec-09-05.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** **Conduct multi-substrate bottom-albedo & water-column optical (`K_d`) reconnaissance before collection.** Map dominant benthic habitats (bright carbonate sand, coral pavement, seagrass meadows, macroalgae) and measure or retrieve historical satellite `K_d(490)` / `Secchi` clarity so `Stumpf` band-ratio coefficients or `HOPE`/`ACOLITE` physics priors account for substrate albedo shifts. *Evidence:* Benthic class & `K_d` map archived in prep folder referencing [ch23](../../fable/chapters/ch23-satellite-derived-bathymetry.md) and [sec-11-03](../../gemini4/book/ch-11/sec-11-03.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** **Run a pre-tasking dry-run metadata & coordinate-transform verification (`PROJ` / `EPSG`, `allow_ballpark=False`).** Test forward and inverse datum transformations (`ITRF2020 <-> NAD 83(2011) <-> EGM2008 / GEOID18 / Chart Datum`) on one sample coordinate inside the AOI and verify round-trip consistency within `< 0.005 m` with zero silent ballpark shifts before satellite windows open. *Evidence:* Logged `projinfo` / `cs2cs` round-trip check output in [Error Budget](../_shared/template-error-budget.md) referencing [ch08](../../fable/chapters/ch08-horizontal-datums.md), [ch09](../../fable/chapters/ch09-vertical-datums.md), and [ch49](../../fable/chapters/ch49-metadata.md).
