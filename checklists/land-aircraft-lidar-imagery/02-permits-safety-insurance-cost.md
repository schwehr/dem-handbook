## Part A: Pre-Mobilization Administration (Permits, Legal, Insurance & Budget)

### A.1 Airworthiness, Operating Authority & Airspace Approvals

- [ ] **[GATE]** Inspect and verify valid **FAA Supplemental Type Certificate (STC)** or **FAA Form 337** (Field Approval / `PMA` / `DER` structural substantiation) for every belly camera/LiDAR port cutout, optical-port glass pressure dome, external helicopter nose/belly/landing-step hardpoint, or cable-slung pod mount, plus an updated **Aircraft Equipment List** and **Weight & Balance (`W&B`)** baseline covering sensor racks and fuel-burn CG shift. *Evidence:* Signed STC / Form 337 copy, A&P/IA logbook return-to-service entry, and baseline W&B sheet in flight binder. *Refs:* [ch-07/sec-07-03](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-03.md), [ch16-platforms](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Verify commercial aerial survey operating authority under **`14 CFR § 119.1(e)(4)(iii)`** (Part 91 aerial photography/survey exemption from Part 135 carriage, ensuring zero non-survey passenger/cargo transport) and document **`14 CFR § 91.119`** minimum safe altitudes across congested (`305 m` / `1,000 ft` above highest obstacle within `610 m` [`2,000 ft`] horizontal radius for fixed-wing) vs. uncongested areas and low-AGL helicopter corridor exceptions. *Evidence:* Operator `§ 119.1` compliance memo and minimum-AGL terrain/obstacle clearance table. *Refs:* [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md#13-external-statutory-safety-hazmat--insurance-register), [ch33-wires-and-thin-structures](../../fable/chapters/ch33-wires-and-thin-structures.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Execute **ATC Class B / Class C / Class D Letters of Agreement (LOAs)** and coordinate Special Use Airspace (`MOA`, Restricted, Prohibited, Warning areas) for persistent grid vectoring inside controlled terminal airspace. *Evidence:* Countersigned ATC facility LOA(s) and `SUA` scheduling confirmation numbers. *Refs:* [ch26-survey-planning](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md), [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md#13-external-statutory-safety-hazmat--insurance-register).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Submit **FAA AC 70-1B (`FAA Form 7140-1`)** outdoor laser notification and obtain **DoD Laser Clearinghouse (`LCH`)** clearance where required before energizing beams aloft; calculate **Nominal Ocular Hazard Distance (`NOHD`)** and MPE under **ANSI Z136.1-2022 / Z136.6-2015**:
  $$\mathrm{NOHD} = \frac{1}{\beta_t}\!\left(\sqrt{\frac{4\,\Phi_{\text{eff}}}{\pi\,\mathrm{MPE}}} - D_0\right)$$
  noting `532 nm` (`SPL`) carries `~5×` tighter retinal MPE than `1,064 nm` (while `1,550 nm` corneal MPE is orders of magnitude higher), checking **`Class 1M` aided/binocular `ENOHD`** risk, and enforcing a conservative **project flight floor `>= 1.2 × NOHD`** (`1.2×` operational margin above statutory NOHD). *Evidence:* Signed Laser Safety Officer (`LSO`) NOHD/ENOHD calculation, FAA Service Center non-objection letter, and LCH approval. *Refs:* [ch-09/sec-09-01](../../gemini4/book/ch-09/sec-09-01.md), [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md#13-external-statutory-safety-hazmat--insurance-register).
- [ ] **[COND]** Obtain **foreign diplomatic overflight/landing permits** and host-nation mapping clearances (e.g., India `2021` Geospatial Guidelines, China `GCJ-02` / surveying permit laws, `14 CFR Part 375` foreign-registry permits) before ferry launch. *Trigger:* Any survey crossing an international border or operating a foreign-registered aircraft. *Evidence:* Approved diplomatic clearance numbers and host-ministry survey permit. *Refs:* [ch68-legal-issues](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md), [ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md).

### A.2 Export Control, Hazmat, Licensure, Privacy & Ground Access

- [ ] **[GATE]** Screen tactical/nav-grade IMUs, lasers, and sensors against **ITAR (`22 CFR 120–130`)** and **EAR (`15 CFR 730–774`, ECCNs `7A003`, `7A005`, `6A005`, `6A008`)** prior to international transit or foreign-national access; prepare **ATA Carnet** customs carnets and hazmat manifests enforcing **IATA DGR 67th Ed. (eff. Jan 1, 2026) `<= 30 %` State-of-Charge (`SoC`) air-freight rules for `UN 3480 PI 965 (Cargo Aircraft Only)` and `UN 3481 PI 966`**, plus **`UN 3090`/`UN 3091`** (primary lithium-metal cells, `UN 3090 CAO`), **`UN 38.3` test summary**, **`SDS`**, and **`49 CFR 171–180`** (`> 300 Wh` ground transport). *Evidence:* Export Compliance sign-off, ATA Carnet, and hazmat `UN 38.3`/`SDS`/`SoC` packet. *Refs:* [ch68-legal-issues](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md), [ch-27/sec-27-03](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-03.md), [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md#13-external-statutory-safety-hazmat--insurance-register).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Confirm state **Professional Land Surveyor (`PLS`)** statutory supervision (*360 Virtual Drone v. Ritter* precedent) and **ASPRS Certified Photogrammetrist / Lidar Scientist** sign-off authority prior to contract binding, plus complete **`CARE` & `OCAP®`**, **`NHPA § 106` / `ARPA`**, and **`GDPR` / aerial residential privacy** (*Ciraolo*, *Riley*, *Dow Chemical*, *Kyllo*) release screening. *Evidence:* Active PLS license verification and cultural/privacy clearance memo. *Refs:* [ch68-legal-issues](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md), [ch-27/sec-27-02](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-02.md), [ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** Coordinate **Environmental, Wildlife & Community Overflight Liaison** before low-AGL or corridor sweeps: enforce **mandatory `15 CFR Part 922` National Marine Sanctuary overflight altitude restrictions** within regulated sanctuary zones, observe **`FAA AC 91-36D` (`>= 610 m [2,000 ft] AGL` voluntary quiet-flight advisory)** over noise-sensitive National Parks and National Wildlife Refuges, enforce seasonal **raptor/seabird nesting and coastal wildlife buffers**, and issue **advance municipal, tribal, and community flight notices** for repeated low-altitude passes. *Trigger:* Any flight planned `< 610 m` (`2,000 ft`) AGL, helicopter corridor pass, or block intersecting parks, wildlife refuges, regulated National Marine Sanctuaries, or residential areas. *Evidence:* Sanctuary permit/`15 CFR Part 922` compliance record, wildlife-agency buffer log, and advance municipal/tribal/community notice record. *Refs:* [ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md), [ch-27/sec-27-02](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-02.md), [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md#13-external-statutory-safety-hazmat--insurance-register).
- [ ] **[COND]** Secure written **right-of-entry / trespass-avoidance and utility/railroad flagging permits** for all project GNSS base stations and total-station / RTK checkpoints outside public right-of-way. *Trigger:* Any base station or ground checkpoint sited on private, railroad, utility, or restricted federal/tribal land. *Evidence:* Signed landowner ROE permits and utility safety logs. *Refs:* [ch52-ground-truth](../../fable/chapters/ch52-ground-truth.md), [ch68-legal-issues](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md).

### A.3 Aviation Insurance & Program Cost Ledger

- [ ] **[GATE]** Verify active Certificates of Insurance (`COI`) via [template-insurance-coi](../_shared/template-insurance-coi.md) explicitly bridging the standard **`ISO CG 00 01` CGL aviation exclusion**: confirm **Aviation Hull**, **Third-Party Aviation Liability** (`>= $5M–$25M` CSL per contract/airport FBO requirement with **Class 4 / `532 nm` laser ocular endorsement** verified), **Non-Owned Aircraft Liability** (charter/wet-lease), **Over-Water endorsement** (offshore lines beyond glide), **High-Value Payload / Sensor Floater** backed by a current **Condition & Value (`C&V`) appraisal** (covering mounted, hangar, and ground transit), **Commercial Auto & Workers' Comp** (covering ground checkpoint/base vehicles and crews), **Tech / Professional E&O** (*Brocklesby* liability), **Cyber Liability / Data Breach** coverage (telemetry, raw imagery, and PII/incident records), **FVWC / Medevac** (if international), **Waiver of Subrogation & Primary/Non-Contributory** clauses, and **policy expiry >= planned completion + `60 d` weather-standby buffer**. *Evidence:* Broker-signed COI, `C&V` payload schedule, and additional-insured endorsements. *Refs:* [ch68-legal-issues](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md), [ch-27/sec-27-01](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-01.md), [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md#13-external-statutory-safety-hazmat--insurance-register).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Lock the program **Cost Ledger** via [template-cost-ledger](../_shared/template-cost-ledger.md): itemize fixed ferry/mobilization (`> 50 %` on small blocks), indicative aircraft wet hourly burn (`~$1,500–$2,200 h⁻¹` single piston/turboprop vs. `$2,800–$5,000+ h⁻¹` pressurized twin/jet/helicopter — operator quote-dependent as of `2024–2026`), weather-standby minimums, on-line collection efficiency (`50–70 %`), historical North American `3DEP`-era unit benchmarks (`~$115–$386+ km⁻²` [`~$300–$1,000+ mi⁻²`], quote-dependent on QL/terrain/block area/mobilization), `5–10 %` independent validation allocation, fuel/CO₂ burn (`~300–400 L h⁻¹` twin turboprop), and cloud storage (**hot `~$0.02 GB⁻¹ mo⁻¹` vs. cold archive `~$0.001–$0.004 GB⁻¹ mo⁻¹`**) plus egress (`~$0.05–$0.09 GB⁻¹`). *Evidence:* Approved cost ledger and weather-standby de-scope burn cap. *Refs:* [ch28-reducing-cost](../../fable/chapters/ch28-reducing-cost.md), [ch-27/sec-27-04](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-04.md).

## Part B: Daily Pre-Sortie Flight Safety, Oxygen & Crew Fatigue Gates

- [ ] **[GATE]** Enforce **`14 CFR § 91.211` supplemental oxygen rules** based on actual **cabin pressure altitude**: above `3,810 m` (`12,500 ft MSL`) up to `4,267 m` (`14,000 ft MSL`) for `> 30 min`, mandate continuous supplemental O₂ for flight crew; above `4,267 m` (`14,000 ft MSL`), mandate continuous crew O₂ at all times; above `4,572 m` (`15,000 ft MSL`), provide supplemental O₂ to **every occupant** (including sensor operators); verify pressurized cabin `ΔP` schedule and quick-donning O₂ bottle duration (`>= planned flight + 60 min reserve`) plus mask fit check before engine start. *Evidence:* Daily pre-flight oxygen pressure (`kPa` / `psi`), mask leak check, and unpressurized pulse-oximetry log. *Refs:* [ch-07/sec-07-03](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-03.md), [appendix-g-checklists](../../fable/appendices/appendix-g-checklists.md), [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md#13-external-statutory-safety-hazmat--insurance-register).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Brief daily **`NOTAM`s, active `TFR`s (`14 CFR § 91.137` wildfire, `§ 99.7` stadium, VIP)**, verify **sortie Weight & Balance (`W&B`)** within takeoff/landing CG envelope, and enforce **pilot & sensor-operator duty/rest gates (`<= 8–10 h` flight time in `24 h`, `<= 14 h` duty day, `>= 10 h` uninterrupted rest)** before releasing any aircraft. *Evidence:* Timestamped `1800wxbrief`/TFR packet, signed sortie W&B sheet, and daily crew duty-rest ledger. *Refs:* [ch26-survey-planning](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md), [ch-07/sec-07-03](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-03.md), [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md#13-external-statutory-safety-hazmat--insurance-register).
- [ ] **[REC]** Verify **over-water, rotorcraft, hazmat-fire, and roadside ground-crew safety readiness**: inspect `14 CFR § 91.509` life rafts/PFDs (adopted as an operational floor beyond `§ 91.205(b)(12)` for piston survey aircraft crossing beyond coastal glide), inspect helicopter `WSPS` cutters and helmets/Nomex, verify **in-flight cabin Halon (per AFM) plus water-deluge / AVD thermal containment bag** for `UN 3480/3481` Li-ion spares (reserving `Class D` dry powder strictly for primary `UN 3090` metallic cells), and enforce **`MUTCD` roadside traffic control, `ANSI/ISEA 107` Class 2/3 vests, and lone-worker check-ins** for GNSS base and checkpoint teams. *Evidence:* Pre-sortie survival, fire-agent, and roadside-safety check log. *Refs:* [ch33-wires-and-thin-structures](../../fable/chapters/ch33-wires-and-thin-structures.md), [ch52-ground-truth](../../fable/chapters/ch52-ground-truth.md), [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md#13-external-statutory-safety-hazmat--insurance-register).
