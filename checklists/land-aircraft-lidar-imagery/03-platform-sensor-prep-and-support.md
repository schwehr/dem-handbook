## 1. Pressurized Optical Port Glass, Sensor Calibration & Power

- [ ] **[GATE]** Inspect pressurized optical port glass for cleanliness, scratches, seal integrity, and dual-band anti-reflective (AR) transmittance (`1064 nm` / `532 nm` / `RGB-NIR`); record glass thickness $t_{\text{win}}$ and refractive index $n_{\text{win}}$ (`BK7` `1.517`, fused silica `1.450`), note that **cabin-`ΔP` meniscus bowing induces symmetric cross-track `smile/frown` scale distortion unless verified at planned cruise differential pressure**, and verify Snell radial compensation in the scanner angle LUT or camera interior orientation:
  $$\Delta r_{\text{win}}(\theta) = t_{\text{win}} \tan\theta \left(1 - \frac{\cos\theta}{\sqrt{n_{\text{win}}^2 - \sin^2\theta}}\right)$$
  (e.g., `6.70 mm` radial displacement at `θ = 25°` through a `38 mm` BK7 pane). *Evidence:* Port optical log (`t_win`, `n_win`, AR curve, survey-`ΔP` test) and enabled refraction lookup flag recorded in [template-project-header](../_shared/template-project-header.md). *Refs:* [ch-07/sec-07-03](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-03.md), [ch-09/sec-09-01](../../gemini4/book/ch-09/sec-09-01.md), [ch-09/sec-09-07](../../gemini4/book/ch-09/sec-09-07.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Verify current laboratory and airborne range **metric camera Interior Orientation (`IO`) calibration** ($f, x_0, y_0, k_1\text{–}k_3, p_1, p_2$, flat-field) per USGS *Plan for Quality Assurance of Digital Aerial Imagery* (`<= 12–24 months` or post-service), confirm three-line pushbroom line alignment when applicable, and freeze `.cam`/`.xml` parameter hashes. *Evidence:* Signed manufacturer/USGS IO certificate and `SHA-256` manifest of active calibration files. *Refs:* [ch22-photogrammetry-sfm](../../fable/chapters/ch22-photogrammetry-sfm.md), [ch25-calibration-infrastructure](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Verify **aircraft electrical bus & alternator/generator load margin (`>= 20–25 %` reserve)** under simultaneous max draw of LiDAR emitters/chillers, large-format camera drives, optical-port anti-fog heaters, cabin avionics, and oxygen/heater systems to prevent mid-line voltage sags and silent sensor reboots. *Evidence:* Signed avionics load-analysis table and ground full-power soak test (`>= 20 min`). *Refs:* [ch16-platforms](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md), [ch-07/sec-07-03](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-03.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** Verify LiDAR range-walk / Constant Fraction Discriminator (`CFD`), two-target separation ($\Delta R_{\min}$), intensity normalization, and atmospheric group index configuration ($n_{g,\text{air}} \approx 1.0002792$ at `1064 nm`, avoiding `+0.42 m` range bias at `1,500 m` if vacuum speed of light $c_0$ is used uncompensated). *Evidence:* Bench range-calibration sheet and atmospheric refraction parameter log. *Refs:* [ch-09/sec-09-01](../../gemini4/book/ch-09/sec-09-01.md), [ch18-topographic-lidar](../../fable/chapters/ch18-topographic-lidar.md).

## 2. IMU, Lever Arms, GNSS Antennas & Timing Sync

- [ ] **[GATE]** Validate **navigation/tactical FOG or RLG IMU** noise and drift against **IEEE Std 952 Allan variance** limits (gyro bias instability `<= 0.002–0.01 ° h⁻¹` for fixed-wing; six-position static self-check) before locking mounts. *Evidence:* Bench Allan-variance report and passing IMU BIT telemetry. *Refs:* [ch13-imu-ins](../../fable/chapters/ch13-imu-ins.md), [ch25-calibration-infrastructure](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Survey **signed lever-arm vectors** (`IMU → GNSS ARP` and `IMU → Laser/Camera Origin`) via calibrated total station (`<= 0.010 m` `1σ` per axis), distinguishing **`FRD` (`+x` fwd, `+y` stbd, `+z` down) / `RFU` (`+x` right, `+y` fwd, `+z` up) sensor body frames** from geographic **`NED` / `ENU` mapping frames** with an explicitly signed diagram and close-up photos checked by a second engineer via [template-project-header](../_shared/template-project-header.md). *Evidence:* Two-person signed total-station lever-arm report and annotated frame diagram. *Refs:* [ch-07/sec-07-03](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-03.md), [notation-and-symbols](../../gemini4/book/00-front-matter/notation-and-symbols.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Confirm exact `20`-character **IGS `igs20.atx` ANTEX antenna & radome model codes** (`NONE` vs. radome-equipped multi-band L1/L2/L5 airborne antenna and all base antennas — preventing `0.02–0.05 m` vertical phase-center bias) and verify `>= 0.15 m` fuselage clearance from VHF/Satcom whip antennas. *Evidence:* ANTEX lookup printout matching physical antenna serial stickers + crown photo. *Refs:* [ch12-gnss](../../fable/chapters/ch12-gnss.md), [ch25-calibration-infrastructure](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Verify **hardware `1PPS` + NMEA `$GPZDA` (`>= 115,200 baud`)** or **IEEE 1588v2 PTP (`< 100 ns`)** synchronization across GNSS, IMU, LiDAR scanner, and camera shutter trigger (`1 ms` timing error under `5° s⁻¹` roll at `1,500 m AGL` causes `0.06 m` along-track shift at `60 m s⁻¹`, `0.131 m` cross-track horizontal error, and **`0.048 m` swath-edge vertical error $H\tan 20^\circ\,\delta\phi$** at $\theta = 20^\circ$), explicitly verifying **`GPST - UTC` (`18 s` offset in 2026)** handling so no `1.000 s` serial lag or `1,080 m` leap-second jump enters trajectories. *Evidence:* Oscilloscope/bench `1PPS` capture (`< 0.1 ms` sync jitter) and verified GPST timestamp header. *Refs:* [ch-32/sec-32-01](../../gemini4/book/ch-32/sec-32-01.md), [ch-32/sec-32-02](../../gemini4/book/ch-32/sec-32-02.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** Inspect rotorcraft **elastomeric or wire-rope vibration isolators** for cracking and compression set, confirming mount-resonance separation from rotor `1R` (`5–7 Hz`) and `N_b/rev` harmonics (`18–28 Hz`) while keeping IMU and sensor head rigidly coupled downstream of isolators (`<= 0.002 m` relative flexure). *Trigger:* Any helicopter hardpoint or cable-slung pod deployment. *Evidence:* Isolator torque/durometer inspection sheet and idle IMU PSD check. *Refs:* [ch-07/sec-07-03](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-03.md), [ch13-imu-ins](../../fable/chapters/ch13-imu-ins.md).

## 3. GNSS Bases, Boresight Range & Independent Checkpoints

- [ ] **[GATE]** Deploy a **multi-CORS + `>= 2` local GNSS base-station network** keeping every planned flight line within **`<= 18–20 km` baseline (`30 km` hard cap)**; log multi-GNSS observables (`>= 1 Hz`, `>= 5°` mask) for **`>= 4 h` (`2 h` minimum)** tied via **`OPUS` / `NGS 92` (2024) / `NGS-58/59`** (`RMSE <= 0.015 m` vert), and record **two independent `ARP` antenna-height readings strictly in meters** (converting any imperial tape reading immediately on the sheet) plus a timestamped close-up collar photograph. *Evidence:* `OPUS`/`NGS` control sheet + dual-height field log + collar photo for every base. *Refs:* [ch12-gnss](../../fable/chapters/ch12-gnss.md), [ch25-calibration-infrastructure](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Fly a **pre-deployment boresight calibration site** combining a flat paved surface (runway/apron) **and `>= 1` pitched gable roof or 3D multi-slope building structures** to guarantee **Rank-4 Jacobian observability** across latency, pitch, roll, and heading (`>= 4` opposing + orthogonal passes at survey AGL/speed); lock boresight angles before flying production blocks. *Evidence:* Boresight report demonstrating `RMSD_z <= 0.03–0.05 m`, SVD Rank 4 pass, and zero gable hinge step recorded in [template-error-budget](../_shared/template-error-budget.md). *Refs:* [ch25-calibration-infrastructure](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md), [ch-13/sec-13-01](../../gemini4/book/ch-13/sec-13-01.md), [ch-13/sec-13-05](../../gemini4/book/ch-13/sec-13-05.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Survey and seal an **independent Ground-Truth Checkpoint (`CP`) network** strictly separated from any photogrammetric tie/GCP points: collect **`>= 30` `NVA` CPs** on open flat terrain (`slope < 10 %`) and **`>= 30` `VVA` CPs** per land-cover stratum (`60–120` total for large regional blocks); survey every under-canopy `VVA` point by **optical total station** on bare mineral soil (clearing loose leaf litter), verify `>= 25 %` of CPs via an independent base or repeat **`180 s` redundant `RTN` occupation (Henning 2011 best practice)**, and seal the `CP` CSV from production processing staff. *Evidence:* Independent PLS-reviewed `CP` survey report + sealed coordinate SHA-256 hash. *Refs:* [ch52-ground-truth](../../fable/chapters/ch52-ground-truth.md), [ch53-accuracy-assessment](../../fable/chapters/ch53-accuracy-assessment.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** Complete a **`>= 5–10 min` clear-sky ramp static GNSS+IMU burn-in** before taxi and verify aircraft RAID storage `>= 2 ×` daily planned raw volume (`TB`) with two field-swappable backup drives ready. *Evidence:* Pre-taxi INS convergence telemetry and storage audit. *Refs:* [ch13-imu-ins](../../fable/chapters/ch13-imu-ins.md), [ch-19/sec-19-01](../../gemini4/book/ch-19/sec-19-01.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** Establish at least `>= 5–10` separate un-withheld **Field Check / Pilot Verification Targets** near the staging airport or block edge so Sortie 1 field QC can test absolute vertical datum and `GPST–UTC` alignment without unsealing the independent `ASPRS` `CP` dataset. *Evidence:* Un-withheld field verification coordinate table in staging QA folder. *Refs:* [ch52-ground-truth](../../fable/chapters/ch52-ground-truth.md), [ch25-calibration-infrastructure](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md).
