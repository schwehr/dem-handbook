## 2. Reconnaissance, Environmental Windows & Pooled Acquisition

- [ ] **[GATE]** Gate leaf-off mobilization on **real-time satellite phenology (`MODIS` / `Sentinel-2` `NDVI` decay curve)** corroborated by timestamped local ground-canopy photographs (`>= 3` representative hardwood/riparian stands), rather than relying on static calendar dates. *Evidence:* Downloaded `NDVI` time series (`NDVI <= block leaf-off baseline`) plus geotagged canopy photos archived prior to mobilization. *Refs:* [ch36-seasonal-variability](../../fable/chapters/ch36-seasonal-variability.md), [ch56-case-files](../../fable/chapters/ch56-case-files.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Enforce a strict **snow-free, flood-free, and smoke-free environmental collection gate** verified daily from `NOHRSC SNODAS` / `Sentinel-2` `NDSI` and local weather/river gauges; require immediate suspension or explicit **Class 21** perennial-snowpack segregation where snow cover exceeds `0 %` on bare-earth AOIs (`> 0.02 m` snow introduces positive elevation bias). *Evidence:* Daily pre-flight snow/river gauge log and `NDSI` snapshot. *Refs:* [ch36-seasonal-variability](../../fable/chapters/ch36-seasonal-variability.md), [ch30-point-cloud-classification](../../fable/chapters/ch30-point-cloud-classification.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** Execute pre-plan reconnaissance of **prior USGS 3DEP / NOAA / state LiDAR holdings** (`Entwine` / `TNM` / `OpenTopography`), active **NOAA CORS / IGS stations** within `30 km`, staging airports (`FBO` runway length, Jet-A / 100LL, heated hangar security), active `TFR` / `MOA` / `Class B–D` airspace conflicts, and tall towers/transmission lines (`FAA DOF` 56-day cycle & `NGA DVOF`). *Evidence:* Geospatial recon layer (`GeoPackage` + airport/CORS viability table). *Refs:* [ch25-calibration-infrastructure](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md), [ch33-wires-and-thin-structures](../../fable/chapters/ch33-wires-and-thin-structures.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** Evaluate pooled multi-partner funding (`USGS 3DEP Broad Agency Announcement [BAA]` or regional consortium co-funding) and cross-modality tie-ins via [multimodal-white-ribbon](../_shared/multimodal-white-ribbon.md) to amortize fixed aircraft mobilization (`> 50 %` of cost on small blocks) and eliminate duplicated shoreline/jurisdictional flights. *Evidence:* Consortium MoU / 3DEP BAA partner matrix or single-sponsor justification. *Refs:* [ch28-reducing-cost](../../fable/chapters/ch28-reducing-cost.md), [ch-27/sec-27-04](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-04.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** Resolve **conflicting multi-sensor optimal collection windows** explicitly in the contract before hardware integration: topographic LiDAR favors leaf-off canopy and flies cleanly under high overcast above survey AGL, whereas metric RGB/CIR photogrammetry normally requires solar elevation `> 30°`, minimal cloud-shadow contrast, and often green-up / leaf-on phenology; either separate flights or sign a joint compromise window (e.g., leaf-off ortho tolerating longer shadows). *Trigger:* Any dual-mounted LiDAR + large-format camera mission or bundled orthoimagery deliverable. *Evidence:* Signed multi-sensor window decision memo. *Refs:* [ch28-reducing-cost](../../fable/chapters/ch28-reducing-cost.md), [ch22-photogrammetry-sfm](../../fable/chapters/ch22-photogrammetry-sfm.md).

## 3. Weather Climatology, De-Scope Criteria & Data Management

- [ ] **[GATE]** Derive realistic weather-standby float and aircraft allocation from historical **station climatology** (`~10 %` standby probability in arid spring rising to `30–50 %` in marine or winter leaf-off regimes) and codify quantitative **partial-delivery / seasonal-abort milestones** (spring bud-burst cutoff date, maximum cumulative standby burn, and acceptance rules for split-season bare-earth blocks). *Evidence:* Station ceiling/visibility climatology histogram (`ceiling >= planned AGL + 150 m`, `visibility >= 8 km`) plus contractual de-scope/split-season clause. *Refs:* [ch26-survey-planning](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md), [ch28-reducing-cost](../../fable/chapters/ch28-reducing-cost.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Initialize the **Data Management Plan (DMP)** and archival metadata schema before Day 1 takeoff: specify `LAZ 1.4` (`PDRF 6–10`) / `COPC 1.0` point-cloud containers (`Float64` / `0.001 m` scaled coordinates), `PixelIsArea` `Float32` Cloud-Optimized GeoTIFFs (`COG`, `ZSTD`, `PREDICTOR=3`), `STAC` + `ISO 19115`/`19157` XML lineage, immutable raw `SHA-256` archive location (`3-2-1` rule), and public vs. restricted data tiers (critical infrastructure / archaeological / indigenous `CARE` & `OCAP®`). *Evidence:* Version-controlled `DMP.md` and validated `STAC`/`ISO 19115` XML template in project repository. *Refs:* [ch49-metadata](../../fable/chapters/ch49-metadata.md), [ch-19/sec-19-01](../../gemini4/book/ch-19/sec-19-01.md), [ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** Audit **fitness-for-use across downstream disciplines** before locking sensor selection (`1064 nm` linear vs. `532 nm` `SPL` vs. `1064 nm` `GmAPD` vs. photogrammetric `SGM`), confirming `SPL` solar-noise pruning preserves canopy structure if forestry metrics are required and masking shallow `532 nm` water penetration across the upland seam via [multimodal-white-ribbon](../_shared/multimodal-white-ribbon.md). *Evidence:* Signed sensor-selection memo. *Refs:* [ch03-fitness-for-use](../../fable/chapters/ch03-fitness-for-use.md), [ch-09/sec-09-01](../../gemini4/book/ch-09/sec-09-01.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** Confirm regional block size exceeds the **manned-aircraft vs. UAV break-even (`> 10–100 km²` planning estimate; see `§28.2.4`)** and budget expected collection volume (`GB`/`TB`), **hot cloud storage (`~$0.02 GB⁻¹ mo⁻¹` vs. cold archival `~$0.001–$0.004 GB⁻¹ mo⁻¹`)**, and client egress strategy (`COPC`/`COG` byte-range reads avoiding `~$0.05–$0.09 GB⁻¹` bulk transfer fees). *Evidence:* Platform-economics and cloud-storage sheet linked to [template-cost-ledger](../_shared/template-cost-ledger.md). *Refs:* [ch28-reducing-cost](../../fable/chapters/ch28-reducing-cost.md), [ch-27/sec-27-04](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-04.md).
