## 2. Multiple-Time-Around (MTA), Helicopter & Camera Rules

- [ ] **[GATE]** Verify **Multiple-Time-Around (`MTA`) unambiguous range zones** across maximum project terrain relief and scan-edge slant range:
  $$R_{\text{unamb}} = \frac{c_0}{2\,n_{g,\text{air}}\,\text{PRF}}$$
  (`149.85 m` at `1 MHz`, `299.7 m` at `500 kHz` with $n_{g,\text{air}} \approx 1.0002792$); compare total slant-range spread $\Delta R = H_{\max}/\cos(\Theta/2) - H_{\min}$ (including tall trees/towers) against $R_{\text{unamb}}$, and enforce either single-zone operation ($\Delta R < 0.8 R_{\text{unamb}}$) or validated pulse-coding / gating boundaries so steep terrain never folds into `k · R_unamb` ghost sheets. *Evidence:* Relief-vs-PRF `MTA` zone overlay showing zero unresolvable zone boundaries. *Refs:* [ch18-topographic-lidar](../../fable/chapters/ch18-topographic-lidar.md), [ch30-point-cloud-classification](../../fable/chapters/ch30-point-cloud-classification.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** Plan **helicopter utility-corridor sweeps** with conductor-optimized scan geometry: fly perpendicular or within `30° (azimuth tolerance)` (`-30° to +30°`) of cross-track to maximize specular conductor return (`>= 10–20 pts m⁻²` on `2 cm` wires), synchronize **SCADA load ampacity and ambient wind/temp logging** for `IEEE Std 738` thermal sag calculations, and check `FAA DOF` / `NGA DVOF` wire/tower obstacles along every corridor. *Trigger:* Any rotorcraft powerline, pipeline, or catenary engineering mission. *Evidence:* Corridor FMS plan, SCADA weather/load log interface, and `FAA DOF` obstacle plot. *Refs:* [ch33-wires-and-thin-structures](../../fable/chapters/ch33-wires-and-thin-structures.md), [ch56-case-files](../../fable/chapters/ch56-case-files.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** Configure **helicopter alpine oblique tilt (`35°–60°` off-nadir) and dynamic IMU alignment maneuvers**: orient sensor tilt cleanly against cliff faces (`Helimap`-style) to eliminate vertical-wall shadow voids, and insert **`>= 20–30 s` dynamic figure-eight / S-turn heading maneuvers** (`bank <= 20°`) pre- and post-corridor block while strictly excluding banked turns from exported deliverables. *Trigger:* Any helicopter hardpoint or slung-pod survey over steep cliffs or narrow valleys. *Evidence:* Alpine oblique FMS track file with explicitly flagged alignment turns. *Refs:* [ch-07/sec-07-03](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-03.md), [ch13-imu-ins](../../fable/chapters/ch13-imu-ins.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** Enforce **metric camera overlap and solar-geometry windows**: set `60–80 %` forward overlap (`B/H` matched to `σ_Z` precision target) and `30–60 %` sidelap, restrict imaging to **solar elevation `> 30°`** (or documented `> 25°` winter northern-latitude waiver with shadow-polygon screening), and avoid specular sun-glint angles over open water/bright pavement. *Trigger:* Any sortie operating a large-format metric frame or pushbroom camera. *Evidence:* UTC solar-ephemeris window sheet and expected `GSD` / `σ_Z` precision estimate. *Refs:* [ch22-photogrammetry-sfm](../../fable/chapters/ch22-photogrammetry-sfm.md), [ch-08/sec-08-01](../../gemini4/book/ch-08/sec-08-01.md).
