## 2. Laser Illumination, Hazmat Fire & GNSS Spoofing Emergencies

- [ ] **[GATE]** Execute immediate **laser-exposure emergency & LSO response**: upon suspected eye/skin exposure (`532 nm` SPL, `1064 nm`, or `1550 nm`) or unscheduled ground/cockpit illumination, trip the mechanical beam shutter and HV interlock, record full **MPE reconstruction parameters** (**exact `UTC`, lat/lon, AGL, scan angle, heading, aircraft `(phi, theta)` attitude, wavelength `lambda`, pulse energy $E_p$, pulse width $\tau_p$, `PRF`, divergence $\beta_t$ / beamlet count, scan rate $f_{\text{scan}}$, and unaided vs. binocular viewing**), notify the **Laser Safety Officer (`LSO`)**, and require **ophthalmologic dilated retinal + `OCT` exam within `<= 24 h`**. *Evidence:* Incident laser log, shutter interlock check, MPE parameter worksheet, and medical/LSO follow-up. *Refs:* [ch-09/sec-09-01](../../gemini4/book/ch-09/sec-09-01.md), [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md#13-external-statutory-safety-hazmat--insurance-register).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Execute **GNSS jamming and spoofing forensics** in interference zones (cf. Baltic/Tartu cases in [ch56-case-files](../../fable/chapters/ch56-case-files.md)): monitor **`C/N0` spikes/dropouts, receiver `AGC` collapse, clock steps (`>= 1 μs`), and INS-vs-GNSS innovation divergence (`> 2–5 m`)**; verify multi-constellation parity and **Galileo OSNMA** authentication; **keep raw observables (`RINEX`, raw `AGC`/`C/N0`, pseudorange/carrier) recording uninterrupted** (never mid-flight hot-reboot and destroy evidence), navigate out on calibrated INS dead-reckoning, and quarantine affected strips. *Evidence:* Preserved raw receiver `AGC`/`C/N0` capture, ATC interference report, and quarantined line polygon. *Refs:* [ch12-gnss](../../fable/chapters/ch12-gnss.md), [ch56-case-files](../../fable/chapters/ch56-case-files.md), [ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** Follow **chemistry-specific SDS lithium battery fire drills**: for cabin fires, deploy **approved aircraft Halon per AFM** and transfer portable `UN 3480/3481` Li-ion cells into water-deluge / `AVD` thermal containment bags (ABC dry chem/CO₂ alone fails thermal runaway); reserve **Class D dry powder strictly for primary lithium-metal `UN 3090/3091` cells** on ground packs (never apply water to burning metallic lithium). *Evidence:* On-board containment bag / extinguisher inspection tag and crew SDS drill record. *Refs:* [ch16-platforms](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md), [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md#13-external-statutory-safety-hazmat--insurance-register).

## 3. Statutory Reporting Clocks, Insurer Notice & Forensic Freeze

- [ ] **[GATE]** Execute **mandatory statutory & policy notifications** immediately upon securing life safety:
  - **NTSB (`49 CFR §§ 830.2, 830.5, 830.15`):** **Immediate** verbal notification (`+1-844-373-9922`) for fatal/serious injury, substantial aircraft damage, flight-control malfunction, in-flight fire, collision, or **`> $25,000` non-aircraft third-party property damage** (lower of repair cost or fair market value, `§ 830.5(a)(6)`), followed by **written `NTSB Form 6120.1/2` within `10 calendar days` (`§ 830.15`)**; apply **ICAO Annex 13** national authority rules outside US jurisdiction.
  - **FAA FSDO / `DEN`:** Prompt notification to local Flight Standards District Office and FAA Domestic Events Network (`AC 70-1B` laser illumination / airspace emergency).
  - **OSHA (`29 CFR § 1904.39`):** Report within **`8 h` for a work-related fatality** or **`24 h` for inpatient hospitalization, amputation, or loss of an eye** (`+1-800-321-6742`).
  - **NRC / USCG (`[COND]`):** For ramp fuel/oil spills or over-water ditching, notify **NRC (`33 CFR § 153.203`, `+1-800-424-8802`) immediately** and file **USCG `CG-2692` (`5 days`, `46 CFR § 4.05-10`)** where maritime vessels/waterways are involved.
  - **Aviation Hull / Liability / Payload Insurer:** Written claim/circumstance notice per policy notice clause (typically `<= 24–72 h`, policy-specific — verify active COI).
  *Evidence:* Timestamped reference numbers, `Form 6120.1/2` docket, and insurer written acknowledgment. *Refs:* [ch68-legal-issues](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md), [ch-27/sec-27-01](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-01.md), [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md#13-external-statutory-safety-hazmat--insurance-register).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Execute an **immutable black-box & sensor forensic freeze (`49 CFR § 830.10`)** with **`FRE 901 / 902(13)–(14)`** electronic authentication and **medical/PII privacy quarantine**: preserve CVR/FDR, sensor SSDs, `IMU`/`GNSS`/`AGC` raw logs, base `RINEX`, `ADS-B` tracks, and weather packets via write-blocked bit-stream copies + **`SHA-256` custody logs**, while restricting crew medical/injury details to confidential HR/medical channels. *Evidence:* Signed tamper-evident media seals, `SHA-256` custody manifest, and isolated medical PII file. *Refs:* [ch50-archiving-and-provenance](../../fable/chapters/ch50-archiving-and-provenance.md), [ch68-legal-issues](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** Execute **GDPR Art. 33 (`<= 72 h` supervisory-authority notification)** and Data Protection Officer (`DPO`) / CISO escalation if any incident, stolen aircraft media, or cloud leak exposes unencrypted personal/medical PII (crew injury/ophthalmologic records, residential sub-`0.10 m` imagery, or operator credentials), isolating compromised hosts/buckets without wiping volatile audit logs. *Trigger:* Any suspected unauthorized disclosure, loss, or ransomware event involving personal/medical PII or controlled telemetry. *Evidence:* Timestamped DPO/CISO escalation log, volatile log preservation checksum, and Art. 33 filing confirmation (where GDPR applies). *Refs:* [ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md), [ch-27/sec-27-02](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-02.md), [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md#13-external-statutory-safety-hazmat--insurance-register).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Enforce **automatic boresight, lever-arm, camera `IO`, port-seal, and STC airworthiness invalidation** after any hard landing (`> 2–3 g`), bird strike, wire contact, slung-pod shock, or emergency port disassembly: prohibit survey resumption until A&P/IA structural + pressure-seal sign-off, metric camera `IO` verification, total-station lever-arm re-survey, and a passing **gable-roof boresight flight (`RMSD_z <= 0.03–0.05 m`)**. *Evidence:* A&P return-to-service record + post-incident IO/lever-arm/boresight closure report. *Refs:* [ch25-calibration-infrastructure](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md), [ch13-imu-ins](../../fable/chapters/ch13-imu-ins.md).
