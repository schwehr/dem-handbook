## 1. Aircraft Port, Dual-Band Laser & Georeferencing Prep (Q2)

- [ ] **[GATE]** Inspect fuselage **optical belly port glass** for dual-band ($532\text{ nm}$, $1{,}064\text{ nm}$, and $647\text{ nm}$ Raman) anti-reflective (AR) coating integrity, salt-spray/oil residue, optical scratches, and seal integrity before every mobilization. — *Threshold:* Zero salt-film haze, delamination, or cracks; clean strictly per optical manufacturer solvent SOP; confirm retractable mechanical belly door (if unpressurized) clears sensor FOV cleanly. — *Evidence:* Pre-Mob Optical Port Inspection Photos & Glass Calibration Sheet. — *Ref:* [Section 7.3](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-03.md) §7.3.3.
- [ ] **[COND]** Characterize **pressurized cabin optical window thermal and pressure bowing** and Snell planar ray shift (*Trigger:* pressurized turboprop/jet installation such as King Air B200/350 or Twin Commander 690). — *Threshold:* Account for cabin pressure differential $\Delta P \approx 35\text{–}60\text{ kPa}$, thermal gradient $\Delta T \approx 40\text{–}70\text{ K}$ meniscus curvature, planar Snell displacement $\Delta r_{\text{win}}(\theta) = t_{\text{glass}}\tan\theta\left(1 - \frac{\cos\theta}{\sqrt{n_{\text{glass}}^2 - \sin^2\theta}}\right)$ (e.g., $6.70\text{ mm}$ at $\theta = 25^\circ$, $t_{\text{glass}} = 38\text{ mm}$, BK7 $n_{\text{glass}} = 1.517$), and chromatic dispersion across $532\text{ nm}$ ($n_{532}=1.5195$) vs. $1{,}064\text{ nm}$ ($n_{1064}=1.5066$); absorb into scan-angle lookup tables / Brown–Conrady camera distortion coefficients and re-verify $532/1{,}064\text{ nm}$ co-alignment ($\le 0.1\text{–}0.2\text{ mrad}$) **at operational pressurized cruise $\Delta P$**. — *Evidence:* Window Optical Thickness/Dispersion Datasheet & Pressure-Altitude Boresight Note. — *Ref:* [Section 7.3](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-03.md) §7.3.3.
- [ ] **[GATE]** Verify **532 nm green + 1,064 nm NIR (+ 647 nm Raman / RGB / HSI) co-alignment**, laser pulse energy, receiver gain/digitizer baseline, liquid chiller thermal loop, and aircraft **alternator / $28\text{ V DC}$ electrical bus load margin**. — *Threshold:* Co-aligned 532/1,064 nm beam divergence and boresight offset verified within manufacturer tolerance ($\le 0.1\text{–}0.2\text{ mrad}$); chiller coolant loop leak-free and holding setpoint under full $800\text{–}2{,}000\text{ W}$ draw for $\ge 30\text{ min}$ ground burn-in with **$\ge 20\text{–}25\%$ continuous alternator/bus margin** (with avionics, strobes, and pitot/windshield heat active); beam expander optics locked to certified NOHD divergence. — *Evidence:* Ground Burn-In, Alternator Bus-Margin & Channel Co-Alignment Bench Log. — *Ref:* [Chapter 19](../../fable/chapters/ch19-bathymetric-lidar.md) §19.1, [Section 9.2](../../gemini4/book/ch-09/sec-09-02.md) §9.2.2.
- [ ] **[GATE]** Survey **GNSS antenna–to–IMU–to–scanner/camera lever arms** by total station or laser tracker and document explicitly in an **RFU / FRD body-frame** axis diagram (distinct from geographic ENU / NED). — *Threshold:* Lever-arm components $(\ell_x, \ell_y, \ell_z)$ measured to $\le 5\text{–}10\text{ mm}$ ($1\sigma$) and logged in [template-project-header.md](../_shared/template-project-header.md); body-frame sign convention (`FRD`: $+X$ nose / $+Y$ starboard / $+Z$ down vs. `RFU`: $+X$ starboard / $+Y$ nose / $+Z$ up—which coincide with geographic NED / ENU only at heading $\psi = 0^\circ$ due North) cross-checked independently by two engineers to prevent doubled lever-arm sign errors (`2 × offset`); confirm GNSS antenna and IMU/scanner sit on rigid airframe structure (never split across soft isolators). — *Evidence:* Signed Total-Station Lever-Arm Certificate, Axis Diagram, and Installation Photos. — *Ref:* [template-project-header.md](../_shared/template-project-header.md), [Chapter 13](../../fable/chapters/ch13-imu-ins.md) §13.5, [Chapter 25](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md) §25.6.

- [ ] **[GATE]** Verify **1PPS + NMEA ZDA / hardware trigger time synchronization** across GNSS, IMU, green/NIR digitizers, and RGB/HSI cameras. — *Threshold:* Uncalibrated timing latency $\Delta t_{\text{sync}} < 0.2\text{ ms}$ (noting that $1\text{ ms}$ at $60\text{ m s}^{-1}$ induces $6.0\text{ cm}$ along-track displacement, and at $5^\circ\text{ s}^{-1}$ roll rate induces $0.005^\circ$ attitude error = **$\approx 1.59\text{ cm}$ vertical ($H\tan\theta_a\,\delta\phi$) and $\approx 4.36\text{ cm}$ cross-track horizontal ($H\,\delta\phi$; $4.94\text{ cm}$ for a passive camera ray)** at $500\text{ m}$ AGL / $20^\circ$ off-nadir). — *Evidence:* Oscilloscope / Event-Marker Sync Test Log & Adjusted-GPS-Time (`global encoding` bit = 1) Verification. — *Ref:* [Chapter 6](../../fable/chapters/ch06-time-as-coordinate.md), [Chapter 13](../../fable/chapters/ch13-imu-ins.md) §13.5, [Chapter 47](../../fable/chapters/ch47-file-formats.md) §47.1.1, [Section 32.2](../../gemini4/book/ch-32/sec-32-02.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Verify **IGS ANTEX (`igs20.atx`) antenna and radome calibration codes**, receiver firmware, and sensor calibration certificates. — *Threshold:* Exact aircraft GNSS antenna + radome code and shore base antenna + radome code matched in PPK/PPP software (avoiding $1\text{–}10\text{ cm}$ PCO/PCV vertical bias); factory/ISO 17025 scanner range, IMU, CTD, and Secchi/optical calibration certificates current within 12–24 months. — *Evidence:* ANTEX Verification Table & Calibration Certificate Packet. — *Ref:* [Chapter 12](../../fable/chapters/ch12-gnss.md) §12.2, [Chapter 25](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md) §25.1, §25.6.
- [ ] **[REC]** Audit IMU **Allan variance** / static bench noise log prior to deployment or after any transport shock. — *Threshold:* Gyro bias instability ($\le 0.01\text{–}0.1^\circ\text{ h}^{-1}$ for airborne tactical/nav FOG/RLG) and ARW consistent with manufacturer datasheet and prior campaign baseline. — *Evidence:* Static IMU Noise / Allan Deviation Plot. — *Ref:* [Chapter 13](../../fable/chapters/ch13-imu-ins.md) §13.1, [Chapter 25](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md) §25.6.

## 2. Shore Control, Tide Gauges, Water-Clarity Teams & Calibration Surfaces (Q5)

- [ ] **[GATE]** Establish **coastal CORS / temporary GNSS base stations** keeping all airborne baselines **$\le 20\text{ km}$** (tighter during high $K_p$ or low-latitude operations) and verify published or OPUS coordinates. — *Threshold:* Site temporary bases on stable published monuments (verified against $\ge 2$ independent marks) or occupy with **two independent $\ge 4\text{ h}$ static sessions** processed via NGS OPUS / CSRS-PPP agreeing within $\le 2\text{ cm}$ vertical ($h$); log at $\ge 1\text{ Hz}$ ($\ge$ rover rate, $10^\circ$ elevation mask); measure antenna height **twice in two units** (slant + vertical to ARP) with setup photograph; deploy backup base or verify multi-constellation PPP-AR fallback. — *Evidence:* Base Station Occupation Logs, OPUS Reports, and Tape-Read Photos. — *Ref:* [Chapter 12](../../fable/chapters/ch12-gnss.md) §12.5–12.6, [Chapter 26](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md) §26.3.1, [Section 13.2](../../gemini4/book/ch-13/sec-13-02.md) §13.2.1.
- [ ] **[GATE]** Verify **NOAA NWLON / regional tide gauges** and VDatum / separation-model uncertainty, or install and level temporary project tide gauges before acquisition begins. — *Threshold:* Any temporary project gauge leveled in closed second-order/third-order loops to **$\ge 3$ primary tidal benchmarks (NOAA CO-OPS/FPM minimum; `5` recommended on subsiding or estuarine coasts per IOC Manual 14)** and operated simultaneously with a primary control station ($\ge 30\text{ days}$ for datum transfer, targeting $0.03\text{–}0.05\text{ m}$ $1\sigma$ transfer uncertainty); verify VDatum region version and stated transformation uncertainty ($0.05\text{–}0.10\text{ m}$ open coast vs. $0.10\text{–}0.20\text{ m}$ estuary; note NAD 83/NAVD 88 remain official until NATRF2022/NAPGD2022 adoption following Sept 2026 beta). — *Evidence:* Gauge Benchmark Leveling Loop Closure Sheet & VDatum Uncertainty Check. — *Ref:* [Chapter 9](../../fable/chapters/ch09-vertical-datums.md) §9.3, §9.7, [Chapter 25](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md) §25.2–25.3, [Chapter 68](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md) §68.1.3.
- [ ] **[GATE]** Deploy **boat and/or shore water-clarity & hydrographic support teams** with UTC-synchronized **Secchi disks**, **CTD / optical turbidity ($K_d$ / $c_{\text{beam}}$) sensors**, and anemometer/wave-state loggers during active ALB windows, enforcing small-boat, lone-worker, and rising-tide field safety. — *Threshold:* Sample Secchi depth $Z_{\text{SD}}$ (two-observer mean, shaded side of boat), temperature $T$ ($^\circ\text{C}$), and salinity $S$ ($\text{PSU}$) across coastal/estuarine gradients prior to and during each flight window to parameterize Quan & Fry (1995) $n_p$ and $n_g$ (noting default seawater $n_g \approx 1.3645$ in freshwater/brackish inflows [$n_g \approx 1.3566$] injects a **`-0.5% to -0.6%` shallow depth bias**, $\sim 10\text{–}12\text{ cm}$ at $20\text{ m}$); boat/shore crews wear PFDs, monitor **VHF Ch 16** + air-to-ground CTAF radio, **prohibit `7x50` binoculars / magnifying optics during overhead `532 nm` aircraft passes**, and follow timed lone-worker check-ins and rising-tide marsh egress cutoffs. — *Evidence:* Timestamped Boat/Shore Secchi + CTD Log ([09-resources-and-templates.md](09-resources-and-templates.md)) & Small-Boat/Shore Safety Sign-Off. — *Ref:* [Chapter 19](../../fable/chapters/ch19-bathymetric-lidar.md) §19.1, §19.3, [Section 9.2](../../gemini4/book/ch-09/sec-09-02.md) §9.2.3.
- [ ] **[GATE]** Prepare **three distinct calibration & validation surfaces** inside or adjacent to the AOI: (1) an airborne boresight site (flat tarmac/runway + pitched gable roofs), (2) a **dry flat beach / apron** (for green-minus-NIR channel range check), and (3) a **submerged flat hard reference surface** (boat ramp, stable sandy flat, or concrete apron) surveyed independently by MBES or wading RTK at multiple depths. — *Threshold:* Reference surface own uncertainty $\le 1/3$ of ALB $\text{TVU}_{\max}$ (e.g., $\sigma_{\text{ref}} \le 0.05\text{–}0.08\text{ m}$ for Special Order / Order 1a checks); dry beach green-minus-NIR target offset within `<= 0.03–0.05 m RMSE` (`|mean bias| <= 0.02–0.03 m`), documented in [template-error-budget.md](../_shared/template-error-budget.md). — *Evidence:* Reference Surface Survey Report & Boresight Range Target Map. — *Ref:* [template-error-budget.md](../_shared/template-error-budget.md), [Chapter 19](../../fable/chapters/ch19-bathymetric-lidar.md) §19.3, §19.7, [Chapter 25](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md) §25.4–25.5, [Chapter 52](../../fable/chapters/ch52-ground-truth.md) §52.3.
- [ ] **[GATE]** Survey **independent withheld checkpoints** strictly walled off from boresight/strip adjustment, stratified across dry land (**NVA**), marsh/vegetated uplands (**VVA**), and wading/submerged coastal substrate (**bathy CPs**). — *Threshold:* Minimum **$\ge 30$ checkpoints per reported stratum** (ASPRS Ed. 2 v2 / USGS LBS 2024–2025), distributed across edges, interior, flight days, and substrates (sand vs. seagrass vs. cobble); conservative project checkpoint rule **$\sigma_{\text{cp}} \le 1/3\,\sigma_{\text{product}}$** ($\le 0.03\text{ m}$ for $0.10\text{ m}$ $\text{RMSE}_V$ NVA; `ASPRS Ed. 2 v2` sets `<= 1/2` floor plus mandatory quadrature inclusion); two redundant $180\text{ s}$ RTN/PPK occupations hours apart on fixed-height bipod, or total-station traverse from open GNSS clearings for canopy/marsh VVA; plate-footed marsh rod recording vegetation height; at least $25\%$ tied via independent CORS/base/leveling; **zero checkpoints shared with GCPs**. — *Evidence:* Independent Checkpoint Survey Report, Field Photos, and Sealed Holdout GeoPackage. — *Ref:* [Chapter 25](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md) §25.5, [Chapter 52](../../fable/chapters/ch52-ground-truth.md) §52.2, §52.6, [Chapter 53](../../fable/chapters/ch53-accuracy-assessment.md) §53.2.
- [ ] **[REC]** Deploy portable meteorological barometric pressure, wind speed/direction, and sky-view loggers at base staging for atmospheric/non-tidal setup audit and moving-object tracking (AIS/ADS-B receiver for vessels/aircraft in nearshore corridor). — *Threshold:* $1\text{ min}$ meteorological logging covering entire flight window ($\sim 1\text{ cm hPa}^{-1}$ inverse-barometer tide check). — *Evidence:* Base Weather & AIS/ADS-B Log Archive. — *Ref:* [Chapter 27](../../fable/chapters/ch27-moving-and-transient-objects.md), [Chapter 66](../../fable/chapters/ch66-coastal-marine-polar-lakes-rivers.md) §66.7.
