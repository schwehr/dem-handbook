## 2. Solar Window, NOHD Floor, Sea State & White-Ribbon Tidal Sync (Q6, §4.20)

- [ ] **[GATE]** Restrict optical and $532\text{ nm}$ ALB acquisition to the **strict $30^\circ\text{–}45^\circ$ solar elevation window** (or vendor-approved low-solar-background window if flying at night under LSHA/Part 91 rules). — *Threshold:* Sun elevation $\theta_{\text{sun}} \in [30^\circ, 45^\circ]$: below $30^\circ$ casts long bluff/dune/tree shadows that degrade concurrent RGB/HSI and SfM/classification QC; above $45^\circ$ induces severe **specular sun glint** off wave facets that saturates $532\text{ nm}$ PMT/APD detectors and triggers solar background noise spikes. Adjust flight azimuth relative to solar azimuth when cross-shore winds force glare on one scan arc. — *Evidence:* Ephemeris Solar Elevation Window Table per Survey Date/Latitude. — *Ref:* [Section 13.2](../../gemini4/book/ch-13/sec-13-02.md) §13.2.4, [Chapter 26](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md) §26.2.2.
- [ ] **[GATE]** Enforce **NOHD minimum AGL flight floor** and **automatic geofenced laser-shutter blackout polygons** in the flight management and sensor control systems. — *Threshold:* Planned flight altitude $H_{\text{AGL}} \ge H_{\text{NOHD,floor}}$ at every waypoint (including tall coastal bluffs, bridges, ship superstructures, and wind turbines) with $\ge 30\text{–}50\text{ m}$ terrain-clearance margin; load shutter-off geofences over airports, helipads, DoD/LCH PA masks, beaches with high spectator concentration during holidays, and protected wildlife colonies. — *Evidence:* Loaded Geofence Polygon File Hash + Terrain-Profile AGL Floor Check. — *Ref:* [Chapter 19](../../fable/chapters/ch19-bathymetric-lidar.md), [Chapter 26](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md) §26.8.
- [ ] **[GATE]** Synchronize **low-water ALB overflights (within $1.5\text{ h}$ [project window target] of predicted spring low water)** with **high-water vessel/USV MBES operations** per [`../_shared/multimodal-white-ribbon.md`](../_shared/multimodal-white-ribbon.md) to close the coastal white ribbon with a **100–500 m cross-shore overlap zone**. — *Threshold:* Schedule ALB within $1.5\text{ h}$ (project window target) of predicted spring low water so NIR ($1{,}064\text{ nm}$) maps exposed foreshore flats down to $-0.4\text{ m}$ MLLW (and shallow $532\text{ nm}$ bridges below the $0.2\text{–}0.5\text{ m}$ deconvolution limit), while shallow-draft MBES launches/USVs survey the same intertidal apron at spring high water; verify vertical overlap $\ge 1.0\text{–}2.0\text{ m}$ and horizontal overlap belt $W_{\text{overlap}} = \Delta z_{\text{overlap}} / \tan\beta \in [100\text{ m}, 500\text{ m}]$ (e.g., $2.0\text{ m}$ vertical overlap on a $1:80$ beach yields $160\text{ m}$ overlap width). — *Evidence:* Co-Scheduled Tide Table & Multimodal Overlap Strip Plan. — *Ref:* [Section 13.2](../../gemini4/book/ch-13/sec-13-02.md) §13.2.2, §13.2.5, [Chapter 34](../../fable/chapters/ch34-water-in-dems.md) §34.8.
- [ ] **[GATE]** Screen flight dispatch against **sea-state, wind, water-clarity, PDOP, and geomagnetic $K_p$** thresholds prior to engine start. — *Threshold:* Dispatch only when: (1) significant wave height $H_s < 1.0\text{ m}$ and surf-zone breaking width within planned allowance; (2) wind $U_{10} < 5\text{–}8\text{ m s}^{-1}$ (and if sensor lacks $647\text{ nm}$ Raman or cross-polarized surface lock, verify capillary ripples $U_{10} > 2\text{ m s}^{-1}$ so $1{,}064\text{ nm}$ surface returns do not drop out); (3) morning boat Secchi $Z_{\text{SD}}$ confirms achievable $d_{\max}$ meets target coverage; (4) multi-GNSS $\text{PDOP} \le 2.5\text{–}3.0$ ($\ge 6\text{–}8$ satellites above $10^\circ\text{–}15^\circ$ mask) and NOAA SWPC $K_p < 5$. — *Evidence:* Daily Pre-Flight Weather/Clarity/PDOP Dispatch Sheet. — *Ref:* [Chapter 26](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md) §26.4, [Section 9.2](../../gemini4/book/ch-09/sec-09-02.md) §9.2.2, [Section 13.2](../../gemini4/book/ch-13/sec-13-02.md) §13.2.1.
- [ ] **[COND]** Enforce **multi-asset optical and airspace deconfliction** during synchronous white-ribbon campaigns (*Trigger:* concurrent Secchi/CTD boats, USVs, manned MBES launches, or UAS beach teams operating inside the AOI). — *Threshold:* Maintain vertical/lateral separation exceeding $532\text{ nm}$ unaided/optical-aid **NOHD** over surface craft, maintain $\ge 150\text{–}300\text{ m}$ vertical separation and active VHF/CTA-F radio advisories between survey aircraft and any coastal UAS, and halt optical aid (`7x50` binoculars) on chase boats during overhead passes per [multimodal-white-ribbon.md](../_shared/multimodal-white-ribbon.md). — *Evidence:* Daily Multi-Asset Safety Brief & Radio Frequency Plan. — *Ref:* [multimodal-white-ribbon.md](../_shared/multimodal-white-ribbon.md), [Chapter 26](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md) §26.8.
- [ ] **[COND]** Adapt swath geometry, endurance, and safety buffers for **heavy-lift UAS ($\ge 25\text{ kg}$ under 49 U.S.C. § 44807 + COA) or helicopter-pod** ALB deployments (*Trigger:* non-fixed-wing platform). — *Threshold:* Constrain ground speed ($15\text{–}35\text{ m s}^{-1}$), bank angles, and hover/turn transitions to preserve rotor-wash water-surface integrity (`z_s` flat) and optical mount stiffness; schedule battery/fuel hot-swaps so priority intertidal lines finish inside the `30–45°` solar window. — *Evidence:* Platform-Specific Sortie Timeline & Rotor-Wash Exclusion Note. — *Ref:* [Chapter 16](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md), [Section 7.3](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-03.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** Pre-build **cloud-ceiling, sun-glint azimuth, and turbidity fallback blocks** into the FMS flight library before takeoff. — *Threshold:* Stage secondary clear-water offshore/lagoon lines and rotated cross-sun azimuth tracks so crews pivot within $5\text{–}10\text{ min}$ rather than burning ferry fuel if low coastal stratus infringes the `NOHD` ceiling or river-mouth turbidity spikes. — *Evidence:* Primary + Contingency FMS Route Package. — *Ref:* [Chapter 26](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md) §26.4, [Section 13.2](../../gemini4/book/ch-13/sec-13-02.md).

## 3. *A Priori* ALB Total Propagated Uncertainty (TPU) Budget (Q10)

- [ ] **[GATE]** Compute and sign an ***a priori* analytical ALB TPU budget** in [`../_shared/template-error-budget.md`](../_shared/template-error-budget.md) evaluated at the **worst-case operational envelope** ($H_{\text{AGL}} = 500\text{ m}$, $\theta_a = 20^\circ$, maximum depth $d = 20\text{ m}$, slope $\alpha = 10^\circ$, longest baseline $\le 20\text{ km}$), distinguishing **ellipsoidal bottom height** from **reduced chart-datum depth**. — *Threshold:* Verify combined $1.96\,\sigma_z \le \mathrm{TVU}_{\max}(d) = \sqrt{a^2 + (bd)^2}$ with positive margin (at least $15\text{–}25\%$ reserve below the governing Order 1a contract limit before committing flight plan), verify subaerial $\sigma_z \le 0.10\text{ m}$ for LBS QL1/QL2, and compute 2D circular horizontal TPU $\mathrm{THU}_{95\%} = 2.4477\,\sigma_{1\text{D},H}$ against S-44 limits (`2.0 m` Special Order; `5.0 m + 0.05 d` Order 1a). Explicitly account for:
  1. **Airborne Trajectory + Attitude + Boresight + Range:** $\sigma_{z,\text{topo}}^2 = \sigma_{z,\text{GNSS/INS}}^2 + \sigma_r^2\cos^2\theta_a + (H_{\text{AGL}}\tan\theta_a\,\sigma_{\phi,\theta})^2 + \tan^2\alpha\,\sigma_{1\text{D},H}^2$ ($\approx 0.06\text{ m}$ $1\sigma$ at $500\text{ m}$ AGL with FOG/RLG IMU, where $\sigma_{1\text{D},H}$ is single-axis horizontal uncertainty; roll/boresight-roll/scan-encoder multiply $H_{\text{AGL}}\tan\theta_a$ vertically while pitch shifts along-track).
  2. **Water-Surface Model Error ($0.27\,\delta z_s$):** Modeling error $\delta z_s$ in the water surface shifts ellipsoidal bottom elevation by $\delta z_b = \delta z_s \left(1 - \frac{n_a\cos\theta_w}{n_g\cos\theta_a}\right) \approx (1 - 1/n_g)\,\delta z_s \approx 0.27\,\delta z_s$ near nadir ($\approx 0.25\,\delta z_s$ at $\theta_a = 20^\circ$, with $n_a \approx 1.0003$), while relative *depth* $z_s - z_b$ carries $\approx 0.73\,\delta z_s$ nadir ($\approx 0.75\,\delta z_s$ at $20^\circ$); a $0.20\text{ m}$ wave residual yields $\sigma_{z,\text{surf}} \approx 0.05\text{ m}$ ($1\sigma$). Unmodeled Cox–Munk wave slope couples the **1D in-plane slope** $\sigma_{\eta_x} = \sigma_\eta/\sqrt{2}$ into $\sigma_{z,\text{wave}} \approx d\tan\theta_w(0.275)\sigma_{\eta_x} \approx 0.073\,d\,\sigma_{\eta_x}$ and total 2D RMS slope $\sigma_\eta$ into radial $\sigma_{xy,\text{wave}} \approx d(1 - \frac{n_a\cos\theta_a}{n_p\cos\theta_w})\sigma_\eta \approx 0.275\,d\,\sigma_\eta$ before surface-patch averaging.
  3. **Refractive Index ($n_p$ vs. $n_g$):** Natural coastal $T/S$ variations contribute $\sigma_n/n_g \approx 0.15\%$ of depth ($0.01\text{ m}$ at $5\text{ m}$; $0.03\text{ m}$ at $20\text{ m}$); **must** separate $n_p \approx 1.3411\text{–}1.3420$ (Snell bending) from $n_g \approx 1.3638\text{–}1.3651$ (range speed) to prevent $+1.6\text{–}1.8\%$ systematic bias ($+0.33\text{ to }+0.36\text{ m}$ at $20\text{ m}$).
  4. **Turbidity Pulse Stretching & Footprint Slope Bias:** $\beta_{\text{det}}\,d$ grows with turbidity/depth ($0.03\text{ m}$ at $5\text{ m}$; $0.12\text{ m}$ at $20\text{ m}$), and forward-scattered benthic footprint $D_{\text{benthic}}$ on bottom slope $\alpha$ adds $(D_{\text{benthic}}/2)\tan\alpha$ ($0.07\text{ m}$ at $5\text{ m}$; $0.09\text{ m}$ at $20\text{ m}$ for $D \approx 1.5\text{ m}$ effective / $\gamma = 0.05\text{ rad}$ on $10^\circ$ slope).
  5. **Water Level & VDatum Separation Uncertainty:** $\sigma_{\text{wl+SEP}} \approx 0.05\text{ m}$ (open coast) to $0.10\text{–}0.20\text{ m}$ (complex estuary) if delivering reduced chart-datum depths. — *Evidence:* Signed *A Priori* ALB TPU Workbook matching the worked table below. — *Ref:* [Chapter 19](../../fable/chapters/ch19-bathymetric-lidar.md) §19.3 & Mathematics, [Section 9.5](../../gemini4/book/ch-09/sec-09-05.md) §9.5.4–9.5.5.
