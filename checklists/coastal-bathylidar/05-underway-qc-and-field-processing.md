Because coastal water clarity, surf foam, and wind chop change within hours—and aircraft demobilization is prohibitively expensive—every sortie undergoes **in-flight waveform/Secchi monitoring** followed by **same-day PPK, overlap, and SHA-256 3-2-1 custody verification** before standing down bases or releasing the aircraft.

---

## 1. Live In-Flight Operator Quality Checks (Q13)

- [ ] **[GATE]** Monitor **live $532\text{ nm}$ waveform Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR)** and **real-time bottom extinction depth** against concurrent shore/boat **Secchi depth ($Z_{\text{SD}}$)** updates. — *Threshold:* Observed maximum bottom penetration $d_{\max,\text{obs}}$ tracks local $1.5\text{–}2.0\,Z_{\text{SD}}$ (topobathy) or $2.5\text{–}3.5\,Z_{\text{SD}}$ (deep channel) within $20\%$; if a sudden river runoff plume, dredging plume, or algal bloom drops $d_{\max,\text{obs}}$ below target coverage contour or creates thick mid-water nepheloid scattering peaks ($P_{\text{layer}} \gg P_{\text{bed}}$), **halt production over that block** and divert to clear sub-blocks or stand down. — *Evidence:* Live Operator Waveform Screenshots + Radioed Secchi Comparison Log. — *Ref:* [Chapter 19](../../fable/chapters/ch19-bathymetric-lidar.md) §19.1, [Section 9.7](../../gemini4/book/ch-09/sec-09-07.md) Pitfall 1.
- [ ] **[GATE]** Verify **1,064 nm NIR and 647 nm Raman / cross-polarized water-surface channel health** continuously across calm lagoons and choppy nearshore waters. — *Threshold:* Confirm $1{,}064\text{ nm}$ skin returns or $647\text{ nm}$ Raman surface lock maintain $>90\text{–}95\%$ surface-model coverage along active waterlines; flag any **glassy-water NIR specular dropout** ($U_{10} < 2\text{ m s}^{-1}$ forcing green surface fallback bias of $10\text{–}35\text{ cm}$ deep if Raman/polarized lock fails) and any **whitecap foam bias** ($U_{10} > 8\text{ m s}^{-1}$ lifting NIR return onto wave foam). — *Evidence:* Live Multi-Channel Surface Health Strip Log. — *Ref:* [Section 9.2](../../gemini4/book/ch-09/sec-09-02.md) §9.2.2, [Chapter 19](../../fable/chapters/ch19-bathymetric-lidar.md) §19.1.
- [ ] **[GATE]** Monitor live **GNSS constellation health ($C/N_0$, AGC, PDOP, satellite count)**, IMU alignment status, laser chiller temperature, **1PPS trigger lock**, and **NOHD AGL floor / shutter status**. — *Threshold:* Minimum $\ge 6\text{–}8$ tracked multi-GNSS satellites, $\text{PDOP} \le 3.0$, zero sustained $C/N_0$ / AGC jamming or spoofing anomalies (`ch56.19`); zero `1PPS`/camera-event latency slips (`> 0.2 ms`); chiller coolant temperature within $\pm 1.0^\circ\text{C}$ (tolerance) of nominal setpoint; automatic shutter transitions cleanly across geofenced exclusion zones without uncommanded dropouts. — *Evidence:* In-Flight Operator Console Status Log. — *Ref:* [Chapter 6](../../fable/chapters/ch06-time-as-coordinate.md), [Chapter 56](../../fable/chapters/ch56-case-files.md) §56.19, [Section 13.3](../../gemini4/book/ch-13/sec-13-03.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** Log transient coastal objects, vessel traffic, breaking surf boundaries, kelp canopies, and clouds/fog visually and via AIS/ADS-B during every line, and **radio real-time extinction boundaries to concurrent MBES/USV support crews**. — *Threshold:* Timestamped moving-object/kelp/surf notes correlated to line ID so later processors do not mistake vessels, fish schools, or kelp beds for submerged shoals, while boat teams immediately extend high-water acoustic fill lines into unexpected turbidity voids. — *Evidence:* Flight Moving-Object & Environmental Log + Recorded AIS/ADS-B Stream + Vessel Radio Update. — *Ref:* [Chapter 27](../../fable/chapters/ch27-moving-and-transient-objects.md), [Appendix G](../../fable/appendices/appendix-g-checklists.md) §G.2.

## 2. Same-Day Ingest, PPK Trajectory, Overlap & Void QC (Q13, Q14)

- [ ] **[GATE]** Execute **immutable raw ingest, SHA-256 checksum generation, and 3-2-1 backup** immediately upon aircraft engine shutdown (never modify raw flight magazines in place). — *Threshold:* $100\%$ of raw waveform files (`.pls`/`.wvs` or vendor raw), discrete clouds, RGB/HSI frames, airborne GNSS/IMU files, shore GNSS base RINEX, and Secchi/tide logs hashed with `sha256sum` and replicated to **two physically separate on-site drives plus off-site/cloud sync** before wiping sensor magazines. — *Evidence:* Verified `SHA256SUMS.txt` Manifest & Dual-Media Copy Verification Log. — *Ref:* [Chapter 29](../../fable/chapters/ch29-processing-pipelines.md), [Chapter 50](../../fable/chapters/ch50-archiving-and-provenance.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Run **same-day PPK/GNSS-INS trajectory solution** (POSPac / Inertial Explorer) against local shore base stations ($\le 20\text{ km}$) and inspect forward–backward separation and ambiguity fix rate before next day's flight. — *Threshold:* Ambiguity-fixed ratio $\ge 95\text{–}99\%$ across all mission lines; forward–backward separation **`<= 0.05 m` horizontal and `<= 0.10 m` vertical (`95 %`)** (`<0.02\text{–}0.03\text{ m}$ nominal; any spike $>0.10\text{ m}$ sustained $>2\text{–}3\text{ s}$ triggers mandatory segment re-fly); verify base-station antenna height & frame epoch match Project Instructions. — *Evidence:* Daily Trajectory QC Report (`SMRMSG` / Separation Plot & Fix-Status Summary). — *Ref:* [Chapter 12](../../fable/chapters/ch12-gnss.md) §12.6, [Chapter 13](../../fable/chapters/ch13-imu-ins.md) §13.8, [Chapter 26](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md) §26.5.
- [ ] **[GATE]** Compute **same-day swath overlap residuals and dry-beach green-minus-NIR checks** alongside a **flat-seabed overlap difference** to catch boresight or refraction anomalies early. — *Threshold:* On flat dry beach/runway: green-minus-NIR **`|mean bias| <= 0.02–0.03 m, RMSE <= 0.03–0.05 m`**, **inter-swath $\text{RMSD}_z \le 0.08\text{ m}$** (USGS LBS QL1/QL2 ceiling), and smooth-surface **intra-swath $\text{RMSD}_z \le 0.06\text{ m}$**; on flat seabed: overlap $\text{RMSD}_z$ consistent with depth-dependent TPU ($\le 0.15\text{–}0.25\text{ m}$ shallow) with zero cross-track tilt absent on land (which flags surface-model or refraction failure). — *Evidence:* Daily Overlap Difference GeoTIFFs & Dry-Beach Green–NIR Report. — *Ref:* [Chapter 19](../../fable/chapters/ch19-bathymetric-lidar.md) §19.3, §19.6, [Chapter 25](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md) §25.5, [Chapter 26](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md) §26.5 (`Try it`).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Verify **explicit Class 45 ("no bottom found at") extinction tagging** and **surf-zone / turbidity void boundary masks** on preliminary field triage surfaces before releasing the aircraft from the regional base. — *Threshold:* Every offshore extinction strip where water depth exceeds $1.5\text{–}3\,Z_{\text{SD}}$ carries **LAS Class 45** depth tags (proving minimum clear water depth) rather than blank voids; surf-zone aerated foam and turbid river-plume dropouts are masked as voids and correlated with flight imagery/Secchi readings—never bridged with TIN triangles; real-time spatial density/holiday accumulator confirms zero unintended flight gaps $>2\times\text{ANPS}^2$ on dry land or clear seabed. — *Evidence:* Daily Holiday/Void Map, Class 45 Extinction Map, and Infill Line List. — *Ref:* [Chapter 19](../../fable/chapters/ch19-bathymetric-lidar.md) §19.4, [Section 13.3](../../gemini4/book/ch-13/sec-13-03.md) §13.3.2.
- [ ] **[REC]** Execute a **same-day intertidal check** comparing low-water ALB beach/foreshore points against any concurrent high-water vessel/USV swath collected over the 100–500 m white-ribbon overlap zone ([multimodal-white-ribbon.md](../_shared/multimodal-white-ribbon.md)). — *Threshold:* Mean vertical bias across stable sand overlap within $\sqrt{\sigma_{\text{ALB}}^2 + \sigma_{\text{MBES}}^2 + \sigma_{\text{VDatum}}^2}$ ($\le 0.15\text{–}0.25\text{ m}$); flag any $>0.25\text{ m}$ datum/tide offset immediately before boat and aircraft diverge. — *Evidence:* Daily White-Ribbon Seam Check Profile. — *Ref:* [multimodal-white-ribbon.md](../_shared/multimodal-white-ribbon.md), [Chapter 48](../../fable/chapters/ch48-compositing.md) §48.6, [Section 13.2](../../gemini4/book/ch-13/sec-13-02.md) §13.2.2.
- [ ] **[REC]** Spot-check preliminary LAS returns against available early **withheld/reference dry-beach and shallow check points** on Sortie 1–2 and enforce pre-agreed re-fly triggers without schedule-pressure overrides. — *Threshold:* Preliminary open-ground bias within $\pm 0.05\text{ m}$ (tolerance); any unrecoverable trajectory dropout, failed overlap residual ($\text{RMSD}_z > 0.08\text{ m}$), unintended holiday, cloud shadow, or clarity-dropout block scheduled for immediate infill line. — *Evidence:* Sortie 1–2 Sanity Check Table & Signed Re-Fly Tasking Order. — *Ref:* [Chapter 26](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md) §26.5, §26.6.
- [ ] **[COND]** Quarantine and run interference diagnostics if GNSS $C/N_0$, AGC, or IMU innovation residuals indicate **jamming or spoofing** (*Trigger:* elevated $C/N_0$ variance, false fixes, or sudden trajectory jumps). — *Threshold:* Flag affected flight lines, switch to multi-constellation tightly coupled IMU bridging or PPP forensics, and re-fly compromised lines outside interference window. — *Evidence:* GNSS Interference Forensic Log & Re-Fly Record. — *Ref:* [Chapter 56](../../fable/chapters/ch56-case-files.md) §56.19, [Chapter 69](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md) §69.1.4.
- [ ] **[REC]** Update the daily **productive-area, standby-burn, and remaining-clarity-runway log** in [`../_shared/template-cost-ledger.md`](../_shared/template-cost-ledger.md) at day-end debrief. — *Threshold:* Reconcile cumulative $\text{km}^2$ mapped, weather/clarity standby hours burned, and active boat/aircraft day-rates against the project abandon-window trigger so neither team exceeds standby contingency unannounced. — *Evidence:* Signed Daily Burn-Rate & Production Dashboard Entry. — *Ref:* [template-cost-ledger.md](../_shared/template-cost-ledger.md), [Chapter 26](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md) §26.9, [Chapter 28](../../fable/chapters/ch28-reducing-cost.md) §28.1.
- [ ] **[REC]** Monitor real-time **1PPS / sensor-event synchronization delta** and camera-exposure clip rate (`<= 0.1 %` saturated coastal sand pixels) during daily field triage. — *Threshold:* Zero unlogged camera trigger dropouts; inspect dry-sand white-balance/radiometry alongside $532\text{ nm}$ waveform peak saturation to preserve simultaneous ortho/hyperspectral coastal fusion quality. — *Evidence:* Daily Sync-Delta & Camera Exposure Audit Note. — *Ref:* [Chapter 6](../../fable/chapters/ch06-time-as-coordinate.md), [Chapter 26](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md) §26.5.
