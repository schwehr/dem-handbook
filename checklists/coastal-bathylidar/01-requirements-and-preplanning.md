## 1. Survey Requirements & Standards Crosswalk (Q1)

- [ ] **[GATE]** Define the governing coastal decision, economic/safety loss function, and valid-until epoch before selecting sensor parameters (navigation Under-Keel Clearance shoal bias vs. FEMA/SLR inundation increment rule $\ge 2 \times \text{LE95}$ vs. unbiased beach/channel sediment volume budget). — *Threshold:* Written decision memo specifying target sea-level/depth increment ($\ge 0.40\text{ m}$ for $0.10\text{ m}$ $\text{RMSE}_V$ lidar), shoal-retention rule, and storm re-survey trigger. — *Evidence:* Signed Survey Requirements Memo in Project Instructions ([template-project-header.md](../_shared/template-project-header.md)). — *Ref:* [Chapter 3](../../fable/chapters/ch03-fitness-for-use.md), [Chapter 66](../../fable/chapters/ch66-coastal-marine-polar-lakes-rivers.md) §66.1.4.
- [ ] **[GATE]** Publish an explicit dual-domain standards crosswalk separating subaerial requirements (**USGS LBS 2024/2025 rev. A** QL1/QL2 & **ASPRS PAS Ed. 2 v2**) from submerged bathymetric requirements (**IHO S-44 Ed. 6.2.0** [Oct 2024; core Table 1 TVU/THU $a/b$ values unchanged from Ed. 6.1.0, adding §3.8 seabed characteristics] Special Order / Order 1a / Order 1b / Survey Matrix, **NOAA HSSD (2024 edition, restructured; annual updates)**, or **USACE EM 1110-2-1003**), naming one governing standard per stratum and test statistic. — *Threshold:* Zero unmapped "or better" clauses; subaerial $\text{RMSE}_V \le 0.10\text{ m}$ (QL1 $\ge 8\text{ pls m}^{-2}$ / QL2 $\ge 2\text{ pls m}^{-2}$), bathymetric $\text{TVU}_{95\%}(d) = \sqrt{a^2 + (bd)^2}$ ($a=0.50\text{ m}, b=0.013$ for Order 1a; $a=0.25\text{ m}, b=0.0075$ for Special Order), and explicit feature-detection caveat ($532\text{ nm}$ scattering-widened footprints $D_{\text{benthic}} \approx 0.5\text{–}1.0\,d$ rarely satisfy 1 m or 2 m cube search at depth without concurrent MBES). — *Evidence:* Signed Project Specification Crosswalk Table. — *Ref:* [Chapter 70](../../fable/chapters/ch70-specifications-guided-tour.md) §70.7, [Section 24.1](../../gemini4/book/ch-24/sec-24-01.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Lock in a **single unified vertical CRS, realization, geoid/separation model, epoch, and sign convention** across both dry land and seabed enforcing the coastal datum triad **$h = H + N = z_{\text{CD}} + \mathrm{SEP} = \mathrm{SEP} - d_{\text{CD}}$** (processing and archiving in ellipsoidal height $h$ in `ITRF2020`, `WGS 84 (G2296)`, or `NAD83(2011) epoch 2010.0`, then applying one named VDatum/VORF/GEOID grid to reach NAVD88/MLLW/LAT). — *Threshold:* Zero mixed datums (preventing a $0.3\text{–}1.5\text{ m}$ shoreline step from NAVD88 land paired with MLLW sea); EPSG compound code or ISO 19162 WKT2 + named separation grid version + NTDE (`1983–2001` or active modified epoch) locked; units strictly SI metres (US Survey Foot deprecated post-2022-12-31). — *Evidence:* Completed [`../_shared/template-project-header.md`](../_shared/template-project-header.md) with VDatum region/version uncertainty ($1\sigma = 0.05\text{–}0.20\text{ m}$). — *Ref:* [Chapter 9](../../fable/chapters/ch09-vertical-datums.md) §9.7, [Chapter 19](../../fable/chapters/ch19-bathymetric-lidar.md) §19.2, [Chapter 68](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md) §68.2, [Section 3.2](../../gemini4/book/ch-03/sec-03-02.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Lock in **ASPRS LAS 1.4 (R15 or later) PDRF 6–10 Topo-Bathy Domain Profile** classification policy, surface ontology, and void/gap rules across the land–water transition. — *Threshold:* Explicit rules for Classes 1 (unclassified), 2 (bare-earth ground), 7/18 (low/high noise flagged `Withheld`, never deleted), 9 (water), 17 (bridge deck), 20 (ignored ground near breaklines), and **Topo-Bathy Classes 40–45** (`40` bathymetric bottom, `41` water surface, `42` derived water surface, `43` submerged object, `44` IHO S-57 object, `45` no-bottom-found-at); explicit policy forbidding silent TIN interpolation across surf-zone foam or turbidity voids; declared marsh bias protocol (`Spartina` $+0.15\text{ to }+0.45\text{ m}$ pulse-merging). — *Evidence:* Classification & Surface Ontology Policy Appendix in Project Instructions. — *Ref:* [Chapter 30](../../fable/chapters/ch30-point-cloud-classification.md) §30.2, [Chapter 34](../../fable/chapters/ch34-water-in-dems.md) §34.8, [Section 9.7](../../gemini4/book/ch-09/sec-09-07.md).

## 2. Pre-Existing Data & Water-Clarity Reconnaissance (Q3)

- [ ] **[GATE]** Quantify multi-year **water-clarity climatology ($K_d$ and Secchi depth $Z_{\text{SD}}$)** and verify feasibility with **ICESat-2 ATL03 / ATL24** photon bathymetry over the AOI prior to committing ALB mobilization. — *Threshold:* Analyze $\ge 3\text{–}5$ years of monthly MODIS/VIIRS/Sentinel-2 $K_d(490\text{–}560)$ composites translated via empirical Holmes–Poole conversion **$Z_{\text{SD}} \approx (1.44\text{–}1.70)/K_d$** paired with `SlideRule Earth` / `icepyx` ICESat-2 bathymetric transects; note that **ICESat-2 ATLAS penetrates only $\sim 1.0\,Z_{\text{SD}}$** ($\sim 1.4\text{–}1.7 / K_d$), making spaceborne photon depths a **conservative lower bound** ($\sim 0.5\text{–}0.7\times$ topobathy and $\sim 0.33\text{–}0.4\times$ deep-channel ALB capability); confirm seasonal median clarity yields $d_{\max} \approx 1.5\text{–}2.0\,Z_{\text{SD}}$ (topobathy) or $2.5\text{–}3.5\,Z_{\text{SD}}$ / $(3\text{–}4.5)/K_d$ (deep channel) meeting $\ge 80\%$ of target depth zone, accounting for $\sim 1\,Z_{\text{SD}}$ penalty over dark seagrass/mud ($\rho_b \approx 5\%$). — *Evidence:* Pre-Survey Water Clarity & ICESat-2 Recon Report (maps of seasonal $K_d$, ICESat-2 extinction depth tracks, and river-plume masks). — *Ref:* [Chapter 19](../../fable/chapters/ch19-bathymetric-lidar.md) §19.1, §19.5, [Section 9.2](../../gemini4/book/ch-09/sec-09-02.md) §9.2.4.
- [ ] **[REC]** Assemble and audit prior elevation/bathymetry archives (USGS 3DEP, JALBTCX/NOAA Digital Coast NCMP, NOAA NCEI BAGs & BlueTopo, ENCs with CATZOC, GEBCO **with TID grids**, Sentinel-2 SDB) on a common datum to locate hazards, mobile sandbars, and datum discrepancies. — *Threshold:* Separate direct-measurement GEBCO codes (**`TID 14`** bathymetric LiDAR; `TID 10–17` direct surveys) from **`TID 40`** (altimetry-gravity predicted) and `TID 41–44` (interpolated); verify CATZOC confidence and datum offsets, flagging dynamic inlet/shoal changes $>0.5\text{ m}$ for priority coverage. — *Evidence:* GIS Prior-Data Inventory Layer & Seam Screening Note. — *Ref:* [Chapter 51](../../fable/chapters/ch51-finding-data.md), [Chapter 55](../../fable/chapters/ch55-public-products.md), [Chapter 66](../../fable/chapters/ch66-coastal-marine-polar-lakes-rivers.md) §66.3.2.
- [ ] **[REC]** Analyze geodetic, tidal, hydrological, and crustal-motion background data for the coastal AOI. — *Threshold:* Pull NOAA CORS / IGS coordinate time series, NGS benchmark datasheets (recovery condition, stability codes A–D, subsidence/VLM rates via HTDP/ITRF2020), NOAA NWLON harmonic tidal constituents, river gauge flood/discharge hydrographs (avoiding freshet sediment plumes), wind/wave-height rose ($H_s < 1.0\text{ m}$ climatology), and NOAA SWPC geomagnetic $K_p$ / scintillation forecasts. — *Evidence:* Environmental & Geodetic Reconnaissance Dossier. — *Ref:* [Chapter 25](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md) §25.2–25.3, [Chapter 38](../../fable/chapters/ch38-plate-motion-and-vlm.md), [Section 13.2](../../gemini4/book/ch-13/sec-13-02.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** Overlay aviation, maritime, hazard, and GNSS-interference constraints onto the AOI planning GIS. — *Threshold:* Ingest FAA VFR/IFR Sectional charts, airport Class B/C/D shelves, military Warning Areas / Restricted airspace, TFRs, USCG Local Notices to Mariners (LNMs), marine sanctuaries/critical habitat, coastal powerlines/towers, and MARAD/FAA GNSS jamming/spoofing advisories. — *Evidence:* Multi-Layer Airspace & Hazard Planning GeoPackage. — *Ref:* [Chapter 26](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md) §26.8, [Chapter 69](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md) §69.1.4.
