Airborne laser bathymetry combines aviation structural alterations, high-power visible ($532\text{ nm}$) and near-infrared ($1{,}064\text{ nm}$) outdoor laser emissions, low-altitude coastal/overwater flight profiles, sensitive shoreline ecosystems, and tight weather/clarity standby budgets. **Part A (§1, §2, §4, §5)** closes pre-mobilization legal, insurance, and cost controls prior to ferry; **Part B (§3)** governs daily pre-flight ocular, overwater, hazmat, and crew-fatigue safety gates. All thresholds and statutory clocks are stated inline for offline cockpit/binder use.

## Part A — Pre-Mobilization Permits, Insurance & Cost Baseline

## Part B — Daily Pre-Flight Ocular, Overwater, Hazmat & Crew Safety Gates

## 3. Laser Ocular Safety, Overwater Ditching & Flight Crew Safety Checks (Q8, §4.20)

- [ ] **[GATE]** Calculate and certify **ANSI Z136.1 & Z136.6** **Maximum Permissible Exposure (MPE)** and **Nominal Ocular Hazard Distance (NOHD)** using $\mathrm{NOHD} = \frac{1}{\beta_t}\!\left(\sqrt{\frac{4\,\Phi_{\text{eff}}}{\pi\,\mathrm{MPE}}} - D_0\right)$ across all three repetitive-pulse criteria (single-pulse, average-power over exposure duration $T_{\max}$ [$0.25\text{ s}$ blink reflex for $532\text{ nm}$; $10\text{ s}$ for $1{,}064\text{ nm}$], and $N^{-1/4}$ pulse-train thermal rule), verifying that ground/sea-surface exposure meets **Class 1** (or, if **Class 1M** unaided-only, that extended optical-aid hazard $\mathrm{ENOHD} \approx G_{\text{opt}}\,\mathrm{NOHD}$ is explicitly managed) including upward **specular reflection off calm water** ($R_F \approx 2.1\%$ at $\theta_a = 15^\circ\text{–}20^\circ$) and **wet vessel glass** ($4\text{–}10\%$ directing a reflected cone $15^\circ\text{–}20^\circ$ off-zenith toward nearby aircraft or elevated bridge/bluff observers). — *Threshold:* Minimum operating altitude $H_{\text{AGL,min}} \ge \max(\text{NOHD}_{\text{unaided}}, \text{NOHD}_{\text{specular}}) + \text{terrain/vessel-mast buffer}$ (with beam divergence $\beta_t \approx 0.7\text{–}7.5\text{ mrad}$ and exit diameter $D_0$ verified); if Class 1M $\mathrm{ENOHD}$ exceeds $H_{\text{AGL}}$, prohibit `7x50` binoculars / spotting scopes on support craft and enforce observer exclusion or raise AGL floor; verify upward specular irradiance stays within FAA AC 70-1B (`50 nW / 5 uW / 100 uW cm^-2`) flight zones. — *Evidence:* Laser Safety Officer (LSO) Signed NOHD/ENOHD/MPE Worksheet (citing $E_p$, $\tau_p$, PRF, $D_0$, $\beta_t$, and $f_{\text{scan}}$). — *Ref:* [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md), [Chapter 19](../../fable/chapters/ch19-bathymetric-lidar.md) §19.2, [Section 9.2](../../gemini4/book/ch-09/sec-09-02.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Test **automatic hardware/GPS-geofenced laser shutter interlocks**, altitude/roll cutoff switches, and cabin laser protective eyewear prior to every sortie. — *Threshold:* Shutter automatically closes within $<100\text{ ms}$ if $H_{\text{AGL}} < H_{\text{NOHD\_floor}}$, aircraft roll $|\phi| > \phi_{\max}$, scan mirror stalls, aircraft enters a geofenced no-fire polygon (airports, helipads, spectators, wildlife rookeries), or weight-on-wheels switch indicates ground contact; dual-band ($532\text{ nm} + 1{,}064\text{ nm}$) OD-rated eyewear staged for ground bench tests only (never tint-obstructing pilot cockpit vision in flight). — *Evidence:* Pre-Flight Interlock & Geofence Bench Test Log. — *Ref:* [Appendix G](../../fable/appendices/appendix-g-checklists.md) §G.2, [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** Inspect mandatory **overwater survival and ditching equipment** adopted from **14 CFR § 91.509** (statutorily Subpart F large/turbine multiengine, applied here as the operational coastal floor for piston survey twins/singles beyond `§ 91.205(b)(12)`), plus **UN 3090 / UN 3091** primary-lithium beacon cells and **UN 3480 / UN 3481** Li-ion pack hazmat controls (**IATA DGR 67th Ed., eff. Jan 1, 2026**). — *Threshold:* Wearable life preserver (PFD) with survivor locator light for every occupant beyond power-off gliding distance from shore (mandatory $>92.6\text{ km}$ / $50\text{ nmi}$ under § 91.509(a)); for operations $>30\text{ min}$ flying time or $>185.2\text{ km}$ ($100\text{ nmi}$) from shore (§ 91.509(b)), carry rated **life raft(s)** with survivor locator lights, survival kit, pyrotechnic signals, lifeline, and portable water-resistant emergency radio; verify registered **406 MHz ELT** + personal **PLBs**; ship/handle Li-ion (`UN 3480 PI965` CAO and `UN 3481 PI966`) at **`<= 30 % SoC`** for air freight with **SDS + UN 38.3** test summaries (`49 CFR 171–180` ground), use **in-flight cabin Halon / clean-agent strictly per AFM**, **water deluge / AVD (`Lith-EX`)** on ground/boat Li-ion thermal runaway (never ABC/CO2), and reserve **`Class D`** strictly for primary lithium-metal (`UN 3090`). — *Evidence:* Signed Pre-Flight Overwater Ditching, ELT & Hazmat SDS/UN 38.3 Packet. — *Ref:* [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md), [Chapter 16](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** Verify **14 CFR § 91.211** supplemental oxygen compliance (*Trigger:* unpressurized transit/ferry or depressurized operations above $3{,}810\text{ m}$ ($12{,}500\text{ ft}$ MSL) $>30\text{ min}$, $>4{,}267\text{ m}$ ($14{,}000\text{ ft}$ MSL) continuous crew, or $>4{,}572\text{ m}$ ($15{,}000\text{ ft}$ MSL) all occupants). — *Threshold:* Hydrostatic tank inspection current, flow/pressure verified for all flight + sensor crew + reserve. — *Evidence:* Pre-Flight Oxygen Pressure Log. — *Ref:* [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md), [Section 7.3](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-03.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** Enforce flight-crew duty-time, sensor-operator watch rotation, and GNSS jamming/RF resilience protocols. — *Threshold:* Pilot duty/rest within 14 CFR Part 91/135/company SOP limits; sensor operator fatigued-watch cap ($\le 6\text{ h}$ high-cognitive coastal/TFR airspace ops without relief); multi-constellation GNSS $C/N_0$ & AGC interference monitor active (`ch56.19`); encrypted/air-gapped media transfer SOP. — *Evidence:* Crew Rest Log & RF/Cyber Hygiene Sign-Off. — *Ref:* [Chapter 56](../../fable/chapters/ch56-case-files.md) §56.19, [Section 27.3](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-03.md) §27.3.5.
