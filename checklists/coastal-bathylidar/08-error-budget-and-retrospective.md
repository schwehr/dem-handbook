## 2. Systematic Root-Cause Attribution & Remediation

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Wave-Surface & `n_p / n_g` Bias Check:** Test linear regression of `(z_ALB - z_MBES)` against depth $d$ (`m`) and wave height $H_s$; confirm depth slope is indistinguishable from zero (`|slope| < 0.005 m/m`), noting exact sign convention: in **`+z ↑` elevation** ($z = z_s - d$), reusing phase index $n_p$ for slant-range velocity $n_g$ overestimates depth by $+1.6\text{–}1.8\%$ so **`d(z_ALB - z_MBES)/dd = -0.016 to -0.018 m/m`** (`+0.016 to +0.018 m/m` only in **`+d ↓` depth** $d_{\text{ALB}} - d_{\text{MBES}}$; whereas seawater $n_g$ applied in freshwater shifts `+0.005 to +0.006 m/m` on `+z ↑` elevation), and verify wave-model residual stays bounded near `0.27 delta z_s` (`[ ] Zero Depth Slope Confirmed` `[ ] Re-refracted`). — *Threshold:* Regression slope `|d(z_ALB - z_MBES)/dd| < 0.005 m/m` across $1\text{–}25\text{ m}$ MBES overlap. — *Evidence:* Depth-vs-Residual Regression Plot (sign convention stated explicitly). — *Ref:* [Chapter 19](../../fable/chapters/ch19-bathymetric-lidar.md) §19.3, [Section 9.7](../../gemini4/book/ch-09/sec-09-07.md) §9.7.1.
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Turbidity Stretching, Lutoclines, & Shallow Deconvolution Audit:** Compare deep (`> 1.5 Z_SD`) and nearshore (`< 1.0 m`) bathymetric residuals against contemporaneous field CTD, optical backscatter, and Secchi records to separate true pulse-stretch bias or lutocline false bottoms from incomplete surface-bottom deconvolution. — *Threshold:* Zero unattributed `> 0.15 m` bias step across the `< 1.0 m` deconvolution transition or across high-$K_d$ zones. — *Evidence:* Residual-vs-$K_d$ and `< 1.0 m` Deconvolution Audit Plot. — *Ref:* [Section 9.5](../../gemini4/book/ch-09/sec-09-05.md) §9.5.3–9.5.4, [Section 9.7](../../gemini4/book/ch-09/sec-09-07.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** **Submerged Vegetation (`SAV`) & Intertidal Marsh Elevation Audit:** (*Trigger:* presence of seagrass, kelp beds, or saltmarsh in AOI) where ALB overestimates seabed elevation relative to high-frequency MBES over seagrass/kelp or overestimates marsh substrate relative to RTK-GNSS marsh plates (`+0.10–0.45 m` `LEAN` bias in dense *Spartina* marsh), document vertical offset separately in metadata/VVA reporting rather than distorting regional boresight parameters. — *Threshold:* Separate VVA/SAV offset statistics reported; regional bare-earth/sand boresight untouched. — *Evidence:* SAV & *Spartina* Marsh Stratum Bias Appendix. — *Ref:* [Chapter 34](../../fable/chapters/ch34-water-in-dems.md) §34.8, [Section 9.7](../../gemini4/book/ch-09/sec-09-07.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** **Land–Water Seam & Datum Step Closure:** Document residual discontinuity across the shoreline/white-ribbon stitch (`<= 0.05–0.10 m` `RMSE` target), identifying whether any remaining step originates from VDatum SEP interpolation error (`5–20 cm` `1σ` in estuaries), beach-face morphodynamics between tide states, or green–NIR boresight residual. — *Threshold:* Shoreline step magnitude attributed and logged against VDatum SEP stated uncertainty. — *Evidence:* Shoreline Cross-Section & VDatum Residual Diagnostic. — *Ref:* [Chapter 9](../../fable/chapters/ch09-vertical-datums.md) §9.7, [Chapter 48](../../fable/chapters/ch48-compositing.md) §48.6.
- [ ] **[REC]** **Void & Extinction Fraction Accounting (`Class 45`):** Map spatial extent and primary physical driver (`K_d > threshold` river plume, breaking-wave foam, kelp canopy, or depth $> d_{\max}$) for every **LAS Class 45** extinction polygon so repeat-survey or acoustic gap-fill tasking targets actual blind spots cleanly. — *Threshold:* `100 %` Class 45 / void polygons attributed to physical cause (`%` area breakdown). — *Evidence:* Optical Extinction & Gap-Attribution Polygon Layer. — *Ref:* [Chapter 19](../../fable/chapters/ch19-bathymetric-lidar.md) §19.4, [Chapter 35](../../fable/chapters/ch35-voids-and-overhangs.md).

## 3. Mandatory 14–30 Day Post-Demob After-Action Review (AAR)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Hold Multi-Disciplinary AAR Within 14–30 Days of Demobilization:** Convene PIC, LiDAR operator, LSO, lead processor, vessel/ground-truth lead, and project manager using [template-aar-retrospective.md](../_shared/template-aar-retrospective.md) to produce signed closeout metrics across all four required operational axes (`[ ] AAR Signed & Archived`). — *Threshold:* Signed AAR archive stored in `BagIt` / Git campaign folder within `14–30 days` of demob, with crew PII redacted before shared publication. — *Evidence:* Signed [template-aar-retrospective.md](../_shared/template-aar-retrospective.md) Packet. — *Ref:* [template-aar-retrospective.md](../_shared/template-aar-retrospective.md), [Chapter 56](../../fable/chapters/ch56-case-files.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Axis 1 — Schedule & Water-Clarity Climatology Variance:** Compare pre-survey MODIS/VIIRS/Sentinel-2 $K_d$ and ICESat-2 climatology forecasts against realized Secchi depth, wind/swell abort hours, `30–45°` solar-window utilization, and tidal-phase alignment; log trigger criteria that proved too permissive or too conservative. — *Threshold:* Predicted vs. realized flyable days, abort hours, and clarity-window capture quantified cleanly. — *Evidence:* Schedule & Clarity Variance Table in AAR. — *Ref:* [Chapter 26](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md) §26.9, [Section 13.2](../../gemini4/book/ch-13/sec-13-02.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Axis 2 — Cost, Standby Ledger, & Cloud-Storage Variance:** Reconcile [template-cost-ledger.md](../_shared/template-cost-ledger.md) planned vs. actual mobilization/ferry cost (`> 50 %` mob driver on small projects), standby-day burn rate, realized unit cost (`$/km²` [`$/mi²`]), MBES/ground-truth boat standby, and recurring cloud storage/egress (`~$0.02/GB-mo` hot/standard vs. `~$0.001–$0.004/GB-mo` cold archive + `~$0.05–$0.09/GB` egress). — *Threshold:* Variance `> 10 %` in any cost category assigned explicit root cause and updated unit-rate benchmark. — *Evidence:* Reconciled [template-cost-ledger.md](../_shared/template-cost-ledger.md) Variance Sheet. — *Ref:* [template-cost-ledger.md](../_shared/template-cost-ledger.md), [Chapter 28](../../fable/chapters/ch28-reducing-cost.md) §28.1, §28.6.
- [ ] **[REC]** **Axis 3 — Safety, Laser Geofence, Overwater Equipment, & Near-Misses:** Audit every laser geofence shutter trip, ATC airspace/TFR coordination delay, `14 CFR § 91.509` survival-gear inspection finding, vessel/shore team incident, and crew fatigue metric; convert every near-miss into a preventive engineering or procedural control under blameless safety culture. — *Threshold:* `100 %` near-misses and shutter trips assigned preventive controls. — *Evidence:* Safety & Near-Miss Closeout Register. — *Ref:* [template-aar-retrospective.md](../_shared/template-aar-retrospective.md), [Chapter 68](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** **Axis 4 — Technical Performance (`d_max`, Density, & TPU):** Benchmark achieved `d_max / Z_SD` ratio (target `1.5–3.0 Z_SD`), benthic point spacing vs. `2–8 pts/m²` plan, shallow `< 1.0 m` deconvolution completeness, and S-44 / USGS LBS compliance rate. — *Threshold:* Achieved vs. budgeted capability logged by substrate and turbidity class. — *Evidence:* Technical Performance Summary Card in AAR. — *Ref:* [Chapter 19](../../fable/chapters/ch19-bathymetric-lidar.md), [Section 9.2](../../gemini4/book/ch-09/sec-09-02.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Checklist & SOP Revision Loop (Git Diff / PR Within 30 Days):** Open a versioned PR against `checklists/coastal-bathylidar/` (and/or [../_shared/](../_shared/template-aar-retrospective.md)) updating site-calibrated $K_d$ stop-go bounds, aircraft endurance/cost rules, or QA scripts for every unflagged defect, near-miss, or systematic error discovered during the campaign (`[ ] PR Merged / Closed`). — *Threshold:* Merged checklist PR (or documented zero-change confirmation) archived `<= 30 days` post-demob. — *Evidence:* Merged Git Commit / PR Reference in AAR. — *Ref:* [template-aar-retrospective.md](../_shared/template-aar-retrospective.md), [Chapter 50](../../fable/chapters/ch50-archiving-and-provenance.md).
