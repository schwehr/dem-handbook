Immediate life-safety response, optical radiation containment, forensic data freeze, and statutory reporting workflows for coastal airborne bathymetric LiDAR (ALB) operations. Any gate below stops normal survey flying immediately until closed by the Pilot in Command (PIC), Laser Safety Officer (LSO), or Accountable Manager. ## 1. Life Safety & Immediate Aircraft / Optical Containment

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Aircraft Ditching or Off-Airport Forced Landing (Coastal / Surf / Marsh):** Execute emergency checklist, squawk `7700`, transmit Mayday (`121.5 MHz` or active ATC frequency) with GPS position and persons on board (POB), shut off master avionics and laser power prior to water impact, don **14 CFR § 91.509** wearable PFD with survivor locator light, deploy life raft + survival kit + **406 MHz ELT** / personal PLB once clear of the aircraft, and account for all flight and LiDAR operator crew before attempting shore egress through surf (`[ ] Evacuated / SAR Notified` `[ ] N/A`). — *Threshold:* Zero unconfirmed POB; laser master power isolated before water impact; ELT/PLB distress active until USCG / SAR pickup. — *Evidence:* PIC Ditching Log, SAR Case Number, & POB Headcount Sheet. — *Ref:* [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md), [template-accident-card.md](../_shared/template-accident-card.md), [Chapter 16](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Unintended 532 nm or 1,064 nm Laser Exposure or Cockpit Illumination Report:** Immediately trip the physical laser master-kill switch and verify hardware shutter closure upon any suspected unintended exposure (ground observer, vessel bridge, beachgoer, nearby aircraft cockpit, or upward specular reflection off calm water/vessel glass); log all parameters required to reconstruct corneal radiant exposure against **ANSI Z136.1 / Z136.6** MPE, and refer any exposed individual (or anyone reporting flash blindness, afterimage, or eye discomfort) for **urgent dilated ophthalmologic / retinal OCT examination on the same calendar day (`< 24 h` urgent clinical/LSO policy target)** (`[ ] Contained & Referred` `[ ] N/A`). — *Threshold:* Capture exact **UTC time, lat/lon, AGL altitude, ground speed, per-pulse energy $E_p$ (`mJ`), pulse width $\tau_p$ (`ns`), PRF (`kHz`) per active channel (`532` deep/shallow, `1,064`), beam exit diameter $D_0$ (`mm`) & divergence $\beta_t$ (`mrad`), Palmer scan rate $f_{\text{scan}}$ (`Hz`), aircraft attitude $(\phi,\theta,\psi)$, shutter state, and observer viewing mode (direct vs. calm-water/glass specular; unaided vs. `7x50` binoculars)**; compute worst-case corneal irradiance/fluence vs. ANSI MPE. — *Evidence:* Signed LSO Ocular Exposure Reconstruction Sheet & Masked Clinical Referral Receipt. — *Ref:* [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md), [Section 9.2](../../gemini4/book/ch-09/sec-09-02.md), [Chapter 19](../../fable/chapters/ch19-bathymetric-lidar.md) §19.2.
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Mid-Air Collision, Near-Miss (NMAC), Bird Strike, or Airspace / TFR Excursion:** Clear conflict immediately, inspect flight controls and structural/window integrity, declare emergency and divert if any control anomaly, windscreen damage, or dual-engine ingestion is suspected, and file ATC / NASA ASRS / FSDO event record immediately upon landing (`[ ] Cleared / Landed Safely` `[ ] N/A`). — *Threshold:* Zero continuation of survey passes with suspected structural, windshield, or engine-ingestion damage; horizontal/vertical conflict distance and transponder/ADS-B tracks preserved. — *Evidence:* PIC Occurrence Log, Maintenance Inspection Release, & ASRS/ATC Receipt. — *Ref:* [template-accident-card.md](../_shared/template-accident-card.md), [Chapter 26](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md) §26.8.
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Hard Landing, Belly-Window / Optical Port Impact, Tail Strike, or Cabin Depressurization Shock:** Ground the aircraft and quarantine the ALB sensor pod immediately; inspect dual-band `532 nm / 1,064 nm` belly glass for hairline cracks, delamination, pressure-seal loss, or mount shear-pin/witness-mark displacement, extract IMU peak-$g$ / gyro-saturation logs, and **invalidate all calibration and boresight parameters** (`0°` / angular drift risk, per [sec-07-03.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-03.md) and [ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md)) until a certified Part 43 airframe/STC inspection and post-repair pressurized $\Delta P$ `532/1,064 nm` co-alignment & boresight flight verify airworthiness and optical alignment (`[ ] Quarantined & Inspected` `[ ] N/A`). — *Threshold:* Zero survey dispatch after exceedance/impact without signed Part 43 IA return-to-service and new boresight verification (`<= 0.1–0.2 mrad` co-alignment; `< 0.005°` boresight closure). — *Evidence:* Airframe IA Return-to-Service Entry, IMU Shock Extract, & Re-Boresight Report. — *Ref:* [Section 7.3](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-03.md) §7.3.3, [Chapter 25](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md) §25.6.
- [ ] **[COND]** **In-Flight Payload Rack Smoke, Thermal Runaway, or Chiller Coolant Leak:** (*Trigger:* smoke, electrical odor, chiller fault, or battery overheating inside survey cabin) isolate payload bus breakers immediately, turn off laser diode/pump power, execute aircraft smoke/fire checklist using cockpit/cabin Halon/clean-agent extinguisher strictly per AFM (applying water deluge / AVD `Lith-EX` cooling on accessible Li-ion packs after landing, reserving `Class D` strictly for primary lithium-metal `UN 3090`), divert to nearest suitable coastal airfield, and prohibit re-energizing the rack until OEM diagnostics clear all HV/glycol circuits. — *Threshold:* Master payload bus isolated within `< 30 s` of smoke/thermal indication; aircraft diverted immediately. — *Evidence:* Flight-Deck Emergency Log & OEM Rack Inspection Clearance. — *Ref:* [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md), [Chapter 16](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** **Coastal Boat / Shore Ground-Truth Team Incident:** (*Trigger:* capsizing, prop strike, hypothermia, heat stroke, lone-worker check-in failure, or rising-tide surf entrapment involving the Secchi/CTD vessel or GNSS checkpoint crew) halt aerial sorties overhead if distraction or SAR airspace conflict (`TFR`) arises, trigger USCG (`VHF Ch 16 [voice] / Ch 70 [DSC]`) / `911` emergency response, and execute vessel casualty log. — *Threshold:* Aircraft clears low-level survey block if SAR rotorcraft approach; missing-crew overdue protocol triggers within `<= 15–30 min` of missed check-in. — *Evidence:* Vessel/Shore Casualty Log & SAR Dispatch Timestamp. — *Ref:* [template-accident-card.md](../_shared/template-accident-card.md), [Chapter 68](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** **GNSS Jamming, Spoofing, or Unrecoverable In-Flight Data Loss:** (*Trigger:* simultaneous multi-constellation $C/N_0$ collapse, AGC spike, time jump `> 1 ms`, or sudden position jump) drop to INS dead-reckoning / pilotage navigation immediately, turn off active survey acquisition if spoofed triggers endanger geofenced laser shuttering, clear the interference corridor, and log RF anomaly coordinates for FAA/FCC reporting. — *Threshold:* Laser inhibit verified whenever GNSS position error threatens geofenced no-fire zones; unverified lines quarantined from production. — *Evidence:* RF/AGC Forensic Log & Quarantine Line List. — *Ref:* [Chapter 56](../../fable/chapters/ch56-case-files.md) §56.19, [Chapter 69](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md) §69.1.4.

## 2. Evidence Preservation & Forensic Chain of Custody

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Immediate Telemetry, Waveform, and Media Freeze:** Within **2 hours** of safe recovery (or immediately upon crew rescue), seal and write-protect all removable survey SSDs, digitizer waveform drives, IMU/GNSS raw files, laser geofence/shutter state logs, cockpit/operator audio-video, and STC/maintenance logbooks; compute SHA-256 hashes and transfer read-only forensic copies under **`FRE 901 / 902(13)–(14)`** authentication rules using [template-accident-card.md](../_shared/template-accident-card.md), while segregating **medical/PII records** (retinal OCT exams, crew health files) in restricted-access custody. — *Threshold:* `100 %` raw media hashed with `sha256sum` prior to analysis; zero in-place modification; medical PII stripped from general operational/engineering shares. — *Evidence:* Signed Forensic Custody Log, `SHA256SUMS.txt`, & Restricted Medical Envelope Receipt. — *Ref:* [template-accident-card.md](../_shared/template-accident-card.md), [Chapter 68](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md) §68.5, [Section 27.1](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-01.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** **Physical Scene & Environmental Snapshot:** Photograph aircraft/sensor orientation, belly-window damage, mount shear pins, cockpit breaker panel, and laser control console prior to movement (unless life safety or tidal immersion requires urgent recovery), and archive simultaneous METAR/TAF, NDBC buoy sea state, tide stage, and Secchi/turbidity logs from the incident hour. — *Threshold:* Timestamped overview + macro photos covering all four corners of sensor mount and belly glass before unbolting payload. — *Evidence:* Timestamped Incident Photo Archive & Environmental Snapshot Folder. — *Ref:* [template-accident-card.md](../_shared/template-accident-card.md), [Chapter 25](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** **ATC Radar/Audio & Airspace Hold Preservation:** Request preservation of ATC voice recordings, ADS-B tracks, NOTAM/TFR effective timestamps, and FAA Form 7140-1 / DoD Laser Clearinghouse (LCH) transmission logs covering `30 min` before and after the event timestamp. — *Threshold:* Written ATC/FBO data-preservation request dispatched before routine radar/voice loop purge. — *Evidence:* Sent ATC Preservation Request & LCH/7140-1 Copy. — *Ref:* [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md), [Chapter 26](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md) §26.8.
- [ ] **[REC]** **Human Factors & Crew Medical / Duty Log:** Record flight- and duty-time totals, hypoxia/oxygen log (`14 CFR § 91.211`), last rest period, pre-flight briefing notes, and factual crew statements separately from company PR or speculative root-cause narratives, protecting crew medical/privacy records under restricted access. — *Threshold:* Completed within `<= 24 h` of event while recall is intact; medical PII stored offline/encrypted with HR/LSO only. — *Evidence:* Sealed Crew Duty/Factual Narrative Packet. — *Ref:* [Section 27.2](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-02.md), [Chapter 68](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md).

## 3. Statutory Notification Clocks & Agency Matrix

- [ ] **[GATE]** **NTSB Immediate Notification (`49 CFR § 830.5`) & Written Report (`§ 830.15`):** Notify the NTSB Response Operations Center (**`844-373-9922` / `202-314-6290`**) **immediately and by the most expeditious means** for any aircraft accident (`49 CFR § 830.2` fatality, serious injury, or substantial damage—including UAS holding an airworthiness certificate/approval) or listed `§ 830.5` incident (flight-control malfunction, crew incapacity, in-flight fire, mid-air collision, turbine rotor failure, or `> $25,000` [lower of repair cost or fair market value] damage to property *other than the aircraft*); file **NTSB Form 6120.1/2** within **10 calendar days** (`[ ] Filed` `[ ] Non-U.S. ICAO Annex 13 Equivalent` `[ ] Non-NTSB Event Documented`). — *Threshold:* Immediate phone notification (`0 h`) + written Form 6120.1/2 filed `<= 10 calendar days` (or equivalent host-state Civil Aviation Authority under **ICAO Annex 13** outside the U.S.). — *Evidence:* NTSB Case ID & Timestamped Form 6120.1/2 Submission Receipt. — *Ref:* [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md), [template-accident-card.md](../_shared/template-accident-card.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** **FAA Small-UAS Accident Notice (`14 CFR § 107.9`):** (*Trigger:* ALB or coastal reconnaissance flight operated under 14 CFR Part 107 `< 25 kg`) report to the FAA within **10 calendar days** after any operation involving serious injury, loss of consciousness, or damage (`> $500`, lower of repair cost or fair market value) to any property other than the UA. — *Threshold:* Report submitted via FAA DroneZone / FSDO within `<= 10 calendar days` (in addition to `§ 830.2` immediate call if airworthiness-certificated). — *Evidence:* FAA DroneZone / FSDO Report Confirmation. — *Ref:* [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md), [Section 27.1](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-01.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **FAA FSDO, AC 70-1B Laser Incident, and DoD LCH Notice:** Notify the local **FAA Flight Standards District Office (FSDO)** immediately alongside any NTSB trigger or STC airworthiness event, report any unintended laser illumination of aircraft/public under **FAA AC 70-1B** / **Form 7140-1** procedures, and notify the **DoD Laser Clearinghouse (LCH)** (`0 min–2 h` SOP/cleared-window target) if firing occurred inside or adjacent to a cleared LCH window. — *Threshold:* Verbal notice to FSDO/ATC immediately upon unintended aircraft illumination; LCH notified per active-window blackout SOP. — *Evidence:* FSDO Event Reference & LCH Incident Log. — *Ref:* [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md), [Chapter 68](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **LSO & OSHA Occupational Injury / Laser Exposure Clock (`29 CFR § 1904.39`):** Notify the company Laser Safety Officer (**LSO**) (`< 1 h` company SOP target) for any `532 nm` or `1,064 nm` ocular/skin exposure event; inside U.S. OSHA jurisdiction (noting USCG statutory preemption on USCG-inspected commercial vessels), report work-related fatalities to OSHA (**`800-321-6742`**) within **8 hours** and inpatient hospitalizations, amputations, or **loss of an eye** within **24 hours**, documenting incident fluence vs. ANSI Z136.1/Z136.6 MPE while protecting employee medical privacy. — *Threshold:* `< 1 h` LSO notice; `<= 8 h` fatality / `<= 24 h` hospitalization or eye-loss OSHA report. — *Evidence:* LSO Incident Ticket & OSHA Case Confirmation (`if triggered`). — *Ref:* [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md), [Section 27.2](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-02.md).
- [ ] **[COND]** **USCG Marine Casualty (`46 CFR § 4.05-1/10` Form CG-2692, `§ 4.06` SMI Testing) & NRC Spill (`33 CFR § 153.203`):** (*Trigger:* ditching, vessel collision, boat-based Secchi/CTD/checkpoint casualty, or coastal fuel/oil/glycol release; outside U.S. waters, notify coastal **MRCC** and flag state) notify the USCG Sector/COTP **immediately** and submit **Form CG-2692** within **5 days** (`> $75,000` vessel damage, loss of propulsion/seaworthiness, grounding, missing person, or injury beyond first aid); initiate **46 CFR § 4.06** Serious Marine Incident alcohol (**`<= 2 h`**) and drug (**`<= 32 h`**) testing when triggered; notify the National Response Center (**`800-424-8802`**) **immediately** for any coastal oil/fuel/chiller-glycol discharge. — *Threshold:* Immediate verbal SAR/NRC call; `<= 2 h` / `<= 32 h` SMI testing; `<= 5 days` written CG-2692. — *Evidence:* USCG/NRC Case Number & Submitted Form CG-2692. — *Ref:* [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md), [template-accident-card.md](../_shared/template-accident-card.md).
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Aviation Hull, Liability, Overwater & E&O Insurer Notice:** Transmit formal written incident notice to the aviation hull/liability/overwater broker, payload inland-marine underwriter, and survey Errors & Omissions (E&O) carrier within the policy COI clock (commonly **`24–72 h`**; verify exact policy notice wording in Phase 2), and prohibit unilateral admission of fault or unauthorized repair before adjuster sign-off (`[ ] Notified` `[ ] N/A`). — *Threshold:* Written notice delivered within contract COI notification deadline with `0` unauthorized repairs or liability admissions. — *Evidence:* Sent Broker/Underwriter Notice & Claim Acknowledgment Number. — *Ref:* [template-insurance-coi.md](../_shared/template-insurance-coi.md), [Section 27.1](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-01.md) §27.1.1.
- [ ] **[COND]** **Personal / Medical PII Breach Notification (`GDPR Art. 33`):** (*Trigger:* unauthorized disclosure, loss, or unencrypted exposure of retinal-OCT/casualty medical PII, crew personnel files, or identifiable coastal curtilage/PII imagery under GDPR or applicable breach statute) quarantine affected media/accounts immediately and notify the competent **supervisory authority within `<= 72 h`** under **GDPR Art. 33** (`Regulation [EU] 2016/679`), notifying affected individuals without undue delay where high risk applies. — *Threshold:* Forensic access freeze `< 2 h`; written supervisory-authority notification `<= 72 h` of becoming aware of personal-data breach (`[ ] Notified` `[ ] N/A`). — *Evidence:* Timestamped Supervisory-Authority Breach Notice & PII Quarantine Receipt. — *Ref:* [09 External Register](09-resources-and-templates.md), [template-accident-card.md](../_shared/template-accident-card.md), [Section 27.2](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-02.md).
- [ ] **[REC]** **Client / Contracting Officer & Coastal Land-Manager Notification:** Brief the contracting officer / COR (NOAA, USACE, USGS, state agency, or tribal liaison) within `24 h` (project SOP) of any survey-halting accident, airspace violation, marine-mammal/shorebird disturbance complaint, or privacy/curtilage security breach ([ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md)), and suspend public distribution of affected imagery/raw point clouds until cleared. — *Threshold:* Written COR brief delivered `<= 24 h`; distribution embargo active on affected blocks. — *Evidence:* Sent COR Briefing Email & Data Embargo Flag. — *Ref:* [Chapter 69](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md) §69.5, [Section 27.2](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-02.md).

## 4. Return-to-Operations Clearance Gate

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Formal Accident Closeout & Airworthiness / Calibration Sign-Off:** Resume coastal ALB survey operations only after written release from FAA FSDO / Part 43 IA (if aircraft or belly-glass/STC was affected), LSO closure of any ANSI Z136.1/Z136.6 corrective action, crew medical/fitness clearance, and execution of a fresh pre-survey calibration & boresight flight confirming roll/pitch/yaw offsets and `532 nm / 1,064 nm` range alignment within spec. — *Threshold:* Zero open airworthiness, LSO, or boresight hold flags prior to next production sortie. — *Evidence:* Signed Return-to-Operations Release & Post-Incident Boresight Verification Report. — *Ref:* [Chapter 25](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md) §25.6, [Chapter 56](../../fable/chapters/ch56-case-files.md).
