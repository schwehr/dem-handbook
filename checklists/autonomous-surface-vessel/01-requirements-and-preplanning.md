## 1. Survey Requirements, CRS & Vertical Reduction Strategy

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Lock governing bathymetric standard, coverage class, and sign convention.** Record exact edition (**IHO S-44 Ed. 6.2.0 [Oct 2024; core Table 1 TVU/THU $a/b$ values unchanged from Ed. 6.1.0, adding §3.8 seabed characteristics]**, NOAA HSSD [2024 edition, restructured; annual updates], or USACE EM 1110-2-1003 [2013]), order/class, required feature-detection cube size ($0.5\text{ m}$, $1.0\text{ m}$, or $2.0\text{ m}$), and sign convention ($+d \downarrow$ m for S-44/S-102 Ed. 3.0.0 soundings vs. $+z \uparrow$ m for BAG 2.0.1/CUDEM elevations). — *Threshold:* Zero unit ambiguity (SI meters primary; if a legacy USACE contract cites Survey/International feet, explicitly log `1 US ft = 1200/3937 m` vs. `0.3048 m` factor and convert all deliverables to SI primary). — *Evidence:* Signed project header matching [`template-project-header.md`](../_shared/template-project-header.md). — *Ref:* [Ch. 70 Specifications Guided Tour](../../fable/chapters/ch70-specifications-guided-tour.md) · [Ch. 56 Units & Datum Case Files](../../fable/chapters/ch56-case-files.md)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Fix Horizontal CRS, epoch, and velocity model (avoiding `float32` truncation).** Document EPSG code, tectonic plate reference frame (e.g., `ITRF2020`, `NAD83(2011) epoch 2010.0`, `ETRS89`), projection vs. geographic coordinates, and dynamic epoch transformation parameters if multi-year repeat surveys are planned. — *Threshold:* All grid, BAG, S-102, and trajectory processing pipelines store coordinates in `float64` (never `float32`, whose ~24-bit mantissa truncates UTM northing/easting coordinates by $0.2\text{–}0.5\text{ m}$). — *Evidence:* CRS & PROJ pipeline definition string archived in project metadata. — *Ref:* [Ch. 8 Horizontal Datums](../../fable/chapters/ch08-horizontal-datums.md) · [Ch. 47 File Formats](../../fable/chapters/ch47-file-formats.md)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Execute the Ellipsoidally Referenced Survey (ERS) vs. Water-Level (Tide) Decision Tree.** Choose ERS (`GNSS + RTS SBET -> SEP/VDatum/VORF -> Chart Datum`) or zoned water-level gauges (`Dynamic Draft + Heave + Measured Tide -> Chart Datum`) and document the combined vertical datum uncertainty budget before hardware procurement. — *Threshold:* Select **ERS** whenever VDatum/VORF/AusCoastVDT SEP uncertainty ($\sigma_{\text{SEP}}$) is $\le 0.08\text{ m}$ ($1\sigma$ offshore) to $\le 0.12\text{ m}$ ($1\sigma$ coastal) OR when real-time RTK/PPP-AR + SBET post-processing is available; switch to or add **co-located temporary tide gauges** (see decision tree below) in unmodeled estuaries ($\sigma_{\text{SEP}} > 0.15\text{ m}$ $1\sigma$), braided tidal rivers, or strict USACE hard-bottom dredging payment surveys. — *Evidence:* Signed ERS vs. Tide trade-study memo with SEP model version and grid hash pinned. — *Ref:* [Ch. 9 Vertical Datums](../../fable/chapters/ch09-vertical-datums.md) · [Ch. 26 Survey Planning](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md)

### ERS vs. Tide Reduction Decision Tree (Offline Reference)

```text
START: Can the ASV achieve >= 98% valid GNSS RTK / PPP-AR + post-processed SBET (< 0.05 m 1-sigma ellipsoid height)?
  |-- NO (e.g., heavy bridge/cliff/fjord shadowing, high Kp >= 5 scintillation, or contested RF/GNSS jamming)
  |     --> MANDATORY WATER-LEVEL REDUCTION: Real-time heave (or delayed TrueHeave) + static/dynamic draft + verified tide gauge(s) / TCARI.
  |-- YES --> Is a vetted, verified Separation Model (VDatum / VORF / AusCoastVDT / local GGXF) available with sigma_SEP <= 0.10 m (1-sigma)?
        |-- NO (unmodeled estuary, remote Arctic/Pacific atoll, steep river gradient, or contract requires gauge-only)
        |     --> DEPLOY TEMPORARY TIDE GAUGE (>= 3 leveled benchmarks [5 recommended], IOC Manual 14) + simultaneous GNSS@TG tie to establish local SEP.
        |-- YES --> Are you in a strict S-44 Exclusive / USACE hard-bottom <= 0.15 m (95%) dredging tolerance or complex river mouth?
              |-- YES --> HYBRID MODE: Run ERS as primary reduction AND maintain 1+ local tide gauge as independent cross-check (residual < 0.05 m RMSE).
              |-- NO  --> FULL ERS MODE: GNSS-INS SBET ellipsoid height -> pinned SEP grid (verify sign: z_CD = h - SEP; d_CD = SEP - h; check PROJ +proj=vgridshift!).
```

## 2. Environmental Reconnaissance, Traffic Climatology & Risk Screening

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Acquire and inspect prior bathymetry, chart hazards, and GEBCO Type Identifier (TID) coverage.** Compile ENC coverage (note CATZOC A1–D/U ratings), NOAA CUDEM / BlueTopo / regional multibeam archives, wreck/obstruction AWOIS/ENC point lists, and GEBCO TID metadata (`TID 10`–`17` direct measurements vs. `TID 40` satellite-altimetry-predicted, `TID 41` computer-interpolated, and `TID 44` bathymetric/hybrid grids). — *Threshold:* Zero reliance on predicted or interpolated bathymetry (`TID 40` / `TID 41`) for minimum USV under-keel clearance (UKC); mandate $\ge 1.5\text{ m}$ safety UKC above shoalest charted feature or unknown `CATZOC C/D/U` zones for semi-submersible/ocean USVs, and shoreline visual spotter/slow speed ($<1.5\text{ m/s}$) for micro-USVs inside the $2\text{ m}$ contour. — *Evidence:* GIS hazard overlay package loaded into mission planner and ROC electronic chart display. — *Ref:* [Ch. 62 Navigation & Charting](../../fable/chapters/ch62-navigation-and-charting.md) · [Sec. 24.2 Key Public DEM Products & TID](../../gemini4/book/ch-24/sec-24-02.md)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Quantify AIS marine traffic density, fishing-gear windows, and port traffic patterns.** Pull historical 12-month AIS heatmaps, commercial ferry/tug schedules, naval/military range closures, and local trap/pot/gillnet fishing seasons across the operational polygon. — *Threshold:* Under this checklist's operational CONOPS screening rule, any corridor exceeding $10\text{ AIS transits/day/km}^2$, ferry crossing lanes, or active crab/lobster buoy fields prohibits Tier 3 OTH unsupervised autonomy and mandates Tier 1 Attended or Tier 2 Monitored watch with active chase-boat or fisheries liaison clearance. — *Evidence:* AIS heatmap layer + seasonal fishery conflict calendar attached to CONOPS. — *Ref:* [Ch. 68 Legal Issues](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md) · [Phase 09 External Regulatory Register](./09-resources-and-templates.md#e-external-regulatory--operational-basis-register-non-handbook-sources)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Model sea-state, swell, surf, and weather-standby climatology.** Extract 10+ years of NDBC buoy / ERA5 / WW3 wave height ($H_s$), dominant period ($T_p$), tidal currents, wind speed, and river discharge for the planned survey months. — *Threshold:* Verify expected workable days versus hull sea-state limits (micro-USV typically $H_s \le 0.5\text{–}0.8\text{ m}$; semi-submersible DriX $H_s \le 2.0\text{–}2.5\text{ m}$; ocean USV $H_s \le 4.0\text{ m}$ operation / survival higher) and budget **30–50 % weather standby** for temperate coastal winter/spring windows so project schedule does not force unsafe launch decisions. — *Evidence:* Monthly $H_s / T_p$ exceedance percentile plot and weather-days budget line in cost ledger. — *Ref:* [Ch. 26 Survey Planning](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md) · [Ch. 28 Reducing Cost](../../fable/chapters/ch28-reducing-cost.md)

- [ ] **[REC]** **Screen GNSS space weather, ionospheric scintillation, and RF interference climatology.** Check NOAA SWPC 27-day outlook and known GPS/GNSS interference corridors (ports, naval bases, border seas). — *Threshold:* Flag windows with forecast geomagnetic index $K_p \ge 5$ or active GPS L1/L2 interference advisories; verify multi-constellation (GPS + Galileo + GLONASS/BeiDou) + Galileo OSNMA or dual-frequency anti-spoof receiver capability and inertial dead-reckoning bridge durations. — *Evidence:* SWPC outlook log and GNSS receiver interference-mitigation spec sheet. — *Ref:* [Ch. 12 GNSS](../../fable/chapters/ch12-gnss.md) · [Ch. 69 Security & GNSS Interference](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md)

- [ ] **[COND]** **Define coastal acoustic, bio-fouling, and marine-mammal seasonal closures** (*Trigger:* surveys inside critical marine habitats, pinniped haul-outs, estuarine submerged aquatic vegetation, or near whale migration corridors). — *Threshold:* Map seasonal exclusion buffers (typically $100\text{–}500\text{ m}$ from haul-outs/rookeries), verify MBES frequency ($200\text{–}400\text{ kHz}$ exceeds odontocete/mysticete peak hearing vs. $12\text{–}34\text{ kHz}$ deep sonar or sub-bottom profilers requiring soft-start and Protected Species Observers [PSOs]), and establish invasive-species washdown zones between waterbodies. — *Evidence:* Environmental compliance screening matrix attached to permits file. — *Ref:* [Ch. 69 Security, Sovereignty & Ethics](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md)

## 3. Platform Selection, CONOPS Tiering & Operational Governance

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Select ASV hull class and lock CONOPS supervision tier per operational sub-zone.** Match vessel regime (micro-catamaran, semi-submersible, or endurance ocean USV) to shallowest water depth, sea state, endurance, and payload weight/power, then assign **Tier 1 (Attended)**, **Tier 2 (Monitored)**, or **Tier 3 (OTH)** boundaries on the chart. — *Threshold:* Every sub-zone transition waypoint has explicit link-health, AIS/EO-IR visibility, and chase-boat range criteria ($\le 500\text{ m}$ in Tier 1; $\le 2\text{–}4\text{ h}$ tow/intercept radius in Tier 2; vetted emergency port-of-refuge plan in Tier 3). — *Evidence:* Signed CONOPS zone polygon chart and platform capability compliance matrix. — *Ref:* [Ch. 16 Platforms](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md) · [Ch. 26 Survey Planning](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Establish critical-spares kit, MTBF readiness, and weather-abandonment / abort thresholds.** Define on-site spare inventory (propellers/thrusters, strut seals, LiFePO₄ cells/chargers, spare GNSS/RF antennas, wet-mate cables, backup SVS/SVP probe, e-stop fobs) and quantitative **weather-abandonment** and **technical-abort** rules before leaving port. — *Threshold:* Zero single-point-of-failure sensors lacking either a hot/cold spare on site or a <48 h courier/air-freight replenishment SLA; abort mobilization/survey if continuous unworkable standby consumes >100 % of budgeted weather reserve without client change-order sign-off. — *Evidence:* Critical spares manifest verified physically packed; signed Go/Abort decision matrix. — *Ref:* [Ch. 28 Reducing Cost](../../fable/chapters/ch28-reducing-cost.md) · [Ch. 26 Survey Planning](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md)

- [ ] **[COND]** **Coordinate multi-modal "white-ribbon" intertidal synchronization** (*Trigger:* nearshore coastal DEM projects stitching USV multibeam with airborne topobathy lidar, coastal drone lidar, or RTK beach walkers). — *Threshold:* Define an explicit spatial overlap zone (**100–500 m cross-shore width**, spanning $\ge 2\text{ m}$ vertical overlap between MLLW and MHW) where USV operates on spring **high tide** while airborne/drone lidar captures exposed flats on spring **low tide** within $\le 3\text{–}7\text{ days}$ (before significant storm morphodynamics); lock common epoch and datum ($+z \uparrow$ in merged product). — *Evidence:* Joint multi-modal tidal-window calendar signed by USV and lidar leads matching [`multimodal-white-ribbon.md`](../_shared/multimodal-white-ribbon.md). — *Ref:* [Ch. 34 Water in DEMs](../../fable/chapters/ch34-water-in-dems.md) · [Sec. 13.2 Environmental & Temporal Scheduling](../../gemini4/book/ch-13/sec-13-02.md)
