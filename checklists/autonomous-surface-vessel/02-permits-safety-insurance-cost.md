## PART A — Pre-Mobilization Administration (Permits, Hazmat, Insurance & Cost)

### A.1 Maritime Clearances, COLREGS Equipment & Spectrum Licensing

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Submit USCG Sector/COTP CONOPS packet and activate Local Notice to Mariners (LNM) + Harbor Master permit.** File ASV/USV Concept of Operations under **USCG Work Instruction 5P-WI-002(1)** (*Oversight of Unmanned, Autonomous, and Remote-Control Operations*) with the cognizant USCG Sector / Captain of the Port (COTP) and Vessel Traffic Service (VTS), secure Harbor Master written authorization for restricted channels, and confirm active LNM publication. — *Threshold:* Submit COTP CONOPS package $\ge 30\text{ days}$ pre-mob and LNM request $\ge 14\text{ days}$ prior (operational planning lead times); verify active LNM text lists USV call sign, MMSI, hull color, lighting, survey polygon, working VHF channel, and 24/7 ROC phone number before leaving pier. — *Evidence:* Signed COTP/Harbor Master letter of no-objection + PDF screenshot of published USCG LNM. — *Ref:* [Phase 09 External Regulatory Register](./09-resources-and-templates.md#e-external-regulatory--operational-basis-register-non-handbook-sources) · [Ch. 68 Legal Issues](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Verify COLREGS Rules 5–8 & 23/27 hardware fit-out, MMSI/AIS registration, and RF spectrum licenses.** Confirm physical compliance with **COLREGS (33 CFR Part 81 / Inland 33 CFR Part 83)** lookout, safe speed, risk-of-collision plotting, and visibility standards: Rule 5 (360° EO/IR cameras + low-light thermal + marine X-band radar + Class A or B AIS + optional deck microphone/VHF relay to ROC), Rule 23 power-driven lights (plus **Rule 27(b)** Red-White-Red all-round vertical lights and Ball-Diamond-Ball day shapes when claiming Restricted in Ability to Maneuver [RAM], **Rule 27(c)** when towing, or **Rule 27(d)** for dredging/underwater operations), active X/S-band radar reflector, and valid **FCC Part 80/90** (or national regulator) RF telemetry, VHF, MMSI, and radar station licenses. — *Threshold:* Radar reflector RCS $\ge 10\text{ m}^2$ (X-band); Class A AIS broadcast verified at $2\text{–}10\text{ s}$ dynamic rate; EO/IR optical+thermal visibility $\ge 3.7\text{ km}$ ($2\text{ nmi}$) in clear conditions; never rely on Rule 18/27 stand-on status to delay Rule 8/17 early avoidance. — *Evidence:* FCC/MMSI certificates, dockside night-light photo, AIS receiver capture, and radar target-return screenshot. — *Ref:* [Ch. 16 Platforms](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md) · [Phase 09 External Regulatory Register](./09-resources-and-templates.md#e-external-regulatory--operational-basis-register-non-handbook-sources)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Execute Local Fishery & Mariner Liaison deconfliction.** Contact regional commercial fishing associations, harbor pilots, tug dispatchers, and aquaculture leaseholders to map active fixed gear (crab/lobster pots, longlines, gillnets) and broadcast daily operational schedules on Harbor Working / VHF Ch 13/16 advisories. — *Threshold:* Documented liaison contact $\ge 7\text{ days}$ pre-survey with plotted gear-exclusion polygons loaded into USV mission geofence. — *Evidence:* Outreach log, pilot/fishery acknowledgment emails, and geofence `.geojson`/`.shp`. — *Ref:* [Phase 09 External Regulatory Register](./09-resources-and-templates.md#e-external-regulatory--operational-basis-register-non-handbook-sources) · [Ch. 26 Survey Planning](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md)

- [ ] **[COND]** **Secure UNCLOS Part XIII Marine Scientific Research (MSR) consent and sovereign/port clearances** (*Trigger:* survey enters foreign Territorial Sea [$\le 22.2\text{ km}$ ($12\text{ nmi}$)], Exclusive Economic Zone [EEZ, $\le 370.4\text{ km}$ ($200\text{ nmi}$)], or continental shelf). — *Threshold:* Submit diplomatic MSR consent request **$\ge 6\text{ months}$** ahead per UNCLOS Art. 248 (plan **$\ge 7\text{ months}$** for foreign-ministry routing; noting coastal-state right to participate and mandatory post-cruise data sharing under **Art. 249**); verify whether "hydrographic surveying" vs. "MSR" distinction is accepted or contested by the coastal state before entry. — *Evidence:* Written coastal-state diplomatic clearance / port entry permit carried onboard mothership and filed in ROC. — *Ref:* [Ch. 69 Security & UNCLOS Part XIII](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md) · [Ch. 68 Legal Issues](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md) · [Sec. 27.1 Legal Issues](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-01.md)

- [ ] **[COND]** **Verify Export Controls (ITAR/EAR), National Bathymetry Restrictions, and ATA Carnet Customs** (*Trigger:* international mobilization, foreign-built USV/sonar, or restricted-country borders). — *Threshold:* Screen dual-use Commerce Control List items (**EAR 15 CFR 730–774**: ECCN `6A001` acoustic systems, `7A003` inertial sensors, `7A005` GNSS, `8A001` marine systems) and **ITAR 22 CFR 120–130** military/encrypted sonar exclusions; confirm national high-res bathymetry laws (e.g., India 2021 rules restricting accuracy finer than **`< 10 m`** depth accuracy within $500\text{ m}$ of shore or **`< 100 m`** beyond; China GCJ-02/Surveying & Mapping Law ban on unapproved foreign collection); clear **ATA Carnet** serial-number manifest matching hull, sonar head, IMU, and spare thrusters. — *Evidence:* Export-control classification determination, ATA Carnet stamped sheets, and local survey partner license if applicable. — *Ref:* [Ch. 68 Legal Issues](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md) · [Ch. 69 Security & Sovereignty](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md) · [Sec. 27.3 National Security & Sovereignty](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-03.md)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Verify Hazardous Materials Transport, Bunkering & Battery Logistics (UN 3480/3481, UN 3090/3091, Diesel/Gasoline).** Audit shipping manifests and deck storage for **UN 3480 / UN 3481** Class 9 Li-ion / LiFePO₄ propulsion packs, **UN 3090 / UN 3091** lithium-metal primary cells inside emergency PLBs/EPIRBs/AIS beacons/acoustic releases, and diesel/gasoline fueling hoses. — *Threshold:* Current **UN 38.3** test summaries and **Safety Data Sheets (SDS)** on file for every battery chemistry; **49 CFR 171–180** Class 9 hazmat placards/shipping papers for packs $>300\text{ Wh}$ (ground), **IMDG Code** for sea freight, and **IATA DGR (67th Ed., Jan 2026) PI965 / PI966** ($\le 30\%$ SoC mandatory for both standalone and equipment-packed Li-ion air shipments, Cargo Aircraft Only [`CAO`] for PI965 > limit); fuel spill kit + secondary containment rated $\ge 110\%$ of largest tank onboard chase/support boat. — *Evidence:* Signed SDS binder, UN 38.3 certificates, and hazardous-goods shipper declaration. — *Ref:* [Phase 09 External Regulatory Register](./09-resources-and-templates.md#e-external-regulatory--operational-basis-register-non-handbook-sources)

- [ ] **[COND]** **Establish Marine Mammal / ESA Acoustic Mitigation & Soft-Start Protocol** (*Trigger:* deep $12\text{–}34\text{ kHz}$ MBES, sub-bottom profiler, or operations inside protected-species critical habitats). — *Threshold:* Enforce **$\ge 30\text{ min}$ visual pre-watch** (**$60\text{ min}$** in water depths $>200\text{ m}$ per JNCC 2017 guidelines) prior to a **$20\text{–}30\text{ min}$ ramp-up soft-start** and **$100\text{–}500\text{ m}$ exclusion shutdown radius** monitored by trained Protected Species Observers (PSOs) on chase/mothership or dedicated EO/IR thermal watch. — *Evidence:* Permit-specific IHA / BOEM / NMFS mitigation protocol sheet signed by lead scientist. — *Ref:* [Ch. 69 Security, Sovereignty & Marine Mammal Rules](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md) · [Phase 09 External Regulatory Register](./09-resources-and-templates.md#e-external-regulatory--operational-basis-register-non-handbook-sources)

### A.2 Insurance Matrix & Contractor Liability Verification

Private survey contractors working on USACE/NOAA/State contracts **do not share government sovereign immunity or Federal Tort Claims Act (FTCA) discretionary-function protection**. Verify every line below in [`template-insurance-coi.md`](../_shared/template-insurance-coi.md) before lifting the USV onto the water:

| Policy Line | Minimum Scope & Critical ASV/USV Endorsements | Mandatory Check |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hull & Machinery (H&M)** | Explicitly lists uncrewed/autonomous operation, submerged gondola/strut, & launch/recovery (LARS) transit; stated agreed value | `[ ] Agreed Value >= Replacement` |
| **Protection & Indemnity (P&I)** | Third-party vessel collision/allision, harbor infrastructure damage, **Compulsory Wreck Removal**, pollution/bunkers | `[ ] P&I + Wreck Removal Active` |
| **Charterer's Legal Liability** | Covers damage to chartered chase boat / mothership / pier caused by USV LARS operations or fuel transfer | `[ ] Required if Vessel Chartered` |
| **Scheduled Marine Floater + C&V** | Covers MBES, FOG INS, SVS/SVT, winches during shop/truck/wharf/deck **Condition & Valuation (C&V)** surveyed | `[ ] C&V Survey + Transit Sublimit` |
| **Jones Act / MEL & USL&H** | Maritime Employer's Liability (Jones Act crew) + Longshore & Harbor Workers' Comp (USL&H dock/LARS staff) | `[ ] Chase/LARS Crew Covered` |
| **Professional E&O + CGL + Cyber** | Bathymetric chart/UKC survey errors & omissions; shore ROC premises CGL; link-hijack/ransomware Cyber | `[ ] E&O >= Contract Cap` |

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Verify all Certificates of Insurance (COIs), expiry buffers, IWL limits, and Waivers of Subrogation.** Inspect underwriter endorsements directly (not broker marketing summaries) for explicit autonomous/uncrewed surface craft approval. — *Threshold:* COI expiration dates cover full mobilization **plus $\ge 30\text{–}60\text{ days}$ weather-extension buffer**; include **Waiver of Subrogation & Primary/Non-Contributory** wording for client/port authority, **Institute Warranty Limits (IWL)** endorsement for polar/ice/high-risk waters, and **Foreign Voluntary Workers' Compensation (FVWC) + Medevac** for OCONUS deployments. — *Evidence:* Stamped COIs and named-endorsement pages filed in project binder matching [`template-insurance-coi.md`](../_shared/template-insurance-coi.md). — *Ref:* [Ch. 68 Legal Issues](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md) · [Sec. 27.1 Legal Issues](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-01.md) · [Phase 09 External Regulatory Register](./09-resources-and-templates.md#e-external-regulatory--operational-basis-register-non-handbook-sources)

## PART B — Daily Pre-Sortie Safety, Link-Security & Burn-Rate Gates

Complete Part B on **every sortie day** (or **every 24 h** for multi-week OTH USVs) before dropping mooring lines or crossing a CONOPS tier boundary.

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Verify Marine Weather Forecast, Sea-State Window ($H_s / T_p$) & Tidal Current Go/No-Go.** Check local marine VHF/NOAA forecast, nearest wave buoy $H_s$ and dominant period $T_p$, breaking-bar surf advisories, and peak tidal currents against hull and LARS limits before unstrapping vessel from cradle. — *Threshold:* Forecast wind and significant wave height stay strictly below hull/LARS launch ceiling (micro-USV $H_s \le 0.5\text{–}0.8\text{ m}$; semi-submersible DriX LARS $\le 1.5\text{–}2.0\text{ m}$ / ops $\le 2.5\text{ m}$; ocean USV ops $\le 4.0\text{ m}$) with zero gale/small-craft warnings inside sortie endurance window. — *Evidence:* Timestamped NOAA/buoy forecast capture logged on daily sortie header. — *Ref:* [Ch. 26 Survey Planning](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md) · [Sec. 7.4 ASV Sea-State Limits](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-04.md)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Bench-test Hardware RF Kill-Switch / Emergency Stop (E-Stop) & Lost-Link / Lost-GNSS Failsafe Matrix.** Test physical short-range RF e-stop fob and ROC soft-kill command, verify dead-man timer behavior on simulated RF/SATCOM drop, and confirm lost-GNSS heading/INS coast behavior. — *Threshold:* Hardware RF e-stop drops propulsion to zero thrust within **$\le 1.0\text{ s}$** at tested LOS dock range ($\ge 300\text{ m}$); **Lost-Link Failsafe:** loiter/station-keep circle for **$60\text{–}180\text{ s}$** (Tier 1/2) or return to pre-vetted safe muster waypoint / heave-to away from shipping lanes (Tier 3); **Lost-GNSS Failsafe:** dead-reckon on INS for $\le 120\text{ s}$ without entering shoal geofence, then abort line and hold safe water. — *Evidence:* Timestamped e-stop & failsafe log entry in daily sortie log. — *Ref:* [Ch. 12 GNSS](../../fable/chapters/ch12-gnss.md) · [Ch. 13 IMU & INS](../../fable/chapters/ch13-imu-ins.md)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Verify Command-Link Cybersecurity, Telemetry Encryption & GNSS Anti-Spoofing Health.** Confirm AES-256 / WireGuard VPN encrypted command-and-control (C2) tunnel, hardware MFA on ROC helm consoles, locked firewall ports on onboard acquisition PC, and GNSS carrier-to-noise ($C/N_0$) / AGC jamming baseline. — *Threshold:* Zero unencrypted plaintext UDP/serial helm links over public cellular/SATCOM; $C/N_0$ nominal ($40\text{–}52\text{ dB-Hz}$), zero unexpected AGC gain drop $>3\text{ dB}$ or >$5\text{ m}$ clock-jump anomaly indicating spoofing; Galileo OSNMA or multi-constellation consistency active where supported. — *Evidence:* ROC security health panel screenshot & pre-launch GNSS spectrum/RAIM green status. — *Ref:* [Ch. 69 GNSS Interference & Security](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md) · [Sec. 27.3 National Security & GNSS Interference](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-03.md)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Enforce ROC Watchstander Fatigue Limits, Chase-Boat Briefing & Marine Safety Gear Check.** Audit ROC operator shift hours, assign unambiguous single-person helm authority (USV never receives conflicting helm commands from ROC and chase boat simultaneously), and inspect deck PFD/VHF/fire-extinguisher readiness. — *Threshold:* Maximum **4 h continuous helm/COLREGS watch rotation** ($\le 12\text{ h}$ total shift duty, $\ge 10\text{ h}$ off-duty rest between shifts); chase-boat crew wears Type I/III/V PFD + kill-cord + VHF Ch 16 + AIS transponder; SDS-compliant Class B / water-deluge or Lith-EX fire suppression verified on chase boat (note: dry-chemical ABC / CO₂ fails against Li-ion thermal runaway). — *Evidence:* Signed ROC Watch-Handoff Sheet ([Template 09](./09-resources-and-templates.md)) and chase-boat pre-departure check. — *Ref:* [Ch. 26 Survey Planning](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md) · [Phase 09 External Regulatory Register](./09-resources-and-templates.md#e-external-regulatory--operational-basis-register-non-handbook-sources)

- [ ] **[REC]** **Run Daily Burn-Rate vs. Survey-Progress Abort Check.** Compare cumulative effective survey line-kilometers acquired against daily weather-standby burn rate and remaining contingency days. — *Threshold:* Trigger formal PM/Client hold-or-relocate review if rolling 3-day acquisition rate drops **$<40\%$ of planned line-km/day** OR remaining weather contingency falls **$<20\%$** with $\ge 25\%$ polygon incomplete. — *Evidence:* Updated daily tracking tab in [`template-cost-ledger.md`](../_shared/template-cost-ledger.md). — *Ref:* [Ch. 28 Reducing Cost](../../fable/chapters/ch28-reducing-cost.md)
