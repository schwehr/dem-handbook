## 1. Line Spacing, Overlap & Crossline Design

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Compute limiting-case shallow-edge swath spacing ($S$) and sounding density.** Design mainscheme line spacing using the **shoalest expected water depth** ($d_{\min}$) along each sub-line or contour-parallel corridor—never mean depth—via:
  $$W = 2\,d_{\min}\tan\theta_{\text{eff}}, \qquad S = W\,(1 - f)$$
  where $\theta_{\text{eff}}$ is the usable half-swath angle ($55^\circ\text{–}65^\circ$, capped if outer beams exceed TVU budget) and $f$ is fractional sidelap. — *Threshold:* Maintain **$f = 0.15\text{–}0.25$ sidelap ($S = 0.75\text{–}0.85\,W$) for 100 % Complete Coverage**, and **$f = 0.50\text{–}0.55$ sidelap ($S = 0.45\text{–}0.50\,W$) for 200 % bathymetric coverage / Object Detection** so outer beams receive adjacent mid-swath/nadir overlap (reserve $f \ge 0.67$ [300 %] solely for critical engineering targets); verify along-track ping density and across-track beam density yield **$\ge 5$ soundings per grid node** at maximum survey speed $v$. — *Evidence:* Polygon line-plan GIS layer with shoal-contour overlap audit plot. — *Ref:* [Ch. 26 Survey Planning](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md) · [Sec. 13.1 Line Planning & Coverage](../../gemini4/book/ch-13/sec-13-01.md)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Plan $\ge 4\text{–}8\%$ orthogonal crosslines spanning distinct tide, time-of-day, and sound-speed windows.** Lay out crosslines within $15^\circ$ of perpendicular to mainscheme lines, distributed evenly across bathymetric relief and depth bands rather than clustered solely down flat harbor centers. — *Threshold:* IHO S-44 sets no fixed crossline percentage; follow **NOAA HSSD ($\ge 4\%$ linear distance for full-coverage MBES, rising to $\sim 8\%$ where coverage is partial such as single-beam or sparse micro-USV recon)**; schedule crosslines across **opposite tidal stages and separate calendar days** so outer-beam refraction smiles, tide-zoning phase lags, and lever-arm/pitch errors cannot hide behind identical conditions. — *Evidence:* Mainscheme-vs-crossline linear mileage ratio table and crossline tidal-phase plan. — *Ref:* [Ch. 20 Sonar](../../fable/chapters/ch20-sonar.md) · [Ch. 56 Refraction Smile Case File](../../fable/chapters/ch56-case-files.md)

- [ ] **[REC]** **Run bathymetric-contour-following lines on steep slopes and surf zones.** Align lines parallel to coastal isobaths on sloped beaches ($>5^\circ$), reef walls, and dredged channel flanks so depth changes slowly across the swath rather than running up-and-down slope (which wastes swath width on the shoal end and starves density on the deep end). — *Threshold:* Switch to adaptive contour-following spacing whenever within-swath depth varies by **$>30\%$** across planned mainscheme lines. — *Evidence:* Adaptive line-spacing plan exported to USV autopilot route. — *Ref:* [Sec. 13.1 Adaptive MBES Planning](../../gemini4/book/ch-13/sec-13-01.md)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Lock sonar frequency, swath sector cap, ping mode, and backscatter calibration settings.** Select single/dual-frequency profile ($200\text{–}400\text{ kHz}$ shallow shelf vs. $26\text{–}34\text{ kHz}$ deep ocean), pulse length, and angular sector width (`-55° to +55°` or `-65° to +65°` full swath nominal; tighten to `-50° to +50°` where refraction curl threatens TVU). — *Threshold:* Dual-swath / high-density equidistant mode verified so across-track beam footprint $d\theta/\cos^2\phi$ meets required S-44 object detection; keep backscatter TX power/pulse width constant within each geological block if backscatter mosaics are a deliverable. — *Evidence:* Acquisition sonar preset XML / config exported and locked. — *Ref:* [Ch. 20 Sonar](../../fable/chapters/ch20-sonar.md) · [Ch. 26 Survey Planning](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md)

- [ ] **[REC]** **Embed hard geofence exclusion polygons, UKC floor contours, and safe-muster loiter points.** Load fish traps, kelp beds, submerged wrecks, anchorage fairways, and shoal depth alarms into the onboard autonomy safety supervisor rather than relying solely on line waypoints. — *Threshold:* Minimum **$\ge 1.5\text{ m}$ charted UKC** buffer for semi-submersible/ocean USVs (`CATZOC C/D/U` waters padded $\ge 3\text{ m}$); at least **2 vetted safe-loiter waypoints** plotted clear of shipping lanes for lost-link loiter recovery. — *Evidence:* Verified `.geojson` / autopilot geofence upload check. — *Ref:* [Ch. 62 Navigation & Charting](../../fable/chapters/ch62-navigation-and-charting.md) · [Phase 09 External Regulatory Register](./09-resources-and-templates.md#e-external-regulatory--operational-basis-register-non-handbook-sources)

## 2. Four-Step Patch Test & Dynamic Calibration Plan

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Program the mandatory 4-Step Patch Test Sequence and verify full parameter observability.** Schedule dedicated calibration lines over the Phase 03 site immediately after initial launch, after any pole re-mounting/grounding/impact, and prior to closing demob, strictly in the **exact decoupling order**:
  1. **Latency ($\Delta t$):** Two same-direction passes over a steep slope ($\ge 15^\circ$) at $1.0\times$ vs. $2.0\times$ speed (e.g., $1.5\text{ m/s}$ vs. $3.5\text{ m/s}$) — verify 1PPS/PTP locks $\Delta t \approx 0.000\text{ s}$.
  2. **Pitch ($\Delta\theta$):** Reciprocal passes (opposite headings) at constant speed over the same $\ge 15^\circ$ slope or distinct ridge crest.
  3. **Roll ($\Delta\phi$):** Reciprocal passes over a smooth, flat seabed ($<2^\circ$ slope).
  4. **Yaw ($\Delta\psi$):** Adjacent parallel same-heading passes straddling a proud 3D target (boulder/pile/test frame) so outer port vs. outer starboard beams insonify the target at $\approx 35^\circ\text{–}55^\circ$.
  — *Threshold:* Residual post-patch uncertainty **$\le 0.05^\circ$ roll** ($1\sigma$), **$\le 0.10^\circ$ pitch** ($1\sigma$), **$\le 0.15^\circ$ yaw** ($1\sigma$), and **$\le 1\text{ ms}$ latency**; add a static-heel check on sail-driven ocean USVs across port vs. starboard tacks to isolate steady heel bias from true sensor roll boresight. — *Evidence:* Patch-test line file + design-matrix rank/condition check (zero collinear speed/slope passes). — *Ref:* [Ch. 20 Sonar](../../fable/chapters/ch20-sonar.md) · [Sec. 13.1 Geometric Survey Design & Boresight](../../gemini4/book/ch-13/sec-13-01.md) · [Sec. 13.5 Boresight Calibration Math](../../gemini4/book/ch-13/sec-13-05.md)

- [ ] **[REC]** **Incorporate turn run-in lead-ins for marine heave filter settling.** Program straight approach lead-ins before every production and patch-test line so high-pass marine heave filters ($T_c \approx 10\text{–}20\text{ s}$) recover from centrifugal turn-induced tilt before entering the survey boundary. — *Threshold:* Minimum **$45\text{–}90\text{ s}$ straight steady-speed run-in** before start-of-line (SOL) when relying on real-time heave (reduce to $15\text{–}20\text{ s}$ autopilot settle if running **delayed TrueHeave** or **ERS** with zero heave-filter dependence). — *Evidence:* Waypoint turn-extension geometry visible in mission route file. — *Ref:* [Ch. 13 IMU & INS](../../fable/chapters/ch13-imu-ins.md)

## 3. Sea-Keeping, Angular-Rate Limits, Current Crabbing & Endurance

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Set swell-heading line orientation and hard angular-rate abort thresholds.** Orient lines either **oblique ($30^\circ\text{–}45^\circ$)** or **quartering** to dominant steep chop when pure head-seas drive sonar aeration or pure beam-seas saturate roll stabilization; enforce autopilot pause/abort limits on instantaneous angular rates. — *Threshold:* On micro-catamarans (1–4 m), flag/re-run any line segment experiencing **sustained angular rates $\dot{\phi}, \dot{\theta} > 30^\circ/\text{s}$** (peak shock $>50^\circ/\text{s}$), sustained pitch aeration dropouts $>5\%$, or roll excursion exceeding active swath-stabilization cone (typically **`12°–15°` port/starboard** mechanical/electronic limit); semi-submersibles (DriX) cap operational survey roll/pitch at platform spec ($\approx 15^\circ$ roll / $10^\circ$ pitch at $H_s \le 2.0\text{–}2.5\text{ m}$). — *Evidence:* Mission abort criteria loaded into ROC alert dashboard. — *Ref:* [Sec. 7.4 Marine Platforms & USVs](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-04.md) · [Ch. 16 Platforms](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Compute tidal-current crabbing angles, dual-antenna heading coupling, and battery/fuel reserve margins.** Calculate required heading crab angle $\beta = \arcsin\!\left(\frac{v_{\text{current}}\sin\alpha}{v_{\text{through-water}}}\right)$ for peak ebb/flood cross-currents and check track-keeping margin before deploying micro-USVs. — *Threshold:* Do not plan cross-current lines where $v_{\text{current}} > 0.50\,v_{\text{max}}$ ($\beta > 30^\circ$ risks beam-skew footprint gaps and severe cross-track drift); ensure minimum through-water headway $\ge 1.5\text{ m/s}$ above peak local tidal velocity; reserve **$\ge 25\%$ LiFePO₄/Li-ion battery SoC** or **$\ge 20\%$ diesel fuel** at end-of-sortie return waypoint under worst-case head-current return. — *Evidence:* Current-adjusted line-time & endurance sheet saved in sortie pack. — *Ref:* [Sec. 7.4 Marine Platforms & USVs](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-04.md) · [Ch. 26 Survey Planning](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Schedule Autonomous Winched-SVT / MVP Sound-Speed Cast Windows & Seabed Clearance Stops.** Program automated SVP cast intervals and maximum payout depth into the mission planner using prior bathymetry + tidal stage so autonomous winches never strike uncharted ledges. — *Threshold:* Maintain **$\ge 3\text{–}5\text{ m}$ bottom clearance** below programmed winch stop depth; budget **2–4 h** cast intervals in stratified coastal zones ($30\text{–}90\text{ min}$ across river/estuary fronts) and pause survey speed as specified by winch OEM (typically $\le 1.0\text{–}2.0\text{ m/s}$ during cast). — *Evidence:* Pre-programmed SVT cast polygon/depth table in mission plan. — *Ref:* [Sec. 13.2 Environmental & Temporal Scheduling](../../gemini4/book/ch-13/sec-13-02.md) · [Sec. 10.4 Refraction & Ray Tracing](../../gemini4/book/ch-10/sec-10-04.md) · [Ch. 20 Sonar](../../fable/chapters/ch20-sonar.md)

- [ ] **[COND]** **Add Opposite-Tack Static Wind-Sail Heel Calibration Runs** (*Trigger:* wind-propelled endurance ocean USVs such as Saildrone Surveyor). — *Threshold:* Plan reciprocal calibration runs across steady beam winds ($8\text{–}15\text{ m/s}$) on **both port and starboard tacks** to decouple steady wind-induced roll offset ($5^\circ\text{–}15^\circ$ heel) from sonar/IMU boresight residuals. — *Evidence:* Opposite-tack heel comparison track geometry in calibration plan. — *Ref:* [Ch. 16 Platforms](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md) · [Sec. 7.8 Vessel Squat & Dynamic Draft](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-08.md)

- [ ] **[REC]** **Plan Opportunistic Water-Column / Backscatter / Sub-Bottom Logging Without Degrading Bathymetry.** Co-log raw backscatter (`WCI`/snippets) and auxiliary ADCP/SBP only after confirming CPU, disk I/O, and acoustic trigger synchronization cause zero bathymetric ping drops. — *Threshold:* Combined disk write throughput $< 50\%$ sustained storage bandwidth and zero acoustic crosstalk spikes on main MBES waterfall. — *Evidence:* Dockside multi-sensor full-logging stress-test log. — *Ref:* [Ch. 20 Sonar](../../fable/chapters/ch20-sonar.md) · [Ch. 28 Reducing Cost](../../fable/chapters/ch28-reducing-cost.md)

- [ ] **[COND]** **Lock high-tide USV / low-tide airborne white-ribbon overlap timing** (*Trigger:* multi-modal coastal/intertidal project combining ASV MBES with topobathy lidar, drone lidar, or beach RTK). — *Threshold:* Schedule micro-USV shallowest perimeter passes **within $1.0\text{ h}$ of spring high water** (maximizing flooded depth over mudflats/bars) paired with airborne/UAV lidar **within $1.0\text{ h}$ of spring low water**, ensuring **100–500 m cross-shore overlap** and **$\le 3\text{–}7\text{ days}$ temporal separation**; enforce common vertical datum and airspace/water-surface safety separation (e.g., 532 nm green laser Nominal Ocular Hazard Distance [NOHD] clear of chase-boat crew). — *Evidence:* Synchronized high/low tide operational timeline matching [`multimodal-white-ribbon.md`](../_shared/multimodal-white-ribbon.md). — *Ref:* [Ch. 34 Water in DEMs](../../fable/chapters/ch34-water-in-dems.md) · [Sec. 13.2 Environmental & Temporal Scheduling](../../gemini4/book/ch-13/sec-13-02.md)

## 4. Worked *A Priori* Hare–Godin–Mayer Total Propagated Uncertainty (TPU) Budget

| Uncertainty Component ($1\sigma$ unless noted) | Regime A: Micro-USV Shallow (10 m, RTK + ERS, $-60^\circ\text{ to }+60^\circ$ sector) | Regime C: Endurance Ocean USV Deep (4,000 m, PPP + Winched SVT, $-60^\circ\text{ to }+60^\circ$ sector) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vertical Positioning / Heave ($\sigma_z$)** | $0.030\text{ m}$ (RTK + RTS SBET) | $0.060\text{ m}$ (PPP-AR + FOG INS + ERS) |
