## 1. Immediate On-Water Emergency Response Gates

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Collision, Allision, or Near-Miss Response.** Command all-stop / safe station-keep away from channel traffic; hail involved vessel immediately on **VHF Ch 16 / Ch 13** to check human safety and hull watertightness; lock EO/IR camera sweep on impact zone and log AIS target MMSI/CPA. — *Threshold:* Zero departure from scene until USCG Sector / VTS clears vessel recovery; verify zero hull breach or bilge-pump runaway before towing. — *Evidence:* Incident timestamp log, VHF audio/transcript, and frozen EO/IR + AIS replay clip. — *Ref:* [Ch. 68 Legal Issues](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md) · [Sec. 27.1 Legal Issues](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-01.md) · [Phase 09 External Regulatory Register](./09-resources-and-templates.md#e-external-regulatory--operational-basis-register-non-handbook-sources)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Runaway / Unresponsive Vessel Chase-Boat Intercept & Safe Tow.** When ROC loses helm control or vessel departs safe geofence, intercept from upwind/up-wave beam using chase boat, trigger hardware RF e-stop inside $300\text{ m}$ LOS range, verify zero propulsion spin before hands-on contact, and rig bow/stern bridle tow. — *Threshold:* Maintain **$\ge 20\text{ m}$ bow standoff** until thrusters visually/telemetrically confirmed dead (`0 RPM`); tow below critical hull/strut drag speed (typically **$\le 2.5\text{–}3.5\text{ m/s}$ ($5\text{–}7\text{ kn}$)**) with continuous astern lookout so submerged gondola/sonar pole is not torn off or broached. — *Evidence:* Intercept timestamp log and bridle/hull post-tow inspection photos. — *Ref:* [Ch. 16 Platforms](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md) · [Sec. 7.4 Marine Platforms & USVs](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-04.md)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Capsize, Flooding, or Swamping Recovery.** Isolate high-voltage battery contactors remotely before chase-boat crew or swimmers touch the hull; deploy high-visibility marker buoy/strobe if sinking risk develops, secure underwater sonar head/gondola against seabed drag, and lift only via certified LARS lifting points. — *Threshold:* Zero ungrounded hands in flooded battery/power compartment (`>48–400 V DC`); verify remaining buoyancy reserve before hoisting (flooded hull can exceed dry crane SWL by **$>2\times$**). — *Evidence:* Contactor isolation log, rigging weight reassessment, and recovery photos. — *Ref:* [Ch. 16 Platforms](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Surf-Zone Grounding / Reef Stranding Discipline.** Hold chase boat strictly **outside the breaking surf zone** ($>2\times$ breaker depth); do not commit small craft or swimmers into shorebreak over a stranded USV; kill thrusters to prevent rock-grinding sonar crystals, assess dominant swell period $T_p$, and plan recovery on rising tide slack or shore-based line/kellet tow. — *Threshold:* Zero crew exposure inside plunging breakers; document exact seabed contact time for P&I/H&M hull & acoustic-array inspection. — *Evidence:* Breaker-zone assessment log, tide-stage table, and shore/boat recovery log. — *Ref:* [Ch. 66 Coastal & Surf Zones](../../fable/chapters/ch66-coastal-marine-polar-lakes-rivers.md)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **LiFePO₄ / Li-ion Thermal Runaway, Primary Lithium-Metal (`UN 3090`), or Diesel-Engine Fire Suppression.** Evacuate all personnel **upwind** of off-gas plumes (HF, CO, flammable electrolyte vapors); cut fuel shutoff/engine-bay ventilation for diesel fires (CO₂/foam); for **rechargeable Li-ion / LiFePO₄ (`UN 3480 / UN 3481`)** thermal runaway, fight strictly per SDS using **copious continuous seawater deluge or aqueous vermiculite dispersion (AVD / `Lith-EX`) cooling** (or tow clear of piers/vessels if safe and COTP-authorized); reserve **`Class D` dry powder strictly for primary lithium-metal (`UN 3090`) cells** (PLBs/EPIRBs/acoustic beacons—never apply water to burning metallic lithium). — *Threshold:* Never rely on standard dry-chemical ABC or CO₂ extinguishers alone on rechargeable Li-ion/LiFePO₄ thermal runaway (cells self-oxidize and reignite); monitor battery bay temperature for **$\ge 24\text{ h}$** post-extinguishment against re-flash. — *Evidence:* Battery pack SDS consulted on scene, thermal camera check log, and fire-suppression record. — *Ref:* [Phase 09 External Regulatory Register](./09-resources-and-templates.md#e-external-regulatory--operational-basis-register-non-handbook-sources)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Oil, Diesel, or Hydraulic Sheen Containment & Immediate NRC Reporting.** Deploy chase-boat absorbent booms/pads around any visible fuel or hydraulic sheen from damaged tanks/hoses, plug hull vents if safe, and call the **National Response Center (NRC `1-800-424-8802`) immediately**. — *Threshold:* Zero delay on **33 CFR § 153.203** NRC notification (or coastal-state MRCC abroad) for ANY oil discharge producing a visible sheen on navigable waters, regardless of estimated liters spilled. — *Evidence:* NRC Case Reference Number, wind/drift sheen photos, and containment log. — *Ref:* [Ch. 68 Legal Issues](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md) · [Phase 09 External Regulatory Register](./09-resources-and-templates.md#e-external-regulatory--operational-basis-register-non-handbook-sources)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Deck Crew Injury or Man-Overboard Emergency Rescue & Medical Escalation.** Deploy flotation/recovery sling immediately for any person in water during LARS or chase-boat tow, broadcast **VHF Ch 16 (voice) / Ch 70 (DSC) MAYDAY / PAN-PAN** as warranted, initiate first-aid/AED, and trigger statutory crew-testing/OSHA clocks. — *Threshold:* Track **OSHA 29 CFR § 1904.39** (**8 hours** fatality / **24 hours** inpatient hospitalization, amputation, or **eye loss**, where OSHA has jurisdiction) and **46 CFR § 4.06** SMI **2 h alcohol / 32 h drug testing** for involved marine crew. — *Evidence:* Medical triage log, Coast Guard SAR coordination log, and OSHA/SMI receipts. — *Ref:* [Ch. 68 Legal Issues](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md) · [Phase 09 External Regulatory Register](./09-resources-and-templates.md#e-external-regulatory--operational-basis-register-non-handbook-sources)

- [ ] **[COND]** **Mark Unrecovered Submerged Asset / Sunken USV Hazard in Navigable Channel** (*Trigger:* USV sinks or breaks loose unrecovered in shipping fairway, harbor approach, or shallow channel). — *Threshold:* Mark wreck position immediately per **33 CFR Part 64** with temporary lighted buoy / daymark if feasible without endangering crew, verify exact GPS sink coordinates, and broadcast immediate hazard warning to USCG Sector / VTS / Harbor Master for emergency Broadcast Notice to Mariners (BNM). — *Evidence:* USCG BNM confirmation, GPS sink coordinate log, and wreck-marking photo. — *Ref:* [Ch. 62 Navigation & Charting](../../fable/chapters/ch62-navigation-and-charting.md) · [Ch. 68 Legal Issues](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **GNSS Spoofing, RF Jamming, or Command-Link Cyber Compromise.** If telemetry displays impossible position/velocity jumps, abrupt $C/N_0$ collapse, or uncommanded helm changes, sever external cellular/SATCOM C2 network interfaces immediately, revert to local RF LOS kill/override, and steer visually via chase boat to safe harbor. — *Threshold:* Trigger anomaly protocol on any **$>5\text{ m}$ instantaneous GNSS jump**, sustained `PDOP > 5`, or unrecognized helm packet; isolate acquisition/helm storage from corporate networks until forensic review. — *Evidence:* Captured NMEA/RINEX spoofing log, firewall packet dump, and C2 auth logs. — *Ref:* [Ch. 69 Security & GNSS Interference](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md) · [Sec. 27.3 National Security & GNSS Interference](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-03.md)

## 2. Evidence Preservation, Statutory Clocks & Insurer Notifications

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Execute Immediate "Black Box" Digital & Physical Evidence Freeze (with PII / Privacy Quarantine).** Before powering off, wiping, rebooting, or overwriting any USV storage, create hash-verified read-only images of raw sonar logs, INS/GNSS streams, autopilot mission & C2 command logs, EO/IR video buffers, radar tracks, AIS captures, and ROC watch-handoff sheets. — *Threshold:* Zero post-incident edits, test re-runs, or drive wipes on original media; seal SHA-256 chain-of-custody manifest under **FRE 901 / 902(13)–(14)** digital record authentication (supporting **FRE 702 / *Daubert*** method review) within **$\le 6\text{ hours}$** of recovery; quarantine third-party vessel/bystander EO/IR video, VHF voice recordings, and crew medical PII in access-controlled legal storage (redacting PII prior to internal debriefs or public release). — *Evidence:* Read-only forensic copy manifest with timestamps, access log, and witness signatures. — *Ref:* [Ch. 50 Archiving & Provenance](../../fable/chapters/ch50-archiving-and-provenance.md) · [Ch. 68 Legal Issues](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md) · [Sec. 27.2 Privacy Issues](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-02.md)

- [ ] **[COND]** **Notify Supervisory Authority Under GDPR Art. 33 ($\le 72\text{ h}$) for Personal / EO-IR / Medical PII Breaches** (*Trigger:* unauthorized disclosure, theft, cyber exfiltration, or unencrypted media loss involving EO/IR bystander imagery, VHF crew audio, crew SMI/medical records, or EU/UK subject PII). — *Threshold:* Notify the competent Data Protection / Supervisory Authority within **$\le 72\text{ hours}$** of breach awareness per **GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) Art. 33** (and affected individuals under Art. 34 where high risk exists, plus applicable local breach-notification laws). — *Evidence:* Timestamped DPO/legal breach notification docket and quarantine confirmation log. — *Ref:* [Sec. 27.2 Privacy Issues](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-02.md) · [Ch. 69 Security, Sovereignty & Ethics](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md) · [Phase 09 External Regulatory Register](./09-resources-and-templates.md#e-external-regulatory--operational-basis-register-non-handbook-sources)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Satisfy USCG Immediate & 5-Day CG-2692, NRC Immediate Spill, and OSHA 8 h / 24 h Clocks.** Assign one dedicated shore/PM compliance officer (not the on-water salvage lead) to log exact UTC trigger times and file all required notices within statutory deadlines (U.S. waters/flag; file host flag-state/MRCC equivalent abroad). — *Threshold:*
  - **USCG Sector Verbal:** **Immediate** (46 CFR § 4.05-1); **Written Form CG-2692:** within **5 calendar days** (§ 4.05-10; mandatory if property damage exceeds **`$75,000`**, grounding, allision, loss of propulsion/steering, fire/flooding, death, or injury beyond first aid).
  - **USCG SMI Testing:** **2 hours** alcohol / **32 hours** drug testing + Form CG-2692B for involved chase/LARS/ROC marine crew under 46 CFR § 4.03-2 / § 4.06.
  - **NRC Oil/Hazmat Spill:** **Immediate** call to `1-800-424-8802` (33 CFR § 153.203).
  - **OSHA Injury:** **8 hours** fatality; **24 hours** hospitalization, amputation, or eye loss (29 CFR § 1904.39, where applicable).
  — *Evidence:* NRC incident case #, CG-2692 submission receipt, SMI chain-of-custody lab receipt, and OSHA confirmation ID. — *Ref:* [Ch. 68 Legal Issues](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md) · [Phase 09 External Regulatory Register](./09-resources-and-templates.md#e-external-regulatory--operational-basis-register-non-handbook-sources)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Notify H&M / P&I Underwriters and Govern Wreck Removal & Salvage Authorization.** Contact Hull & Machinery (H&M) and Protection & Indemnity (P&I) claims desks within policy prompt-notice deadline and obtain underwriter/salvage surveyor concurrence before contracting non-emergency commercial wreck removal or signing salvage agreements. — *Threshold:* Initial insurer notice within **$\le 24\text{–}48\text{ hours}$**; zero unauthorized abandonment or hull scrapping before marine surveyor inspection (preserves P&I compulsory wreck-removal and H&M constructive-total-loss cover). — *Evidence:* Insurer claim reference number and surveyor attendance log. — *Ref:* [Sec. 27.1 Legal Issues](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-01.md) · [Phase 09 External Regulatory Register](./09-resources-and-templates.md#e-external-regulatory--operational-basis-register-non-handbook-sources)

- [ ] **[COND]** **File Aviation / NTSB Notice if Accompanying Shipboard Aerial UAS Was Involved** (*Trigger:* project deployed an auxiliary aerial drone for white-ribbon recon or LARS overwatch). — *Threshold:* FAA **14 CFR § 107.9** within **10 calendar days** (serious injury/unconsciousness or `>$500` lower of repair/FMV third-party property damage); NTSB **49 CFR § 830.2 / § 830.5** **immediate** notice if death, serious injury, `>$25,000` non-UAS property damage, or substantial damage to an airworthiness-certificated UAS. — *Evidence:* FAA DroneZone / NTSB confirmation receipt. — *Ref:* [Ch. 68 Legal Issues](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md)

- [ ] **[REC]** **Enforce Operational Stand-Down & Re-Qualification Gate Before Re-Launch.** Suspend fleet sorties of the same hull class until Independent Verifier signs off root-cause containment, software/hardware fix verification, and a fresh dockside e-stop + patch-test check. — *Threshold:* Zero return to service without written PM + Independent Verifier release. — *Evidence:* Signed Stand-Down Release Memo. — *Ref:* [Ch. 26 Survey Planning](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md)
