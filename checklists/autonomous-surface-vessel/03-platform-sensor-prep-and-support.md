## 1. Hull, Mechanical, LARS & Propulsion Readiness

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Inspect Hull Integrity, Watertight Hatches, Bilge Pumps, Rudders, Thrusters & Gondola/Wing-Sail Rig.** Verify dry electronics bays, clean O-ring hatch gaskets, auto-float bilge switches + high-water telemetry alarms, zero propeller/jet-impeller fishing-line fouling, positive steering linkage travel limits, and (for DriX/ocean USVs) submerged gondola fairing bolts or wing-sail actuator pins. — *Threshold:* Zero standing water in dry bilges; bilge high-water alarm triggers ROC alert within **$\le 5\text{ s}$** of manual float lift; full port-to-starboard rudder/thruster sweep smooth with zero deadband $>2^\circ$; pressurized electronics housings pass positive/vacuum leak check per OEM pressure-decay spec (typically $\le 1\text{–}2\text{ kPa}$ loss over $15\text{ min}$). — *Evidence:* Dockside mechanical inspection log and hatch/gondola macro photos. — *Ref:* [Ch. 16 Platforms](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md) · [Sec. 7.4 ASV Hull & Gondola Coupling](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-04.md)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Certify Launch and Recovery System (LARS), Lifting Bridle Safe Working Load (SWL), and Towing Gear.** Inspect crane hooks, Spectra/wire lifting bridles, quick-release latches, hull cradle chocks, and bow/stern recovery tow painter before lifting the ASV. — *Threshold:* Certified per qualified rigger / OEM spec (`____`; typically rigging SWL $\ge 2.0\times$ maximum wet/fueled ASV lift mass for dynamic shock allowance, with current annual load-test tag; tow painter breaking strength $\ge 5\times$ displacement at $3\text{ m/s}$ tow speed). — *Evidence:* LARS rigging tag IDs, crane operator certificate, and recovery-painter test photo. — *Ref:* [Ch. 16 Platforms](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Inspect Rigid Over-the-Side / Strut Sonar Pole Stiffness, Fairing & Vortex-Induced Vibration (VIV) Suppression.** Measure pole wall thickness, clamp torque, upper/lower gusset braces, and acoustic fairing profile on micro-USVs or deployable transducer poles. — *Threshold:* Calculate vortex shedding frequency $f_s = \text{St}\,v/D$ (with Strouhal number $\text{St} \approx 0.20$ for circular strut of diameter $D$ at survey speed $v$) and ensure $f_s$ stays well clear of pole structural natural frequency (add helical strakes or teardrop fairing if $v > 2\text{ m/s}$ on unbraced poles); dynamic pole angular deflection $\delta\theta \propto v^2$ must remain **$<0.05^\circ$** at survey speed so outer-beam depth error stays within budget. — *Evidence:* Pole torque marks (witness paint on all fasteners) and high-speed straight-run pitch/roll residual check. — *Ref:* [Sec. 7.4 Marine Platforms & Pole Flexure](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-04.md)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Measure Static Waterline / Draft Reference Marks & Log Payload/Fuel Ballast State.** Record static waterline relative to the Vessel Reference Frame (VRF / CRP) at port and starboard bow, midships, and stern in calm slack water after loading final batteries/fuel/ballast. — *Threshold:* Draft reading precision $\le 0.005\text{ m}$ ($5\text{ mm}$) across 4 hull stations; pre-compute expected fuel-burn draft rise $\Delta z_{\text{fuel}} \approx m_{\text{burned}} / (\rho\,A_{\text{wp}})$ and fresh-to-saltwater salinity buoyancy shift $\Delta z_\rho = \nabla (\rho_2 - \rho_1)/(\rho_2\,A_{\text{wp}})$ (typically $0.02\text{–}0.04\text{ m}$ on compact hulls entering brackish river plumes) if operating without ERS. — *Evidence:* Signed static draft & salinity-compensation table. — *Ref:* [Sec. 7.8 Vessel Squat & Dynamic Draft](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-08.md) · [Ch. 25 Calibration Infrastructure](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md)

## 2. Geodetic Lever Arms, GNSS Heading Baseline & Time Synchronization

### Vessel Coordinate Frame & Sign-Trap Verification (Offline Reference)

```text
Standard Maritime / Hydrographic Body Frame (NED / Right-Hand Rule):
  +X = Forward (Bow)     | +Roll  = Starboard wing DOWN
  +Y = Starboard         | +Pitch = Bow UP
  +Z = Down (Keelward)   | +Yaw   = Clockwise from North (Bow to Starboard)

Compare against ENU (+X East, +Y North, +Z Up) BEFORE typing values into Applanix / SBG / IXblue / Kluster!
Never mix +Z-Down lever arms into a +Z-Up GUI without verifying sign polarity on Z_GNSS - Z_TX!
```

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Measure GNSS Dual-Antenna Heading Baseline ($B_{\text{GNSS}}$) and verify sky visibility.** Measure primary-to-secondary GNSS antenna separation vector in the VRF and confirm unobstructed horizon above $10^\circ$ elevation (clear of mast/wing-sail/crane shadowing). — *Threshold:* Physical antenna baseline **$B_{\text{GNSS}} \ge 0.8\text{–}1.5\text{ m}$** minimum on micro-USVs (yielding heading noise $\sigma_\psi \approx \frac{\sigma_{\Delta\Phi}}{B_{\text{GNSS}}} \approx \frac{0.1^\circ\cdot\text{m}}{B_{\text{GNSS}}} \approx 0.07^\circ\text{–}0.13^\circ$) and **$\ge 1.5\text{–}2.5\text{ m}$** on larger DriX/Saildrone hulls ($\sigma_\psi \le 0.05^\circ$); baseline vector calibration residual $\le 0.005\text{ m}$ length and $\le 0.1^\circ$ alignment after GNSS static heading init. — *Evidence:* Total-station antenna baseline report and INS GAMS / dual-antenna calibration pass screenshot. — *Ref:* [Ch. 13 IMU & INS](../../fable/chapters/ch13-imu-ins.md) · [Sec. 7.4 Marine Platforms & USVs](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-04.md)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Complete Total-Station Lever-Arm Survey (IMU -> CRP -> MBES Acoustic Center -> GNSS L1 Phase Centers -> Draft Marks) with Two-Person Sign Verification.** Survey all offsets with calibrated reflectorless total station in dry dock or stable cradle, reducing GNSS heights precisely to the **IGS20 ANTEX L1/L2 ARP-to-phase-center offset** (not plastic radome top!). — *Threshold:* 3D lever-arm uncertainty **$\le 0.003\text{–}0.005\text{ m}$ ($1\sigma$)**; zero unverified sign flips between vessel frame (`NED +Z down`) and INS/acquisition software (`ENU +Z up` or `Kongsberg +Z down`); independent hydrographer re-enters and verifies signs against physical sketch in [`template-project-header.md`](../_shared/template-project-header.md). — *Evidence:* Signed total-station survey report, VRF coordinate diagram, and screenshot of typed lever arms inside INS & sonar UIs. — *Ref:* [Ch. 25 Calibration Infrastructure](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md) · [Notation & Reference Frames](../../gemini4/book/00-front-matter/notation-and-symbols.md)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Verify Hardware 1PPS / NMEA ZDA / IEEE 1588 PTP Time Synchronization across all payloads.** Confirm all sonar PUs, INS, GNSS receivers, SVS probes, and EO/IR timestamp logs lock strictly to UTC GPS time via hardware 1PPS/PTP rather than Windows/Linux OS NTP clocks. — *Threshold:* Timing offset and jitter **$\Delta t_{\text{sync}} \le 0.5\text{–}1.0\text{ ms}$** ($<0.1\text{ ms}$ preferred under PTP/1PPS). Why critical for micro-USVs: at angular rate $\dot{\phi} = 40^\circ/\text{s}$ and beam angle $60^\circ$, even a $10\text{ ms}$ latency residual induces $\delta\phi = 0.40^\circ$ roll error ($\Delta z = d\tan 60^\circ\,\delta\phi \approx 0.48\text{ m}$ vertical bias at **$40\text{ m}$ water depth** / $80\text{ m}$ slant range!). — *Evidence:* Capture of `1PPS LOCKED` / `ZDA VALID` / `PTP SYNC` diagnostics and zero step jumps across 10-minute bench soak. — *Ref:* [Ch. 13 IMU & INS](../../fable/chapters/ch13-imu-ins.md) · [Ch. 6 Time as a Coordinate](../../fable/chapters/ch06-time-as-coordinate.md) · [Sec. 32.2 Hardware Time Synchronization](../../gemini4/book/ch-32/sec-32-02.md)

## 3. Acoustic, Sound-Speed & Shore Control Support Infrastructure

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Verify Calibration Certificates for SVS, Winched SVT/CTD Profilers & GNSS ANTEX Models.** Check factory/ISO-17025 lab calibration dates for hull-mounted Sound Velocity Sensor (SVS), autonomous winched Sound Velocity & Temperature (SVT) or CTD profilers, pressure depth sensors, and antenna `.atx` calibration files (`igs20.atx`), and register sensor uncertainties in [`template-error-budget.md`](../_shared/template-error-budget.md). — *Threshold:* SVS and SVP/CTD certificates within **$\le 12\text{ months}$**; dockside cross-comparison bucket/dip test between surface SVS and profiling CTD agrees within **$\le 0.5\text{ m/s}$** at ambient temperature/salinity; pressure sensor zeroed at local atmospheric pressure before launch. — *Evidence:* PDF calibration certificates + dockside SVS-vs-SVP bucket-test log. — *Ref:* [Ch. 25 Calibration Infrastructure](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md) · [Ch. 20 Sonar](../../fable/chapters/ch20-sonar.md)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Establish Shore CORS / Local RTK Base Station & Verify Monument Coordinate Check.** Connect to vetted CORS / NTRIP network **or** deploy a local dual-frequency RTK/PPK GNSS base station over a known brass disk / benchmark before launch. — *Threshold:* Local RTK/PPK baseline **$\le 15\text{–}20\text{ km}$** for RTK accuracy ($1\text{–}2\text{ cm H} / 2\text{–}3\text{ cm V}$ $1\sigma + 1\text{ ppm}$); double-measure fixed tripod/pole height at start and end of deployment (agreeing to **$\le 0.002\text{ m}$**) with dated photograph of tape against ARP/bottom-of-notch; occupy an independent known monument as blind check ($\Delta H, \Delta V \le 0.03\text{ m}$). — *Evidence:* Base station datasheet, OPUS/PPP solution printout, double-measurement photo, and blind-check residual. — *Ref:* [Ch. 12 GNSS](../../fable/chapters/ch12-gnss.md) · [Ch. 25 Calibration Infrastructure](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md)

- [ ] **[COND]** **Install Temporary Subordinate Tide Gauge & Level $\ge 5$ Tidal Benchmarks** (*Trigger:* Tide-reduction mode OR Hybrid ERS validation selected in Phase 01 Decision Tree). — *Threshold:* Follow **IOC Manuals & Guides No. 14 / NOAA CO-OPS** standards: install staff + vented pressure/acoustic/radar gauge logging at $1\text{–}6\text{ min}$ intervals across **$\ge 30\text{ days}$ simultaneous comparison** to an NWLON/primary gauge when transferring tidal datums (`1–3 cm` for 1 yr, `3–8 cm` for 1 month); level across **$\ge 5$ local benchmarks** including primary TGBM (operational NOAA CO-OPS / IOC Manual 14 rule, exceeding the bare $\ge 3$ mark minimum cited in `fable` §26.3) using Second-Order Class I closed loops ($\le 6\text{ mm}\sqrt{K}$, $K$ in km) plus static GNSS observation on TGBM (`GNSS@TG`) to link chart datum directly to ellipsoid. — *Evidence:* Benchmark leveling abstract, gauge zero-to-staff check log, and `GNSS@TG` tie sheet. — *Ref:* [Ch. 9 Vertical Datums](../../fable/chapters/ch09-vertical-datums.md) · [Ch. 25 Calibration Infrastructure](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Identify Local Patch-Test Site & Pre-Existing Reference Surface.** Designate a calm, protected dock-adjacent calibration site containing: (1) flat horizontal seabed for roll, (2) steep featureless **$\ge 15^\circ\text{–}25^\circ$** slope or dredge bank for latency and pitch, and (3) distinct 3D proud target (pier pile, boulder, pipeline, or submerged test frame) for yaw + object-detection verification, plus a stable hard-bottom **Reference Surface** corridor for before/after repeatability checks. — *Threshold:* Patch-test depth **$\ge 10\text{–}30\text{ m}$** (or deepest accessible protected water $\ge 5\text{ m}$ for ultra-shallow micro-USVs) with well-mixed water column or immediate SVP cast before patch runs. — *Evidence:* Pre-plotted patch-test & reference-surface line files loaded into USV helm computer. — *Ref:* [Ch. 20 Sonar](../../fable/chapters/ch20-sonar.md) · [Sec. 13.1 Geometric Survey Design & Boresight](../../gemini4/book/ch-13/sec-13-01.md) · [Sec. 13.5 Boresight Calibration Math](../../gemini4/book/ch-13/sec-13-05.md)

- [ ] **[REC]** **Run Pre-Deployment Acoustic Wet-Test & Self-Noise Baseline.** Submerge the ASV alongside pier or in calm basin to log 5 minutes of thruster-idle vs. survey-speed sonar waterfalls and raw backscatter. — *Threshold:* Zero cavitation/aeration dropouts across **`-60° to +60°` (`120°` full swath sector)** at target survey speed; no electrical 50/60/400 Hz switching harmonics contaminating acoustic receivers. — *Evidence:* Clean dockside sonar waterfall screenshot saved in calibration archive. — *Ref:* [Ch. 20 Sonar](../../fable/chapters/ch20-sonar.md)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **End-to-End Test AIS, Marine Radar, EO/IR Cameras, Navigation Lights & Hydrophone/VHF Relay to ROC.** Verify from the actual ROC watch station (not just bench cable) that radar sweep targets, Class A/B AIS targets, 360° thermal/daylight EO/IR streams, and Rule 23/27(b) mast lighting update continuously under nominal link bandwidth. — *Threshold:* EO/IR frame refresh **$\ge 2\text{–}5\text{ Hz}$** (Tier 1/2) or adaptive snapshot burst **$\le 10\text{ s}$** (Tier 3 OTH) with zero radar blind sectors $>10^\circ$ around bow/mast. — *Evidence:* ROC bridge screenshot showing live video + radar + AIS overlay on test harbor target. — *Ref:* [Phase 09 External Regulatory Register](./09-resources-and-templates.md#e-external-regulatory--operational-basis-register-non-handbook-sources) · [Ch. 16 Platforms](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md)

- [ ] **[REC]** **Verify Autopilot Line-Tracking Gains, Dynamic Squat Table & Sail Heel Compensation Curves.** Check PID cross-track tuning in calm basin maneuvers and verify any real-time dynamic draft vs. RPM/speed lookup table uses signed SI values matching the vessel frame. — *Threshold:* Cross-track steady error **$< 0.5\text{–}1.5\text{ m}$ ($1\sigma$)** in calm water without rudder oscillation. — *Evidence:* Dock-trial figure-eight / straight-line track plot and signed squat table screenshot. — *Ref:* [Sec. 7.8 Vessel Squat & Dynamic Draft](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-08.md)
