## 1. UNOLS Fleet Scheduling & Ship Cruise Planning Manual

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Submit Ship-Time & Marine Equipment (SME) request in Marine Facilities Planning (`MFP`) ahead of fleet scheduling lock:** Follow typical UNOLS scheduling practice—submit Global/Ocean Class or National Deep Submergence Facility (`NDSF`) SME requests `>= 18 months` prior to target field window (and `>= 12 months` for Intermediate/Regional/Coastal vessels) ahead of the annual Ship Scheduling Committee (`SSC`, typically locked in September)—always confirming exact current NSF/operator `MFP` submission dates. — *Threshold:* MFP SME submission ID recorded and verified against current operator/NSF scheduling cutoff before annual fleet lock. — *Evidence:* Exported `mfp.us` SME PDF with funded proposal ID, berth count, and requested acoustic systems. — *Ref:* [09-resources-and-templates.md](09-resources-and-templates.md)
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Verify ship Cruise Planning Manual & Vessel Data Sheet envelope:** Confirm target vessel berth capacity, electrical power (`120/208/440 VAC`, clean instrument UPS capacity), deck ISO bolt grid (`0.61 m` standard UNOLS spacing), winch wire length/SWL, hull/gondola `MBES` depth band, and operating temperature limits match science party and portable vans. — *Threshold:* `100 %` of portable vans, deck weights (`kg`), berths, and acoustic frequencies cleared in writing by the vessel Marine Superintendent `>= 120 d` before sailing. — *Evidence:* Signed Vessel Capability & Deck-Plan Worksheet appended to MFP Cruise Folder. — *Ref:* [ch16-platforms.md](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md)
- [ ] **[REC]** **Align science objectives across primary, secondary, and shared-use investigators:** Hold a formal science-party & Marine Tech scoping call `>= 6 months` pre-sail to resolve conflicting line orientations (e.g., `MBES` corridor speed `5.1–6.2 m/s [10–12 kn]` vs `SBP`/towed sensor slow speed `1.5–2.6 m/s [3–5 kn]` vs `CTD` station time). — *Threshold:* Zero unresolved speed/sensor conflicts in ship time-budget worksheet (`24 h/d` sum within scheduled sea days). — *Evidence:* Time-motion spreadsheet signed by Chief Scientist and Co-PIs. — *Ref:* [ch26-survey-planning.md](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md)

## 3. Prior Archive Screening, GEBCO TID Audit & Transit Mapping

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Screen prior R2R, NOAA NCEI/IHO DCDB, GMRT, and GEBCO_2025/2026 archives across the survey polygon and transit track:** Interrogate `GEBCO` Type Identifier (`TID`) codes (`10–17` direct single/multibeam, `40` satellite-altimetry-gravity predicted, `41–44` computer/contour interpolated & hybrid grids, `70–72` pre-generated / unknown / steering) plus `R2R` and `GMRT` footprints to avoid duplicate surveying of clean modern `MBES` (`TID 11`) and prioritize unmapped `TID 40` / `41–44` gaps. — *Threshold:* `100 %` of planned survey boxes and transit corridors overlaid against `R2R` cruise tracks, `NCEI/DCDB` polygons, and `GEBCO TID` raster; overlap with existing `< 10 yr` modern `MBES` justified explicitly or re-routed. — *Evidence:* GIS screening map + summary coverage table (`km²` by `TID` class) approved by Chief Scientist. — *Ref:* [ch55-public-products.md](../../fable/chapters/ch55-public-products.md)
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Flag `TID 40` (gravity-predicted `SRTM15+`) and `TID 41–44` / `70–72` regions as unverified navigation hazards:** Because satellite altimetry bathymetry (`TID 40`) carries `100–200 m` vertical error (`1σ`) and misses sub-`10 km` seamounts (USS *San Francisco* 2005 grounding precedent), prohibit assuming `GEBCO TID 40` / `41–44` / `70–72` depths represent clear under-keel depth for towed gear, `MVP` fish, or ship routing over uncharted rises. — *Threshold:* Minimum `500 m` vertical clearance margin applied to towed bodies and `MVP` maximum cast depth over `TID 40` / `41–44` / `70–72` terrain until real-time `MBES` confirms seabed depth. — *Evidence:* Bridge & Watchstander Hazard Overlay imported into `ECDIS`/`Qinsy`/`SIS`. — *Ref:* [ch56-case-files.md](../../fable/chapters/ch56-case-files.md)
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Prepare a formal Transit-Mapping ("Mapping While Steaming") & Cross-Modality White-Ribbon Plan:** Coordinate with Master and Port Office to run primary hull `MBES` continuously during `> 200 m` port-to-box transits (adjusted `5–10 km` laterally off existing `R2R` tracks where fuel/schedule permits), and verify coastal white-ribbon overlap (`>= 50–100 m` horizontal band) where survey joins airborne bathylidar, `USV`, or `AUV` operations. — *Threshold:* Transit corridor offset logic + `XBT`/`MVP` underway allotment budgeted across `100 %` of international/open-ocean transit legs; shared junction zone defined if multi-modal. — *Evidence:* Signed Transit Mapping & White-Ribbon Annex in Cruise Plan. — *Ref:* [multimodal-white-ribbon.md](../_shared/multimodal-white-ribbon.md)

## 4. Climatology, GNSS Space Weather, Spares, Medical & Standby Governance

- [ ] **[REC]** **Run historical sea-state climatology and GNSS/RF interference screening for the operational window:** Quantify historical significant wave height (`H_s`), swell direction, and wind probabilities for the season (`tolerance` of `40–50 %` non-acquisition standby time for high-latitude winter multibeam projects), check solar-cycle `Kp >= 5` risk, and screen Black Sea, Baltic, Red Sea, or Eastern Mediterranean regions for active GNSS jamming/spoofing NOTAMs. — *Threshold:* Probabilistic weather-day reserve (`%` of sea days) explicitly added to schedule; GNSS `C/N0` / `AGC` monitoring flagged mandatory if operating within `500 km` of active EW advisories. — *Evidence:* Climatology & EW Risk Memo logged in MFP Cruise Folder. — *Ref:* [ch26-survey-planning.md](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md)
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Approve pre-sail Data Management Plan (`DMP`), directory/naming schema, spares kit & MFP Medical initiation:** Finalize `DMP` with funding sponsor and `R2R` cruise-registration schedule, enforce immutable raw stream folder layout (`YYYYMMDD_HHMMSS_Ship_LineID`), verify physical spares (`>= 2` calibrated `SVS` sensors, `XBT` probes for `4–6 h` transit cadence + `25 %` reserve, `MVP` tether/tailfin spares, `>= 2` offline `LTO-9`/`NVMe` sets), and initiate **UNOLS `MFP` Medical History / Fit-for-Duty forms (`>= 60 d` pre-sail)** for all berths. — *Threshold:* `100 %` spares packed, `DMP` approved `>= 30 d` pre-sail, and `100 %` science berths registered in `MFP` Medical portal `>= 45–60 d` prior to departure. — *Evidence:* Approved DMP + signed Spares Packing List + `MFP` Medical Roster Status. — *Ref:* [ch50-archiving-and-provenance.md](../../fable/chapters/ch50-archiving-and-provenance.md)
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Ratify Weather-Standby, Science-Abandonment, and Alternate-Site Contingency Matrix:** Define objective wave-height / pitch-roll thresholds (`H_s`, bubble-sweepdown percentage, `> 30 %` outer-beam dropout) triggering speed reduction, leeward alternate-polygon switching, or temporary science pause, alongside explicit Master + Chief Scientist decision authority for premature cruise termination or port shelter. — *Threshold:* Primary + `>= 2` sheltered alternate mapping boxes loaded in survey planner with clear go/no-go thresholds. — *Evidence:* Co-signed Contingency & Abandonment Protocol attached to Ship Sailing Orders. — *Ref:* [sec-13-02.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-13/sec-13-02.md)
- [ ] **[REC]** **Budget dedicated calibration, patch-test, and wire-cast hours explicitly in ship time ledger:** Reserve `4–8 h` pre-production sea trials (`GAMS` figure-8, `4-step` patch test, `K-Sync`, `VIV` speed steps) plus explicit `10–15 min` XBT and `30–60 min` CTD station blocks so science sampling never squeezes out geometric calibration. — *Threshold:* `100 %` calibration and SVP station time explicitly itemized in the `24 h/d` cruise schedule (`<= 5 %` unallocated time deficit). — *Evidence:* Itemized Cruise Time-Motion Schedule approved by Chief Scientist & Marine Tech. — *Ref:* [ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md)
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Lock software licensing (`SIS`/`Qimera`/`CARIS`/`POSPac`), R2R vessel-profile validation, and shipboard SOW:** Confirm all dongle/floating software licenses expire `>= 30 d` past demobilization, verify the ship's `R2R` vessel-profile port map matches installed serial/network feeds, and confirm satellite bandwidth quotas (`Starlink`/`VSAT`) support daily `DTON` and QC transfers. — *Threshold:* `0` unverified license expirations or unmatched `R2R` sensor endpoints prior to mobilization. — *Evidence:* Software License Audit & R2R Profile Match Receipt. — *Ref:* [appendix-f-software-index.md](../../fable/appendices/appendix-f-software-index.md)
