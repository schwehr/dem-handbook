## 1. Depth-Band Swath Geometry, Ping Speed Ceiling & Turn Dynamics

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Design depth-band line spacing using conservative effective swath multiples (`S = W (1 - f)`):** Plan `2–3x d` (`~110°–125°` total swath, outer beams `55°–62°`) in deep water (`> 1,000 m`) and `3–4x d` (`130°–145°`) across continental shelf/slope (`< 200–500 m`) with `10–30 %` sidelap (`f = 0.10–0.30`) so steeper shoal rises never open holidays between adjacent swaths. — *Threshold:* Zero planned coverage gaps on `GEBCO`/contour bathymetry assuming `15 %` shoaling tolerance and conservative outer-beam cutoff (`60°` deep / `65°` shelf). — *Evidence:* Exported `Qinsy`/`SIS`/`Hypack` line-plan overlay on `100 %` bathymetric base map. — *Ref:* [ch26-survey-planning.md](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md)
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Verify along-track ping-rate ceiling (`v_max`) against acoustic beam footprint:** Using exact flat-bed across-track footprint $D_{\text{fp,across}}(\theta) = d\,\beta_x / \cos^2\theta$ (`= 4 d β_x` at $\theta = 60^\circ$, `2 d β_x` at $45^\circ$, `8.55 d β_x` at $70^\circ$) and along-track footprint $D_{\text{fp,along}}(\theta) = d\,\beta_y / \cos\theta$ (`= 2 d β_y` at $60^\circ$, $\beta_{x,y}$ in `rad`) alongside two-way travel time $\tau = 2 R_{\max} / c$ (`~9–11 s` per single ping at `4,000 m` water depth, i.e., `~8,000 m` outer slant range at `60°` off-nadir), cap vessel survey speed `v_max` (or enable dual-swath / multi-ping mode where supported) so consecutive pings sample every `<= 1.0` footprint along track (deepwater) or hit required `>= 3–9` soundings per grid cell (shelf charting). — *Threshold:* Maximum survey speed computed per depth zone (`<= 4.1–5.1 m/s [8–10 kn]` steep continental slope single-swath; `<= 5.1–6.2 m/s [10–12 kn]` abyssal flat) yielding `0 %` along-track ping gaps. — *Evidence:* Ping-Rate & Speed Ceiling Worksheet signed by Mapping Lead. — *Ref:* [ch20-sonar.md](../../fable/chapters/ch20-sonar.md)
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Enforce `2–3 × T_c` (`30–90 s`) straight turn run-ins to eliminate causal heave-filter hook:** Because real-time causal heave filters (`T_c ≈ 20–30 s`) experience centripetal horizontal-acceleration bias during turns that decays exponentially over `2–3 × T_c`, extend every mainscheme line start/end run-in by `30–90 s` (`~150–450 m` at `5.1 m/s [10 kn]`) of steady straight-and-level steaming before logging primary survey cells; run initial figure-8 alignment before entering box 1. — *Threshold:* `100 %` of survey line plans include `>= 60 s` (or `>= 3 * T_c`) straight lead-in and lead-out segments outside the target polygon boundary. — *Evidence:* Line-plan waypoints verified in bridge ECDIS and `SIS`/`Qinsy` planner. — *Ref:* [sec-07-08.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-08.md)
- [ ] **[REC]** **Orient mainscheme tracks for terrain safety, swell trim, and backscatter azimuth consistency:** Run shelf/canyon contours parallel to isobaths where slope gradients threaten grounding or rapid swath pinching; bias dominant line azimuth within `20°–30°` of quartering/following swell when possible to minimize bow pitch/bubble sweepdown while keeping alternating line azimuths uniform for backscatter mosaics. — *Threshold:* Bubble-sweepdown orientation trade-off reviewed with Bridge watch officers before committing line azimuths. — *Evidence:* Line Orientation Memo signed by Chief Scientist & Chief Mate. — *Ref:* [sec-13-01.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-13/sec-13-01.md)
- [ ] **[REC]** **Set dynamic outer-beam angular cutbacks (`50°–75°`) for steep continental slope & abyssal noise control:** Pre-program depth-zone angular sector ceilings in `SIS`/`Qinsy` (`<= 70°–75°` max on flat shelf, narrowing dynamically to `<= 55°–62°` across abyssal/steep `> 15°` slopes) so low-grazing outer-beam range walk and extreme `tan^2(φ)` refraction drift do not pollute `CUBE` hypotheses. — *Threshold:* Pre-configured runtime sector limits documented per depth band; outer-beam cutoff keeps planned `TVU_95` within governing spec. — *Evidence:* Runtime Angular Sector Policy Table in Survey Plan. — *Ref:* [sec-10-04.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-10/sec-10-04.md)
- [ ] **[COND]** **Lock backscatter & `WCI` radiometric parameters (*Trigger:* Habitat mapping, sediment characterization, or gas-seep `WCI` objectives):** Freeze `CW` vs `FM` chirp pulse-mode transitions by depth bracket, prohibit unlogged manual TX-power/gain changes within a mosaic polygon, budget `WCI` `.wcd` storage (`~10–30 GB/h`), and schedule a backscatter calibration pass over a flat homogeneous sand/mud patch. — *Threshold:* `100 %` backscatter lines inside a polygon acquire with uniform TX-level/gain settings and budgeted `.wcd` disk capacity `+ 25 %` headroom. — *Evidence:* Backscatter/WCI Acquisition Spec Sheet & Disk Budget. — *Ref:* [ch20-sonar.md](../../fable/chapters/ch20-sonar.md)
- [ ] **[COND]** **Deconflict slow-speed `SBP`/`AUV`/`CTD` operations from high-speed `MBES` transit/box blocks (*Trigger:* Multi-disciplinary shared cruise):** Group `5.1–6.2 m/s (10–12 kn)` regional `MBES` mapping corridors separately from `1.5–2.6 m/s (3–5 kn)` Chirp `SBP`, towed-body, or `CTD`/`AUV` blocks so repeated speed changes and acoustic mode switches do not inflate heave-hook turn overhead or split swath grids. — *Threshold:* Separate line blocks and `K-Sync` profiles assigned per speed/sensor regime; zero unscheduled mid-line speed transitions. — *Evidence:* Multi-Sensor Block Schedule attached to Cruise Plan. — *Ref:* [ch26-survey-planning.md](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md)

## 2. Crosslines & Rank-4 Patch-Test Site Plan

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Allocate `>= 4–5 %` crosslines (`>= 8 %` on sparse deep grids) orthogonal to mainscheme direction:** Plan dedicated crosslines (`60°–90°` to mainscheme lines) spanning every planned depth band, acoustic mode switch (`deep` vs `medium` vs `shallow`), and `50°–65°` outer-beam overlaps to detect systematic refraction smiles, roll drift, or heave offsets. — *Threshold:* Crossline linear distance `>= 4.5 %` of mainscheme line-km across full depth span (increasing to `>= 8 %` where line spacing leaves sparse coverage). — *Evidence:* Line Plan summary metrics (`Crossline km / Mainscheme km >= 0.045`). — *Ref:* [ch20-sonar.md](../../fable/chapters/ch20-sonar.md)
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Pre-locate a rank-4 observable 4-step Patch Test site and lock strict execution order:** Select a dedicated local test site featuring both a flat abyssal/shelf floor and a crisp ridge/seamount (`>= 10–15°` slope, non-aliased footprint) and execute strictly in decoupled mathematical observability order: **Step 1 — Latency** (**collinear same-direction passes** over steep slope at `v_fast` vs `v_slow` at `2:1` speed ratio, isolating $\Delta x = (v_{\text{fast}} - v_{\text{slow}})\delta t$ without confounding Pitch!); **Step 2 — Pitch** (reciprocal opposing passes over sharp ridge at constant survey speed); **Step 3 — Roll** (reciprocal passes over flat seafloor); **Step 4 — Yaw** (**same-direction parallel offset tracks spaced `1.0–1.5x d` apart**, i.e., `~0.5–0.8x d` on each side of a distinct ridge/feature inside the usable `55°–60°` swath). — *Threshold:* Two independent repeats per step confirming residual offsets `< 0.005 s` latency, `< 0.05°` pitch/yaw, and `< 0.02°` roll (`1σ` repeatability). — *Evidence:* Patch-Test Polygon Plan + Post-Run Calibration Matrix in `Qimera`/`CARIS`/`MB-System`. — *Ref:* [sec-13-05.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-13/sec-13-05.md)

## 3. Dynamic Squat, Fuel-Burn Waterline Curve & MVP Cast Cadence

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Model Tuck/ICORELS & Barrass dynamic squat plus daily fuel-burn draft shift before sailing:** Compute expected bow/stern sinkage at survey speeds `3.1–6.2 m/s (6–12 kn)` and shallow shelf depth-to-draft ratios ($h/T$) using **Tuck/ICORELS** $S_{\text{Tuck}} = C_S (\nabla_{\text{disp}} / L_{\text{PP}}^2)\,F_{rh}^2 / \sqrt{1 - F_{rh}^2}$ ($C_S \approx 2.4$ displacement coefficient, $\nabla_{\text{disp}}$ displaced volume in $\text{m}^3$, $L_{\text{PP}}$ length between perpendiculars in $\text{m}$, $F_{rh} = v/\sqrt{g h} < 0.7$) and **Barrass** confined-channel $S_{\text{Barrass}} = C_B\,S_2^{2/3}\,V_k^{2.08}/30$ ($C_B$ block coefficient, $S_2 = A_S / (A_C - A_S)$ channel blockage ratio; or open-water $S_{\text{open}} \approx C_B V_k^2 / 100$ valid specifically for $h/T \in [1.1, 1.4]$, noting **Barrass overpredicts squat in unrestricted deep shelf waters $h/T > 3$**; speed $V_k$ in `kn`, $1\text{ kn} = 0.5144\text{ m/s}$), alongside daily fuel/water buoyancy rise (`~1–3 cm/day`). Note the opposite sign traps: unmodeled squat injects shoal bias while unmodeled fuel burn injects dangerous deep bias. — *Threshold:* Dynamic waterline curve $z_{\text{waterline}}(v, h, t)$ parameterized across `0–7.2 m/s (0–14 kn)` speeds and cruise days (`1–60 d`) loaded into processing plans; dynamic draft uncertainty kept `<= 0.08 m` (`1σ`). — *Evidence:* Speed-Draft-Squat Lookup Table attached to Survey Plan. — *Ref:* [sec-07-08.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-08.md)
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Lock dynamic `MVP` / `XBT` / `CTD` sound-velocity cast cadence and `WOA` deep-tail splice plan:** Set baseline spatial-temporal cast intervals by oceanography regime—`15–30 min` continuous `MVP` tow-yo cycling across coastal fronts, shelf breaks, or internal waves; `2–4 h` in stratified ocean margins; `<= 6–12 h` maximum in uniform abyssal basins—and pre-download `World Ocean Atlas (WOA)` climatology profiles for splicing below `MVP`/`XBT` terminal cast depth (`1,000–2,000 m`) down to abyssal seabed (`4,000–11,000 m`). — *Threshold:* XBT probe count + MVP wire winch hours cover planned cadence plus `25 %` eddy reserve; `WOA` deep-tail splice profiles validated to preserve monotonic density/sound-speed gradient continuity (`<= 0.5 m/s` step at splice). — *Evidence:* Cast Cadence Schedule + verified `WOA` splice directory loaded onboard. — *Ref:* [sec-10-04.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-10/sec-10-04.md)

## 4. Worked *A Priori* Deepwater vs. Shelf TPU Budget
