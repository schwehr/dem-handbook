## 1. MAC Baseline Review, Offset Survey & Axis Verification

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Review latest UNOLS Multibeam Advisory Committee (`MAC`) `SAT` / `QAT` Baseline Report:** Pull the vessel's most recent Sea Acceptance Test (**SAT**) and Quality Assurance Test (**QAT**) dossier from the MAC database; check maximum swath extinction depth vs speed, propeller cavitation/self-noise RPM curves, transducer element impedance logs, and backscatter beam-pattern normalization. — *Threshold:* All TX/RX array elements active (`>= 98 %` healthy elements, zero shorted impedance banks); vessel cruising RPM/pitch band kept below acoustic self-noise knee (`+6 dB` noise floor ceiling). — *Evidence:* Annotated MAC QAT Summary PDF in Main Lab binder. — *Ref:* [ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md)
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Verify pier/dry-dock total-station Vessel Offset Survey (e.g., `Westlake VSR`) via Two-Person Independent Check:** Compare dry-dock/pier total-station coordinates for the granite reference block (`POS MV` / `Seapath` IMU), primary and secondary GNSS antennas, `MBES` TX/RX acoustic centers, `SBP`, `ADCP`, and hull draft marks (`1σ` survey uncertainty `0.001–0.005 m`) directly against every sign and meter entry in `POS MV`, `SIS`, or `Qinsy`. — *Threshold:* Zero unverified offsets; **Phase Lead** and **Independent Verifier** both sign off `0.000 m` discrepancy across `X` (forward `+`), `Y` (starboard `+`), and `Z` (downward `+` in vessel NED frame) after translating from any ENU IMU convention. — *Evidence:* Signed Lever-Arm Sign-Off Sheet showing `POS MV` & `SIS`/`Qinsy` configuration screenshots. — *Ref:* [notation-and-symbols.md](../../gemini4/book/00-front-matter/notation-and-symbols.md)
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Check Induced-Heave Single-Application Rule (Zero Double Counting):** Verify in `POS MV` / `Seapath` and `SIS` / `Qinsy` that the IMU-to-transducer vertical/horizontal lever-arm compensation (`heave lever arm`) is applied **exactly once**—either transferred to transducer position inside the INS OR computed inside `SIS`/`Qinsy` from raw IMU center-of-rotation heave, never in both systems. — *Threshold:* Zero double-applied heave (`0.000 m` induced-heave residual tested by inspecting roll-coupled vertical heave on calm-pier or roll-trial logs). — *Evidence:* Screenshot proof of lever-arm parity + Two-Person Sign-Off. — *Ref:* [ch13-imu-ins.md](../../fable/chapters/ch13-imu-ins.md)

## 2. Over-the-Side / Moonpool Pole VIV & Dynamic Deflection

- [ ] **[COND]** **Inspect over-the-side or moonpool pole mount for Strouhal Vortex-Induced Vibration (`VIV`) and quadratic speed bending (*Trigger:* Portable coastal/regional `MBES` or `ADCP` rigged on an over-the-side pole or moonpool carriage):** Compute vortex-shedding frequency $f_s = \text{St}\,(v_{\text{ship}} / D_{\text{pole}})$ (`St ≈ 0.20` for circular steel/aluminum cylinders) and verify $f_s$ stays well clear of pole structural cantilever frequency $f_n$ (or fit helical strakes / hydrodynamic fairing); run progressive speed steps `2.1 -> 4.1 -> 6.2 m/s (4 -> 8 -> 12 kn)` monitoring inclinometer / dynamic pitch-roll delta proportional to $v_{\text{ship}}^2$. — *Threshold:* Dynamic bending deflection $\delta\theta \le 0.05^\circ$ (`1σ` stability tolerance for `IHO S-44 Order 1a / Special` shelf surveys—since $0.15^\circ$ bend at $60^\circ$ beam in $50\text{ m}$ injects $0.23\text{ m}$ vertical bias; $\le 0.15^\circ$ acceptable only for deepwater Order 2 / reconnaissance) and zero harmonic lock-in (`|f_s - f_n| / f_n >= 0.30`). — *Evidence:* Strouhal Worksheet + speed-step pitch/roll stability plot. — *Ref:* [sec-07-04.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-04.md)

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Measure pier-side static draft marks, seawater density, and tank soundings at departure (`t_0`):** Read port and starboard forward, midships, and aft draft marks simultaneously (averaging swell oscillation over `10` wave periods), sample ambient water density (`rho_w` in `kg/m^3` via calibrated hydrometer/CTD), and record Chief Engineer fuel, potable water, and ballast tank soundings to compute $z_{\text{waterline}}(t_0)$. — *Threshold:* Pier draft bias `<= 0.02 m` (`1σ`); freshwater/estuarine salinity sinkage ($\Delta z_\rho \approx 0.025\,d_{\text{draft}}$, `~12.5 cm` on a `5 m` draft hull) modeled explicitly before entering `SIS`/`Qinsy` waterline offset. — *Evidence:* Co-signed Chief Mate + Mapping Lead Initial Static Draft & Tank Ledger ([09-resources-and-templates.md](09-resources-and-templates.md)). — *Ref:* [sec-07-08.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-08.md)

## 4. GNSS/INS Heading (`GAMS`), `1PPS` / `PTP` Timing & Sound-Velocity Calibration

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Verify `1PPS` + `$GPZDA` baud rate (`>= 115,200 baud`) or IEEE 1588v2 `PTP` synchronization:** Confirm physical `1PPS` coaxial pulse (`< 1 us` jitter) locked onto `POS MV`/`Seapath` and `SIS`/`Qinsy`, confirm NMEA `$GPZDA` serial speed `>= 115,200 baud` (or native `PTP` synchronization) so slow serial buffering never injects the silent `1.000 s` timestamp lag, and verify `GPST = UTC + 18 s` handling. — *Threshold:* Measured timing offset residual `< 0.002 s` (`2 ms`) across `POS MV`, `SIS`, and `K-Sync` clock health monitors; zero dropped `1PPS` epochs over `15 min` soak test. — *Evidence:* Time-Sync Diagnostic Screenshot (`POS MV` Controller + `SIS` Clock Status). — *Ref:* [sec-32-02.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-32/sec-32-02.md)
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Execute `POS MV` `GAMS` calibration and load `igs20.atx` antenna phase-center model:** Verify primary-to-secondary GNSS baseline separation (`L >= 2.0–4.0 m`, yielding theoretical heading noise `0.1°·m / L <= 0.05°` `1σ`), load current `igs20.atx` antenna PCV/PCO files, enable raw `.000` / `T04` GNSS/IMU recording for post-cruise `SBET`, and complete figure-8 / hard-turn `GAMS` initialization outside pier multipath before starting survey lines. — *Threshold:* `GAMS` status steady `Ready / Online` with heading uncertainty `<= 0.05°` (`1σ`) and baseline solution variance within `<= 0.015 m` of pier offset survey baseline. — *Evidence:* `POS MV` `GAMS` Parameter Setup Screenshot + initial `.000` raw file verified on disk. — *Ref:* [ch12-gnss.md](../../fable/chapters/ch12-gnss.md)
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Cross-calibrate hull `SVS`, `MVP` fish, ship `CTD`, and `XBT` suite:** Perform simultaneous deck pressure tare (`0.0 dbar`), bucket/flow-through surface cross-check (`SVS` vs calibrated `CTD`), and pre-cruise cast comparison confirming consistent sound-speed equations (**TEOS-10**, **UNESCO Chen–Millero**, or **Del Grosso** matched cleanly without mixing `P_gauge` vs `P_abs`). — *Threshold:* `|c_SVS - c_CTD(0)| <= 0.5 m/s` (tolerance `1σ`) and `|c_MVP - c_CTD| <= 1.0 m/s` over common upper water column; zero expired XBT thermistor batches. — *Evidence:* Signed Sensor Sound-Velocity Cross-Check Log + calibration certificates in cruise binder. — *Ref:* [sec-10-01.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-10/sec-10-01.md)
