## Part A: Pre-Mobilization Permits, Exports, Insurance & Rate Governance

### A1. UNCLOS Part XIII Foreign Clearance, Environmental Acoustic Permits & Sovereignty

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Secure Foreign Marine Scientific Research (`MSR`) diplomatic clearance under UNCLOS Part XIII:** For any mapping, `MVP`/`XBT`/`CTD` profiling, or transit logging inside a foreign Territorial Sea (`<= 22.2 km [12 nmi]`, `Art. 245`), Exclusive Economic Zone (`<= 370.4 km [200 nmi]`, `Art. 246`), or legal Continental Shelf, submit the U.S. State Department Office of Ocean and Polar Affairs **RATS** (*Research Application Tracking System*) dossier well ahead of the statutory `6-month` deadline (`Art. 248`) so host-state conditions (`Art. 249` shipboard observer berth, pre-release review, no-ping corridors) are binding before mobilization. — *Threshold:* RATS package submitted `>= 7 months` prior to target foreign-water entry; signed diplomatic Note Verbale / written coastal-state consent on the Bridge before entering EEZ/territorial waters. — *Evidence:* Bridge folder containing Note Verbale, RATS tracking ID, `Art. 249` conditions, and observer berth roster. — *Ref:* [ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md)
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Obtain Marine Mammal Protection Act (`MMPA`) / Endangered Species Act (`ESA`) acoustic authorizations:** Secure `IHA`, `LOA`, and `ESA` Biological Opinion (plus National Marine Sanctuary / Essential Fish Habitat permits if applicable) covering `MBES`, `SBP`, `ADCP`, `USBL`, and seismic/WCI sources; align mitigation watch protocols with **`NMFS Technical Guidance (2018)`** permit-defined acoustic harassment/exclusion zones and **`JNCC (2017)`** (`60 min` pre-start visual search in water depths `> 200 m`, `30 min` in `<= 200 m`, plus `20–40 min` soft-start ramp-up), documenting `12 kHz` precautions citing the 2008 Madagascar melon-headed whale stranding (`IWC 2013`). — *Threshold:* Valid IHA/LOA/BiOp permits and qualified `MMO` berths locked prior to first ping; exact permit exclusion radii posted in Main Lab & Bridge. — *Evidence:* Signed IHA/LOA/BiOp permit binder + MMO watch schedule. — *Ref:* [ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md)
- [ ] **[COND]** **Complete cultural heritage, telecom cable, and Indigenous CARE/OCAP® governance clearances (*Trigger:* Operating within coastal/ancestral waters, `UNESCO 2001` wreck zones, or submarine cable corridors):** Record written consent/escrow agreements under **CARE** (*Collective Benefit, Authority to Control, Responsibility, Ethics*) and **OCAP®** (*Ownership, Control, Access, Possession*) principles, secure `NHPA § 106` / `ARPA` archaeological clearance, and plot `ICPC` submarine cable exclusion buffers (`>= 500–1,000 m` off-bottom towed/corer exclusion). — *Threshold:* Zero unpermitted operations inside cultural buffers or Indigenous-governed waters; signed data-escrow protocol if resolution masking applies. — *Evidence:* Signed CARE/OCAP® Data Agreement + ECDIS Cable/Sanctuary Exclusion Layer. — *Ref:* [sec-27-01.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-01.md)

### A2. Export Control (ITAR/EAR), Customs & Naval Sanitization

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Screen payload hardware and personnel under `ITAR` (`22 CFR 120–130`) and `EAR` (`15 CFR 730–774`):** Classify high-grade IMUs (`ECCN 7A003`), anti-spoof/precision GNSS (`7A005`), military/deep-towed sonars (`6A001`), and `AUV` interfaces (`8A001`); verify institutional Export Control Officer sign-off for all foreign-national scientists/techs accessing raw sensor configurations or shipping gear to foreign ports via **ATA Carnet** or `TMP`/`BAG` license exceptions. — *Threshold:* `100 %` of portable hardware ECCNs, foreign-national Deemed Export reviews, and stamped ATA Carnet serial inventories completed `>= 45 d` prior to port shipment. — *Evidence:* Export Control Clearance Memo + stamped Carnet manifest. — *Ref:* [ch68-legal-issues.md](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md)
- [ ] **[COND]** **Confirm naval security and host-nation bathymetric sanitization rules (*Trigger:* Naval Escort, Defense-Funded Cruise, or Restricted Host Jurisdiction):** Lock post-acquisition masking rules (e.g., `400 m` public grid vs classified full resolution; Indian 2021 DST geospatial rule restricting bathymetry finer than `< 10 m` within `500 m` of shore or `< 100 m` beyond `500 m` so exported grids must be coarsened to `>= 10 m` / `>= 100 m`; or China 2017 Surveying Law bans) prior to logging so dual-partition storage isolates restricted tracks cleanly. — *Threshold:* Written Security Classification Guide / Host Sanitization Addendum signed before departure. — *Evidence:* Approved Security Segregation Plan. — *Ref:* [sec-27-03.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-03.md)

### A3. Hazmat, Radiation (RSO), Lithium Batteries & Insurance

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Clear MFP Chemical Hazmat, `SDS` Binders, and Radioisotope (`RSO`) Authorizations:** Submit full chemical/hazmat inventory (`kg`/`L` by hazard class) in `MFP`, supply `100 %` up-to-date Safety Data Sheets (**SDS**), stock compatible spill-containment kits, and secure institutional **Radiation Safety Officer (`RSO`)** transfer approval plus pre-sail baseline contamination wipe (**SWAB**) tests for any radioisotope work. — *Threshold:* Zero undeclared chemicals or unapproved isotopes; baseline pre-sail SWAB results strictly `<= institutional RSO release limit (____ dpm/100 cm^2)` (`100 %` documented). — *Evidence:* Marine Supt approved MFP Hazmat Manifest + signed RSO SWAB Baseline Report. — *Ref:* [09-resources-and-templates.md](09-resources-and-templates.md)
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Validate Lithium Battery Transport (`IATA DGR 67th Ed. 2026`, `IMO IMDG`, `49 CFR 171–180`):** Confirm `UN 38.3` test summaries for all portable AUV/release/UPS/PLB batteries; under **IATA DGR 67th Ed. (eff. Jan 1, 2026)** ship air-freighted Li-ion cells (`UN 3480 PI965` Cargo Aircraft Only `CAO`, and **`UN 3481 PI966` `> 2.7 Wh` packed with equipment**) strictly at mandatory `<= 30 %` State of Charge (`SoC`, with `<= 30 %` recommended for `PI967`), segregate primary Li-metal packs (`UN 3090 CAO / 3091`) in certified fire-rated deck storage, and comply with **`IMO IMDG Code`** (ocean freight) and **`49 CFR Parts 171–180`** (U.S. ground `> 300 Wh`). — *Threshold:* `100 %` battery packs carry `UN 38.3` summaries, `<= 30 %` air-freight SoC logs (`PI965`/`PI966`), and `DGD` paperwork prior to port delivery. — *Evidence:* Signed DGD Packet + Battery Cage Inspection Sheet. — *Ref:* [09-resources-and-templates.md](09-resources-and-templates.md)
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Verify Vessel Insurance (`H&M`/`P&I`/`Jones Act`/`USL&H`) vs Science Equipment Floater & `C&V` Coverage:** Audit operator Hull & Machinery (**H&M**), Protection & Indemnity (**P&I**, policy limit `________`), **Jones Act / Maintenance & Cure / MEL** (or **MLC 2006** / flag-state marine employer cover for non-U.S. flag ships), dockside **`USL&H`** for port-side mob crews, **Foreign Voluntary Workers' Compensation (`FVWC`) + Medevac**, **Waiver of Subrogation**, and area-specific **Institute Warranty Limits (`IWL`)** navigating endorsements; verify sponsor/operator risk-allocation terms and secure a separate **PI/Institutional Scheduled Marine / Ocean Cargo & Subsea Equipment Floater** backed by an underwriter **Condition & Value (`C&V`) valuation appraisal** (`+ E&O` where charted deliverables apply), with **`COI` expirations `>= 30–60 d` beyond scheduled demobilization**. — *Threshold:* Active `COIs` + `C&V` asset schedule covering `100 %` portable van/sensor replacement value verified `>= 14 d` pre-sail (`>= 30 d` post-demob expiry cushion). — *Evidence:* Completed [template-insurance-coi.md](../_shared/template-insurance-coi.md) + verified broker COIs. — *Ref:* [ch68-legal-issues.md](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md)

### A4. Indicative UNOLS Daily Rate Ledger & Standby Budget Lock

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Lock charter burn rate, transit mob days, MMO/freight pass-throughs, and weather-standby burn ceiling:** Baseline project budget against **indicative** UNOLS day-rate bands (`$15k–$25k/day` Regional/Coastal, `$25k–$40k/day` Ocean/Intermediate, `$40k–$60k+/day` Global Class—verify current vessel-specific operator quote; [sec-07-04.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-04.md) cites `$30k–$80k/day` ocean-ship benchmarks), explicitly line-item **MMO/PSO daily labor + berths**, **ATA Carnet/customs/hazmat freight**, fuel/port-agent surcharges, and set a Chief Scientist daily spend alert before eroding weather-standby reserve (`40–50 %` winter multibeam `tolerance`). — *Threshold:* Budget ledger reconciles operator-quoted ship days, mob/demob transit, MMO/freight line items (`<= 15 %` overrun buffer), and explicit `$/km²` unit cost threshold. — *Evidence:* Signed Cost Baseline in [template-cost-ledger.md](../_shared/template-cost-ledger.md). — *Ref:* [ch28-reducing-cost.md](../../fable/chapters/ch28-reducing-cost.md)

## Part B: Pre-Sail & Daily Underway Safety, Fatigue, Cyber & Spend Gates

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Verify `100 %` UNOLS `MFP` Medical History / Fit-for-Duty Clearances prior to gangway boarding:** Confirm with vessel Marine Superintendent / Medical Officer that every scientist, tech, and MMO boarding the vessel holds an active, cleared UNOLS `MFP` Medical Screening (valid for operating region/remote offshore distance, with emergency medications declared privately to Ship Medical Officer). — *Threshold:* `100 %` personnel on Ship's Articles cleared `Fit-for-Duty` in `MFP` prior to departure (`0` un-cleared riders boarding). — *Evidence:* Confidential Marine Supt / Master Medical Go-No-Go Roster Confirmation. — *Ref:* [09-resources-and-templates.md](09-resources-and-templates.md)
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Enforce `UNOLS RVSS 11th Ed. (2021, rev. 2023)` Appendix A & B Rigging Gates:** Inspect all winches, A-frames, cranes, sheaves, and wires against **RVSS 11th Ed. Appendix A** (`>= 5.0` default rope/wire safety factor, with reduced `2.5 / 2.0 / 1.5` factors permitted only under continuous line-tension monitoring and documented approval) and **Appendix B** (*Overboard Handling Systems* & `Overboard Handling Data Document` [`OHDD`] dynamic shock factors); verify the **Ship Master's final legal authority** over all over-side deployments (`MVP`, pole, `CTD`), certified PPE (hard hats, steel-toe boots, work vests, harnesses), and clear snap-back exclusion zones. — *Threshold:* Zero expired wire terminations, zero uncertified shackles, and `100 %` `OHDD` load-factor verification completed before leaving pier. — *Evidence:* Chief Mate / Marine Tech signed RVSS Pre-Sail Rigging Inspection Sheet. — *Ref:* [09-resources-and-templates.md](09-resources-and-templates.md)
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Execute Pre-Departure Safety Briefing, Immersion Suit Fit Test & MOB Drill Protocol:** Complete vessel safety indoctrination, assign lifeboat/fire/MOB stations, physically don survival/immersion suits (`<= 60 s` target donning time), and verify `ECDIS`/`AIS-MOB` drill protocol before clearing the departure sea buoy (`<= 24 h` of sailing per SOLAS/46 CFR). — *Threshold:* `100 %` science party signed onto Muster List with fitted immersion suit and confirmed PFD inspection. — *Evidence:* Ship Master's Log entry + signed Science Safety Muster Roster. — *Ref:* [template-accident-card.md](../_shared/template-accident-card.md)
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Enforce `STCW` Mandatory Crew Rest (`46 CFR § 15.1111`) and Science Watch Fatigue Limits:** Verify bridge/deck/engineering crew maintain statutory STCW rest (`>= 10 h` rest in any `24 h` period, split into `<= 2` periods with one `>= 6 h` unbroken and `<= 14 h` max between rests, plus `>= 77 h` rest per `7 d`) and lock science party watch rotation (`4-on / 8-off` or strictly capped `12 h` split shifts with zero double-shift `MVP`/`SIS` troubleshooting). — *Threshold:* `0` watchstanders exceeding `14 h` duty in `24 h` or falling below `77 h` rest per `7 d` rolling window without written Master/Chief Scientist fatigue waiver. — *Evidence:* Daily Bridge STCW Log + Posted Lab Watch Schedule. — *Ref:* [ch26-survey-planning.md](../../fable/chapters/ch26-survey-planning.md)
- [ ] **[REC]** **Maintain `JNCC (2017)` / `NMFS (2018)` acoustic MMO watch and soft-start logs underway:** Log pre-start visual clearance (**`60 min` in water depths `> 200 m`**, **`30 min` in `<= 200 m`** per `JNCC 2017`), `20–40 min` soft-start ramp-up timestamps, and immediate shutdown/pause events whenever protected species enter permit-defined exclusion zones. — *Threshold:* `100 %` compliance with `IHA`/`LOA`/`JNCC` mitigation timeline across every cruise startup or line-pause recovery. — *Evidence:* Timestamped MMO Acoustic Log cross-matched to `SIS`/`Qinsy` ping timestamps. — *Ref:* [ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md)
- [ ] **[REC]** **Verify shipboard cyber hygiene, air-gapped acquisition VLAN, and hull biofouling quarantine:** Isolate `MBES`/`POS MV`/`K-Sync`/workstation network from vessel satellite/crew Wi-Fi (`Starlink`/`VSAT`), scan portable drives on quarantine machine before transfer, and document hull/moonpool/MVP biofouling inspection prior to entering protected or foreign ports. — *Threshold:* `0` unapproved USB mounts on acquisition hosts; `0` unauthorized external ports open on survey VLAN; pre-entry hull biofouling log signed if port requires quarantine. — *Evidence:* Marine Tech Network Topology Check + Biofouling Declaration. — *Ref:* [sec-27-03.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-27/sec-27-03.md)
- [ ] **[REC]** **Track daily burn-rate and standby hours against budgeted mapping yield:** Update cumulative cruise days, transit vs mapping vs weather-standby hours, and daily `km²` mapped (`$/km²` real-time metric) at the daily `08:00` Master/Chief Scientist meeting. — *Threshold:* Variance `> 10 %` in weather-standby burn triggers immediate switch to sheltered alternate survey boxes. — *Evidence:* Updated daily log in [template-cost-ledger.md](../_shared/template-cost-ledger.md). — *Ref:* [ch28-reducing-cost.md](../../fable/chapters/ch28-reducing-cost.md)
