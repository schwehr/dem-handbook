## 2. Pre-Sail Card Arming & Life-Safety Action Checklists

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Arm laminated Bridge and Main-Lab Emergency Action Cards with filled vessel numbers prior to departure:** Post completed weatherproof copies of this checklist and [template-accident-card.md](../_shared/template-accident-card.md) beside the Bridge chart table and Main-Lab `MBES`/`MVP` winch watch console with verified `VHF Ch 16 / Ch 70`, `Iridium`/`Starlink`, `DPA`, `USCG Sector`, `MRCC`, `TMAS` tele-medical, `NRC`, `NSF`, and `RSO` numbers. — *Threshold:* `100 %` contact rows filled, laminated, and initialed by Ship Master and Chief Scientist before dropping lines. — *Evidence:* Posted Laminated Cards checked on pier-side Safety Walkthrough. — *Ref:* [template-accident-card.md](../_shared/template-accident-card.md)
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Execute Man Overboard (`MOB`) immediate recovery sequence:** Shout `"MAN OVERBOARD, PORT/STARBOARD"`, immediately press `MOB` on `ECDIS` and `SIS`/`Qinsy` (capturing exact GNSS/drift coordinates), deploy illuminated lifering + smoke float + `AIS MOB` beacon, keep a dedicated spotter pointing continuously at the person in water, notify Bridge so the Master executes a Williamson/Anderson turn and launches the fast rescue boat / recovery cradle, and initiate controlled hypothermia rewarming upon recovery. — *Threshold:* `MOB` mark logged `< 5 s` of sighting; bridge notified `< 15 s`; immersion-suit/rescue recovery launched under Master direction. — *Evidence:* Timestamped `ECDIS`/`SIS` `MOB` Track + Deck Log entry. — *Ref:* [ch16-platforms.md](../../fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md)
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Respond to Winch, A-Frame, Crane, or `MVP` Wire Failure & Snap-Back:** Immediately hit emergency stop / brake on winch or crane HPU, clear all personnel out of wire snap-back corridors, notify Bridge and render trauma first aid, **immediately tag out the failed hoisting system as disqualified under `UNOLS RVSS 11th Ed.` Appendix A/B**, and bag the parted wire ends untouched (no torching or degreasing) for metallurgical forensics. — *Threshold:* `0` personnel permitted inside snap-back zones post-failure; `100 %` failed rigging locked out (`LOTO` tag) until shore crane/wire recertification. — *Evidence:* Signed RVSS Equipment Disqualification Tag + Chain-of-Custody Photos. — *Ref:* [sec-07-04.md](../../gemini4/book/ch-07/sec-07-04.md)
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Execute Grounding, Allision, or Collision vessel-stabilization and bathymetric evidence freeze:** On Bridge alarm or hull shock, assist Master by closing watertight boundaries, conducting manual tank/void soundings, checking perimeter water for fuel sheen (`NRC` trigger), halting non-essential hull acoustic transmissions if grounding risk persists, and immediately locking `ECDIS`, `VDR`, `SIS`/`Qinsy`, and `POS MV` depth files read-only as legal evidence (`USS Guardian` / `QE2` precedent). — *Threshold:* All tank soundings completed `< 15 min`; `VDR` and `MBES` bathymetry frozen untouched `< 30 min` of stabilization. — *Evidence:* Bridge Tank Sounding Sheet + Read-Only `VDR`/`SIS` Evidence Snapshot. — *Ref:* [ch56-case-files.md](../../fable/chapters/ch56-case-files.md)
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Contain Hazmat, Chemical, or Radioisotope (`RSO`) spills underway:** Evacuate non-essential personnel upwind/up-deck, isolate lab HVAC dampers, notify Bridge (`VHF Ch 16`/internal phone), pull exact chemical sheet from Main-Lab **SDS** binder, deploy compatible spill kit only within trained PPE limits, and—if radioisotopes are involved—seal ventilation, execute immediate **SWAB** wipe survey inside containment perimeter, and contact institutional **Radiation Safety Officer (`RSO`)**. — *Threshold:* Spill perimeter quarantined `< 5 min`; `SDS` hazard class and spill volume (`L`/`kg`) reported to Master `< 15 min` for `NRC` assessment. — *Evidence:* Incident SDS Log + Post-Cleanup RSO SWAB Clearance Sheet. — *Ref:* [09-resources-and-templates.md](09-resources-and-templates.md)
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Execute Medical Evacuation (`Medevac`) ship-lab coordination with medical/PII privacy safeguards:** Report casualty to Bridge & Ship Medical Officer immediately, initiate tele-medical (`TMAS`) consultation, coordinate `USCG` / Maritime RCC helo hoist or fast vessel transfer under Master command, clear aft working deck of portable poles/antennas/loose gear (`0` foreign-object debris hazard), match ship heading/speed to helo wind envelope, and transmit sealed **medical-PII-protected** patient summary exclusively to Medical/SAR/authorized Next-of-Kin channels. — *Threshold:* Aft deck cleared of FOD hazards `< 30 min` prior to helo ETA; emergency hoist team briefed in PPE; zero unencrypted medical PII posted on open lab shares. — *Evidence:* Master's Medevac Readiness Check + Sealed Medical Transfer Receipt. — *Ref:* [template-accident-card.md](../_shared/template-accident-card.md)

## 3. Catastrophic Data Loss, GNSS EW Quarantine & Forensic Chain of Custody

- [ ] **[GATE]** **Isolate undamaged `RAID`/`LTO-9`/`NVMe` media & suppress Lithium battery thermal runaway during Fire or Flood:** Upon lab fire or flooding, prioritize clean-agent suppression on AC server racks while using **copious seawater deluge / thermal cooling for Li-ion (`UN 3480/3481`) thermal runaway** and reserving **Class D dry powder strictly for primary Li-metal (`UN 3090`) cells** (never rely on Halon/CO2 alone to arrest Li-ion cell runaway); NEVER attempt blind `RAID` array rebuilds on degraded disks at sea, relocate surviving offline **LTO-9** tapes and **NVMe** vault drives into waterproof Pelican cases in a safe compartment, and image failing drives strictly bit-for-bit (`ddrescue`) only ashore or under Tech supervision. — *Threshold:* Offline Vault Copy (`Copy 3`) physically secured away from wet/fire compartment `< 10 min` once life safety is clear. — *Evidence:* Vault Custody Transfer Log signed by Lead Marine Tech. — *Ref:* [ch50-archiving-and-provenance.md](../../fable/chapters/ch50-archiving-and-provenance.md)
- [ ] **[COND]** **Quarantine GNSS Electronic-Warfare (`EW`) Spoofing / Jamming telemetry (*Trigger:* Unexplained `> 5 m` navigation offset, `GAMS` loss accompanied by sharp `C/N0`/`AGC` excursion, or falsified UTC timestamps):** Switch bridge navigation immediately to radar + optical/gyro dead-reckoning, preserve raw `.000` / `T04` GNSS observables (`RINEX`, `C/N0`, `AGC`, `OSNMA`) and `POS MV` IMU logs without overwriting, and log UTC onset/recovery times for naval/maritime reporting. — *Threshold:* `100 %` raw GNSS observable epochs retained over anomaly window; survey box paused or logged as `EW_QUARANTINE`. — *Evidence:* Quarantined GNSS Raw Archive + UTC EW Anomaly Report. — *Ref:* [ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md](../../fable/chapters/ch69-security-sovereignty-privacy-ethics.md)
- [ ] **[GATE]** **Execute Statutory Notifications (`USCG`, `SMI`, `NRC`, `OSHA`, `MRCC`), Policy Notices (`UNOLS`/`NSF`/`P&I`), & `SHA-256` Forensic Custody (`FRE 901/902`):** Ensure the Master and Institutional Lead dispatch all statutory notices within exact table clocks (`Immediate` verbal `USCG`/`NRC`/`MRCC`, `2 h` / `32 h` `SMI` testing, `8 h` / `24 h` `OSHA`, `5 d` `CG-2692`) plus policy insurer alerts (`1–24 h`), immediately lock `VDR`, `ECDIS`, raw `.kmall`/`.all`, `POS MV` `.000`, offset survey records, and winch-tension logs read-only with cryptographic **`sha256sum`** manifests (**`FRE 901/902(13)–(14)`** electronic self-authentication alongside `*Daubert* FRE 702` technical validity), and keep **medical / `SMI` toxicology records strictly segregated under PII privacy locks**. — *Threshold:* `100 %` statutory clocks met with confirmation IDs; `sha256sum` forensic bundle generated `<= 6 h` of stabilization with zero unredacted medical PII in general technical shares. — *Evidence:* Completed Regulatory Notification Tracker + Signed `SHA-256` Forensic Bundle. — *Ref:* [ch68-legal-issues.md](../../fable/chapters/ch68-legal-issues.md)
- [ ] **[COND]** **Enforce Personal/Medical PII Breach Containment & `GDPR Art. 33` (`<= 72 h`) Notification Clock (*Trigger:* Ransomware, cyber breach, unencrypted cloud transfer, or misdelivery exposing `Medevac`, `SMI` toxicology, or EU/EEA personnel PII):** Isolate affected host/storage accounts immediately while preserving volatile forensic logs (do not wipe disk/memory), revoke compromised tokens/keys, notify Institutional DPO/CISO/Legal, and submit supervisory-authority breach notification within **`<= 72 h`** under **`GDPR Art. 33`** wherever EU/EEA or equivalent data-protection rules apply. — *Threshold:* Compromised endpoints isolated `< 1 h`; `GDPR Art. 33` supervisory notification (`<= 72 h`) filed and receipt logged when triggered. — *Evidence:* DPO Breach Assessment Ticket + Credential Revocation & Isolation Log. — *Ref:* [template-accident-card.md](../_shared/template-accident-card.md)
- [ ] **[COND]** **Enforce Post-Impact Hull Dive, Structural, and 4-Step Patch-Test Re-Verification Gate (*Trigger:* Any grounding touch, severe slamming wave impact, gondola/pole strike, or crane drop):** Prohibit resuming science bathymetry until an approved underwater hull/gondola inspection confirms zero transducer face cracking or mount misalignment, followed by a complete decoupled **4-Step Patch Test** (collinear Latency, reciprocal Pitch/Roll, `1.0–1.5x d` offset Yaw) comparing new offsets against pre-incident MAC/VSR values. — *Threshold:* Measured patch-test shift `> 0.05°` roll/pitch or `> 0.2°` yaw triggers mandatory re-survey of post-impact lines and formal dry-dock re-survey request. — *Evidence:* Diver/ROV Gondola Inspection Video + Pre/Post Patch-Test Delta Report. — *Ref:* [ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md](../../fable/chapters/ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md)
