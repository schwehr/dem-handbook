## 2. Product Vision, Target Users, and Design Philosophy

### 2.1 Vision
Build a practitioner-grade, modality-specific suite of operational checklists under `checklists/` for planning, permitting, insuring, executing, monitoring, demobilizing, processing, validating, and learning from Digital Elevation Model (DEM) and bathymetric data-collection campaigns.

Whereas the existing handbook drafts in [fable/](../../fable/README.md) (73 chapters, 9 appendices including [Appendix G](../../fable/appendices/appendix-g-checklists.md)) and [gemini4/](../../gemini4/TOC.md) (35 chapters) excel at measurement physics, geodesy, standards crosswalks, uncertainty propagation, and forensic validation, they provide only high-level generic checklists and omit deep operational realities such as platform-specific pre-deployment engineering, safety checks, accident/emergency response, maritime/aviation/E&O insurance, hazmat battery logistics, permitting lead times, and cost-tracking ledgers. This checklist suite closes that gap by turning handbook theory and external operational manuals into signed, auditable field-ready gates.

### 2.2 Target Personas
1. **Principal Investigator / Chief Scientist / Project Manager:** Owns requirements definition, funding/cost tracking, UNOLS/agency scheduling, permitting timelines, risk/insurance sign-off, and post-survey retrospective.
2. **Survey Lead / Hydrographer / Lead Mapping Engineer:** Owns datum/epoch specification, mission/flight/line planning, calibration (patch test, boresight, GCPs), pre-survey TPU/error budgeting, underway QC triggers, and acceptance testing.
3. **Platform / Field / Flight / Watch Operations Crew:** Executes hardware prep, pre-launch/pre-flight safety checks, support-gear deployment (tide gauges, GNSS bases, USBL/LBL beacons, targets), field data backups, emergency/accident response, and demobilization.
4. **Data Processor / QA/QC Reviewer / Contracting Officer:** Executes trajectory/sensor processing, cross-source validation, uncertainty budgeting, deliverable formatting (COG, COPC, BAG, S-102, STAC), release reviews, and contract acceptance.

### 2.3 Core Design Principles
- **Every item is actionable and signable:** No vague prose ("check calibration"); every item states what must be checked, the pass/fail threshold or reference formula, what artifact proves completion, and whether it is a hard **`[GATE]`** (equivalent to `(must)` in `fable/` Appendix G) before proceeding.
- **Self-contained per modality subdirectory (Offline Binder Rule):** Field teams on vessels, aircraft, or remote sites with intermittent connectivity must be able to take a single modality directory (e.g., `checklists/underwater-auv/`) plus `checklists/_shared/` offline as a complete operational binder (see [checklists/README.md](../../checklists/README.md) §4). Every `[GATE]` in `01`–`08` MUST inline actionable numeric thresholds, equations, and statutory clocks so no gate fails offline, and `06-accidents-and-emergencies.md` + `template-accident-card.md` MUST be printable as a single-page laminated emergency card while still linking back to canonical theory in `fable/` and `gemini4/`.
- **Strict coordinate, datum, sign, and unit discipline:** Explicitly state horizontal frame + realization + epoch, vertical datum + geoid/separation model, sign convention (elevation $+z\uparrow$ vs. depth $+d\downarrow$; ENU vs. NED body/sensor frames), and SI units (citing imperial equivalents in parentheses only where statute/regulation such as 14 CFR Part 107 400 ft AGL or USACE tolerances requires it, and forbidding ambiguous "feet").
- **Evidence-first validation:** Distinguish *"compiled/produced to meet"* from *"tested to meet"*; enforce strict independence between control/calibration points and withheld validation checkpoints (either $\sigma_{\text{cp}} \le \frac{1}{3}\sigma_{\text{product}}$ under legacy/LBS criteria, or ASPRS Ed. 2 compliance reporting $\text{RMSE}_{\text{rep}} = \sqrt{\text{RMSE}_{\text{test}}^2 + \sigma_{\text{cp}}^2}$ vs. inferring true underlying product error $\widehat{\sigma}_{\text{prod}} = \sqrt{\sigma_{\text{obs}}^2 - \sigma_{\text{cp}}^2}$); require raw data immutability (3-2-1 backup + cryptographic checksums).

## 4. Functional & Lifecycle Requirements (Prompt Questions + Missed Critical Domains)

Every one of the 7 modality subdirectories MUST explicitly address all 19 core operational questions raised in the prompt (Sections 4.1–4.19), plus the 9 cross-cutting planning domains identified in Section 4.20 (answering Question 20's open-ended mandate). Question 21 (inventory and critique of existing resources) is satisfied both in Section 6 of this PRD and in each modality's dedicated resources module (`09-resources-and-templates.md`).

### 4.1 Survey Requirements Definition
- Translate the **decision and economic/safety loss function** (navigation UKC shoal bias, aviation tallest-obstacle bias, flood-model hydraulic connectivity, unbiased earthwork volume, or geomorphic change LoD) into formal specifications (`fable/chapters/ch03-fitness-for-use.md`).
- Specify governing standard and **exact edition/year**: e.g., IHO S-44 Ed. 6.2.0 (Oct 2024; maintenance update retaining Ed. 6.1.0 Table 1 TVU/THU a/b parameters while adding §3.8 seabed characteristics and a priori/a posteriori glossary clarifications; note `fable/` cites Ed. 6.1.0 baseline) order or survey matrix; IHO S-100 Ed. 5.2.0 / S-102 Ed. 3.0.0 (Dec 2024) / S-158:102 Ed. 1.0.0 (Dec 2025); NOAA OCS HSSD (2024 edition, restructured; check annual updates); USGS Lidar Base Specification 2024/2025 rev. A (QL0–QL3); ASPRS Positional Accuracy Standards Ed. 2 v2 (2023/2024, Addenda I–VI incl. UAS Add. V & Oblique Add. VI); USACE EM 1110-2-1003 (2013) / EM 1110-1-1000 (2015); ICAO Annex 15 / Doc 10066 PANS-AIM + RTCA DO-200B; IHO B-13 (SDB Ed. 1.0.0, Mar 2024).
- Lock in **surface ontology** (DSM vs. bare-earth DTM vs. DBM vs. seamless TBDEM; bridge/culvert/pier/wire/solar-panel/fluid-mud/snowpack inclusion rules; hydro-flattening vs. hydro-enforcement vs. hydro-conditioning).
- Lock in **CRS, realization, epoch, vertical datum, geoid/separation model, and units** (e.g., ITRF2020 or NAD83(2011) epoch 2010.0, or modernized NATRF2022/NAPGD2022/GEOID2022 [final public beta Sept 2026, target ~2027 adoption; NAD 83/NAVD 88 remain official pending formal NGS adoption]; MLLW/LAT via VDatum/VORF; positive-up $+z\uparrow$ vs. positive-down $+d\downarrow$; SI metres; explicit handling of legacy US Survey Foot deprecation after 2022-12-31).
- Define quantitative **resolution, density/coverage, feature detection, currency/valid-until epoch, and statistical accuracy metrics** (avoiding unqualified `±X cm`; distinguishing RMSE, 1σ, LE95, LE90, CE90, TVU/THU, NVA, VVA, NMAD, and spatial correlation length).

### 4.2 Platform and Sensor Preparation Tasks
- Mechanical/structural mounting inspection and **dimensional survey of lever arms** ($\text{GNSS} \leftrightarrow \text{IMU} \leftrightarrow \text{sensor}$) with signed axis diagrams (ENU vs. NED) and photo verification (`fable/chapters/ch13-imu-ins.md`, `ch25-calibration-infrastructure.md`).
- **Time-synchronization verification** (1PPS + NMEA ZDA / IEEE 1588 PTP / camera mid-exposure flash hot-shoe sync; verifying $\Delta t_{\text{sync}}$ latency below platform error budget, noting $1\text{ ms}$ at $50\text{ m/s} = 5\text{ cm}$ along-track).
- **Sensor calibration currency** (factory/ISO 17123/ISO 17025 certificates, ANTEX radome/antenna model verification, camera interior orientation/rolling-shutter calibration, CTD/SVS/quartz pressure lab calibration against reference standards, Allan variance IMU check).
- **Platform-specific engineering prep:**
  - *AUV:* Water-density ballasting & trim for site salinity/temperature, O-ring/pressure-housing vacuum/leak tests, deck housing overpressure/vent-valve safety, recovery-beacon (Iridium/Argos/VHF/pinger) bench test, compass/DVL calibration, BMS cell balance & UN 3480/3090/3091 checks, emergency drop-weight burn-wire/corrodible-link installation.
  - *ASV/USV:* Hull integrity, watertight hatch/bilge-pump checks, lifting-bridle SWL certification, propulsion/thruster checks, dual-antenna GNSS baseline $\ge 0.8\text{–}1.5\text{ m}$, COLREGS navigation lights/day shapes/AIS/radar/EO-IR fusion, shore-ROC link & e-stop geofence bench test.
  - *UNOLS:* Ship-specific cruise planning manual review, MFP equipment CAD/power/deck-loading attachments, UNOLS RVSS (fleet safety standard) Appendix A/B crane/winch/wire SWL certificates, gondola/pole VIV/deflection check, science van tie-downs, shipboard MAC SAT/QAT baseline verification.
  - *Coastal Bathylidar & Land Aircraft:* STC / FAA Form 337 belly-port optical window inspection (cleanliness, dual-band AR coating, thermal/pressure bowing), laser chiller & electrical bus/alternator load margin checks, weight & balance recalculation, helicopter hardpoint STC / rotor-wash / door-off harness checks, oxygen systems, aircraft static/dynamic GNSS-INS integration.
  - *Land Drone:* Firmware/geofence unlock verification, Remote ID broadcast check, elastomeric/wire-rope vibration isolator health ($f_n \ll f_{\text{blade}}/\sqrt{2}$), propeller balance, LiPo cycle/internal-resistance log, shutter-type verification (global vs. rolling).
  - *Satellite:* Sensor tasking feasibility confirmation, RPC/rigorous model availability, orbit ephemeris tier, viewing/sun-angle constraints, commercial EULA derivative-rights verification before order placement.

### 4.3 Pre-Existing Documents and Data to Collect and Analyze
- **Geodetic & Tide Infrastructure:** National/CORS station coordinates, velocities, and time series (NGS, IGS, EPN, SONEL GNSS@TG); passive benchmark datasheets (recovery condition, stability code A–D, subsidence warnings); NWLON/GLOSS harmonic constituents and VDatum/VORF separation uncertainties.
- **Prior Elevation, Bathymetry & Sparse Quasi-Truth:** Prior USGS 3DEP / national lidar, NOAA NCEI BAGs, ENC/nautical charts (check CATZOC/QoBD), GEBCO/SRTM15+ **with TID grids**, Copernicus GLO-30/FABDEM, ArcticDEM/REMA/EarthDEM, and **ICESat-2 ATL03/ATL06/ATL08** + **GEDI** tracks (for reconnaissance of clear-water bathymetry, canopy height, and datum/bias sanity checks).
- **Environmental & Operational Hindcasts/Forecasts:** Multi-year cloud-cover climatology, MODIS/VIIRS/Sentinel-2 turbidity ($K_d$/Secchi), NDVI leaf-off phenology, snow cover, river discharge, wind/wave-height rose, tidal currents, space weather / geomagnetic $K_p$ and equatorial/auroral scintillation forecasts, and magnetic declination (WMM).
- **Regulatory, Airspace, Maritime & Hazard Overlays:** FAA Sectional charts, UAS Facility Maps, TFRs, NOTAMs, Local Notices to Mariners (LNMs), submarine cable/pipeline charts, UXO/wrecks, marine protected areas / critical habitat, land parcels/ROW, utility corridors, and GNSS interference/jamming advisories (MARAD, EASA).
- **Tectonic & Dynamic Crustal Context:** Secular plate velocities (HTDP, ITRF2020 PMM), active faults, recent earthquakes (coseismic deformation models), and local subsidence hotspots.

### 4.4 Pre-Planning Tasks
- **Fitness-for-use & platform break-even audit:** Verify chosen platform beats alternatives on cost, coverage, and accuracy (`fable/chapters/ch16-platforms.md` §16.11, `ch28-reducing-cost.md` §28.2.4).
- **Project Instructions / Survey Plan:** Governing spec + edition, GIS line plan, sensor/serial/firmware manifest, control/checkpoint map, datum/epoch/surface definition, environmental go/no-go bounds, daily QC triggers, acceptance criteria, and RACI roles.
- **Documentation & Data Hygiene Setup:** ISO 19115-1/-2 / FGDC / STAC metadata skeleton pre-populated; strict UTC/line-ID file-naming rules; raw-data immutability policy; SHA-256 manifest tooling; 3-2-1 backup hardware staged.
- **Logistics, Spares, Procurement & Staffing:** Critical spares kit (backup GNSS receiver, IMU cable, SVS/CTD probe, propellers, O-rings, SSDs) sized to replacement lead time; watch/flight-crew duty rotation; multi-agency co-funding or shared-mobilization check (3DEP BAA, NOMEC/Seabed 2030 transit mapping).

### 4.5 Support Infrastructure & Field Deployments
- **GNSS Reference Stations / Corrections:** Local base stations sited on published marks or dual 4+ hour OPUS static occupations; baseline length limits ($\le 15\text{–}20\text{ km}$ typical for PPK/RTK, tighter at high $K_p$); logging rate $\ge$ rover rate; dual-measurement (slant + vertical, two units, photographed) antenna height; backup base or PPP-AR plan; NTRIP cellular/radio coverage check.
- **Water-Level & Oceanographic Support:** Temporary project tide gauges leveled in closed loops to $\ge 3$ tidal benchmarks (`fable/` §26.3 minimum; 5–10 benchmarks recommended per NOAA CO-OPS / local stability risk; $\ge 30$-day simultaneous comparison where transferring datums, IOC Manual 14 / NOAA CO-OPS 2); CTD/SVP/MVP/XBT casts and surface SVS cross-checks; turbidity/Secchi/radiometer logging for bathylidar and SDB; wave buoys for swell/sea-state limits.
- **Calibration Targets & Sites:** Boresight flight site (flat hard tarmac + pitched gable roofs); MBES patch-test site (flat seafloor + $\ge 15^\circ$ slope + isolated 3D boulder/wreck) + dedicated stable reference surface; optical checkerboard/Siemens-star/AprilTag panels (5–10 GSD wide) + reflectance panels; SAR **triangular** trihedral corner reflectors ($\sigma_{\max} = \frac{4\pi a^4}{3\lambda^2}$, where $a$ is the inner orthogonal leg length per `gemini4/book/ch-12/sec-12-06.md`) or PARCs; USBL/LBL seabed transponder arrays + box-in calibration.
- **Independent Ground Control & Checkpoints:** Strict wall between **GCPs** (used in BA/strip/RPC bias compensation) and **withheld Checkpoints** ($\ge 30$ per land-cover/slope stratum under ASPRS Ed. 2 / LBS 2024+, distributed across edges/interior/elevation extremes, partially tied via independent base/CORS/leveling; report compliance via $\text{RMSE}_{\text{rep}} = \sqrt{\text{RMSE}_{\text{test}}^2 + \sigma_{\text{cp}}^2}$ while estimating true product accuracy via $\widehat{\sigma}_{\text{prod}} = \sqrt{\sigma_{\text{obs}}^2 - \sigma_{\text{cp}}^2}$ and targeting $\sigma_{\text{cp}} \le \frac{1}{3}\sigma_{\text{product}}$).
- **Local Weather & Telemetry Support:** Portable anemometer, calibrated barometric reference logger (to audit QFE/weather drift), sky-view/cloud camera, ADS-B/AIS receiver (for moving-object masking), shore ROC station, and chase vessel.

### 4.6 Flight, Trackline, Dive, and Orbital Tasking Planning
- **Swath & Spacing Geometry:** Size swath at the **limiting case** (usable half-angle $\theta_{\text{half}}$ after outer-beam/scan-edge clipping, shallowest depth $d_{\min}$ for sonar, lowest safe AGL for aerial); compute spacing $S = W(1 - f)$ with 20–50 % sidelap (topo lidar), 75–85 % forward / 60–80 % side + 10–20° oblique cross-grids (drone/aircraft SfM to break radial doming), and 100–200 % coverage (outer beams over nadir) for MBES.
- **Orthogonal Crosslines / Cross-Strips:** Mandate 4–10 % of main-scheme line-km perpendicular to production lines, run across different days, tides, and depth/elevation ranges to make drift observable.
- **Calibration & Kinematic Maneuvers:** Pre/post-sortie figure-eights or S-turns for single-antenna IMU yaw observability; 4-step patch test (Latency $\to$ Pitch $\to$ Roll $\to$ Yaw) and multi-altitude opposing boresight lines; turn lead-in runs ($\ge 2\text{–}3\times T_c$ for causal heave settling) and turn-point clipping.
- **Modality-Specific Constraints:**
  - *AUV:* Bottom-following altitude vs. isobaric mode over cliff terrain, turn radii, descent current-drift compensation, battery reserve margin (abort at 20–30 % SoC), obstacle-avoidance clearance, acoustic shadow avoidance.
  - *ASV/USV:* Sea-state/swell heading orientation, tidal-current crabbing allowance, COLREGS traffic separation, safe keel/surf buffer, communication relay geometry.
  - *UNOLS:* Transit speed vs. bubble-sweepdown/VIV speed ceiling, daylight vs. night watch tasking, CTD/MVP wire speed & line breaks, Seabed 2030 transit logging.
  - *Coastal Bathylidar:* Sun elevation strictly $30^\circ\text{–}45^\circ$ (avoid shadows $<30^\circ$ and specular water glint $>45^\circ$), **synchronized low-water flight + high-water boat/USV** to produce a 100–500 m intertidal overlap zone across the white ribbon, Secchi/tide window pairing, NOHD geofenced laser shuttering.
  - *Land Drone:* The **"$122\text{ m}$ ($400\text{ ft}$) AGL DEM trap"** (verifying terrain-following DEM datum/canopy offset vs. QFE barometer drift; $120\text{ m}$ in EASA), battery-swap staging waypoints, VLOS/observer sightlines, wind crab angle checks.
  - *Land Aircraft:* Controlled airspace vectoring, mountain terrain-clearance step-downs, MTA ambiguity zone checks at high PRF, synchronized camera/lidar trigger separation, fuel-stop routing.
  - *Satellite:* Orbit LTAN/LTDN selection, along-track stereo/tri-stereo convergence angles ($15^\circ\text{–}25^\circ$ urban/steep to minimize occlusion; $30^\circ\text{–}45^\circ$ open terrain for $B/H \approx 0.5\text{–}0.9$), cloud-cover threshold vs. pricing/priority tier, InSAR $B_\perp$ & $h_{\text{amb}}$ optimization vs. critical baseline and temporal decorrelation, simultaneous/near-epoch ICESat-2 or ground-control alignment.

### 4.8 Safety Checks
- **Marine & Subsea (UNOLS RVSS, AUV, ASV):**
  - UNOLS RVSS (fleet safety standard, not federal statute) Appendix A/B load-handling verification (certified crane/A-frame/winch/wire/rope SWL, shackles, tag lines, hard-hat/PFD/steel-toe PPE, pinch-point exclusion zones, Man Overboard [MOB] & cold-water immersion drills, STCW `46 CFR § 15.1111` watch fatigue limits; hazmat follows vessel MFP/DOT/IMDG/USCG rules).

- **Aviation, UAS & Laser (Bathylidar, Aircraft, Drone):**
  - **ANSI Z136.1 & Z136.6** outdoor laser hazard evaluation, Nominal Ocular Hazard Distance (NOHD) verification against AGL floor, cockpit/ground MPE protection, automatic laser interlock/shutter geofencing.

### 4.10 Pre-Survey Data Quality Prediction & Requirements Verification
- Build an **a priori analytical error budget (TPU / RSS)** parameterized at the **worst-case operating envelope** (swath outer edge $\theta_{\max}$, maximum water depth / Secchi limit, densest canopy, longest GNSS baseline, steepest terrain slope $\alpha$) using equations from `fable/` (Ch. 18–22, 26, 52, 53) and `gemini4/` (Ch. 7–14):
  - Airborne Lidar: $\sigma_z^2 = \sigma_{z,\text{GNSS/INS}}^2 + \sigma_{l_z}^2 + \sigma_r^2\cos^2\theta_{\text{scan}} + (H_{\text{AGL}}\tan\theta_{\text{scan}}\,\sigma_{\phi,\text{cross}})^2 + \sigma_{\text{bore/att}}^2 + \tan^2\alpha\,\sigma_H^2$, where $\theta_{\text{scan}}$ is off-nadir scan angle, $\sigma_{\phi,\text{cross}}^2 = \sigma_\phi^2 + \sigma_{\delta\phi}^2 + \sigma_{\theta_{\text{scan}}}^2$ combines roll/boresight/encoder (pitch $\sigma_{\theta_{\text{pitch}}}$ induces along-track shift $H_{\text{AGL}}\sigma_{\theta_{\text{pitch}}}$ coupling into $z$ via slope), and **1D single-axis** $\sigma_H = \text{RMSE}_r/\sqrt{2} = \text{THU}_{95\%}/2.4477$ (`gemini4/book/00-front-matter/notation-and-symbols.md` §6.2; avoiding the $2\times$ radial slope variance error).
  - Bathylidar: Wave-surface term $(1 - \frac{n_a\cos\theta_w}{n_g\cos\theta_a})\delta z_s \approx (1 - 1/n_g)\delta z_s \approx 0.27\,\delta z_s$ ($\approx 0.25\,\delta z_s$ at Palmer $\theta_a = 20^\circ$) applies to **bottom elevation $z_b$**, whereas **instantaneous-surface depth** $d = z_s - z_b$ incurs $\approx (1/n_g)\delta z_s \approx 0.73\,\delta z_s$; separate the **systematic $+1.6\%$ depth bias** ($n_p \approx 1.341$ vs. $n_g \approx 1.364$ at $532\text{ nm}$ seawater) from random index TPU ($\frac{\sigma_n}{n_g}d \approx 0.15\%\,d$), pulse stretching, wave-slope horizontal shift $d(1 - 1/n_p)\delta\alpha_{\text{wave}}$, and footprint slope bias.
  - Sonar/AUV/USV: Hare–Godin–Mayer TVU/THU separating **surface SVS beam-steering** ($-d\tan^2\theta\,\delta c_0/c_0$) from **column harmonic-mean SVP** ($d(1 - \tan^2\theta)\delta c_p/c$, keeping nadir range scale), roll ($d\tan\theta\,\delta\phi$), squat, heave, and water level/SEP. For **AUVs**, acoustic roll, refraction, and swath footprint scale with **altitude $h_{\text{alt}}$ ($5\text{–}70\text{ m}$)** rather than depth $d$, vertical reference uses **TEOS-10 pressure integration** $+ P_{\text{atm}}(t) + \eta(t)$, and DVL–INS ($0.05\text{–}0.2\%$ distance) + USBL ($0.05\text{–}0.3\%$ slant range) are **THU** terms coupling into TVU via $\tan\alpha\,\sigma_H$.
  - Optical Stereo / SfM: $\sigma_z = \frac{H}{B}\,\text{GSD}\,\sigma_{p,\text{px}}$ plus attitude/jitter/RPC bias/self-calibration doming.
  - InSAR: $\sigma_z = \frac{h_{\text{amb}}}{2\pi}\sigma_\phi(\gamma, N_L)$ with $h_a \equiv h_{\text{amb}} = \frac{\lambda R\sin\theta_i}{p\,B_\perp}$ ($p=1$ bistatic single-TX, $p=2$ repeat-pass/ping-pong; $\theta_i$ incidence angle) plus $k\,h_{\text{amb}}$ integer unwrapping blunders, APS, and penetration bias.
- Verify **observability matrix rank** (can every bias—latency, roll, pitch, yaw, focal length, $c_0$—be separated by the planned lines?) and check **$\chi^2$ sample-size confidence intervals** on planned checkpoint counts ($\nu=n$ zero-mean RMSE $[0.799, 1.337]$ i.e. $[-20.1\%, +33.7\%]$, vs. $\nu=n-1$ sample $\sigma$ $[0.796, 1.344]$ at $n=30$, 95 %) so the plan retains positive margin below specification limits.

### 4.11 Accident Planning, Emergency Response, and Statutory Reporting
- **Modality-Specific Accident Taxonomy:**
  - *AUV:* Subsea loss/non-surfacing, fishing-gear/kelp entanglement, pressure-housing flood/implosion, under-ice trapping, LARS crane drop/crush, Li-ion thermal runaway on deck.
  - *ASV/USV:* Vessel collision (COLREGS failure), capsize/swamping, propulsion loss/grounding in surf, runaway/lost-link, fuel/battery fire.
  - *UNOLS:* Man Overboard (MOB), crane/winch wire snap, ship grounding/collision, hazardous chemical spill, medical evacuation at sea.
  - *Coastal Bathylidar & Land Aircraft:* Ditching/water impact, forced off-field landing, CFIT (Controlled Flight Into Terrain), bird strike, mid-air near-miss, laser ocular exposure incident, hypoxia.
  - *Land Drone:* Flyaway, water ditching, tree/powerline strike, bystander injury, LiPo thermal runaway in vehicle/field.
  - *Ground Truth / Satellite Field Crew:* Roadside vehicle strike, cliff fall, heatstroke/hypothermia, wildlife encounter, security detention.
  - *Cross-Cutting Data/Cyber Accidents:* Unbacked SSD failure, overwritten raw logs, GNSS spoofing corruption, ransomware, ITAR/PII spill.
- **Emergency Response & Statutory Notification Protocols:**
  - Immediate preserve-life -> secure platform/beacon tracking (Iridium, Argos, VHF, strobe, acoustic transponder) -> preserve black-box/raw telemetry/chain-of-custody logs -> activate Emergency Response Plan (ERP) / ICS.
  - Mandatory reporting clocks: **FAA 14 CFR § 107.9** (small UAS serious injury, loss of consciousness, or $> \$500$ damage to property other than the small UA [lower of repair cost or fair market value] within **10 calendar days** via FAADroneZone/FSDO); **NTSB 49 CFR Part 830** (**immediate** notification to NTSB ROC for manned aircraft accidents, serious/fatal injuries, **§ 830.5(a)(6)** $> \$25{,}000$ non-aircraft property damage [lower of repair/FMV], or qualifying UAS accidents under amended **§ 830.2 [2022]** involving death/serious injury or substantial damage to an unmanned aircraft holding an airworthiness certificate/approval); **USCG 46 CFR § 4.05-1 / § 4.05-10** (**immediate** verbal notice to Sector/COTP + **5-day** written Form **CG-2692** for marine casualties $> \$75{,}000$ / grounding / collision / propulsion loss), **33 CFR § 153.203** (**immediate** NRC oil/hazmat discharge notice), and **46 CFR § 4.06** Serious Marine Incident testing (**2 h** alcohol / **32 h** drug); **UNOLS/NSF** incident reporting; **OSHA 29 CFR § 1904.39** (**8 h** fatality; **24 h** inpatient hospitalization, amputation, or **eye loss** where OSHA has jurisdiction) & **ANSI Z136 LSO** exposure log; timely **insurance underwriter** notice.

### 4.12 Insurance Requirements

- **Aviation & UAS:** **Aviation Hull + Third-Party Aviation Liability** (mandatory because standard Commercial General Liability [CGL] policies carry strict *aviation exclusions*), Non-Owned Aircraft Liability (if chartering), **Over-Water / Watercraft / P&I endorsements** (for coastal bathylidar and ship-launched/over-water drones), **Class 3B/4 laser / ocular endorsement check**, and payload coverage for multi-hundred-thousand-dollar LiDAR/camera pods.
- **Professional & Enterprise:** **Errors & Omissions (E&O) / Professional Liability** (covering claims from defective charts, flood maps, dredging volumes, or datum blunders—especially critical since private contractors do not share government sovereign immunity or FTCA discretionary-function protection), Commercial General Liability (CGL) for ground crews, Commercial Auto, Cyber/Data Breach, Workers' Comp + **Foreign Voluntary Workers' Compensation (FVWC) / Medevac**, and ATA Carnet / cargo bonds. Explicitly document UNOLS/NSF federal self-insurance or institutional property-at-sea assumptions where applicable.

### 4.16 End-to-End Data Processing Pipeline
- Pin software versions, container/environment lockfiles, geoid grids (`GEOID18`/`GEOID2022`/`EGM2008`), and random seeds (`fable/chapters/ch29-processing-pipelines.md`–`ch35`, `ch46`–`ch50`).
- Rigorous RTS forward-backward trajectory smoothing (SBET/POSPac/Inertial Explorer/RTKLIB/PRIDE PPP-AR); delayed heave / ERS separation reduction.
- Boresight / strip adjustment / patch test / camera bundle adjustment (fixing IO or adding oblique constraints) / RPC bias compensation & jitter removal.
- Medium corrections: acoustic layered ray tracing (Snell's law), bathylidar wave-surface modeling + group ($n_g$) & phase ($n_p$) refraction correction, optical water-column atmospheric/glint correction (SDB), SAR multi-looking, APS correction & SNAPHU phase unwrapping.
- Outlier filtering (preserving low/high noise classes, CUBE hypothesis disambiguation, designated shoal soundings / DTONs), ground classification validation (Type I/II error matrices), breaklines & hydro-flattening/-enforcement, deliberate gridding (`Pixel-is-Area` vs. `Pixel-is-Point` convention), void masks, per-cell uncertainty (TPU) grids, cloud-native packaging (COG with `ZSTD`/`LERC`, COPC, BAG/S-102, Zarr), ISO 19115/19157/STAC metadata, and security/privacy release review.

### 4.17 Cross-Checking Against Prior and Contemporaneous Data
- Harmonize horizontal frame/epoch (HTDP / PROJ deformation), vertical datum/geoid/tidal separation, surface ontology (DSM vs. DTM), and sign/units before differencing.
- Mandatory **3D co-registration** (Nuth & Kääb 2011 / ICP on stable terrain) to prevent slope-coupled false vertical biases (`fable/chapters/ch41-change-detection.md`).
- Compare against: (a) **withheld independent checkpoints** ($\ge 30$/stratum, $\sigma_{\text{cp}} \le 1/3\,\sigma_p$ or ASPRS Ed. 2 quadrature), (b) **spaceborne altimetry** (ICESat-2 ATL03/ATL06/ATL08, GEDI, SWOT), (c) **adjacent/prior surveys** (junction analysis, intertidal high-tide vessel vs. low-tide bathylidar overlap belt), (d) **contemporaneous tide gauges / GNSS@TG**, and (e) **forensic visual/spectral audits** (4-azimuth hillshades, 2D FFT for corduroy/striping, fractional-elevation histograms for quantization/terracing, sea-level zero sanity test).

### 4.18 Survey Error Sources & Magnitudes Accounting
- Produce a **Reconciled Post-Survey Error Budget Table** comparing *a priori* budgeted 1σ/95 % terms against empirical achieved residuals stratified by stratum, slope, depth, scan/beam angle, day, and distance from base (`fable/chapters/ch53-accuracy-assessment.md`).
- Compute robust and Gaussian metrics ($n$, mean bias, $\sigma$, RMSE + $\chi^2$ 95 % CI, MAE, NMAD, empirical 95th percentile, spatial error correlation length/variogram) and diagnose every systematic excursion (e.g., thermal IMU drift, afternoon thermocline smile, rolling-shutter skew, leaf-on bias, squat miscalibration, GNSS spoofing, InSAR unwrapping jumps).

### 4.19 Post-Survey Retrospective & Continuous Learning
- Mandatory **After-Action Review (AAR) / Post-Cruise Assessment Report (PCAR)** within 14–30 days of de-mob while logs and crew memory are fresh.
- Quantify *planned vs. actual* on four axes: schedule/weather days, cost/burn rate, operational safety/near-misses, and achieved vs. budgeted error magnitudes.

## 7. Quality Bar, Verification Criteria, and Non-Functional Requirements

1. **Structural Completeness:** All 7 modality subdirectories under `checklists/` MUST cover every topic in Sections 4.1–4.20 and Section 5 using a uniform file architecture defined in `PLAN.md`.
2. **Tiered Checkboxes & Sign-Off Traceability:** Every checklist item uses GitHub-flavored Markdown checkboxes (`- [ ]`) tagged with severity (`**[GATE]**`, `**[REC]**`, `**[COND]**`), concise rationale/threshold, verification artifact/evidence, and chapter/standard citation. Every operational file (`01`–`08`) begins with the standardized sign-off header block (`Project/Cruise/Sortie ID`, `Modality`, `CRS/Realization/Epoch`, `Vertical Datum/Geoid or SEP`, `Sign Convention`, `Phase Lead & UTC Timestamp`, plus recommended `Platform/Vessel/Tail #` and `Independent Verifier`), paired with the master RACI sign-off sheet in `00-README-and-signoff.md` and the reference library in `09-resources-and-templates.md`.
3. **Link & Reference Integrity:** All relative links to `../../fable/...` and `../../gemini4/...` MUST resolve to real files; external standards/regulations MUST cite verified editions/CFR parts without fabricated DOIs or numbers (any uncertain item flagged `(verify)` prior to release and resolved to `0` remaining flags).
4. **Honesty & Uncertainty Rigor:** No "±10 cm" without statistic and confidence level; no uncalibrated platform claims; no cost numbers without range/basis and caveat; explicit separation of *a priori* TPU estimates from *a posteriori* checkpoint/crossline results.
5. **Automated Validation Pass:** A Python verification tool (`checklists/tools/check_checklists.py`) MUST pass with zero hard failures before completion is declared.
