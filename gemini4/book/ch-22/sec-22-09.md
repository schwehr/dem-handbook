# Chapter 22: The Dynamic Earth: Temporal Changes, Moving Objects, AIS/ADS-B, Seasonality, and Change Detection

*The Earth's surface and the objects upon it are in constant motion across timescales from milliseconds to millennia.*

---

## 22.9 Curated Key References

The seminal publications, foundational geomorphic papers, and technical reports listed below establish the physical, mathematical, and computational frameworks for dynamic surface monitoring, geomorphic change detection, and spatiotemporal tracking.

---

### Foundational and Seminal Literature

* **Fischler, M. A., & Bolles, R. C.** (1981). Random sample consensus: A paradigm for model fitting with applications to image analysis and automated cartography. *Communications of the ACM*, 24(6), 381–395. DOI: [10.1145/358669.358692](https://doi.org/10.1145/358669.358692)  
  *The landmark paper introducing the RANSAC algorithm, establishing robust geometric model fitting in the presence of extreme outliers.*

* **Heezen, B. C., & Ewing, M.** (1952). Turbidity currents and submarine slumps, and the 1929 Grand Banks earthquake. *American Journal of Science*, 250(12), 849–873. DOI: [10.2475/ajs.250.12.849](https://doi.org/10.2475/ajs.250.12.849)  
  *The historic oceanographic paper analyzing sequential telegraph cable breaks to prove that submarine topography evolves through high-velocity turbidity currents.*

* **Lague, D., Brodu, N., & Leroux, J.** (2013). Accurate 3D comparison of complex topography with terrestrial laser scanner: Application to the Rangitikei canyon (N-Z). *ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing*, 82, 10–26. DOI: [10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2013.05.002](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2013.05.002)  
  *The seminal paper introducing the Multiscale Model-to-Model Cloud Comparison (M3C2) algorithm, establishing surface-normal distance projection and local confidence bounds for ungridded point clouds.*

* **Schwehr, K.** (2013). All the ships in the world: Analysis of global maritime tracking data. *Proceedings of the 21st ACM SIGSPATIAL International Conference on Advances in Geographic Information Systems*, 1–2. DOI: [10.1145/2525314.2525315](https://doi.org/10.1145/2525314.2525315)  
  *Pioneered the large-scale analysis of global satellite Automatic Identification System (AIS) telemetry for maritime traffic density and spatial vessel modeling.*

* **Wheaton, J. M., Brasington, J., Darby, S. E., & Searle, D. A.** (2010). Accounting for uncertainty in DEMs from repeat topographic surveys: Improved sediment budgets. *Earth Surface Processes and Landforms*, 35(2), 136–156. DOI: [10.1002/esp.1886](https://doi.org/10.1002/esp.1886)  
  *The landmark geomorphic paper establishing the DEM of Difference (DoD) framework with spatial Level of Detection (LoD) thresholding to eliminate noise accumulation in volumetric sediment budgets.*

### Modern Trajectory and Machine Learning Specifications

* **Graser, A.** (2019). MovingPandas: Efficient analysis of movement data in Python. *GI_Forum*, 7(1), 54–68. DOI: [10.1553/giscience2019_01_s54](https://doi.org/10.1553/giscience2019_01_s54)  
  *Introduced the MovingPandas open-source Python library, integrating trajectory interpolation and spatiotemporal movement analysis into the GeoPandas ecosystem.*

* **International Telecommunication Union (ITU).** (2014). *Technical characteristics for an automatic identification system using time division multiple access in the VHF maritime mobile frequency band*. Recommendation ITU-R M.1371-5. Geneva: International Telecommunication Union.  
  *The authoritative technical standard defining the data structure, slot timing, and telemetry sentences for marine AIS broadcasts.*

* **Zanaga, D., et al.** (2022). Dynamic World, near-real-time global 10 m land use land cover mapping. *Scientific Data*, 9, 281. DOI: [10.1038/s41597-022-01307-4](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-022-01307-4)  
  *Documents the Google and World Resources Institute deep learning model generating near-real-time global 10-meter land cover classifications from Sentinel-2 satellite passes.*
