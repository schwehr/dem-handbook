## 22.9 Curated Key References

The peer-reviewed articles, oceanographic discoveries, open-source software papers, and international telecommunications standards below establish the physical, statistical, and algorithmic foundations of Chapter 22.

---

### Geomorphic Change Detection, Co-Registration, and Error Propagation

1. **Heezen, B. C., & Ewing, M.** (1952). Turbidity currents and submarine slumps, and the 1929 Grand Banks earthquake. *American Journal of Science*, 250(12), 849–873. https://doi.org/10.2475/ajs.250.12.849  
   *Landmark marine-geology paper decoding sequential 1929 transatlantic telegraph cable breaks to prove that high-velocity submarine turbidity currents dynamically scour continental slopes and canyons.*

2. **Hugonnet, R., Brun, F., Berthier, E., Dehecq, A., Mannerfelt, E. S., Eckert, N., & Farinotti, D.** (2022). Uncertainty analysis of digital elevation models by spatial inference from stable terrain. *IEEE Journal of Selected Topics in Applied Earth Observations and Remote Sensing*, 15, 6456–6472. https://doi.org/10.1109/JSTARS.2022.3188922  
   *Establishes the `xdem` statistical framework for inferring slope/curvature-dependent heteroscedastic DEM errors and integrating multi-range variograms into exact spatially correlated volumetric uncertainty bounds.*

3. **Lague, D., Brodu, N., & Leroux, J.** (2013). Accurate 3D comparison of complex topography with terrestrial laser scanner: Application to the Rangitikei canyon (N-Z). *ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing*, 82, 10–26. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2013.04.009  
   *Introduces Multiscale Model-to-Model Cloud Comparison (`M3C2`), deriving PCA normal-scale selection and cylinder-projected 3D confidence intervals ($\text{LoD}_{95\%}$) on steep and overhanging point clouds.*

4. **Nuth, C., & Kääb, A.** (2011). Co-registration and bias corrections of satellite elevation data sets for quantifying glacier thickness change. *The Cryosphere*, 5(1), 271–282. https://doi.org/10.5194/tc-5-271-2011  
   *Derives the universal analytical 3D cosine-aspect regression ($\Delta z / \tan\alpha = a\cos(b - \psi) + c$) for removing sub-pixel horizontal misregistration prior to multi-temporal DEM differencing.*

5. **Wheaton, J. M., Brasington, J., Darby, S. E., & Sear, D. A.** (2010). Accounting for uncertainty in DEMs from repeat topographic surveys: improved sediment budgets. *Earth Surface Processes and Landforms*, 35(2), 136–156. https://doi.org/10.1002/esp.1886  
   *Establishes spatially variable Fuzzy Inference System (`FIS`) error surfaces and Bayesian spatial-coherence `LoD` thresholding for morphological `DoD` sediment budgeting (`GCD`).*

### Maritime `AIS` Telemetry, Trajectory Analytics, and Spatiotemporal Machine Learning

6. **Brown, C. F., Brumby, S. P., Guzder-Williams, B., Birch, T., Hyde, S. B., Mazzariello, J., Czerwinski, W., Pasquarella, V. J., Haertel, R., Brumby, S., Kontgis, C., & Tait, A. M.** (2022). Dynamic World, near real-time global 10 m land use land cover mapping. *Scientific Data*, 9, 251. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-022-01307-4  
   *Documents the Google and World Resources Institute Sentinel-2 deep-learning framework providing near-real-time global 10 m phenology, crop, snow/ice, and water probability masks.*

7. **Choy, C., Gwak, J., & Savarese, S.** (2019). 4D spatio-temporal ConvNets: Minkowski convolutional neural networks. *2019 IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR)*, 3075–3084. https://doi.org/10.1109/CVPR.2019.00319  
   *Formulates generalized sparse high-dimensional convolutions (`MinkowskiEngine`) for 3D point-cloud and 4D $(x, y, z, t)$ spatiotemporal semantic segmentation.*

8. **Fischler, M. A., & Bolles, R. C.** (1981). Random sample consensus: A paradigm for model fitting with applications to image analysis and automated cartography. *Communications of the ACM*, 24(6), 381–395. https://doi.org/10.1145/358669.358692  
   *Seminal paper introducing `RANSAC`, providing high-breakdown outlier resilience for photogrammetric resection and 3D geometric primitive extraction.*

9. **Gorelick, N., Hancher, M., Dixon, M., Ilyushchenko, S., Thau, D., & Moore, R.** (2017). Google Earth Engine: Planetary-scale geospatial analysis for everyone. *Remote Sensing of Environment*, 202, 18–27. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rse.2017.06.031  
   *Describes the distributed cloud compute architecture underlying planetary multi-decade Earth Engine `Timelapse` and temporal DEM analysis.*

10. **Graser, A.** (2019). MovingPandas: Efficient structures for movement data in Python. *GI_Forum*, 7(1), 54–68. https://doi.org/10.1553/giscience2019_01_s54  
    *Introduces `MovingPandas`, enabling kinematic trajectory slicing, stop detection, and exact timestamp position interpolation in Python.*

11. **International Telecommunication Union (ITU).** (2014). *Technical characteristics for an automatic identification system using time division multiple access in the VHF maritime mobile frequency band* (Recommendation ITU-R M.1371-5). Geneva: ITU.  
    *Authoritative international radio specification defining SOTDMA slot protocols, dynamic position reports (`Messages 1–3, 18`), and hull-dimension offsets (`Message 5`) for marine AIS.*

12. **Kroodsma, D. A., Mayorga, J., Hochberg, T., Miller, N. A., Boerder, K., Ferretti, F., Wilson, A., Bergman, B., White, T. D., Block, B. A., Woods, P., Sullivan, B., Costello, C., & Worm, B.** (2018). Tracking the global footprint of fisheries. *Science*, 359(6378), 904–908. https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aao5646  
    *Landmark Global Fishing Watch study applying convolutional neural networks across 22 billion satellite AIS positions to map global vessel behavior and maritime traffic.*

13. **Schwehr, K., & Schuyler, B.** (2013). *All the Ships: Geospatial in Google Cloud* [Technical lecture]. Google I/O 2013 Conference, San Francisco, CA.  
    *Demonstrated global ingestion, `libais` decoding, and cloud-scale spatiotemporal analytics of worldwide satellite and coastal AIS vessel trajectories.*

14. **Stewart, A. J., Robinson, C., Corley, I. A., Ortiz, A., Lavista Ferres, J. M., & Banerjee, A.** (2022). TorchGeo: Deep learning with geospatial data. *Proceedings of the 30th International Conference on Advances in Geographic Information Systems (ACM SIGSPATIAL '22)*, 1–12. https://doi.org/10.1145/3557915.3560953  
    *Presents `TorchGeo`, unifying on-the-fly CRS reprojection, spatiotemporal bounding-box indexing, and multimodal DEM/optical/SAR tensor loaders for PyTorch foundation models.*
