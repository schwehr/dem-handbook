## 21.8 Curated Key References

The peer-reviewed benchmark studies, algorithm derivations, void-inpainting evaluations, and 3D surface reconstruction papers below establish the theoretical and operational foundations of Chapter 21.

---

### Classical Ground Filtering and Accuracy Benchmarks

1. **Axelsson, P.** (2000). DEM generation from airborne laser scanner data using triangulated irregular networks. *International Archives of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing*, 33(B4/1), 110–117.  
   *Seminal paper introducing Progressive TIN Densification (`PTD`), formulating iterative facet-distance and vertex-angle acceptance criteria for rugged terrain.*

2. **Bartels, M., & Wei, H.** (2010). Threshold-free object and ground point separation in LIDAR data. *Pattern Recognition Letters*, 31(10), 1089–1099. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.patrec.2010.03.007  
   *Formalizes statistical Skewness Balancing (`SBA`), separating positive off-terrain object returns from symmetric Gaussian bare-ground relief without empirical height thresholds.*

3. **Evans, J. S., & Hudak, A. T.** (2007). A multiscale curvature algorithm for classifying discrete return LiDAR in forested environments. *IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing*, 45(4), 1029–1038. https://doi.org/10.1109/TGRS.2006.890412  
   *Introduces Multiscale Curvature Classification (`MCC`), iteratively pruning canopy and shrub returns via thin-plate spline curvature across nested spatial scales.*

4. **Pingel, T. J., Clarke, K. C., & McBride, W. A.** (2013). An improved simple morphological filter for the terrain classification of airborne LIDAR data. *ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing*, 77, 21–30. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2012.12.002  
   *Derives the Simple Morphological Filter (`SMRF`, implemented in `PDAL filters.smrf`), combining linear window growth with iterative thin-plate spline object inpainting.*

5. **Sithole, G., & Vosselman, G.** (2004). Experimental comparison of filter algorithms for bare-Earth extraction from airborne laser scanning point clouds. *ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing*, 59(1–2), 85–101. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2004.05.004  
   *The authoritative ISPRS Working Group III/3 international benchmark defining Type I (omission) and Type II (commission) error rates across steep slopes, bridges, and complex urban roofs.*

6. **Zhang, K., Chen, S.-C., Whitman, D., Shyu, M.-L., Yan, J., & Zhang, C.** (2003). A progressive morphological filter for removing non-ground measurements from airborne LIDAR data. *IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing*, 41(4), 872–882. https://doi.org/10.1109/TGRS.2003.810682  
   *Establishes the Progressive Morphological Filter (`PMF`), deriving the slope-dependent dynamic height threshold $dh_{T,k}$ across expanding grayscale opening windows.*

7. **Zhang, W., Qi, J., Wan, P., Wang, H., Xie, D., Wang, X., & Yan, G.** (2016). An easy-to-use airborne LiDAR data filtering method based on cloth simulation. *Remote Sensing*, 8(6), 501. https://doi.org/10.3390/rs8060501  
   *Formulates the Cloth Simulation Filter (`CSF`, `PDAL filters.csf`), modeling bare-earth extraction via mass-spring cloth dynamics draped over inverted point clouds.*

### Raster DSM-to-DTM Conversion, Deep Learning, Void Filling, and 3D Overhang Reconstruction

8. **Curless, B., & Levoy, M.** (1996). A volumetric method for building complex models from range images. *Proceedings of the 23rd Annual Conference on Computer Graphics and Interactive Techniques (SIGGRAPH '96)*, 303–312. https://doi.org/10.1145/237170.237269  
   *Foundational computer-graphics paper introducing weighted Truncated Signed Distance Fields (`TSDF`) and ray-cast free-space carving for multi-valued 3D surfaces with overhangs and holes.*

9. **Grohman, G., Kroenung, G., & Strebeck, J.** (2006). Filling SRTM voids: The delta surface fill method. *Photogrammetric Engineering & Remote Sensing*, 72(3), 213–216.  
   *Introduces the Delta Surface Fill (`DSF`) paradigm for seamlessly warping auxiliary elevation data across large radar layover and shadow voids without perimeter cliffs.*

10. **Hawker, L., Uhe, P., Paulo, L., Sosa, J., Savage, J., Sampson, C., & Neal, J.** (2022). A 30 m global map of elevation with forests and buildings removed (FABDEM). *Environmental Research Letters*, 17(2), 024016. https://doi.org/10.1088/1748-9326/ac4d4f  
    *Demonstrates global spaceborne DSM-to-DTM conversion by training Random Forest canopy and building height models on NASA GEDI and ICESat-2 laser altimetry over Copernicus GLO-30.*

11. **Hu, Q., Yang, B., Xie, L., Rosa, S., Guo, Y., Wang, Z., Trigoni, N., & Markham, A.** (2020). RandLA-Net: Efficient semantic segmentation of large-scale point clouds. *2020 IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR)*, 11105–11114. https://doi.org/10.1109/CVPR42600.2020.01112  
    *Introduces random point sampling with dilated local feature aggregation, scaling direct 3D neural semantic segmentation to million-point LiDAR tiles.*

12. **Kazhdan, M., & Hoppe, H.** (2013). Screened Poisson surface reconstruction. *ACM Transactions on Graphics (TOG)*, 32(3), 29. https://doi.org/10.1145/2487228.2487237  
    *Adds explicit point-position screening constraints to adaptive octree Poisson reconstruction (`PoissonRecon`), preserving sharp edges on overhanging 3D cliffs, arches, and sea caves.*

13. **Reuter, H. I., Nelson, A., & Jarvis, A.** (2007). An evaluation of void-filling interpolation methods for SRTM data. *International Journal of Geographical Information Science*, 21(9), 983–1008. https://doi.org/10.1080/13658810601169899  
    *Systematic geomorphic and Void-Size-Stratified benchmark of Kriging, IDW, spline, ANUDEM, and auxiliary delta-fill interpolation across SRTM mountain and desert voids.*

14. **Thomas, H., Qi, C. R., Deschaud, J.-E., Marcotegui, B., Goulette, F., & Guibas, L. J.** (2019). KPConv: Flexible and deformable convolution for point clouds. *2019 IEEE/CVF International Conference on Computer Vision (ICCV)*, 6410–6419. https://doi.org/10.1109/ICCV.2019.00651  
    *Defines continuous Euclidean Kernel Point Convolutions (`KPConv`) on unstructured 3D point clouds without voxel quantization.*
