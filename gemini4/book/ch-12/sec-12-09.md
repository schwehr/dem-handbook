# Chapter 12: Calibration Targets, Reference Stations, and Ground Control Points (GCPs)

*Anchoring sensors to physical reality through active networks, static monuments, and engineered targets.*

---

## 12.9 Curated Key References

The standards, seminal papers, and technical manuals listed below establish the metrological and mathematical foundations for sensor calibration, reference station networks, target design, and positional accuracy assessment in elevation modeling.

---

### Standards, Guidelines, and Technical Reports

* **American Society for Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing (ASPRS).** (2015). *ASPRS Positional Accuracy Standards for Digital Geospatial Data*. *Photogrammetric Engineering & Remote Sensing*, 81(3), A1–A26. DOI: [10.14358/PERS.81.3.A1](https://doi.org/10.14358/PERS.81.3.A1)  
  *The landmark standard establishing the strict operational distinction between Ground Control Points (GCPs) and Independent Check Points (CPs), defining the 3x accuracy rule, and separating non-vegetated (NVA) from vegetated (VVA) vertical accuracy assessment.*

* **American Society for Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing (ASPRS).** (2024). *ASPRS Positional Accuracy Standards for Digital Geospatial Data, Edition 2, Version 1.0*. *Photogrammetric Engineering & Remote Sensing*, 90(2), 79–120. DOI: [10.14358/PERS.23-00052R](https://doi.org/10.14358/PERS.23-00052R)  
  *Updated standard incorporating modern 3D UAV Structure-from-Motion, mobile laser scanning, and high-density multi-beam mapping specifications.*

* **Federal Geographic Data Committee (FGDC).** (1998). *Geospatial Positioning Accuracy Standards Part 3: National Standard for Spatial Data Accuracy (NSSDA)*. FGDC-STD-007.3-1998. Reston, VA: Federal Geographic Data Committee.  
  *Foundational U.S. federal standard establishing parametric RMSE testing and 95% positional confidence error thresholds.*

* **International Organization for Standardization (ISO).** (2017). *ISO 12233:2017 Photography — Electronic still picture imaging — Resolution and spatial frequency responses*. Geneva: International Organization for Standardization.  
  *Defines the international standard slanted-edge methodology for extracting the Edge Spread Function (ESF), Line Spread Function (LSF), and Modulation Transfer Function (MTF).*

* **National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA).** (2020). *Field Procedures Manual (FPM) for Hydrographic Surveying*. Office of Coast Survey, National Ocean Service, Silver Spring, MD.  
  *Prescribes mandatory hydrographic patch-test procedures for multibeam sonar calibration (latency, pitch, roll, and yaw) and annual benchmark leveling ties for water-level stations.*

* **Rothacher, M., & Schmid, R.** (2010). *ANTEX: The Exchange Format for Antenna Phase Center Variations, Version 1.4*. International GNSS Service (IGS).  
  *The authoritative specification defining absolute robotic and anechoic chamber phase center offsets (PCO) and phase center variations (PCV) for satellite and ground multi-GNSS antennas.*

### Seminal Papers and Methodological Literature

* **Chander, G., Markham, B. L., Helder, D. L., Xiong, X., & Gao, F.** (2013). Overview of intercalibration of satellite instruments. *IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing*, 51(3), 1056–1080. DOI: [10.1109/TGRS.2012.2228654](https://doi.org/10.1109/TGRS.2012.2228654)  
  *Comprehensive review of Pseudo-Invariant Calibration Sites (PICS) in the CEOS/USGS catalog, detailing surface stability, spectral properties, and cross-satellite calibration methodologies.*

* **Garrity, C. P., & Schwehr, K. D.** (2015). History of GIS and Geodetic Mapping Milestones. GitHub repository: `https://github.com/schwehr/gis-history`.  
  *Curated historical chronology documenting the evolution of geodetic triangulation marks, differential GPS networks, and terrestrial remote sensing milestones.*

* **Knisley, J. M., & Foote, K. E.** (2017). The creation and survival of the 1833 Buttermilk geodetic station. *Surveying and Land Information Science*, 76(1), 15–28.  
  *Detailed historical and geodetic analysis of Ferdinand Hassler's oldest surviving U.S. Coast Survey triangulation mark in Westchester County, New York.*

* **Olson, E.** (2011). AprilTag: A robust and flexible visual fiducial system. *Proceedings of the 2011 IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA)*, 3400–3407. DOI: [10.1109/ICRA.2011.5979561](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICRA.2011.5979561)  
  *The foundational paper detailing the 2D visual barcode encoding, edge detection, and homography decoding algorithms utilized in modern automated photogrammetric targets.*

* **Roberts, C. R., & Jackson, J. C.** (2007). Analysis of trihedral corner reflectors for microwave and SAR calibration. *IEEE Aerospace and Electronic Systems Magazine*, 22(8), 19–25. DOI: [10.1109/MAES.2007.4307525](https://doi.org/10.1109/MAES.2007.4307525)  
  *Derivation of the exact physical optics effective aperture, maximum radar cross section, and misalignment loss curves for radar calibration targets.*

* **Skaloud, J., & Lichti, D.** (2006). Rigorous approach to bore-sight self-calibration in airborne laser scanning. *ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing*, 61(1), 47–59. DOI: [10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2006.07.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2006.07.003)  
  *Derives the geometric observation equations and flight-line configurations necessary to achieve mathematical decoupling and parameter observability of airborne LiDAR boresight angles.*
