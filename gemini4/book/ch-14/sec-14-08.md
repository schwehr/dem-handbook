# Chapter 14: Error Theory, Total Propagated Uncertainty (TPU), and Forensic Evaluation of Third-Party DEMs

*Quantifying what we know, what we don't know, and how to audit someone else's DEM when you don't have the raw data or metadata.*

---

## 14.8 Curated Key References

The seminal articles, geodetic papers, and federal standards listed below establish the theoretical and practical foundations of error theory, robust statistics, 3D co-registration, and forensic quality assurance in digital elevation modeling.

---

### Foundational and Seminal Literature

* **Fisher, P. F., & Tate, N. J.** (2006). Causes and consequences of error in digital elevation models. *Progress in Physical Geography*, 30(4), 467–489. DOI: [10.1191/0309133306pp492ra](https://doi.org/10.1191/0309133306pp492ra)  
  *Comprehensive review of DEM error taxonomy, spatial autocorrelation in terrain derivatives, and the propagation of elevation uncertainties into hydrological and geomorphic models.*

* **Hare, R., Godin, A., & Mayer, L. A.** (1995). *Accuracy Estimation of Canadian Swath (Multibeam) and Sweep (Multi-Transducer) Sounding Systems*. Research Report No. 95-001. Ottawa: Canadian Hydrographic Service.  
  *The landmark hydrographic report deriving the analytical first-order Taylor series error budget for multibeam sonar sounding systems, establishing modern Total Propagated Uncertainty (TPU).*

* **Höhle, J., & Höhle, M.** (2009). Accuracy assessment of digital elevation models by means of robust statistical methods. *ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing*, 64(4), 398–406. DOI: [10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2009.02.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2009.02.003)  
  *Introduced robust non-parametric estimators to elevation model validation, establishing the Normalized Median Absolute Deviation (NMAD) and empirical quantile testing as standard practices.*

* **Kálmán, R. E.** (1960). A new approach to linear filtering and prediction problems. *Journal of Basic Engineering*, 82(1), 35–45. DOI: [10.1115/1.3662552](https://doi.org/10.1115/1.3662552)  
  *The foundational paper introducing the Kalman filter, which forms the core of modern real-time GNSS/INS direct georeferencing and TPU covariance propagation.*

* **Lague, D., Brodu, N., & Leroux, J.** (2013). Accurate 3D comparison of complex topography with terrestrial laser scanner: Application to the Rangitikei canyon (N-Z). *ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing*, 82, 10–26. DOI: [10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2013.05.002](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2013.05.002)  
  *Derives the Multiscale Model-to-Model Cloud Comparison (M3C2) algorithm, defining surface-normal projection distances and local confidence bounds for ungridded point clouds.*

* **Nuth, C., & Kääb, A.** (2011). Co-registration and bias corrections of satellite elevation data sets for quantifying glacier thickness change. *The Cryosphere*, 5(1), 271–290. DOI: [10.5194/tc-5-271-2011](https://doi.org/10.5194/tc-5-271-2011)  
  *The seminal paper deriving the closed-form trigonometric relationship between horizontal terrain misregistration, slope, and aspect, establishing the universal standard for analytical DEM co-registration.*

* **Shannon, C. E.** (1948). A mathematical theory of communication. *Bell System Technical Journal*, 27(3), 379–423. DOI: [10.1002/j.1538-7305.1948.tb01338.x](https://doi.org/10.1002/j.1538-7305.1948.tb01338.x)  
  *Established information entropy and the mathematical sampling theorem governing discrete spatial reconstruction and anti-aliasing.*

### Government Standards and Accuracy Specifications

* **Federal Geographic Data Committee (FGDC).** (1998). *National Standard for Spatial Data Accuracy (NSSDA)*. FGDC-STD-007.3-1998. Reston, VA: Federal Geographic Data Committee.  
  *Established standard parametric root mean square error (RMSE) testing and the 95% linear error (LE95) confidence formulation for digital geospatial products.*

* **International Hydrographic Organization (IHO).** (2020). *IHO Standards for Hydrographic Surveys*. Special Publication No. 44 (S-44), 6th Edition. Monaco: International Hydrographic Bureau.  
  *Defines rigorous Total Vertical Uncertainty (TVU) and Total Horizontal Uncertainty (THU) depth-dependent formulations across four international survey orders.*

* **National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA).** (2024). *Hydrographic Surveys Specifications and Deliverables (HSSD)*. Office of Coast Survey, National Ocean Service, Silver Spring, MD.  
  *Mandates TPU error budgets for acoustic soundings and establishes CUBE multi-hypothesis surface processing parameters.*
