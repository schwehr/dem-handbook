## 15.10 Curated Key References

The seminal papers, standards documents, and algorithm specifications listed below establish the foundational data structures, mathematical PDEs, Bayesian estimators, and multi-resolution formats governing digital elevation and bathymetric surface modeling.

---

### Foundational Papers and Algorithmic Literature

* **Berger, M. J., & Oliger, J.** (1984). Adaptive mesh refinement for hyperbolic partial differential equations. *Journal of Computational Physics*, 53(3), 484–512. https://doi.org/10.1016/0021-9991(84)90073-1  
    *Establishes block-structured Adaptive Mesh Refinement (AMR) with nested spatial-temporal sub-cycling, forming the computational foundation for multi-resolution tsunami and flood inundation solvers (`GeoClaw`).*

* **Billingsley, F. C.** (1967). Processing Ranger and Mariner photography. *Computerized Imaging Techniques, Proceedings of the SPIE*, Vol. 10, XV-1–XV-19 (presented August 1965, San Francisco).  
    *The landmark NASA JPL publication introducing the portmanteau "pixel" (picture element) during digital reconstruction of the first lunar Ranger and Mars Mariner 4 television scans.*

* **Butler, H., Chambers, B., Hartzell, P., & Glennie, C.** (2021). PDAL: An open source library for the processing and analysis of point clouds. *Computers & Geosciences*, 148, 104680. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cageo.2020.104680  
    *Documents the streaming C++ and declarative JSON pipeline architecture of the Point Data Abstraction Library (`PDAL`) across terrestrial, airborne, and cloud-optimized (`COPC`) point clouds.*

* **Calder, B. R., & Rice, G.** (2011). Design and implementation of an extensible variable resolution bathymetric estimator. *Proceedings of the U.S. Hydrographic Conference (US HYDRO 2011)*, Tampa, FL.  
    *Introduces the CUBE with Hierarchical Resolution Techniques (`CHRT`) algorithm, formalizing density- and depth-adaptive supergrid scaling within Open Navigation Surface `VR-BAG` containers.*

* **Calder, B. R., & Mayer, L. A.** (2003). Automatic processing of high-rate, high-density multibeam echosounder data. *Geochemistry, Geophysics, Geosystems*, 4(6), 1048. https://doi.org/10.1029/2002GC000486  
    *The seminal paper introducing the `CUBE` algorithm, deriving sequential Bayesian Kalman hypothesis tracking, West–Harrison outlier intervention, and distance-dependent TPU variance inflation.*

* **Duchaineau, M., Wolinsky, M., Sigeti, D. E., Miller, M. C., Aldrich, C., & Mineev-Weinstein, M. B.** (1997). ROAMing terrain: Real-time optimally adapting meshes. *Proceedings of Visualization '97 (IEEE VIS '97)*, 81–88 (IEEE Xplore digital edition, 2002). https://doi.org/10.1109/VISUAL.1997.663860  
    *Introduces binary triangle tree diamond splitting (`ROAM`), mathematically guaranteeing crack-free continuous $C^0$ level-of-detail transitions across variable-resolution terrain meshes.*

* **Finkel, R. A., & Bentley, J. L.** (1974). Quad trees: A data structure for retrieval on composite keys. *Acta Informatica*, 4(1), 1–9. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00288933  
    *The foundational computer science paper formalizing the recursive 2D quadtree decomposition underlying multi-scale raster pyramids and octree spatial indexing.*

* **Isenburg, M.** (2013). LASzip: Lossless compression of LiDAR data. *Photogrammetric Engineering & Remote Sensing*, 79(2), 209–217. https://doi.org/10.14358/PERS.79.2.209  
    *Details the streaming delta-prediction and arithmetic entropy coding algorithms of `LASzip` (`.laz`), achieving $5\text{–}8\times$ lossless compression and powering `COPC` octree chunks.*

* **Mitasova, H., Mitas, L., & Harmon, R. S.** (2005). Simultaneous spline approximation and topographic analysis for lidar elevation data in open-source GIS. *IEEE Geoscience and Remote Sensing Letters*, 2(4), 375–379. https://doi.org/10.1109/LGRS.2005.848533  
    *Formulates Regularized Spline with Tension (`RST`, GRASS `v.surf.rst`) for noisy LiDAR point clouds, deriving analytic partial derivatives for simultaneous slope and curvature estimation.*

* **Peucker, T. K., Fowler, R. J., Little, J. J., & Mark, D. M.** (1978). The triangulated irregular network. *Proceedings of the Digital Terrain Models (DTM) Symposium*, American Society of Photogrammetry, St. Louis, MO, 516–532.  
    *The foundational automated-cartography paper defining the Triangulated Irregular Network (`TIN`), Very Important Points (`VIPs`), and topological hard breaklines.*

* **Smith, W. H. F., & Wessel, P.** (1990). Gridding with continuous curvature splines in tension. *Geophysics*, 55(3), 293–305. https://doi.org/10.1190/1.1442837  
    *Derives the dimensionally scaled biharmonic spline-in-tension partial differential equation implemented in `GMT surface`, suppressing thin-plate overshoot near steep scarps and trenches.*

* **Wagner, W., Ullrich, A., Ducic, V., Melzer, T., & Studnicka, N.** (2006). Gaussian decomposition and calibration of a novel small-footprint full-waveform digitising airborne laser scanner. *ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing*, 60(2), 100–112. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2005.12.001  
    *Establishes the physical convolution and Gaussian mixture decomposition framework for full-waveform airborne LiDAR range, pulse-width roughness, and radiometric cross-section extraction.*

### Industry Standards and Specifications

* **American Society for Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing (ASPRS).** (2019). *LAS Specification, Version 1.4 – R15*. Bethesda, MD: ASPRS. https://www.asprs.org/divisions-committees/lidar-division/laser-las-file-format-exchange-activities  
    *The authoritative open binary standard for 3D point cloud and full-waveform (`WDP`) storage, specifying Extended Point Data Record Formats 6–10, `Extra Bytes` VLRs, and semantic classes 0–255.*

* **International Hydrographic Organization (IHO).** (2022). *Bathymetric Surface Product Specification* (Publication S-102, Edition 2.1.0 / 2.2.0). Monaco: IHO. https://iho.int/en/s-102-project-team  
    *Standardizes HDF5 positive-up gridded bathymetry and mandatory co-registered uncertainty layers for S-100 Electronic Navigational Charts and Under-Keel Clearance systems.*

* **Open Geospatial Consortium (OGC).** (2019). *OGC GeoTIFF Standard, Version 1.1* (OGC Document 19-008r4). Wayland, MA: OGC. https://docs.ogc.org/is/19-008r4/19-008r4.html  
    *Standardizes CRS GeoKeys, the 6-parameter affine geotransform, and the normative `GTRasterTypeGeoKey` distinction between `RasterPixelIsArea` and `RasterPixelIsPoint`.*

* **Open Navigation Surface (ONS) Working Group.** (2020). *Bathymetric Attributed Grid (BAG) Format Specification, Version 1.6.3* (https://opennavsurf.org/) and Hobu, Inc. (2021), *Cloud-Optimized Point Cloud (COPC) Specification, Version 1.0* (https://copc.io/).  
    *Defines the HDF5 schema for coupled hydrographic elevation, uncertainty, designated-sounding tracking lists, and variable-resolution (`VR-BAG`) supergrids alongside `COPC` octree point streaming.*
