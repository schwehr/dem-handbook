## 15.10 Curated Key References

The seminal papers, standards documents, and algorithm specifications listed below establish the foundational data structures, mathematical PDEs, and multi-resolution algorithms governing digital elevation modeling.

---

### Foundational Papers and Algorithmic Literature

* **Billingsley, F. C.** (1965). Processing of Mariner 4 pictures. *SPIE Journal*, 4(1), 147–152.  
  *The landmark publication coining the term "pixel" (picture element) during digital image reconstruction of the first close-up television scans of Mars at NASA JPL.*

* **Calder, B. R.** (2013). Variable-resolution bathymetric attributed grids. *Proceedings of the U.S. Hydrographic Conference (US HYDRO 2013)*, New Orleans, LA.  
  *Introduced the CUBE with Hierarchical Resolution Techniques (CHRT) algorithm, formalizing power-of-two depth-dependent cell scaling within Open Navigation Surface BAG containers.*

* **Calder, B. R., & Mayer, L. A.** (2003). Automatic processing of high-rate, high-density multibeam echosounder data. *Geochemistry, Geophysics, Geosystems*, 4(6), 1048. DOI: [10.1029/2002GC000486](https://doi.org/10.1029/2002GC000486)  
  *The seminal paper introducing the CUBE algorithm, deriving sequential Kalman hypothesis tracking and distance-dependent TPU variance inflation.*

* **Duchaineau, M., Wolinsky, M., Sigeti, D. E., Miller, M. C., Aldrich, C., & Mineev-Weinstein, M. B.** (1997). ROAMing terrain: Real-time optimally adapting meshes. *Proceedings of the 8th Conference on Visualization (VIS '97)*, 81–88. DOI: [10.1109/VISUAL.1997.663860](https://doi.org/10.1109/VISUAL.1997.663860)  
  *Introduced binary triangle tree diamond splitting, mathematically guaranteeing crack-free continuous level-of-detail (LOD) transitions across variable-resolution terrain meshes.*

* **Finkel, R. A., & Bentley, J. L.** (1974). Quad trees: A data structure for retrieval on composite keys. *Acta Informatica*, 4(1), 1–9. DOI: [10.1007/BF00288933](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00288933)  
  *The foundational computer science paper introducing the quadtree data structure for recursive 2D spatial subdivision.*

* **Peucker, T. K., Fowler, R. J., Little, J. J., & Mark, D. M.** (1978). The triangulated irregular network. *Proceedings of the Digital Terrain Models (DTM) Symposium*, American Society of Photogrammetry, 516–532.  
  *The foundational paper defining the Triangulated Irregular Network (TIN), adaptive surface simplification, very important points (VIPs), and structural breaklines.*

* **Smith, W. H. F., & Wessel, P.** (1990). Gridding with continuous curvature splines in tension. *Geophysics*, 55(3), 293–305. DOI: [10.1190/1.1442837](https://doi.org/10.1190/1.1442837)  
  *Derives the biharmonic spline in tension partial differential equation, establishing the mathematical standard for continuous geophysical and bathymetric gridding without overshoot.*

### Industry Standards and Specifications

* **American Society for Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing (ASPRS).** (2019). *LAS Specification, Version 1.4 – R15*. Bethesda, MD: ASPRS.  
  *The authoritative open binary standard for 3D point cloud storage, specifying point data record formats 0 through 10, spatial scaling, and semantic classification codes.*

* **Open Geospatial Consortium (OGC).** (2019). *OGC GeoTIFF Standard, Version 1.1*. OGC Document 19-008r4. Wayland, MA: Open Geospatial Consortium.  
  *Standardizes the Affine Geotransform and formalizes the operational distinction between PixelIsArea and PixelIsPoint raster registrations.*

* **Open Navigation Surface (ONS) Working Group.** (2020). *Bathymetric Attributed Grid (BAG) Format Specification, Version 1.6.3*. Open Navigation Surface.  
  *The official international specification defining the HDF5 hierarchical storage schema for coupled elevation and uncertainty bathymetric surfaces, including variable-resolution extensions.*
