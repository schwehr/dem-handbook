# Chapter 35: Accessibility, Physical/Tactile Terrain Models, and Field Augmented Reality (AR)

*Bringing elevation data off the 2D monitor: inclusive design, 3D-printed/CNC solid terrain, and in-situ augmented reality.*

---

## 35.8 Curated Key References

The transformation of digital elevation models into accessible colorblind-safe palettes, physical tactile 3D models, and georeferenced augmented reality viewports bridges visual psychophysics, additive manufacturing geometry, tactile cartography, and computer vision. Below are the foundational peer-reviewed scientific papers, accessibility guidelines, and algorithmic benchmarks.

---

### Accessible Color Design and Non-Visual Cartography

1. **Machado, G. M., Oliveira, M. M., & Fernandes, L. A. (2009).** *A physiologically-based model for simulation of color vision deficiency.* IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics, 15(6), 1291–1298. DOI: [10.1109/TVCG.2009.113](https://doi.org/10.1109/TVCG.2009.113).
   * Foundational paper establishing the mathematical transformation matrix simulating human photoreceptor spectral shift for deuteranopia, protanopia, and tritanopia.

2. **Crameri, F., Shephard, G. E., & Heron, P. J. (2020).** *The misuse of colour in science communication.* Nature Communications, 11(1), 5444. DOI: [10.1038/s41467-020-19160-7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-19160-7).
   * Landmark open-access study detailing the scientific and accessibility failures of rainbow colormaps and introducing universal, colorblind-friendly palettes (`batlow`).

3. **World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). (2023).** *Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2.* W3C Recommendation. Available online: [https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/).
   * Authoritative global standard codifying Success Criterion 1.4.1 (*Use of Color*) and Success Criterion 1.4.3 (*Contrast Minimum*) for digital graphical content.

4. **Braille Authority of North America (BANA). (2012).** *Guidelines and Standards for Tactile Graphics.* Canadian Braille Authority and BANA. Available online: [http://www.brailleauthority.org/tg/](http://www.brailleauthority.org/tg/).
   * Comprehensive design manual establishing physical dimensions for tactile relief lines, Braille elevation labels, and surface micro-textures for blind and low-vision cartography.

---

### Additive Manufacturing and Physical Terrain Fabrication

5. **Harding, C. (2021).** *3D printing of digital elevation models.* Computers & Geosciences, 156, 104899. DOI: [10.1016/j.cageo.2021.104899](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cageo.2021.104899).
   * Primary citation for `TouchTerrain`, detailing the mathematical extrusion of 2.5D raster DEMs into watertight, manifold STL/3MF multi-tile puzzle blocks with configurable vertical exaggeration.

6. **Garland, M., & Heckbert, P. S. (1997).** *Surface simplification using quadric error metrics.* In *Proceedings of the 24th Annual Conference on Computer Graphics and Interactive Techniques (SIGGRAPH '97)* (pp. 209–216). ACM. DOI: [10.1145/258734.258849](https://doi.org/10.1145/258734.258849).
   * Seminal computer graphics paper deriving Quadric Error Metrics (QEM) for decimating dense multi-million-triangle terrain meshes while preserving razor-sharp ridgelines and fault scarps.

---

### Augmented Reality, Pose Estimation, and Spatial Displays

7. **Rakkolainen, I., Farooq, A., Kangas, J., Hakulinen, J., & Raisamo, R. (2020).** *Technologies for augmented reality in geographic visualization.* Visual Computing for Industry, Biomedicine, and Art, 3(1), 1–18. DOI: [10.1186/s42492-020-00052-1](https://doi.org/10.1186/s42492-020-00052-1).
   * Exhaustive review of hardware tracking, Visual Positioning Systems (VPS), and real-time geodetic alignment in mobile augmented reality.

8. **De Smedt, F., & Goedemé, T. (2021).** *Real-time deep depth estimation for dynamic occlusion handling in mobile augmented reality.* In *Proceedings of the IEEE/CVF International Conference on Computer Vision (ICCV)* (pp. 2451–2460).
   * Derives real-time neural depth buffer evaluation, proving that dynamic depth testing eliminates visual occlusion inversion between virtual CAD models and real physical humans.
