# Chapter 35: Accessibility, Physical/Tactile Terrain Models, and Field Augmented Reality (AR)

*Bringing elevation data off the 2D monitor: inclusive design, 3D-printed/CNC solid terrain, and in-situ augmented reality.*

---

## 35.8 Curated Key References

The transformation of digital elevation models into accessible colorblind-safe palettes, physical tactile 3D models, and georeferenced augmented reality viewports bridges visual psychophysics, additive manufacturing geometry, tactile cartography, and computer vision. Below are the foundational peer-reviewed scientific papers, accessibility guidelines, and algorithmic benchmarks.

---

### Accessible Color Design, Perceptual Psychophysics, and Sonification

1. **Machado, G. M., Oliveira, M. M., & Fernandes, L. A. (2009).** *A physiologically-based model for simulation of color vision deficiency.* IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics, 15(6), 1291–1298. DOI: [10.1109/TVCG.2009.113](https://doi.org/10.1109/TVCG.2009.113).
   * Foundational paper establishing the linear-RGB cone-shift transformation matrices simulating human photoreceptor deficiencies across deuteranopia, protanopia, and tritanopia.

2. **Crameri, F., Shephard, G. E., & Heron, P. J. (2020).** *The misuse of colour in science communication.* Nature Communications, 11(1), 5444. DOI: [10.1038/s41467-020-19160-7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-19160-7).
   * Landmark open-access study detailing the perceptual and accessibility failures of rainbow colormaps and introducing universal, colorblind-friendly palettes (`batlow`, `oleron`, `bukavu`).

3. **Sharma, G., Wu, W., & Dalal, E. N. (2005).** *The CIEDE2000 color-difference formula: Implementation notes, supplementary test data, and mathematical observations.* Color Research & Application, 30(1), 21–30. DOI: [10.1002/col.20070](https://doi.org/10.1002/col.20070).
   * Authoritative reference for computing CIEDE2000 ($\Delta E_{00}$) perceptual color contrast steps in automated accessibility CI/CD linters.

4. **Bearman, N., & Lovett, A. (2010).** *Using sound to represent positional accuracy of address locations.* The Cartographic Journal, 47(4), 308–314. DOI: [10.1179/000870410X12911302296833](https://doi.org/10.1179/000870410X12911302296833).
   * Empirical geospatial study demonstrating how pitch and timbre sonification encode spatial attributes and positional uncertainty for accessible GIS interfaces.

5. **World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). (2023).** *Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2.* W3C Recommendation (5 October 2023; Eds. C. Adams, A. Campbell, M. Cooper, A. Kirkpatrick, & R. B. Montgomery). Available online: [https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/).
   * Authoritative global standard codifying Success Criterion 1.4.1 (*Use of Color*) and Success Criterion 1.4.3 (*Contrast Minimum*) for digital graphical and cartographic content.

6. **Braille Authority of North America (BANA). (2012).** *Guidelines and Standards for Tactile Graphics.* Braille Authority of North America and Canadian Braille Authority (2010; Web Ed. 2012). Available online: [https://www.brailleauthority.org/tg](https://www.brailleauthority.org/tg).
   * Comprehensive design manual establishing physical dimensions for tactile relief lines, Braille elevation labels, and surface micro-textures for blind and low-vision cartography.

---

### Additive Manufacturing, Tactile Maps, and Physical Terrain Fabrication

7. **Hasiuk, F. J., Harding, C., Renner, A. R., & Winer, E. (2017).** *TouchTerrain: A simple web-tool for creating 3D-printable topographic models.* Computers & Geosciences, 109, 25–31. DOI: [10.1016/j.cageo.2017.07.005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cageo.2017.07.005).
   * Foundational peer-reviewed paper introducing `TouchTerrain`, detailing watertight extrusion of 2.5D raster DEMs into multi-tile 3D-printable STL models with configurable vertical exaggeration.

8. **Harding, C., Hasiuk, F., & Wood, A. (2021).** *TouchTerrain—3D Printable Terrain Models.* ISPRS International Journal of Geo-Information, 10(3), 108. DOI: [10.3390/ijgi10030108](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijgi10030108).
   * Documents the standalone Python CLI, Earth Engine integration, `.3mf` support, and advanced multi-tile interlocking fabrication enhancements of `TouchTerrain`.

9. **Rase, W.-D. (2012).** *Creating physical 3D maps using rapid prototyping techniques.* In M. F. Buchroithner (Ed.), *True-3D in Cartography: Autostereoscopic and Solid Visualisation of Geodata* (pp. 119–134). Springer, Berlin, Heidelberg. DOI: [10.1007/978-3-642-12272-9_8](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-12272-9_8).
   * Seminal chapter detailing vertical exaggeration guidelines, color 3D printing, and tactile/transparent multi-material physical thematic and terrain maps.

10. **Garland, M., & Heckbert, P. S. (1997).** *Surface simplification using quadric error metrics.* In *Proceedings of the 24th Annual Conference on Computer Graphics and Interactive Techniques (SIGGRAPH '97)* (pp. 209–216). ACM. DOI: [10.1145/258734.258849](https://doi.org/10.1145/258734.258849).
    * Seminal computer graphics paper deriving Quadric Error Metrics (QEM) for decimating dense multi-million-triangle terrain meshes while preserving sharp ridgelines and fault scarps.

### Virtual Globes, Field Augmented Reality, and Occlusion Handling

11. **Çöltekin, A., Lochhead, I., Madden, M., Christophe, S., Devaux, A., Pettit, C., Lock, O., Shukla, S., Herman, L., Stachoň, Z., Kubíček, P., Snopková, D., Bernardes, S., & Hedley, N. (2020).** *Extended Reality in Spatial Sciences: A Review of Research Challenges and Future Directions.* ISPRS International Journal of Geo-Information, 9(7), 439. DOI: [10.3390/ijgi9070439](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijgi9070439).
    * Comprehensive review of VR, AR, and MR in geospatial visualization, examining perceptual cognition, geodetic registration, and field decision-support challenges.

12. **Zollmann, S., Langlotz, T., Grasset, R., Lo, W. H., Mori, S., & Regenbrecht, H. (2021).** *Visualization techniques in augmented reality: A taxonomy, methods and patterns.* IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics, 27(9), 3808–3825. DOI: [10.1109/TVCG.2020.2986247](https://doi.org/10.1109/TVCG.2020.2986247).
    * Establishes design patterns for in-situ X-ray portals, depth-cued registration, and occlusion handling in outdoor and engineering AR systems.

13. **Macedo, M. C. F., & Apolinário, A. L. (2023).** *Occlusion handling in augmented reality: Past, present and future.* IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics, 29(2), 1590–1609. DOI: [10.1109/TVCG.2021.3117866](https://doi.org/10.1109/TVCG.2021.3117866).
    * Authoritative survey comparing hardware LiDAR/ToF depth-buffer masking, contour matting, and deep monocular depth estimation for eliminating occlusion inversion in mobile AR.

14. **Holynski, A., & Kopf, J. (2018).** *Fast depth densification for occlusion-aware augmented reality.* ACM Transactions on Graphics (SIGGRAPH Asia), 37(6), 194. DOI: [10.1145/3272127.3275083](https://doi.org/10.1145/3272127.3275083).
    * Derives real-time edge-aware depth densification from sparse SLAM/VIO features to enforce crisp foreground-background occlusion in handheld mobile AR.

15. **Cozzi, P., & Ring, K. (2011).** *3D Engine Design for Virtual Globes.* A K Peters / CRC Press. DOI: [10.1201/9781439865583](https://doi.org/10.1201/9781439865583).
    * Foundational reference on planetary virtual-globe rendering architectures, WGS84 terrain LoD streaming, and `RTC_CENTER` floating-point jitter mitigation.
