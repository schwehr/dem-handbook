# Chapter 26: Navigating, Charting, Topographic Maps, and Cartographic Production

*Turning elevation models into operational navigation systems, official nautical charts, and publication-grade topographic maps.*

---

## 26.8 Curated Key References

The transformation of digital elevation models into operational navigation systems, official topographic map series, and regulatory nautical charts represents one of the most rigorously codified disciplines in applied geodesy. The references below provide the authoritative scientific, mathematical, and historical foundation for terrain-aided navigation and cartographic production.

---

### Authoritative Cartographic and Geodetic References

1. **Imhof, E. (2007).** *Cartographic Relief Presentation* (Classic facsimile ed., H. J. Steward, Ed.). Esri Press, Redlands, CA. ISBN: [978-1589480261](https://isbnsearch.org/isbn/9781589480261).
   * The undisputed foundational masterwork on manual and theoretical topographic relief cartography. Covers contour interval selection, index line weighting, rock drawing, hachuring, and aerial perspective.

2. **Wessel, P., Luis, J. F., Uieda, L., Scharroo, R., Wobbe, F., Smith, W. H. F., & Tian, D. (2019).** *The Generic Mapping Tools Version 6.* Geochemistry, Geophysics, Geosystems, 20(11), 5556–5564. DOI: [10.1029/2019GC008515](https://doi.org/10.1029/2019GC008515).
   * The architectural reference for GMT 6, detailing the mathematical implementation of spherical projections, dual-grid borders, uphill contour labeling (`grdcontour`), and automated publication-grade EPS/PDF generation.

3. **Snyder, J. P. (1987).** *Map Projections—A Working Manual.* U.S. Geological Survey Professional Paper 1395, Washington, D.C. DOI: [10.3133/pp1395](https://doi.org/10.3133/pp1395).
   * Definitive mathematical treatise governing UTM, Transverse Mercator, Lambert Conformal Conic, Mercator scale factors ($k = \sec\varphi$), and grid convergence ($\gamma_{\text{conv}}$) used on topographic sheets and nautical charts.

4. **Davis, L. R., Fishburn, K. A., Lestinsky, H., Moore, L. R., & Walter, J. L. (2019).** *US Topo Product Standard (ver. 2.0, February 2019).* U.S. Geological Survey Techniques and Methods, book 11, chap. B2, Reston, VA. DOI: [10.3133/tm11B2](https://doi.org/10.3133/tm11B2).
   * The technical baseline specification governing modern US Topo layered GeoPDF production from 3DEP airborne LiDAR DTMs, National Hydrography Dataset vectors, and NAIP aerial orthoimagery.

5. **American Society for Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing (ASPRS). (2015).** *ASPRS Positional Accuracy Standards for Digital Geospatial Data (Edition 1, Version 1.0).* Photogrammetric Engineering & Remote Sensing, 81(3), A1–A26. DOI: [10.14358/PERS.81.3.A1-A26](https://doi.org/10.14358/PERS.81.3.A1-A26).
   * Authoritative Edition 1 standard linking DEM vertical accuracy ($\text{RMSE}_z$) to NMAS-equivalent contour intervals ($\text{CI} \ge 3.29 \cdot \text{RMSE}_z$) and defining 95%-confidence NVA/VVA testing.

6. **Abdullah, Q. (2023).** *The ASPRS Positional Accuracy Standards, Edition 2: The Geospatial Mapping Industry Guide to Best Practices.* Photogrammetric Engineering & Remote Sensing, 89(10), 581–588. DOI: [10.14358/PERS.89.10.581](https://doi.org/10.14358/PERS.89.10.581).
   * Summarizes Edition 2 (2023/2024) updates transitioning positional accuracy grading from 95%-confidence thresholds to direct $\text{RMSE}_V / \text{RMSE}_H$ classes and 30-checkpoint minimums.

### Nautical Charting and Generalization

7. **Peters, R., Ledoux, H., & Meijers, M. (2014).** *A Voronoi-Based Approach to Generating Depth-Contours for Hydrographic Charts.* Marine Geodesy, 37(2), 145–166. DOI: [10.1080/01490419.2014.902882](https://doi.org/10.1080/01490419.2014.902882).
   * Rigorous mathematical analysis of the asymmetric, shoal-safe bathymetric contour generalization problem, formulating algorithmic guarantees that generalized isobaths displace strictly seaward into deeper water.

8. **International Hydrographic Organization (IHO). (2023).** *S-102 Bathymetric Surface Product Specification (Edition 2.2.0; updated Ed. 3.0.0, 2024).* International Hydrographic Organization, Monaco. Available online: [https://iho.int/en/s-100-universal-hydrographic-data-model](https://iho.int/en/s-100-universal-hydrographic-data-model).
   * Formal geospatial standard defining high-resolution gridded bathymetric deliverables (positive-up HDF5 BAG structure) and dynamic safety-depth contour rendering inside next-generation Electronic Chart Display and Information Systems (ECDIS).

9. **National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA). (1992; continuously updated).** *Nautical Chart Manual (7th ed.).* Office of Coast Survey, Marine Chart Division, Silver Spring, MD. Available online: [https://nauticalcharts.noaa.gov/publications/nautical-chart-manual.html](https://nauticalcharts.noaa.gov/publications/nautical-chart-manual.html).
   * The exhaustive operational standard for compiling nautical paper charts and Electronic Navigational Charts (ENCs), mandating shoal-biased sounding selection and dangerous pinnacle overrides.

### Terrain-Relative Navigation (TRN) and Aviation Safety

10. **Bateman, C. D. (1976).** *Aircraft ground proximity warning instrument.* U.S. Patent No. 3,946,358. Washington, DC: U.S. Patent and Trademark Office.
    * Foundational GPWS patent by C. Donald Bateman establishing automated flight-path terrain clearance alerting, laying the groundwork for GPS- and DEM-driven EGPWS/TAWS.

11. **Muller, H. R., Conner, K. J., & Johnson, S. C. (1998).** *Ground proximity warning system.* U.S. Patent No. 5,839,080. Washington, DC: U.S. Patent and Trademark Office.
    * Core AlliedSignal/Honeywell EGPWS patent detailing forward-looking terrain clearance envelopes computed against an onboard digital terrain and obstacle database.

12. **Meduna, D. K., Rock, S. M., & McEwen, R. (2008).** *Low-cost terrain relative navigation for long-range AUVs.* In *MTS/IEEE OCEANS 2008*, Quebec City, QC, pp. 1–7. DOI: [10.1109/OCEANS.2008.5152043](https://doi.org/10.1109/OCEANS.2008.5152043).
    * Derives the Bayesian particle filtering (SMC) architecture for subsea bathymetric-aided navigation, demonstrating real-time covariance bounding over multibeam acoustic grids.

13. **Hostetler, L. D., & Andreas, R. D. (1983).** *Nonlinear Kalman filtering techniques for terrain-aided navigation.* IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control, 28(3), 315–323. DOI: [10.1109/TAC.1983.1103232](https://doi.org/10.1109/TAC.1983.1103232).
    * Landmark Sandia National Laboratories paper establishing recursive Bayesian/SITAN terrain-aided navigation matching radar altimeter profiles against onboard DEM matrices.
