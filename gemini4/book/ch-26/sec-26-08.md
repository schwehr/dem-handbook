# Chapter 26: Navigating, Charting, Topographic Maps, and Cartographic Production

*Turning elevation models into operational navigation systems, official nautical charts, and publication-grade topographic maps.*

---

## 26.8 Curated Key References

The transformation of digital elevation models into operational navigation systems, official topographic map series, and regulatory nautical charts represents one of the most rigorously codified disciplines in applied geodesy. The references below provide the authoritative scientific, mathematical, and historical foundation for terrain-aided navigation and cartographic production.

---

### Authoritative Cartographic and Geodetic References

1. **Imhof, E. (2007).** *Cartographic Relief Presentation* (Classic facsimile ed., H. J. Steward, Ed.). Esri Press, Redlands, CA. ISBN: [978-1589480261](https://isbnsearch.org/isbn/9781589480261).
   * The undisputed foundational masterwork on manual and theoretical topographic relief cartography. Covers contour interval selection, index line weighting, rock drawing, hachuring, and aerial perspective.

2. **Wessel, P., Luis, J. F., Uieda, L., Scharroo, R., Wobbe, F., Smith, W. H. F., & Tian, D. (2019).** *The Generic Mapping Tools Version 6.* Geochemistry, Geophysics, Geosystems, 20(11), 5556–5564. DOI: [10.1029/2019GC008515](https://doi.org/10.1029/2019GC008515).
   * The architectural reference for GMT 6, detailing the mathematical implementation of spherical projections, dual-grid borders, uphill contour labeling (`grdcontour`), and automated publication-grade EPS/PDF generation.

3. **U.S. Geological Survey (USGS). (2020).** *US Topo Product Standard.* U.S. Geological Survey Techniques and Methods, book 11, chap. B2, Reston, VA. DOI: [10.3133/tm11B2](https://doi.org/10.3133/tm11B2).
   * The technical baseline specification governing modern US Topo layered GeoPDF production from 3DEP airborne LiDAR DTMs, National Hydrography Dataset vectors, and NAIP aerial orthoimagery.

4. **American Society for Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing (ASPRS). (2024).** *ASPRS Positional Accuracy Standards for Digital Geospatial Data (Edition 2).* Photogrammetric Engineering & Remote Sensing, 90(2), 75–104. DOI: [10.14358/PERS.23-00050R](https://doi.org/10.14358/PERS.23-00050R).
   * Authoritative standard defining the mathematical relationship between DEM vertical accuracy ($\text{RMSE}_z$) and legal contour intervals ($\text{CI} \ge 3.3 \cdot \text{RMSE}_z$).

---

### Nautical Charting and Generalization

5. **Peters, R., Ledoux, H., & Biljecki, F. (2014).** *Visibility-based generalization of bathymetric contours.* Marine Geodesy, 37(2), 145–166. DOI: [10.1080/01490419.2014.902886](https://doi.org/10.1080/01490419.2014.902886).
   * Rigorous mathematical analysis of the asymmetric, shoal-safe bathymetric contour generalization problem, formulating algorithmic guarantees that generalized isobaths displace strictly seaward into deeper water.

6. **International Hydrographic Organization (IHO). (2022).** *S-102 Bathymetric Surface Product Specification (Edition 2.2.0).* International Hydrographic Bureau, Monaco.
   * Formal geospatial standard defining high-resolution gridded bathymetric deliverables (HDF5 BAG structure) and dynamic safety-depth contour rendering inside next-generation Electronic Chart Display and Information Systems (ECDIS).

7. **National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA). (2023).** *Nautical Chart Manual (7th ed.).* Office of Coast Survey, Marine Chart Division, Silver Spring, MD.
   * The exhaustive operational standard for compiling nautical paper charts and Electronic Navigational Charts (ENCs), mandating shoal-biased sounding selection and dangerous pinnacle overrides.

### Terrain-Relative Navigation (TRN) and Aviation Safety

8. **Bateman, C. D. (1998).** *Terrain Awareness and Warning System (TAWS).* U.S. Patent No. 5,835,046. Washington, DC: U.S. Patent and Trademark Office.
   * Foundational patent detailing Honeywell's Enhanced Ground Proximity Warning System (EGPWS), combining GPS horizontal positioning with global digital elevation and obstacle matrices to eliminate CFIT accidents.

9. **Meduna, D. K., Rock, S. M., & McEwen, R. (2010).** *Low-cost terrain relative navigation for long-range autonomous underwater vehicles.* In *Field and Service Robotics* (pp. 269–279). Springer, Berlin, Heidelberg. DOI: [10.1007/978-3-642-13408-1_24](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-13408-1_24).
   * Derives the particle filtering (SMC) architecture for subsea bathymetric-aided navigation, demonstrating real-time covariance bounding over multi-beam acoustic grids.

10. **Hostetler, L. D., & Kohler, R. H. (1983).** *Transitional TERCOM/SITAN terrain-aided navigation.* In *Proceedings of the IEEE National Aerospace and Electronics Conference (NAECON)* (pp. 1282–1289). DOI: [10.1109/PLANS.1982.8427909](https://doi.org/10.1109/PLANS.1982.8427909).
    * Landmark Sandia National Laboratories report establishing the continuous Kalman and particle filter formulations matching radar altimeter swaths against digital terrain matrices for aerospace guidance.
