## 13.8 Curated Key References

The historical manuals, agency specifications, and peer-reviewed articles listed below define the operational methodologies and mathematical principles governing survey planning, boresight calibration, and quality assurance.

---

### Government Standards, Manuals, and Specifications

* **ASPRS.** (2015). ASPRS Positional Accuracy Standards for Digital Geospatial Data (Edition 1, Version 1.0; updated in Edition 2, Version 1.0, 2023). *Photogrammetric Engineering & Remote Sensing*, 81(3), A1–A26. DOI: [10.14358/PERS.81.3.A1-A26](https://doi.org/10.14358/PERS.81.3.A1-A26)  
  *Establishes horizontal and vertical accuracy classes (Edition 1 $\text{RMSE}_z$, $\text{NVA} = 1.9600\,\text{RMSE}_z$, and $\text{VVA}$; updated in Edition 2 to direct $\text{RMSE}_V$ classes with $\ge 30$ checkpoints and informational $\text{VVA}$) plus relative swath-to-swath overlap tolerances for airborne LiDAR and photogrammetric elevation surveys.*

* **Hawley, J. H.** (1928). *Hydrographic Manual*. U.S. Coast and Geodetic Survey Special Publication No. 143. Washington, D.C.: U.S. Government Printing Office, 170 pp.  
  *The landmark historical manual that first standardized orthogonal cross-lines, systematic sounding line spacing, and lead-line calibration protocols in the United States.*

* **Heidemann, H. K.** (2014/2018). *Lidar Base Specification* (ver. 1.2, November 2014; ver. 1.3, February 2018). U.S. Geological Survey Techniques and Methods, book 11, chap. B4. Reston, VA: U.S. Geological Survey. DOI: [10.3133/tm11B4](https://doi.org/10.3133/tm11B4)  
  *Establishes aggregate nominal pulse density requirements (QL1 $\ge 8\text{ pulses/m}^2$, QL2 $\ge 2\text{ pulses/m}^2$), swath overlap consistency limits, and cross-line flight planning specifications for the 3D Elevation Program (3DEP).*

* **International Hydrographic Organization (IHO).** (2020). *IHO Standards for Hydrographic Surveys*. Special Publication No. 44 (S-44), 6th Edition. Monaco: International Hydrographic Organization.  
  *Defines global standards for hydrographic survey orders (Exclusive, Special, Order 1a/1b, Order 2), establishing mathematical Total Vertical Uncertainty (TVU) and Total Horizontal Uncertainty (THU) formulas.*

* **National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA).** (2020). *Field Procedures Manual (FPM) for Hydrographic Surveying*. Office of Coast Survey, National Ocean Service, Silver Spring, MD.  
  *The authoritative operational guide detailing multibeam patch-test maneuvers, moving vessel profiler (MVP) protocols, ellipsoidally referenced surveys (ERS), and real-time QA.*

* **National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA).** (2024). *Hydrographic Surveys Specifications and Deliverables (HSSD)*. Office of Coast Survey, National Ocean Service, Silver Spring, MD.  
  *Binding technical specifications establishing mandatory cross-line coverage, CUBE gridding parameters, and automated holiday detection requirements.*

* **Umbach, M. J.** (1976). *Hydrographic Manual, Fourth Edition*. Rockville, MD: National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration, National Ocean Survey.  
  *Seminal manual modernizing survey planning for the electronic era, formalizing the daily acoustic bar check and radio navigation geometric dilution constraints.*

### Seminal Papers and Methodological Literature

* **Calder, B. R., & Mayer, L. A.** (2003). Automatic processing of high-rate, high-density multibeam echosounder data. *Geochemistry, Geophysics, Geosystems*, 4(6), 1048. DOI: [10.1029/2002GC000486](https://doi.org/10.1029/2002GC000486)  
  *The original paper deriving the Combined Uncertainty and Bathymetry Estimator (CUBE) multi-hypothesis Kalman filter algorithm.*

* **Glennie, C.** (2007). Rigorous 3D error analysis of kinematic scanning LIDAR systems. *Journal of Applied Geodesy*, 1(3), 147–157. DOI: [10.1515/JAG.2007.017](https://doi.org/10.1515/JAG.2007.017)  
  *Quantifies first-order 3D error propagation from GNSS/INS trajectory, boresight angles, lever arms, and laser scan geometry across airborne and terrestrial platforms.*

* **Glira, P., Pfeifer, N., Briese, C., & Ressl, C.** (2015). Rigorous strip adjustment of airborne laserscanning data based on the ICP algorithm. *ISPRS Annals of the Photogrammetry, Remote Sensing and Spatial Information Sciences*, II-3/W5, 73–80. DOI: [10.5194/isprsannals-II-3-W5-73-2015](https://doi.org/10.5194/isprsannals-II-3-W5-73-2015)  
  *Formulates point-to-plane Iterative Closest Point (ICP) strip adjustment and normal-matrix observability analysis for multi-strip airborne LiDAR calibration.*

* **Godin, A.** (1998). *The Calibration of Shallow Water Multibeam Echo-Sounding Systems*. M.Eng. Report, Technical Report No. 190, Department of Geodesy and Geomatics Engineering, University of New Brunswick, Fredericton, NB, Canada, 182 pp.  
  *Derives the complete geometric formulation of the multibeam patch test, detailing parameter observability and residual sensitivity across variable seafloor slopes.*

* **Habib, A., Bang, K. I., Kersting, A. P., & Chow, J.** (2010). Alternative methodologies for LiDAR system calibration. *Remote Sensing*, 2(3), 874–907. DOI: [10.3390/rs2030874](https://doi.org/10.3390/rs2030874)  
  *Compares rigorous, quasi-rigorous, and tie-feature flight configurations for decoupling airborne LiDAR lever-arm and boresight parameters.*

* **Hughes Clarke, J. E.** (2003). Dynamic motion residuals in swath sonar data: Ironing out the creases. *International Hydrographic Review*, 4(1), 6–23.  
  *Forensic analysis of dynamic attitude, latency, and refraction artifacts in multibeam swath bathymetry and how survey geometry separates motion wobbles from sound-speed errors.*

* **James, M. R., & Robson, S.** (2014). Mitigating systematic error in topographic models derived from UAV and ground-based image networks. *Earth Surface Processes and Landforms*, 39(10), 1413–1420. DOI: [10.1002/esp.3609](https://doi.org/10.1002/esp.3609)  
  *Proves that parallel nadir UAV flight plans induce radial doming deformation during camera self-calibration and demonstrates how convergent oblique cross-lines restore parameter observability.*

* **Skaloud, J., & Lichti, D.** (2006). Rigorous approach to bore-sight self-calibration in airborne laser scanning. *ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing*, 61(1), 47–59. DOI: [10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2006.07.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2006.07.003)  
  *Rigorous derivation of the boresight observation equations, proving the rank deficiencies that occur over flat ground and defining optimal flight-strip geometries.*
