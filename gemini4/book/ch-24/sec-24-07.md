# Chapter 24: Survey Standards, Guide Documents, and Key Public DEM Products

*The official specifications that govern professional elevation and hydrographic surveys, and a critical comparison of the world's major open DEM datasets.*

---

## 24.7 Curated Key References

The standards, statistical accuracy thresholds, and public datasets discussed in this chapter represent foundational technical accomplishments across federal agencies, international bodies, and scientific consortia. Below are the key authoritative references, standards documents, and peer-reviewed benchmark publications.

---

### Authoritative Standards and Guide Documents

1. **NOAA Office of Coast Survey (OCS). (2024).** *Hydrographic Surveys Specifications and Deliverables (HSSD).* National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration, Silver Spring, MD. Available online: [https://nauticalcharts.noaa.gov/publications/standards-and-requirements.html](https://nauticalcharts.noaa.gov/publications/standards-and-requirements.html).
   * The annual benchmark specification governing multibeam coverage, feature detection, Total Vertical Uncertainty (TVU), cross-line checks, designated soundings, and Bathymetric Attributed Grid (BAG) deliverables for U.S. waters (overhauled in 2024 for S-100 alignment).

2. **International Hydrographic Organization (IHO). (2022).** *Standards for Hydrographic Surveys (Special Publication No. 44 — S-44, Edition 6.1.0; Ed. 6.0.0 published 2020).* International Hydrographic Organization, Monaco. Available online: [https://iho.int/en/standards-and-specifications](https://iho.int/en/standards-and-specifications).
   * Universal international standard defining Exclusive Order, Special Order, Order 1a, Order 1b, and Order 2 survey tiers, along with the Specification Matrix and depth-dependent $\text{TVU}_{\max}(d)$ and $\text{THU}_{\max}(d)$ formulations.

3. **Heidemann, H. K. (2014; rev. 2018, ver. 1.3).** *Lidar Base Specification.* U.S. Geological Survey Techniques and Methods, book 11, chap. B4, 101 p. DOI: [10.3133/tm11B4](https://doi.org/10.3133/tm11B4).
   * Foundational specification for the USGS 3D Elevation Program (3DEP), establishing Quality Levels (QL0–QL3), nominal pulse spacing/density, vertical accuracy, and cartographic hydro-flattening rules.

4. **American Society for Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing (ASPRS). (2015).** *ASPRS Positional Accuracy Standards for Digital Geospatial Data (Edition 1, Version 1.0).* Photogrammetric Engineering & Remote Sensing, 81(3), A1–A26. DOI: [10.14358/PERS.81.3.A1-A26](https://doi.org/10.14358/PERS.81.3.A1-A26).
   * Landmark Edition 1 standard establishing Non-Vegetated Vertical Accuracy ($\text{NVA} = 1.9600 \cdot \text{RMSE}_z$) and Vegetated Vertical Accuracy ($\text{VVA}$) via non-parametric 95th percentile analysis.

5. **Abdullah, Q. (2023).** *The ASPRS Positional Accuracy Standards, Edition 2: The Geospatial Mapping Industry Guide to Best Practices.* Photogrammetric Engineering & Remote Sensing, 89(10), 581–588. DOI: [10.14358/PERS.89.10.581](https://doi.org/10.14358/PERS.89.10.581).
   * Authoritative overview of ASPRS Edition 2 (v1.0, 2023; v2.0 adopted June 2024), detailing the shift to direct $\text{RMSE}_V$ reporting, the 30-checkpoint minimum, survey checkpoint error integration, and VVA diagnostic reporting.

6. **Umbach, M. J. (1976).** *Hydrographic Manual (4th Edition).* U.S. Department of Commerce, National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration, National Ocean Survey, Rockville, MD (prepared under Capt. R. C. Munson). Available online: [https://nauticalcharts.noaa.gov/publications/docs/standards-and-requirements/historical-publications/hydrographic-manual-4th-ed.pdf](https://nauticalcharts.noaa.gov/publications/docs/standards-and-requirements/historical-publications/hydrographic-manual-4th-ed.pdf).
   * Historic masterwork establishing standard operational procedures for analog single-beam hydrographic surveys, bar checks, vessel squat corrections, and electronic microwave positioning.

7. **Hawley, J. H. (1928).** *Hydrographic Manual.* U.S. Coast and Geodetic Survey, Special Publication No. 143, U.S. Government Printing Office, Washington, D.C. Available online: [https://library.oarcloud.noaa.gov/docs.lib/htdocs/rescue/cgs_specpubs/QB275U35no1431928.pdf](https://library.oarcloud.noaa.gov/docs.lib/htdocs/rescue/cgs_specpubs/QB275U35no1431928.pdf).
   * Landmark manual codifying 19th- and early 20th-century hand-lead sounding practices, sextant three-point fixes, and wire-drag obstruction sweep operations.

### Key Public Dataset and Scientific References

8. **Tozer, B., Sandwell, D. T., Smith, W. H. F., Olson, C., Beale, J. R., & Wessel, P. (2019).** *Global bathymetry and topography at 15 arc sec: SRTM15+.* Earth and Space Science, 6(10), 1847–1864. DOI: [10.1029/2019EA000658](https://doi.org/10.1029/2019EA000658).
   * Documents the construction of the global 15 arc-second bathymetric grid combining acoustic soundings with satellite marine gravity inversions, forming the core of GEBCO 2019–2024.

9. **Smith, W. H. F., & Sandwell, D. T. (1997).** *Global Sea Floor Topography from Satellite Altimetry and Ship Depth Soundings.* Science, 277(5334), 1956–1962. DOI: [10.1126/science.277.5334.1956](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.277.5334.1956).
   * Landmark paper establishing the geophysical inversion of intermediate-wavelength satellite radar altimetry marine gravity anomalies into seafloor topography.

10. **Mayer, L., Jakobsson, M., Allen, G., Dorschel, B., Falconer, R., Ferrini, V., Lamarche, G., Snaith, H., & Weatherall, P. (2018).** *The Nippon Foundation—GEBCO Seabed 2030 Project: The Quest to See the World's Oceans Completely Mapped by 2030.* Geosciences, 8(2), 63. DOI: [10.3390/geosciences8020063](https://doi.org/10.3390/geosciences8020063).
    * Defines the global Seabed 2030 compilation framework, depth-dependent resolution targets, and Type Identifier (TID) metadata architecture.

11. **Amante, C. J., Love, M., Carignan, K., Sutherland, M. G., MacFerrin, M., & Lim, E. (2023).** *Continuously Updated Digital Elevation Models (CUDEMs) to Support Coastal Inundation Modeling.* Remote Sensing, 15(6), 1702. DOI: [10.3390/rs15061702](https://doi.org/10.3390/rs15061702).
    * Documents NOAA NCEI's seamless coastal topo-bathymetric DEMs, VDatum datum harmonization, and `waffles`/`fetches` gridding pipeline.

12. **Farr, T. G., Rosen, P. A., Caro, E., Crippen, R., Duren, R., Hensley, S., ... & Alsdorf, D. (2007).** *The Shuttle Radar Topography Mission.* Reviews of Geophysics, 45(2), RG2004. DOI: [10.1029/2005RG000183](https://doi.org/10.1029/2005RG000183).
    * Definitive mission overview of STS-99 SRTM, detailing C-band single-pass interferometry, the 60-meter deployable mast, calibration, and global elevation product accuracy.

13. **Crippen, R., Buckley, S., Agram, P., Belz, E., Gurrola, E., Hensley, S., ... & Tung, W. (2016).** *NASADEM Global Elevation Model: Methods and Progress.* The International Archives of the Photogrammetry, Remote Sensing and Spatial Information Sciences, XLI-B4, 125–128. DOI: [10.5194/isprs-archives-XLI-B4-125-2016](https://doi.org/10.5194/isprs-archives-XLI-B4-125-2016).
    * Details the reprocessing of raw SRTM radar signal data with ICESat GLAS vertical control and multi-source void filling.

14. **Rizzoli, P., Martone, M., Gonzalez, C., Wecklich, C., Borla Tridon, D., Bräutigam, B., ... & Moreira, A. (2017).** *Generation and performance assessment of the global TanDEM-X digital elevation model.* ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing, 132, 119–139. DOI: [10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2017.08.008](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2017.08.008).
    * Documents the bistatic X-band InSAR TanDEM-X DEM underlying WorldDEM and Copernicus DEM (GLO-30/GLO-90).

15. **Hawker, L., Uhe, P., Paulo, L., Sosa, J., Savage, J., Sampson, C., & Neal, J. (2022).** *A 30 m global map of elevation with forests and buildings removed.* Environmental Research Letters, 17(2), 024016. DOI: [10.1088/1748-9326/ac4d4f](https://doi.org/10.1088/1748-9326/ac4d4f).
    * Primary paper introducing FABDEM, using machine learning on Copernicus GLO-30, GEDI, and ICESat-2 to create an open global 30 m bare-earth DTM.

16. **Howat, I. M., Porter, C., Smith, B. E., Noh, M.-J., & Morin, P. (2019).** *The Reference Elevation Model of Antarctica.* The Cryosphere, 13(2), 665–674. DOI: [10.5194/tc-13-665-2019](https://doi.org/10.5194/tc-13-665-2019).
    * Technical specification of REMA and the PGC SETSM photogrammetric pipeline for high-resolution polar DEM strips and mosaics.

17. **Markus, T., Neumann, T., Martino, A., Abdalati, W., Brunt, K., Csatho, B., ... & Zwally, H. J. (2017).** *The Ice, Cloud, and land Elevation Satellite-2 (ICESat-2): Science requirements, concept, and implementation.* Remote Sensing of Environment, 190, 260–273. DOI: [10.1016/j.rse.2016.12.029](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rse.2016.12.029).
    * Technical specification of the ATLAS photon-counting orbital laser altimeter, its 532 nm micropulse physics, and global surface profiling capabilities.

18. **Dubayah, R., Blair, J. B., Goetz, S., Fatoyinbo, L., Hansen, M., Healey, S., ... & Silva, C. (2020).** *The Global Ecosystem Dynamics Investigation: High-resolution laser ranging of the Earth's forests and topography.* Science of Remote Sensing, 1, 100002. DOI: [10.1016/j.srs.2020.100002](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.srs.2020.100002).
    * Documents the ISS-mounted 1064 nm full-waveform GEDI LiDAR mission providing global bare-earth canopy-penetrating control points.
