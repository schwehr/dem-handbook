# Chapter 24: Survey Standards, Guide Documents, and Key Public DEM Products

*The official specifications that govern professional elevation and hydrographic surveys, and a critical comparison of the world's major open DEM datasets.*

---

## 24.7 Curated Key References

The standards, statistical accuracy thresholds, and public datasets discussed in this chapter represent foundational technical accomplishments across federal agencies, international bodies, and scientific consortia. Below are the key authoritative references, standards documents, and peer-reviewed benchmark publications.

---

### Authoritative Standards and Guide Documents

1. **NOAA Office of Coast Survey (OCS). (2024).** *Hydrographic Surveys Specifications and Deliverables (HSSD).* National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration, Silver Spring, MD. Available online: [https://nauticalcharts.noaa.gov](https://nauticalcharts.noaa.gov).
   * The annual benchmark specification governing multibeam coverage, feature detection, Total Vertical Uncertainty (TVU), cross-line checks, designated soundings, and Bathymetric Attributed Grid (BAG) deliverables for US waters.

2. **International Hydrographic Organization (IHO). (2020).** *Standards for Hydrographic Surveys (Special Publication No. 44 - S-44, 6th Edition).* International Hydrographic Bureau, Monaco.
   * Universal international standard defining Exclusive Order, Special Order, Order 1a, Order 1b, and Order 2 survey tiers, along with depth-dependent $\text{TVU}_{\max}(d)$ and $\text{THU}_{\max}(d)$ formulations.

3. **Heidemann, H. K. (2018).** *Lidar Base Specification (ver. 1.3, February 2018).* U.S. Geological Survey Techniques and Methods, book 11, chap. B4, 105 p. DOI: [10.3133/tm11B4](https://doi.org/10.3133/tm11B4).
   * Foundational specification for the USGS 3D Elevation Program (3DEP), establishing point density, Quality Levels (QL0–QL3), pulse spacing, relative/absolute vertical accuracy, and cartographic hydro-flattening rules.

4. **American Society for Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing (ASPRS). (2024).** *ASPRS Positional Accuracy Standards for Digital Geospatial Data (Edition 2, Version 1.0).* Photogrammetric Engineering & Remote Sensing, 90(2), 75–104. DOI: [10.14358/PERS.23-00050R](https://doi.org/10.14358/PERS.23-00050R).
   * Definitive standard establishing Non-Vegetated Vertical Accuracy (NVA) via Gaussian $1.9600 \cdot \text{RMSE}_z$ and Vegetated Vertical Accuracy (VVA) via non-parametric 95th percentile analysis.

5. **Umbach, M. J. (1976).** *Hydrographic Manual (4th Edition).* National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration, National Ocean Survey, Rockville, MD.
   * Historic masterwork establishing standard operational procedures for analog single-beam hydrographic surveys, bar checks, vessel squat corrections, and electronic microwave positioning.

6. **Hawley, J. H. (1928).** *Hydrographic Manual.* U.S. Coast and Geodetic Survey, Special Publication No. 143, Washington, D.C.
   * Landmark manual codifying 19th- and early 20th-century lead-line sounding practices, sextant three-point fixes, and wire-drag obstruction sweep operations.

### Key Public Dataset and Scientific References

7. **Tozer, B., Sandwell, D. T., Smith, W. H. F., Olson, C., Beale, J. R., & Wessel, P. (2019).** *Global bathymetry and topography at 15 arcsec: SRTM15+.* Earth and Space Science, 6(10), 1847–1864. DOI: [10.1029/2019EA000658](https://doi.org/10.1029/2019EA000658).
   * Documents the construction of the global 15 arc-second bathymetric grid combining acoustic soundings with satellite marine gravity inversions, forming the core of GEBCO 2019–2024.

8. **Smith, W. H. F., & Sandwell, D. T. (1997).** *Bathymetric topography from satellite altimetry and ship depth soundings.* Science, 277(5334), 1956–1962. DOI: [10.1126/science.277.5334.1956](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.277.5334.1956).
   * Landmark paper establishing the geophysical inversion of intermediate-wavelength satellite radar altimetry marine gravity anomalies into seafloor topography.

9. **Farr, T. G., Rosen, P. A., Caro, E., Crippen, R., Duren, R., Hensley, S., ... & Alsdorf, D. (2007).** *The Shuttle Radar Topography Mission.* Reviews of Geophysics, 45(2), RG2004. DOI: [10.1029/2005RG000183](https://doi.org/10.1029/2005RG000183).
   * Definitive mission overview of STS-99 SRTM, detailing C-band single-pass interferometry, the 60-meter deployable mast, calibration, and global elevation product accuracy.

10. **Hawker, L., Uhe, P., Paulo, L., Sosa, J., Savage, J., Sampson, C., & Neal, J. (2022).** *A 30 m globally consistent candidate digital terrain model of the Earth's land surface to support flood risk analysis: FABDEM.* Environmental Research Letters, 17(2), 024035. DOI: [10.1088/1748-9326/ac4d4f](https://doi.org/10.1088/1748-9326/ac4d4f).
    * Primary paper introducing FABDEM, using machine learning on Copernicus GLO-30, GEDI, and ICESat-2 to create the first open global bare-earth DTM.

11. **Markus, T., Neumann, T., Martino, A., Abdalati, W., Brunt, K., Csatho, B., ... & Zwally, H. J. (2017).** *The Ice, Cloud, and land Elevation Satellite-2 (ICESat-2): Science requirements, concept, and implementation.* Remote Sensing of Environment, 190, 260–273. DOI: [10.1016/j.rse.2016.12.029](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rse.2016.12.029).
    * Technical specification of the ATLAS photon-counting orbital laser altimeter, its 532 nm micropulse physics, and global surface profiling capabilities.
