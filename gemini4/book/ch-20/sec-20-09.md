## 20.9 Curated Key References

The seminal publications, national agency monographs, and international engineering standards listed below define the official ontologies, semantic classifications, structural catenary mechanics, and 3D city standards in digital elevation modeling.

---

### Foundational and Seminal Literature

* **Anderson, J. R., Hardy, E. E., Roach, J. T., & Witmer, R. E.** (1976). *A Land Use and Land Cover Classification System for Use with Remote Sensor Data*. Geological Survey Professional Paper 964. Washington, D.C.: U.S. Government Printing Office.  
  *The landmark foundational classification system establishing the hierarchical Level I and Level II land-cover taxonomy used across national remote sensing programs.*

* **Gröger, G., & Plümer, L.** (2012). CityGML — Interoperable semantic 3D city models. *ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing*, 71, 12–33. DOI: [10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2012.04.004](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2012.04.004)  
  *Comprehensive architectural review of the CityGML standard, detailing semantic class hierarchies, boundary representations, and Levels of Detail (LoD 0–4).*

* **Kiessling, F., Nefzger, P., Nolasco, J. F., & Kaintzyk, U.** (2003). *Overhead Power Lines: Planning, Design, Construction*. Berlin, Heidelberg: Springer-Verlag. DOI: [10.1007/978-3-642-18788-9](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-18788-9)  
  *The authoritative engineering textbook deriving the hyperbolic catenary curve, parabolic approximations, and the governing thermo-elastic state change equation for electrical conductors.*

* **Zhang, J.** (2014). Multi-source remote sensing data fusion: Status and trends. *International Journal of Image and Data Fusion*, 5(3), 192–215. DOI: [10.1080/19479832.2014.894135](https://doi.org/10.1080/19479832.2014.894135)  
  *Review of modern methodologies for merging terrestrial DTMs, photogrammetric DSMs, and structural vector footprints into coherent 3D landscape models.*

---

### Industry Standards and Engineering Specifications

* **American Society for Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing (ASPRS).** (2019). *LAS Specification, Version 1.4 – R15*. Bethesda, MD: ASPRS.  
  *Defines the authoritative 8-bit point classification schema (Classes 0–255), including specialized classes for electrical conductors (14), towers (15), bridge decks (17), and temporal exclusions (22).*

* **buildingSMART International.** (2018). *Industry Foundation Classes (IFC) for data sharing in the construction and facility management industries — Part 1: Data schema*. ISO 16739-1:2018. Geneva: International Organization for Standardization.  
  *The international open standard governing Building Information Modeling (BIM) parametric solid geometry and architectural semantics.*

* **Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE).** (2012). *IEEE Standard for Calculating the Current-Temperature Relationship of Bare Overhead Conductors*. IEEE Std 738-2012. New York: IEEE. DOI: [10.1109/IEEESTD.2013.6692858](https://doi.org/10.1109/IEEESTD.2013.6692858)  
  *The standard mathematical engineering specification relating electrical current (Joule heating), ambient weather, wind cooling, and thermal conductor elongation.*

* **Open Geospatial Consortium (OGC).** (2021). *OGC City Geography Markup Language (CityGML) Part 1: Conceptual Model Standard, Version 3.0*. OGC Document 20-010. Wayland, MA: Open Geospatial Consortium.  
  *The modernized international standard establishing multi-scale spatial modeling of urban topography, multi-layer infrastructure, and building interiors.*
