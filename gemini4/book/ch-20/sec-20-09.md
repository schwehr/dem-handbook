## 20.9 Curated Key References

The peer-reviewed articles, national agency monographs, and international engineering specifications below establish the formal surface ontologies, point-cloud classification schemas, multi-scale 3D urban/BIM standards, and thermo-elastic conductor mechanics covered in Chapter 20.

---

### Foundational Literature and Research Articles

1. **Amante, C. J., Love, M., Carignan, K., Sutherland, M. G., MacFerrin, M., & Lim, E.** (2023). Continuously Updated Digital Elevation Models (CUDEMs) to support coastal inundation modeling. *Remote Sensing*, 15(6), 1702. https://doi.org/10.3390/rs15061702  
   *Defines NOAA's operational methodology for fusing subaerial bare-earth DTMs, coastal bathymetric LiDAR, and hydrographic soundings—while preserving solid coastal jetties and removing piers/bridges—into seamless topo-bathymetric DEMs.*

2. **Anderson, J. R., Hardy, E. E., Roach, J. T., & Witmer, R. E.** (1976). *A Land Use and Land Cover Classification System for Use with Remote Sensor Data* (USGS Professional Paper 964). Washington, DC: U.S. Government Printing Office. https://doi.org/10.3133/pp964  
   *Seminal taxonomy separating physical remote-sensing land cover from human land use across hierarchical Level I and Level II classes.*

3. **Biljecki, F., Ledoux, H., & Stoter, J.** (2016). An improved LOD specification for 3D building models. *Computers, Environment and Urban Systems*, 59, 25–37. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compenvurbsys.2016.04.005  
   *Resolves geometric and elevation ambiguities in CityGML building representations through a rigorous 16-stage Level of Detail taxonomy (`LOD0.0`–`LOD3.3`).*

4. **Gröger, G., & Plümer, L.** (2012). CityGML — Interoperable semantic 3D city models. *ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing*, 71, 12–33. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2012.04.004  
   *Foundational architectural paper establishing semantic-geometric coherence, Terrain Intersection Curves (`TIC`), and multi-scale urban ontologies in CityGML.*

5. **Kiessling, F., Nefzger, P., Nolasco, J. F., & Kaintzyk, U.** (2003). *Overhead Power Lines: Planning, Design, Construction*. Berlin, Heidelberg: Springer-Verlag. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-97879-1  
   *Authoritative engineering treatise deriving exact hyperbolic catenary statics, inclined mountain span geometry, wind/ice vector loading, and the cubic thermo-elastic sag-tension state-change equation.*

6. **Kutzner, T., Chaturvedi, K., & Kolbe, T. H.** (2020). CityGML 3.0: New functions open up new applications. *PFG – Journal of Photogrammetry, Remote Sensing and Geoinformation Science*, 88(1), 43–61. https://doi.org/10.1007/s41064-020-00095-z  
   *Details the CityGML 3.0 conceptual architecture unifying exterior terrain with physical/occupied spaces (`Space`) and subterranean/indoor hollow spaces (`HollowSpace`).*

7. **Matikainen, L., Lehtomäki, M., Ahokas, E., Hyyppä, J., Karjalainen, M., Jaakkola, A., Kukko, A., & Heinonen, T.** (2016). Remote sensing methods for power line corridor surveys. *ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing*, 119, 10–31. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2016.04.011  
   *Comprehensive synthesis of airborne/UAS LiDAR, synthetic aperture radar, and stereo photogrammetry capabilities and failure modes for conductor catenary extraction and corridor vegetation auditing.*

8. **Mitchell, W. B., Guptill, S. C., Anderson, K. E., Fegeas, R. G., & Hallam, C. A.** (1977). *GIRAS: A Geographic Information Retrieval and Analysis System for Handling Land Use and Land Cover Data* (USGS Professional Paper 1059). Washington, DC: U.S. Government Printing Office. https://doi.org/10.3133/pp1059  
   *Documents the pioneering 1973–1977 USGS topological vector GIS built to encode and validate national land-cover semantics.*

9. **Noardo, F., Harrie, L., Arroyo Ohori, K., Biljecki, F., Ellul, C., Krijnen, T., Eriksson, H., Guler, D., Hintz, D., Jadidi, M. A., Pla, M., Sanchez, S., Soini, V.-P., Stouffs, R., Tekavec, J., & Stoter, J.** (2020). Tools for BIM-GIS integration (IFC georeferencing and conversions): Results from the GeoBIM Benchmark 2019. *ISPRS International Journal of Geo-Information*, 9(9), 502. https://doi.org/10.3390/ijgi9090502  
   *Empirical multi-software benchmark quantifying georeferencing, scale-factor, and CSG-to-mesh conversion pitfalls between ISO 16739 IFC BIM models and 3D GIS.*

10. **Yao, Z., Nagel, C., Kunde, F., Hudra, G., Willkomm, P., Donaubauer, A., Adolphi, T., & Kolbe, T. H.** (2018). 3DCityDB – a 3D geodatabase solution for the management, analysis, and visualization of semantic 3D city models based on CityGML. *Open Geospatial Data, Software and Standards*, 3, 5. https://doi.org/10.1186/s40965-018-0046-7  
    *Describes the open-source PostgreSQL/PostGIS relational schema and OGC 3D Tiles export pipeline for semantic urban terrain and building databases.*

11. **Zhang, J.** (2010). Multi-source remote sensing data fusion: status and trends. *International Journal of Image and Data Fusion*, 1(1), 5–24. https://doi.org/10.1080/19479830903561035  
    *Reviews pixel-, feature-, and decision-level fusion frameworks for combining LiDAR DTMs, optical/SAR DSMs, and vector building/infrastructure footprints.*

### International Standards and Specifications

12. **American Society for Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing (ASPRS).** (2019). *LAS Specification, Version 1.4 – R15*. Bethesda, MD: ASPRS.  
    *Establishes the authoritative 8-bit point classification standard (`Classes 0–255`), including Ground (`2`), Road (`11`), Wire-Guard (`13`), Wire-Conductor (`14`), Transmission Tower (`15`), Wire-Structure Connector (`16`), Bridge Deck (`17`), Overhead Structure (`19`), and Topo-Bathy Domain classes (`40–45`).*

13. **buildingSMART International & International Organization for Standardization (ISO).** (2018; updated 2024). *Industry Foundation Classes (IFC) for data sharing in the construction and facility management industries — Part 1: Data schema* (ISO 16739-1:2018; IFC 4.3 infrastructure extension ISO 16739-1:2024). Geneva: ISO.  
    *Defines parametric BIM solid representations alongside `IfcProjectedCRS` and `IfcMapConversion` geodetic transformation entities.*

14. **Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE).** (2012). *IEEE Standard for Calculating the Current-Temperature of Bare Overhead Conductors* (IEEE Std 738-2012). New York: IEEE. https://doi.org/10.1109/IEEESTD.2013.6692858  
    *Specifies the governing convective, radiative, solar, and Joule ($I^2 R$) heat-balance equations linking electrical dispatch and weather to conductor core temperature.*

15. **Open Geospatial Consortium (OGC).** (2021). *OGC City Geography Markup Language (CityGML) Part 1: Conceptual Model Standard, Version 3.0* (OGC Document 20-010). Wayland, MA: Open Geospatial Consortium.  
    *Formalizes interoperable 3D semantic modeling of terrain, buildings, bridges, tunnels, vegetation, and indoor spaces.*
