## 17.9 Curated Key References

The seminal publications, government monographs, and international standards listed below establish the theoretical foundations of spatial topology, robust geometric predicates, drainage-enforced surface interpolation, and 3D vector representations in digital elevation modeling.

---

### Foundational and Seminal Literature

* **Corbett, J. P.** (1979). *Topological Principles in Cartography*. Technical Paper 48. Washington, D.C.: U.S. Department of Commerce, Bureau of the Census.  
  *The landmark foundational monograph establishing the mathematical planar graph model, node-arc-polygon duality, and algebraic boundary complexes for computer cartography.*

* **Hutchinson, M. F.** (1989). A new procedure for gridding elevation and stream line data with automatic removal of spurious pits. *Journal of Hydrology*, 106(3–4), 211–232. DOI: [10.1016/0022-1694(89)90073-5](https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-1694(89)90073-5)  
  *The seminal paper deriving the ANUDEM algorithm, introducing finite-difference multigrid relaxation with active stream monotonicity penalties and automated spurious sink clearance.*

* **Peucker, T. K., & Chrisman, N.** (1975). Cartographic data structures. *The American Cartographer*, 2(1), 55–69. DOI: [10.1559/152304075784447289](https://doi.org/10.1559/152304075784447289)  
  *Early foundational work detailing the transition from unorganized spaghetti vector drafting lines to structured topological networks.*

* **Shewchuk, J. R.** (1997). Adaptive precision floating-point arithmetic and fast robust geometric predicates. *Discrete & Computational Geometry*, 18(3), 305–363. DOI: [10.1007/PL00009321](https://doi.org/10.1007/PL00009321)  
  *The milestone paper deriving exact 4-stage adaptive-precision floating-point arithmetic filters for the Orient2D, Orient3D, and InCircle determinants, eliminating numerical failure in Delaunay triangulation.*

---

### Government Standards and Industry Specifications

* **Gröger, G., Kolbe, T. H., Nagel, C., & Häfele, K. H.** (2012). *OGC City Geography Markup Language (CityGML) Encoding Standard, Version 2.0*. OGC Document 12-019. Wayland, MA: Open Geospatial Consortium.  
  *The international open standard defining multi-scale semantic 3D vector modeling of urban topography, buildings, bridges, and tunnels across Levels of Detail (LoD 0–4).*

* **International Organization for Standardization (ISO) & Open Geospatial Consortium (OGC).** (2011). *Geographic Information — Simple Feature Access — Part 1: Common Architecture*. ISO 19125-1:2004 / OGC Document 06-103r4. Geneva / Wayland, MA.  
  *The definitive open standard specifying vector geometry primitives (Point, LineString, Polygon, PolyhedralSurfaceZ, TINZ) and Well-Known Text (WKT) serializations.*

* **U.S. Geological Survey (USGS).** (2020). *USGS 3DEP Elevation Program: Hydro-Flattening Specifications for Airborne LiDAR Data*. National Geospatial Program Standards and Guidelines. Reston, VA: U.S. Geological Survey.  
  *Prescribes mandatory hydro-flattening rules for inland water bodies, estuaries, and tidal waters in national bare-earth digital elevation models.*
