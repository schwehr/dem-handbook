## 17.9 Curated Key References

The seminal publications, government monographs, and international standards listed below establish the theoretical foundations of spatial topology, robust geometric predicates, drainage-enforced surface interpolation, and 3D vector representations in digital elevation modeling.

---

### Foundational and Seminal Literature

* **Biljecki, F., Ledoux, H., & Stoter, J.** (2016). An improved LOD specification for 3D building models. *Computers, Environment and Urban Systems*, 59, 25–37. DOI: [10.1016/j.compenvurbsys.2016.04.005](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compenvurbsys.2016.04.005)  
  *Establishes a rigorous 16-level geometric and semantic taxonomy for 3D vector building and infrastructure models.*

* **Chew, L. P.** (1989). Constrained Delaunay triangulations. *Algorithmica*, 4(1), 97–108. DOI: [10.1007/BF01553881](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01553881)  
  *Derives the optimal $\mathcal{O}(N \log N)$ algorithm and visibility-constrained empty-circumcircle theorem for enforcing hard vector breaklines in Delaunay triangulations.*

* **Corbett, J. P.** (1979). *Topological Principles in Cartography*. Technical Paper 48. Washington, D.C.: U.S. Department of Commerce, Bureau of the Census.  
  *The landmark foundational monograph establishing the mathematical planar graph model, node-arc-polygon duality, and algebraic boundary complexes for computer cartography.*

* **Egenhofer, M. J., & Franzosa, R. D.** (1991). Point-set topological spatial relations. *International Journal of Geographical Information Systems*, 5(2), 161–174. DOI: [10.1080/02693799108927841](https://doi.org/10.1080/02693799108927841)  
  *Introduces the intersection matrix formalism for interior, boundary, and exterior point sets that underpins OGC topological predicates.*

* **Hobby, J. D.** (1999). Practical segment intersection with finite precision output. *Computational Geometry: Theory and Applications*, 13(4), 199–214. DOI: [10.1016/S0925-7721(99)00021-8](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0925-7721(99)00021-8)  
  *Formulates the hot-pixel snap-rounding algorithm used in JTS and GEOS to guarantee topologically valid, self-intersection-free vector overlays on fixed-precision grids.*

* **Hutchinson, M. F.** (1989). A new procedure for gridding elevation and stream line data with automatic removal of spurious pits. *Journal of Hydrology*, 106(3–4), 211–232. DOI: [10.1016/0022-1694(89)90073-5](https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-1694(89)90073-5)  
  *The seminal paper deriving the ANUDEM algorithm, introducing finite-difference multigrid relaxation with active stream monotonicity penalties and automated spurious sink clearance.*

* **Ledoux, H., Arroyo Ohori, K., Kumar, K., Dukai, B., Labetski, A., & Vitalis, S.** (2019). CityJSON: a compact and easy-to-use encoding of the CityGML data model. *Open Geospatial Data, Software and Standards*, 4, 4. DOI: [10.1186/s40965-019-0064-0](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40965-019-0064-0)  
  *Defines the compact JSON-based 3D vector city modeling specification adopted by OGC.*

* **Lindsay, J. B., & Dhun, K.** (2015). Modelling surface drainage patterns in altered landscapes using LiDAR. *International Journal of Geographical Information Science*, 29(3), 397–411. DOI: [10.1080/13658816.2014.975715](https://doi.org/10.1080/13658816.2014.975715)  
  *Demonstrates least-cost topological breaching of road embankments and culverts in high-resolution LiDAR DEMs as a superior alternative to pit filling.*

* **Peucker, T. K., & Chrisman, N.** (1975). Cartographic data structures. *The American Cartographer*, 2(1), 55–69. DOI: [10.1559/152304075784447289](https://doi.org/10.1559/152304075784447289)  
  *Early foundational work detailing the transition from unorganized spaghetti vector drafting lines to structured topological networks and TINs.*

* **Shewchuk, J. R.** (1997). Adaptive precision floating-point arithmetic and fast robust geometric predicates. *Discrete & Computational Geometry*, 18(3), 305–363. DOI: [10.1007/PL00009321](https://doi.org/10.1007/PL00009321)  
  *The milestone paper deriving exact 4-stage adaptive-precision floating-point arithmetic filters for the Orient2D, Orient3D, and InCircle determinants, eliminating numerical failure in Delaunay triangulation.*

### Government Standards and Industry Specifications

* **Butler, H., Daly, M., Doyle, A., Gillies, S., Hagen, S., & Schaub, T.** (2016). *The GeoJSON Format*. IETF Request for Comments (RFC) 7946. DOI: [10.17487/RFC7946](https://doi.org/10.17487/RFC7946)  
  *Formalizes the WGS84 coordinate requirement, antimeridian cutting guidelines, and the Counter-Clockwise (CCW) exterior / Clockwise (CW) interior right-hand winding rule.*

* **Gröger, G., Kolbe, T. H., Nagel, C., & Häfele, K. H.** (2012). *OGC City Geography Markup Language (CityGML) Encoding Standard, Version 2.0*. OGC Document 12-019. Wayland, MA: Open Geospatial Consortium.  
  *The international open standard defining multi-scale semantic 3D vector modeling of urban topography, buildings, bridges, and tunnels across Levels of Detail (LoD 0–4).*

* **Heidemann, H. K.** (2014). *Lidar Base Specification* (ver. 1.2, 2014; rev. 2018/2024). U.S. Geological Survey Techniques and Methods, Book 11, Chap. B4 (TM 11-B4). DOI: [10.3133/tm11B4](https://doi.org/10.3133/tm11B4)  
  *Prescribes mandatory 3D breakline compilation and hydro-flattening rules for inland ponds/lakes ($\ge 2\text{ acres}$), streams/rivers ($\ge 30\text{ m}$ wide), and bridge decks in USGS 3DEP bare-earth DEMs.*

* **International Organization for Standardization (ISO) & Open Geospatial Consortium (OGC).** (2011). *Geographic Information — Simple Feature Access — Part 1: Common Architecture*. ISO 19125-1:2004 / OGC Document 06-103r4. Geneva / Wayland, MA.  
  *The definitive open standard specifying vector geometry primitives (Point, LineString, Polygon, PolyhedralSurfaceZ, TINZ) and Well-Known Text (WKT) serializations.*
