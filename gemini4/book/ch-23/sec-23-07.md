# Chapter 23: Local vs. Integrated Surveys and Building Composite DEM Products

*Reconciling isolated local surveys with global frameworks, and how to order, crop, blend, and override multi-source elevation datasets without introducing seams or destroying critical shoals.*

---

## 23.7 Curated Key References

The development of modern composite elevation modeling spans more than a century of geodetic, hydrographic, and computational evolution—from early 20th-century manual contour synthesis to 21st-century gradient-domain Poisson blending, cloud-native automated fetching, and dynamic uncertainty inflation. The works below constitute the primary theoretical, computational, and institutional foundations for merging disparate topographic and bathymetric surfaces.

---

### Primary Scientific and Methodological References

1. **Amante, C. J., Love, M., Carignan, K., Sutherland, M. G., MacFerrin, M., & Lim, E. (2023).** *Continuously Updated Digital Elevation Model (CUDEM) High-Resolution Bathymetric-Topographic Digital Elevation Models.* NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information (NCEI), Technical Report NESDIS 158. DOI: [10.25923/584x-bn24](https://doi.org/10.25923/584x-bn24).
   * Seminal operational framework describing NOAA's automated data ingestion (`fetches`), multi-criteria source prioritization, spline-in-tension blending (`waffles`), and seamless coastal land-sea integration across variable datums.

2. **Pérez, P., Gangnet, M., & Blake, A. (2003).** *Poisson image editing.* ACM Transactions on Graphics (TOG), 22(3), 313–318. DOI: [10.1145/882262.882269](https://doi.org/10.1145/882262.882269).
   * Foundational paper establishing gradient-domain Poisson blending ($\nabla^2 f = \nabla \cdot \mathbf{v}$ with Dirichlet boundary constraints), adapted universally in modern geospatial terrain synthesis to eliminate edge steps while preserving high-frequency terrain slopes.

3. **Burt, P. J., & Adelson, E. H. (1983).** *A multiresolution spline with application to image mosaics.* ACM Transactions on Graphics (TOG), 2(4), 217–236. DOI: [10.1145/357318.357323](https://doi.org/10.1145/357318.357323).
   * Original mathematical derivation of Laplacian and Gaussian pyramid multi-band decomposition for seamless boundary blending, preventing ghosting and boundary scarps across disparate resolution sources.

4. **Smith, W. H. F., & Wessel, P. (1990).** *Gridding with continuous curvature splines in tension.* Geophysics, 55(3), 293–305. DOI: [10.1190/1.1442837](https://doi.org/10.1190/1.1442837).
   * Mathematical formulation of the biharmonic spline-in-tension equation $(1 - T)\nabla^4 z - T \nabla^2 z = 0$ implemented in GMT (`surface`), resolving the trade-off between minimum curvature oscillation and piecewise-linear facets when interpolating bathymetric gaps.

5. **Wessel, P., Luis, J. F., Uieda, L., Scharroo, R., Wobbe, F., Smith, W. H. F., & Tian, D. (2019).** *The Generic Mapping Tools Version 6.* Geochemistry, Geophysics, Geosystems, 20(11), 5556–5564. DOI: [10.1029/2019GC008515](https://doi.org/10.1029/2019GC008515).
   * Comprehensive architecture of GMT 6, detailing grid mathematical operators, 2D continuous tension splines, and spatial grid blending (`grdblend`) using cosine-tapered distance weighting.

6. **Caress, D. W., & Chayes, D. N. (1996).** *Improved processing of Hydrosweep DS multibeam data on the R/V Maurice Ewing.* Marine Geophysical Researches, 18(6), 631–650. DOI: [10.1007/BF00313878](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00313878).
   * Primary citation for MB-System, detailing open-source multibeam swath bathymetry filtering, ray-tracing, outer-beam incidence cropping, and mosaic compilation (`mbgrid`).

7. **Hell, B., Broman, B., Jakobsson, M., & Schenke, H. W. (2012).** *A comparison of methods to interpolate bathymetric data.* Marine Geodesy, 35(4), 375–395. DOI: [10.1080/01490419.2012.699561](https://doi.org/10.1080/01490419.2012.699561).
   * Rigorous benchmarking of gridding and interpolation algorithms (kriging, inverse distance weighting, tension splines, and triangulated irregular networks) across heterogeneous marine depth soundings.

8. **International Hydrographic Organization (IHO). (2020).** *IHO Standards for Hydrographic Surveys (Special Publication No. 44, 6th Edition).* International Hydrographic Bureau, Monaco.
   * Authoritative international standard defining Total Vertical Uncertainty (TVU), Total Horizontal Uncertainty (THU), and strict feature-detection requirements that prohibit automated smoothing across navigation shoals.

9. **Open Navigation Surface Working Group (ONSWG). (2010).** *Bathymetric Attributed Grid (BAG) Format Specification, Version 1.5.2.* Open Navigation Surface Project.
   * Technical specification for the HDF5-based hydrographic standard incorporating elevation, uncertainty, tracking lists, and designated shoal soundings into an interoperable spatial data structure.

10. **Weatherall, P., Marks, K. M., Jakobsson, M., Schmitt, T., Tani, S., Arndt, J. E., ... & Ferrini, V. (2015).** *A new digital bathymetric model of the world's oceans.* Earth and Space Science, 2(8), 331–345. DOI: [10.1002/2015EA000107](https://doi.org/10.1002/2015EA000107).
    * Documents the global compilation of GEBCO, explaining source supercession hierarchies, satellite-derived gravity bathymetry integration, and Type Identifier (TID) provenance tracking.
