## 23.7 Curated Key References

The peer-reviewed publications, mathematical derivations, global compilation papers, and hydrographic standards below establish the theoretical and operational foundations of multi-source composite DEM synthesis in Chapter 23.

---

### Gradient-Domain Blending, Multi-Resolution Splines, and Swath Mosaicking

1. **Burt, P. J., & Adelson, E. H.** (1983). A multiresolution spline with application to image mosaics. *ACM Transactions on Graphics (TOG)*, 2(4), 217–236. https://doi.org/10.1145/245.247  
   *Seminal formulation of Gaussian and Laplacian pyramid multi-band blending, allowing coarse vertical biases to blend across wide buffers while preserving crisp high-frequency terrain edges.*

2. **Caress, D. W., & Chayes, D. N.** (1996). Improved processing of Hydrosweep DS multibeam data on the R/V *Maurice Ewing*. *Marine Geophysical Researches*, 18(6), 631–650. https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00313878  
   *Foundational `MB-System` reference detailing multibeam outer-beam footprint filtering, beam-weighted spatial binning, and tensioned-spline background relaxation (`mbgrid` / `mbmosaic`).*

3. **Hell, B., & Jakobsson, M.** (2011). Gridding heterogeneous bathymetric data sets with stacked continuous curvature splines in tension. *Marine Geophysical Research*, 32(4), 493–501. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11001-011-9141-1  
   *Demonstrates hierarchical multi-resolution spline-in-tension stacking for combining dense multibeam corridors with sparse single-beam and historical lead-line soundings without aliasing.*

4. **Pérez, P., Gangnet, M., & Blake, A.** (2003). Poisson image editing. *ACM Transactions on Graphics (TOG)*, 22(3), 313–318. https://doi.org/10.1145/882262.882269  
   *Derives variational gradient-domain Poisson editing ($\nabla^2 z = \nabla\cdot\mathbf{g}$ with Dirichlet boundary conditions) and mixed-gradient guidance fields for seamless local-to-regional surface insertion.*

5. **Smith, W. H. F., & Wessel, P.** (1990). Gridding with continuous curvature splines in tension. *Geophysics*, 55(3), 293–305. https://doi.org/10.1190/1.1442837  
   *Establishes the continuous-curvature spline-in-tension PDE $(1-T)L_c^2\nabla^4 z - T\nabla^2 z = 0$ (`GMT surface`), suppressing minimum-curvature overshoot pits adjacent to steep continental slopes and seamounts.*

6. **Wessel, P., Luis, J. F., Uieda, L., Scharroo, R., Wobbe, F., Smith, W. H. F., & Tian, D.** (2019). The Generic Mapping Tools version 6. *Geochemistry, Geophysics, Geosystems*, 20(11), 5556–5564. https://doi.org/10.1029/2019GC008515  
   *Documents the modern `GMT 6` architecture, including cosine-tapered multi-grid blending (`grdblend`) and reverse-Polish raster algebra (`grdmath`).*

### National Topo-Bathymetric Frameworks, Global Compilations, and Hydrographic Standards

7. **Amante, C. J., Love, M., Carignan, K., Sutherland, M. G., MacFerrin, M., & Lim, E.** (2023). Continuously Updated Digital Elevation Models (CUDEMs) to support coastal inundation modeling. *Remote Sensing*, 15(6), 1702. https://doi.org/10.3390/rs15061702  
   *Authoritative reference for NOAA's `CUDEM` (`fetches` & `waffles`) pipeline, detailing automated multi-agency harvesting, `VDatum` alignment, supercession stacking, and uncertainty/provenance masks.*

8. **Danielson, J. J., Poppenga, S. K., Brock, J. C., Evans, G. A., Tyler, D. J., Gesch, D. B., Thatcher, C. A., & Barras, J. A.** (2016). Topobathymetric elevation model development using a new methodology: Coastal National Elevation Database. *Journal of Coastal Research*, Special Issue 76, 75–89. https://doi.org/10.2112/SI76-008  
   *Details the USGS `CoNED` multi-source topo-bathymetric validation, datum transformation, and priority mosaicking methodology across dynamic coastal barriers and estuaries.*

9. **Gesch, D., Oimoen, M., Greenlee, S., Nelson, C., Steuck, M., & Tyler, D.** (2002). The National Elevation Dataset. *Photogrammetric Engineering & Remote Sensing*, 68(1), 5–11.  
   *Documents the transition of U.S. terrestrial mapping from isolated quadrangle DEMs with edge seams to a datum-harmonized, priority-ordered seamless national framework.*

10. **International Hydrographic Organization (IHO).** (2020; rev. Ed. 6.1.0, 2022). *IHO Standards for Hydrographic Surveys* (Special Publication No. 44, S-44, 6th ed.). Monaco: IHO.  
    *Specifies `TVU`/`THU` survey orders, bathymetric coverage rules, and feature-detection mandates governing shoal-preservation overrides in composite hydrographic surfaces.*

11. **Mayer, L., Jakobsson, M., Allen, G., Dorschel, B., Falconer, R., Ferrini, V., Lamarche, G., Snaith, H., & Weatherall, P.** (2018). The Nippon Foundation—GEBCO Seabed 2030 Project: The quest to see the world's oceans completely mapped by 2030. *Geosciences*, 8(2), 63. https://doi.org/10.3390/geosciences8020063  
    *Outlines the global multi-regional compilation architecture, depth-stratified resolution targets, and crowd-sourced/multibeam integration strategy of `Seabed 2030`.*

12. **Open Navigation Surface Working Group (ONSWG).** (2010). *Bathymetric Attributed Grid (BAG) Format Specification, Version 1.5.2*. Open Navigation Surface Project.  
    *Defines the HDF5 dual-band (`elevation` + `uncertainty`) hydrographic grid structure and immutable `Tracking List` for designated shoal overrides.*

13. **Tozer, B., Sandwell, D. T., Smith, W. H. F., Olson, C., Beale, J. R., & Wessel, P.** (2019). Global bathymetry and topography at 15 arc sec: SRTM15+. *Earth and Space Science*, 6(10), 1847–1864. https://doi.org/10.1029/2019EA000658  
    *Describes Remove-Compute-Restore synthesis of over 33 million multibeam/single-beam ship soundings onto satellite radar-altimetry gravity bathymetry across the global ocean.*

14. **Weatherall, P., Marks, K. M., Jakobsson, M., Schmitt, T., Tani, S., Arndt, J. E., Rovere, M., Chayes, D., Ferrini, V., & Wigley, R.** (2015). A new digital bathymetric model of the world's oceans. *Earth and Space Science*, 2(8), 331–345. https://doi.org/10.1002/2015EA000107  
    *Establishes the `GEBCO` global compilation workflow and per-pixel Type Identifier (`TID`) provenance encoding.*
