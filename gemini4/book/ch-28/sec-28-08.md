# Chapter 28: Acoustic Backscatter, LiDAR Intensity, and Substrate/Benthic Co-Products

*Why elevation and depth sensors never measure geometry alone—and how co-registered return amplitude disambiguates terrain surfaces and hazards.*

## 28.8 Curated Key References

The integration of 3D surface geometry with co-registered return amplitude represents an interdisciplinary synthesis of underwater acoustics, optical radiometry, and geomorphology. Below are the foundational peer-reviewed scientific papers, calibration manuals, and standards documents governing backscatter and intensity processing.

---

### Marine Acoustic Backscatter and Seafloor Characterization

1. **Lamarche, G., & Lurton, X. (2018).** *Recommendations for improved and coherent acquisition and processing of backscatter data from seafloor-mapping sonars.* Marine Geophysical Research, 39(1–2), 5–22. DOI: [10.1007/s11001-017-9315-6](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11001-017-9315-6).
   * Seminal synthesis by the GeoHab Backscatter Working Group establishing standardized protocols for multibeam sonar calibration, transmission loss removal, and slope-corrected ensonified area calculations.

2. **Lurton, X., & Lamarche, G. (Eds.). (2015).** *Backscatter measurements by seafloor-mapping sonars: Guidelines and recommendations.* A collective report by members of the GeoHab Backscatter Working Group, 150 p.
   * The exhaustive international handbook detailing the physical sonar equations, transducer beam patterns, TVG handling, and Angular Range Analysis (ARA) for marine habitat mapping.

3. **Jackson, D. R., Winebrenner, D. P., & Ishimaru, A. (1986).** *Application of the composite roughness model to high-frequency bottom backscattering.* The Journal of the Acoustical Society of America, 79(5), 1410–1422. DOI: [10.1121/1.393669](https://doi.org/10.1121/1.393669).
   * Foundational acoustic physics paper deriving the composite roughness and volume scattering model (the Jackson model) used worldwide to invert backscatter curves into physical sediment grain size.

4. **Fonseca, L., & Mayer, L. (2007).** *Remote estimation of surficial seafloor properties through the application Angular Range Analysis to multibeam sonar data.* Marine Geophysical Researches, 28(2), 119–126. DOI: [10.1007/s11001-007-9019-4](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11001-007-9019-4).
   * Derives the Angular Range Analysis (ARA) inversion engine implemented in QPS FMGT (Geocoder), demonstrating objective sediment classification using true DEM grazing angles.

5. **Fonseca, L., Brown, C., Calder, B., Mayer, L., & Rzhanov, Y. (2009).** *Angular range analysis of acoustic themes from Stanton Banks Ireland: A link between visual interpretation and multibeam echosounder angular signatures.* Applied Acoustics, 70(10), 1298–1304. DOI: [10.1016/j.apacoust.2008.09.008](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apacoust.2008.09.008).
   * Validates Angular Range Analysis (ARA) acoustic themes against ground-truth benthic imagery and geological samples over complex glaciated shelf terrain.

6. **Hughes Clarke, J. E., Mayer, L. A., & Wells, D. E. (1996).** *Shallow-water imaging multibeam sonars: A new tool for investigating seafloor processes in the coastal zone and on the continental shelf.* Marine Geophysical Researches, 18(6), 607–629. DOI: [10.1007/BF00313877](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00313877).
   * Foundational paper demonstrating how co-registered high-resolution multibeam bathymetry and acoustic backscatter resolve continental-shelf bedforms and sediment transport processes.

7. **Caress, D. W., & Chayes, D. N. (1996).** *Improved processing of Hydrosweep DS multibeam data on the R/V Maurice Ewing.* Marine Geophysical Researches, 18(6), 631–650. DOI: [10.1007/BF00313878](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00313878).
   * Primary citation for MB-System, introducing automated swath bathymetry cleaning paired with open-source backscatter angular normalization (`mbbackangle`) and mosaicking (`mbmosaic`).

8. **Lurton, X. (2010).** *An Introduction to Underwater Acoustics: Principles and Applications (2nd ed.).* Springer-Praxis, Chichester, UK. DOI: [10.1007/978-3-642-13835-5](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-642-13835-5).
   * Comprehensive textbook deriving the active sonar equations, spherical spreading and absorption losses, Rayleigh roughness criteria, and seafloor target strength formulations.

### Terrestrial LiDAR and SAR Radiometric Calibration

9. **Kashani, A. G., Olsen, M. J., Parrish, C. E., & Wilson, N. (2015).** *A review of LIDAR radiometric processing: From ad hoc intensity correction to rigorous calibration.* Sensors, 15(11), 28099–28128. DOI: [10.3390/s151128099](https://doi.org/10.3390/s151128099).
   * Comprehensive review detailing the physics of laser return amplitude, range-squared loss, incidence angle normalization using DEM normals, and empirical calibration using spectral targets.

10. **Höfle, B., & Pfeifer, N. (2007).** *Correction of laser scanning intensity data: Data and model-driven approaches.* ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing, 62(6), 415–433. DOI: [10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2007.05.008](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2007.05.008).
    * Derives the laser radar range equation applied to airborne laser scanning, proving that combining true 3D surface slope angles with distance normalization isolates true physical target reflectance.

11. **Wagner, W., Ullrich, A., Ducic, V., Melzer, T., & Studnicka, N. (2006).** *Gaussian decomposition and calibration of a novel small-footprint full-waveform digitising airborne laser scanner.* ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing, 60(2), 100–112. DOI: [10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2005.12.001](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2005.12.001).
    * Establishes the physical formulation for full-waveform LiDAR pulse decomposition, backscatter cross-section calibration, and pulse-width echo analysis across canopy and ground returns.

12. **Small, D. (2011).** *Flattening gamma: Radiometric terrain correction for SAR imagery.* IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing, 49(8), 3081–3093. DOI: [10.1109/TGRS.2011.2120616](https://doi.org/10.1109/TGRS.2011.2120616).
    * Seminal algorithm for SAR Radiometric Terrain Correction (RTC), integrating 3D DEM facets to normalize radar backscatter into terrain-flattened $\gamma_T^0$ free of foreshortening bias.

13. **American Society for Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing (ASPRS). (2019).** *LAS Specification 1.4 - R15.* ASPRS Standards Committee, Bethesda, MD.
    * Authoritative specification standardizing the 16-bit integer `Intensity` attribute and multi-wavelength point records across the global airborne LiDAR industry.
