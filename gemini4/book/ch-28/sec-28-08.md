# Chapter 28: Acoustic Backscatter, LiDAR Intensity, and Substrate/Benthic Co-Products

*Why elevation and depth sensors never measure geometry alone—and how co-registered return amplitude disambiguates terrain surfaces and hazards.*

## 28.8 Curated Key References

The integration of 3D surface geometry with co-registered return amplitude represents an interdisciplinary synthesis of underwater acoustics, optical radiometry, and geomorphology. Below are the foundational peer-reviewed scientific papers, calibration manuals, and standards documents governing backscatter and intensity processing.

---

### Marine Acoustic Backscatter and Seafloor Characterization

1. **Lamarche, G., & Lurton, X. (2018).** *Recommendations for improved and coherent acquisition and processing of backscatter data from seafloor-mapping sonars.* Marine Geophysical Research, 39(1–2), 5–22. DOI: [10.1007/s11001-017-9315-6](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11001-017-9315-6).
   * Seminal synthesis by the GeoHab Backscatter Working Group establishing standardized protocols for multibeam sonar calibration, transmission loss removal, and slope-corrected ensonified area calculations.

2. **Lurton, X., & Lamarche, G. (Eds.). (2015).** *Backscatter measurements by seafloor-mapping sonars: Guidelines and recommendations.* A collective report by members of the GeoHab Backscatter Working Group, 150 p.
   * The exhaustive international handbook detailing the physical sonar equations, transducer beam patterns, TVG handling, and Angular Range Analysis (ARA) for marine habitat mapping.

3. **Jackson, D. R., Winebrenner, D. P., & Ishimaru, A. (1986).** *Application of the composite roughness model to high-frequency bottom backscattering.* The Journal of the Acoustical Society of America, 79(5), 1410–1422. DOI: [10.1121/1.393669](https://doi.org/10.1121/1.393669).
   * Foundational acoustic physics paper deriving the composite roughness and volume scattering model (the Jackson model) used worldwide to invert backscatter curves into physical sediment grain size.

4. **Fonseca, L., & Mayer, L. (2007).** *Remote estimation of surficial seafloor properties through the angular response of multibeam backscatter.* Marine Geophysical Researches, 28(2), 119–132. DOI: [10.1007/s11001-007-9019-3](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11001-007-9019-3).
   * Derives the Angular Range Analysis (ARA) inversion engine implemented in QPS FMGT (Geocoder), demonstrating objective sediment classification using true DEM grazing angles.

5. **Caress, D. W., & Chayes, D. N. (1996).** *Improved processing of Hydrosweep DS multibeam data on the R/V Maurice Ewing.* Marine Geophysical Researches, 18(6), 631–650. DOI: [10.1007/BF00313878](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00313878).
   * Primary citation for MB-System, introducing automated swath bathymetry cleaning paired with open-source backscatter angular normalization (`mbbackangle`) and mosaicking (`mbmosaic`).

---

### Terrestrial LiDAR Radiometric Calibration

6. **Kashani, A. G., Olsen, M. J., Parrish, C. E., & Wilson, N. (2015).** *A review of LIDAR radiometric processing: From ad hoc intensity correction to rigorous calibration.* Sensors, 15(11), 28099–28128. DOI: [10.3390/s151128099](https://doi.org/10.3390/s151128099).
   * Comprehensive review detailing the physics of laser return amplitude, range-squared loss, incidence angle normalization using DEM normals, and empirical calibration using spectral targets.

7. **Höfle, B., & Pfeifer, N. (2007).** *Correction of laser scanning intensity data: Data and model-driven approaches.* ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing, 62(6), 415–433. DOI: [10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2007.05.008](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2007.05.008).
   * Derives the radar range equation applied to airborne laser scanning, proving that combining true 3D surface slope angles with distance normalization isolates true physical target reflectance.

8. **American Society for Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing (ASPRS). (2019).** *LAS Specification 1.4 - R15.* ASPRS Standards Committee, Bethesda, MD.
   * Authoritative specification standardizing the 16-bit integer `Intensity` attribute and multi-wavelength point records across the global airborne LiDAR industry.
