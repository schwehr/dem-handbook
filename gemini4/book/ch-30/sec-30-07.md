# Chapter 30: Cryospheric and Subglacial / Sub-Ice-Shelf Bed Topography

*Mapping the shifting ice surfaces of the poles and mountain glaciers—and the hidden bedrock continents beneath kilometers of ice.*

---

## 30.7 Curated Key References

The mathematical modeling of polar ice sheet elevation, subglacial bed topography, and sub-ice-shelf ocean cavities represents a landmark synthesis of glaciological continuum mechanics, spaceborne stereophotogrammetry, and airborne radar geophysics. Below are the foundational peer-reviewed scientific papers, dataset specifications, and algorithmic benchmarks.

---

### Subglacial Bed Topography and Mass Conservation

1. **Morlighem, M., Williams, C. N., Rignot, E., An, L., Arndt, J. E., Bamber, J. L., ... & Zinglersen, K. B. (2017).** *BedMachine v3: Complete bed topography and ocean bathymetry mapping of Greenland from multibeam echo sounding combined with mass conservation.* Geophysical Research Letters, 44(21), 11051–11061. DOI: [10.1002/2017GL074954](https://doi.org/10.1002/2017GL074954).
   * Seminal paper deriving the 2D mass conservation inversion equation and demonstrating the elimination of artificial Kriged bedrock dams across Greenland's marine-terminating glaciers.

2. **Morlighem, M., Rignot, E., Binder, T., Blankenship, D., Casassa, R., Cui, X., ... & Young, D. A. (2020).** *Deep glacial troughs and stabilizing ridges unveiled beneath the margins of the Antarctic ice sheet.* Nature Geoscience, 13(2), 132–137. DOI: [10.1038/s41561-019-0510-8](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41561-019-0510-8).
   * Documents BedMachine Antarctica, compiling 1.5 million line-kilometers of radar soundings and discovering Earth's deepest subglacial canyon beneath Denman Glacier ($3,500\text{ m}$ below sea level).

3. **Fretwell, P., Pritchard, H. D., Vaughan, D. G., Bamber, J. L., Barrand, N. E., Bell, R., ... & Zirizzotti, A. (2013).** *Bedmap2: improved ice bed, surface and thickness datasets for Antarctica.* The Cryosphere, 7(1), 375–393. DOI: [10.5194/tc-7-375-2013](https://doi.org/10.5194/tc-7-375-2013).
   * The benchmark compilation of continental Antarctic ice thickness and bedrock topography prior to mass-conservation inversion, detailing airborne radio-echo sounding coverage.

---

### High-Resolution Polar Surface Models and Co-Registration

4. **Howat, I. M., Porter, C., Smith, B. E., Noh, M. J., & Morin, P. (2019).** *The Reference Elevation Model of Antarctica.* The Cryosphere, 13(2), 665–674. DOI: [10.5194/tc-13-665-2019](https://doi.org/10.5194/tc-13-665-2019).
   * Documents the creation of REMA, detailing petascale stereophotogrammetry from WorldView imagery, SETSM processing, and ICESat laser altimetry co-registration.

5. **Noh, M. J., & Howat, I. M. (2015).** *Automated stereo-photogrammetric DEM generation at high latitudes: Surface Extraction with TIN-based Search-space Minimization (SETSM) validation and demonstration over glaciated terrain.* GIScience & Remote Sensing, 52(2), 198–217. DOI: [10.1080/15481603.2015.1008621](https://doi.org/10.1080/15481603.2015.1008621).
   * The foundational algorithmic paper for SETSM, proving robust, fully automated 3D surface reconstruction over low-contrast polar snowfields without manual ground control.

6. **Nuth, C., & Kääb, A. (2011).** *Co-registration and bias corrections of DEMs.* The Cryosphere, 5(1), 271–290. DOI: [10.5194/tc-5-271-2011](https://doi.org/10.5194/tc-5-271-2011).
   * Landmark analytical formulation for 3D DEM co-registration over stable non-glaciated terrain, solving for sub-pixel horizontal shifts, elevation-dependent biases, and aspect-correlated errors.

---

### Ice Shelf Hydrostatics and Radar Firn Penetration

7. **Griggs, J. A., & Bamber, J. L. (2011).** *Antarctic ice-shelf thickness from satellite radar altimetry.* Journal of Glaciology, 57(203), 485–498. DOI: [10.3189/002214311796905659](https://doi.org/10.3189/002214311796905659).
   * Exhaustive formulation of hydrostatic buoyancy equilibrium for floating ice shelves, deriving the sensitivity of ice thickness to firn air content ($h_{\text{air}}$) and seawater density.

8. **Nilsson, J., Vallelonga, P., Simonsen, S. B., Sorensen, L. S., Forsberg, R., Dahl-Jensen, D., ... & Hvidberg, C. S. (2015).** *External validation of CryoSat-2 surface elevations using airborne laser scanning and ice core data on the Greenland ice sheet.* The Cryosphere, 9(2), 673–689. DOI: [10.5194/tc-9-673-2015](https://doi.org/10.5194/tc-9-673-2015).
   * Validates Ku-band radar altimeter penetration into polar firn against airborne laser scanning and physical ice cores, quantifying multi-meter dielectric penetration depths.
