## 18.9 Curated Key References

The seminal publications, foundational mathematical treatises, and modern open-source specifications listed below establish the physical, information-theoretic, and computational foundations of spatial resolution, numerical precision, and terrain super-resolution.

---

### Foundational and Information Theory Literature

* **Billingsley, F. C.** (1965). Processing of Mariner 4 pictures. *SPIE Journal*, 4(1), 147–152.  
  *The historic paper coining the word "pixel" (picture element) for discrete digital image reconstruction at NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory.*

* **Horn, B. K. P.** (1975). Obtaining shape from shading information. In P. H. Winston (Ed.), *The Psychology of Computer Vision* (pp. 115–155). New York: McGraw-Hill.  
  *The pioneering formulation of Shape-from-Shading (photoclinometry), deriving the non-linear reflectance map equation relating optical radiance to physical surface gradients.*

* **Horn, B. K. P.** (1990). Height and gradient from shading. *International Journal of Computer Vision*, 5(1), 37–75. DOI: [10.1007/BF00056772](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00056772)  
  *Derives the variational Euler-Lagrange PDE for height reconstruction from shading with integrability and smoothness constraints.*

* **Manning, R.** (1891). On the flow of water in open channels and pipes. *Transactions of the Institution of Civil Engineers of Ireland*, 20, 161–207.  
  *The foundational hydraulic paper establishing the empirical Manning equation and roughness formulation governing open-channel fluid mechanics.*

* **Shannon, C. E.** (1948). A mathematical theory of communication. *Bell System Technical Journal*, 27(3), 379–423. DOI: [10.1002/j.1538-7305.1948.tb01338.x](https://doi.org/10.1002/j.1538-7305.1948.tb01338.x)  
  *The landmark treatise establishing information theory, entropy, and the mathematical sampling theorem defining the Nyquist limit.*

### Modern Super-Resolution, Machine Learning, and Software

* **Beyer, R. A., Alexandrov, O., & McMichael, S.** (2018). The Ames Stereo Pipeline: NASA's open source software for deriving and processing terrain data. *Earth and Space Science*, 5(9), 537–548. DOI: [10.1029/2018EA000409](https://doi.org/10.1029/2018EA000409)  
  *Authoritative documentation of the NASA Ames Stereo Pipeline, detailing the implementation of the altimetry-anchored Shape-from-Shading (`sfs`) tool for lunar and planetary DEMs.*

* **Ledig, C., Theis, L., Huszár, F., Caballero, J., Cunningham, A., Acosta, A., Aitken, A., Tejani, A., Totz, J., Wang, Z., & Shi, W.** (2017). Photo-realistic single image super-resolution using a generative adversarial network. *Proceedings of the IEEE Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR)*, 4681–4690. DOI: [10.1109/CVPR.2017.19](https://doi.org/10.1109/CVPR.2017.19)  
  *The foundational paper introducing SRGAN, establishing perceptual adversarial loss functions for deep learning super-resolution.*

* **Stewart, A. J., Robinson, C., Corley, I. A., Ortiz, A., Lavista Ferres, J. M., & Banerjee, A.** (2022). TorchGeo: Deep learning with geospatial data. *Proceedings of the 30th International Conference on Advances in Geographic Information Systems (SIGSPATIAL '22)*, 1–12. DOI: [10.1145/3557915.3560953](https://doi.org/10.1145/3557915.3560953)  
  *Introduced the TorchGeo library, standardizing geospatial deep learning data loaders, bounding-box indexers, and spatial cross-validation strategies.*

* **Wang, X., Yu, K., Wu, S., Gu, J., Liu, Y., Dong, C., Qiao, Y., & Change Loy, C.** (2018). ESRGAN: Enhanced super-resolution generative adversarial networks. *Proceedings of the European Conference on Computer Vision (ECCV) Workshops*, 63–79. DOI: [10.1007/978-3-030-11021-5_5](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-11021-5_5)  
  *Pioneered the Residual-in-Residual Dense Block (RRDB) architecture for high-perceptual-quality single-image super-resolution.*
