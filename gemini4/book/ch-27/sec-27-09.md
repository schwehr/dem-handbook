# Chapter 27: Legal, Privacy, Sovereignty, National Security, and Cost Optimization

*The human, legal, geopolitical, and economic dimensions of collecting and publishing 3D models of the world.*

---

## 27.9 Curated Key References

The legal, economic, ethical, and geopolitical frameworks governing digital elevation modeling represent a convergence of constitutional law, international treaties, economic decision theory, and open-source governance. The references below provide the authoritative legal statutes, judicial precedents, and peer-reviewed scientific works underpinning spatial data stewardship.

---

### International Treaties, Law, and Privacy

1. **United Nations. (1982).** *United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea (UNCLOS).* United Nations Treaty Series, Vol. 1833, pp. 3–397. Available online: [https://www.un.org/depts/los/convention_agreements/texts/unclos/unclos_e.pdf](https://www.un.org/depts/los/convention_agreements/texts/unclos/unclos_e.pdf).
   * The fundamental international treaty establishing maritime zones, the 200 nautical mile Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ), Part XIII Marine Scientific Research rules, and Article 76 criteria (Foot of Slope, Gardiner sediment thickness, and 2,500 m isobath) governing Extended Continental Shelf claims.

2. **Carroll, S. R., Garba, I., Figueroa-Rodríguez, O. L., Holbrook, J., Lovett, R., Materechera, S., ... & Hudson, M. (2020).** *The CARE Principles for Indigenous Data Governance.* Data Science Journal, 19, 43. DOI: [10.5334/dsj-2020-043](https://doi.org/10.5334/dsj-2020-043).
   * Foundational articulation of the CARE Principles (Collective Benefit, Authority to Control, Responsibility, Ethics) governing Indigenous sovereignty over tribal lands, sacred sites, and high-resolution spatial data.

3. **Carroll, S. R., Herczog, E., Hudson, M., Russell, K., & Stall, S. (2021).** *Operationalizing the CARE and FAIR Principles for Indigenous data futures.* Scientific Data, 8(1), 108. DOI: [10.1038/s41597-021-00892-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-021-00892-0).
   * Operational blueprint integrating CARE Indigenous data governance alongside FAIR open-science repository metadata standards.

4. **European Parliament and Council. (2016).** *Regulation (EU) 2016/679 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data (General Data Protection Regulation — GDPR).* Official Journal of the European Union, L 119, 4 May 2016, pp. 1–88.
   * Authoritative European data protection statute establishing legal obligations to blur, redact, or strip Personally Identifiable Information (faces, license plates, private curtilage) from mobile mapping streams.

5. **UNESCO. (2001).** *Convention on the Protection of the Underwater Cultural Heritage.* United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization, Paris. Available online: [https://www.unesco.org/en/underwater-heritage/2001-convention](https://www.unesco.org/en/underwater-heritage/2001-convention).
   * International treaty prohibiting commercial exploitation and looting of historical shipwrecks, establishing legal requirements to restrict high-resolution bathymetric coordinates of sensitive heritage sites.

6. **Supreme Court of the United States. (1955).** *Indian Towing Co. v. United States*, 350 U.S. 61.
   * Landmark judicial decision establishing that while the federal government has discretion whether to provide a navigational aid, it is legally liable under the Federal Tort Claims Act for operational negligence in maintaining it.

7. **U.S. Court of Appeals for the Fifth Circuit. (1971).** *De Bardeleben Marine Corp. v. United States*, 451 F.2d 140.
   * Foundational admiralty ruling defining the government's duty of reasonable care in publishing official nautical charts alongside mariners' responsibility to apply *Notices to Mariners*.

### Economic Decision Theory, 3DEP ROI, and Value of Information

8. **Macauley, M. K. (2006).** *The value of information: Measuring the economic benefits of earth science research.* Space Policy, 22(4), 274–282. DOI: [10.1016/j.spacepol.2006.08.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.spacepol.2006.08.003).
   * Foundational paper formulating Bayesian Value of Information (VoI) models to quantify the economic return on investment of satellite remote sensing and national elevation programs.

9. **National Research Council (NRC). (2007).** *Elevation Data for Floodplain Mapping.* The National Academies Press, Washington, D.C. DOI: [10.17226/11829](https://doi.org/10.17226/11829).
   * Landmark National Academies consensus study demonstrating that legacy 2D contours were inadequate for FEMA floodplain engineering and recommending a coordinated national high-resolution LiDAR elevation program.

10. **Snyder, G. I. (2012).** *The 3D Elevation Program: Summary of Program Direction.* U.S. Geological Survey Fact Sheet 2012-3089, Reston, VA. DOI: [10.3133/fs20123089](https://doi.org/10.3133/fs20123089).
    * Concise USGS policy summary outlining the 3DEP multi-agency partnership model ("map once, use many times") and NEEA cost-benefit foundation.

11. **Sugarbaker, L. J., Constance, E. W., Heidemann, H. K., Jason, A. L., Lukas, V., Saghy, D. L., & Stoker, J. M. (2014).** *The 3D Elevation Program Initiative—A Call for Action.* U.S. Geological Survey Circular 1399, Reston, VA. DOI: [10.3133/cir1399](https://doi.org/10.3133/cir1399).
    * Comprehensive USGS implementation blueprint detailing 3DEP Quality Levels, coalition funding mechanisms, and national economic return on investment.

12. **International Hydrographic Organization (IHO). (2022).** *Guidance on Crowdsourced Bathymetry (Publication B-12, Edition 3.0.0).* International Hydrographic Organization, Monaco. Available online: [https://iho.int/en/crowdsourced-bathymetry](https://iho.int/en/crowdsourced-bathymetry).
    * Authoritative technical and policy guidance for collecting, validating, and submitting opportunistic depth data from commercial ships and citizen mariners to the IHO DCDB.

### Algorithmic Optimization and Open Standards

13. **Calder, B. R., & Mayer, L. A. (2003).** *Automatic processing of high-rate, high-density multibeam echosounder data.* Geochemistry, Geophysics, Geosystems, 4(6), 1048. DOI: [10.1029/2002GC000486](https://doi.org/10.1029/2002GC000486).
    * Primary citation for the CUBE algorithm, deriving the multi-hypothesis Kalman filter framework that slashed hydrographic manual processing costs by over 80%.

14. **European Parliament and Council. (2007).** *Directive 2007/2/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council establishing an Infrastructure for Spatial Information in the European Community (INSPIRE).* Official Journal of the European Union, L 108, 25 April 2007, pp. 1–14.
    * Legislative framework mandating open, standardized geodetic access to European elevation grids (Theme 11), bathymetry, and spatial metadata.
