# Chapter 27: Legal, Privacy, Sovereignty, National Security, and Cost Optimization

*The human, legal, geopolitical, and economic dimensions of collecting and publishing 3D models of the world.*

---

## 27.9 Curated Key References

The legal, economic, ethical, and geopolitical frameworks governing digital elevation modeling represent a convergence of constitutional law, international treaties, economic decision theory, and open-source governance. The references below provide the authoritative legal statutes, judicial precedents, and peer-reviewed scientific works underpinning spatial data stewardship.

---

### International Treaties, Law, and Privacy

1. **United Nations. (1982).** *United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea (UNCLOS).* United Nations Treaty Series, 1833, 3–397.
   * The fundamental international treaty establishing maritime zones, the 200 nautical mile Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ), and Article 76 criteria (Foot of Slope and 2,500m isobath) governing sovereign claims to the Extended Continental Shelf.

2. **Carroll, S. R., Herczog, E., Hudson, M., Russell, K., & Stall, S. (2021).** *Operationalizing the CARE and FAIR Principles for Indigenous data futures.* Scientific Data, 8(1), 108. DOI: [10.1038/s41597-021-00892-0](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-021-00892-0).
   * Seminal paper establishing the CARE Principles for Indigenous Data Governance (Collective Benefit, Authority to Control, Responsibility, Ethics) in spatial, genomic, and environmental data repositories.

3. **European Parliament and Council. (2016).** *Regulation (EU) 2016/679 on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of personal data (General Data Protection Regulation - GDPR).* Official Journal of the European Union, L 119, 1–88.
   * Authoritative European data protection statute establishing legal obligations to blur, redact, or strip Personally Identifiable Information (faces, license plates, private curtilage) from mobile mapping streams.

4. **UNESCO. (2001).** *Convention on the Protection of the Underwater Cultural Heritage.* United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization, Paris.
   * International treaty prohibiting the commercial exploitation and looting of historical shipwrecks, establishing legal requirements to restrict high-resolution bathymetric coordinates of sensitive heritage sites.

5. **Supreme Court of the United States. (1955).** *Indian Towing Co. v. United States, 350 U.S. 61.*
   * Landmark judicial decision establishing that while the federal government has discretion whether to provide a navigational aid, it is legally liable under the Federal Tort Claims Act for operational negligence in maintaining it.

### Economic Decision Theory and Value of Information

6. **Macauley, M. K. (2006).** *The value of information: Measuring the economic benefits of earth science research.* Space Policy, 22(4), 274–282. DOI: [10.1016/j.spacepol.2006.08.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.spacepol.2006.08.003).
   * Foundational paper formulating Bayesian Value of Information (VoI) models to quantify the economic return on investment of satellite remote sensing and national elevation programs.

7. **National Research Council (NRC). (2012).** *Designing a National Digital Elevation Model (NDEM).* The National Academies Press, Washington, DC. DOI: [10.17226/13506](https://doi.org/10.17226/13506).
   * Exhaustive economic and technical assessment that led directly to the creation of the USGS 3D Elevation Program (3DEP), documenting the multi-billion-dollar societal return of high-density airborne LiDAR.

8. **International Hydrographic Organization (IHO). (2020).** *Guidance on Crowdsourced Bathymetry (Publication B-12, Edition 2.0.3).* International Hydrographic Bureau, Monaco.
   * Authoritative technical and policy guidance for collecting, validating, and submitting opportunistic depth data from commercial ships and citizen mariners to the IHO DCDB.

---

### Algorithmic Optimization and Open Standards

9. **Calder, B. R., & Mayer, L. A. (2003).** *Automatic processing of high-rate, high-density multibeam echosounder data.* Geochemistry, Geophysics, Geosystems, 4(6), 1048. DOI: [10.1029/2002GC000486](https://doi.org/10.1029/2002GC000486).
   * Primary citation for the CUBE algorithm, deriving the multi-hypothesis Kalman filter framework that slashed hydrographic manual processing costs by over 80%.

10. **European Commission. (2007).** *Directive 2007/2/EC of the European Parliament and of the Council establishing an Infrastructure for Spatial Information in the European Community (INSPIRE).* Official Journal of the European Union, L 108, 1–14.
    * Legislative framework mandating open, standardized geodetic access to European elevation grids, bathymetry, and spatial metadata.
